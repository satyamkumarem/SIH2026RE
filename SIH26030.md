## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
The Smart India Hackathon Problem Statement **SIH26030**, titled *"Automated Cable Specimen Preparation System for IS 10810 and IS 7098 Compliance,"* sponsored by the **Department of Consumer Affairs (DoCA)** under the **Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution**, seeks an electro-mechanical smart automation solution to eliminate manual errors and inconsistencies in preparing cable test specimens [PS]. Physical testing of electrical cables—such as conductor resistance, insulation tensile strength, hot set, water absorption, and thermal ageing—requires strict adherence to Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications, specifically **IS 10810** (Parts 2, 7, 33) and **IS 7098** (Parts 1 & 2) [PS]. Currently, sample preparation involves manual cable cutting, manual straightening, hand-slicing PVC/XLPE/HDPE sheaths, and manual punching of dumbbell-shaped test pieces [PS]. These manual operations introduce cut irregularities, non-uniform thickness, edge micro-tears, and thermal/mechanical prestrain, leading to invalid test results, high rejection rates, operator safety hazards, and audit non-compliance in certified laboratories [INFERENCE]. 

The proposed solution is a compact, benchtop automated cable specimen preparation machine controlled by a embedded microcontroller/PLC system with touchscreen HMI. It features an automated motorized feeding and self-centering clamping mechanism, an integrated contactless/optical diameter sensor, a high-precision multi-axis motorized cutter and circumferential stripper module, an integrated dumbbell die press, automated specimen-vs-waste ejection, and Category 3 hardware safety interlocks [INFERENCE].

### Fundamental System Objective
To design and construct an automated electro-mechanical machine with integrated auto-diameter sensing, self-centering feed rollers, programmable depth cutting/stripping, dumbbell shape punching, and PLC/HMI closed-loop control that prepares compliant cable test specimens according to IS 10810 and IS 7098 standards without human bias or safety risks [PS].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

### Field & Operational Realities

1.  **Material Hardness Variability:**
    *   **PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride):** Flexible to semi-rigid (Shore A 70–95) [KNOWN]. Easy to cut at room temperature but tears if blade is dull [INFERENCE].
    *   **XLPE (Cross-linked Polyethylene):** Tough, rubbery elastomeric memory (Shore D 50–65) [KNOWN]. Resists cutting; requires sharp tungsten carbide edges and steady feeding force without thermal melting [INFERENCE].
    *   **HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene):** Rigid, high yield strength (Shore D 60–70) [KNOWN]. Requires high clamping force to prevent slipping and higher cutting force [INFERENCE].
2.  **Lab Footprint & Power Constraints:**
    *   Standard cable testing labs run on **220V AC single-phase** power, with standard compressed air supply (6–8 bar) available in industrial QA labs [KNOWN].
    *   The equipment must fit on a standard laboratory workbench (Max footprint: $800 \times 600 \times 600 \text{ mm}$, weight $< 45 \text{ kg}$) [ASSUMPTION].
3.  **Cable Diameter & Profile Diversity:**
    *   Cable outer diameters range from **4 mm** (single-core LT wire) up to **35 mm** (multi-core armored HT cable sheaths) [RESEARCHED]. The clamping and sensing mechanism must accommodate this range seamlessly [INFERENCE].
4.  **No Thermal Degradation Permitted:**
    *   Thermal cutting (e.g. heated wire or laser slitting) **cannot be used** because high temperatures alter the cross-linking, molecular structure, and mechanical tensile properties of XLPE and PVC, invalidating IS 10810 Part 7 and Part 11 testing [RESEARCHED]. Cold mechanical slitting is mandatory [INFERENCE].

## 6. Research Questions

1.  **RQ-1:** What are the exact dimensional tolerances for IS 10810 Part 7 dumbbell specimens?
    *   *Finding:* Standard dumbbell dies require a 75 mm overall length, 12.5 mm width at ends, 4 mm width of narrow parallel portion, and gauge length of 20 mm or 50 mm, with specimen thickness between 0.8 mm and 2.0 mm [RESEARCHED].
2.  **RQ-2:** How does IS 10810 Part 2 require aluminum conductor wires to be prepared?
    *   *Finding:* Aluminum conductors must be stripped of all insulation without nicking or scratching the metal surface. The required gauge length is 250 mm with straight alignment for gripping in a Universal Testing Machine (UTM) [RESEARCHED].
3.  **RQ-3:** What is the specimen geometry for IS 10810 Part 33 (Water Absorption Gravimetric Test)?
    *   *Finding:* Requires thin cylindrical slices or tubular strips of insulation/sheath cut cleanly perpendicular to the cable axis with uniform thickness and known surface area [RESEARCHED].
4.  **RQ-4:** What is the required blade positioning resolution to strip sheath without nicking inner conductors?
    *   *Finding:* Radial blade positioning resolution must be $\le 0.05 \text{ mm}$ (50 microns) driven by micro-stepper lead screws [INFERENCE].
5.  **RQ-5:** What non-contact optical sensing technology is reliable for measuring black carbon XLPE/HDPE cable diameters?
    *   *Finding:* Standard infrared diffuse sensors fail on high-absorbance black sheaths. A laser thrum-beam curtain sensor or high-precision mechanical encoder linked to self-centering clamping jaws is required [RESEARCHED].

### Justification
1.  **Compliance Preservation:** Cold mechanical cutting via hardened tungsten carbide blades preserves the virgin mechanical, elastomeric, and cross-linked properties of PVC/XLPE/HDPE without thermal damage [RESEARCHED].
2.  **Feasibility & Cost:** Can be fully fabricated within hackathon timelines using precision 3D printed housings, aluminum T-slot extrusions, NEMA steppers, linear rails, and standard microcontroller boards [INFERENCE].
3.  **Rejection of Alternatives:**
    *   *Approach 3 (Laser)* was rejected because heat-affected zones (HAZ) compromise material tensile strength, directly violating IS 10810 Part 7 [RESEARCHED].
    *   *Approach 1 (Pure Heavy Pneumatics)* was rejected for the hackathon prototype due to transportability limits, compressed air dependence during stage pitching, and higher component lead times [INFERENCE].

### Data-Flow Narrative

1.  **Initialization:** Operator powers on system, safety canopy interlock check verifies enclosure is closed [INFERENCE]. System performs homing sequence on all axes [INFERENCE].
2.  **Recipe Selection:** Operator selects desired test method on HMI (e.g., "IS 10810 Part 7 - XLPE Sheath Dumbbell") [INFERENCE].
3.  **Cable Loading & OD Sensing:** Cable sample is inserted into feed funnel. Motorized feed rollers engage. The integrated diameter sensor measures outer diameter (OD) and feeds the value to the control unit [INFERENCE].
4.  **Auto-Clamping & Depth Calculation:** Self-centering V-clamps close to grip cable axis precisely. Controller calculates required radial blade plunge depth based on OD and stored sheath thickness profile [INFERENCE].
5.  **Circumferential & Longitudinal Slitting:**
    *   The slitting module plunges the carbide blade to calculated depth [INFERENCE].
    *   The rotary cutting head rotates $360^\circ$ around the cable for circumferential end-cuts [INFERENCE].
    *   The carriage traverses axially along the cable length to perform a clean longitudinal slit [INFERENCE].
6.  **Core Extraction & Sheath Flattening:** The inner conductor core is automatically pushed forward into the conductor collection chute. The severed sheath ribbon is drawn into the flattening rollers leading to the dumbbell punch station [INFERENCE].
7.  **Dumbbell Punching (for IS 10810 Pt 7):** The sheath ribbon is indexed under the IS 10810 Part 7 standard die. The pneumatic/toggle punch fires, stamping a clean dumbbell specimen [INFERENCE].
8.  **Ejection & Waste Sorting:** The ejection gate switches angle: the punched dumbbell specimen falls into the "Validated Specimen Bin", while leftover edge trims drop into the "Waste Bin" [INFERENCE]. HMI displays execution summary and logs batch data [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Is AI/ML Strictly Required for Specimen Preparation?
**NO.** The primary task of measuring cable diameter, positioning cutting blades, stripping sheaths, and punching dumbbell dies is a **deterministic mechanical precision problem** best solved by closed-loop mechatronics, micro-stepping motors, and geometric math [INFERENCE]. Forcing AI into blade trajectory control introduces non-deterministic latency and potential positioning failure [INFERENCE].

### Optional/Secondary AI Application: Computer Vision Quality Verification
A lightweight computer vision (CV) system can be added strictly as an **inspection stage** after specimen ejection [INFERENCE].

1.  **Exact Problem Solved:** Automated post-cut inspection of prepared dumbbell specimens to verify edge smoothness and detect micro-nicks, burrs, or dimensional non-conformance before tensile testing [INFERENCE].
2.  **Input:** High-resolution RGB macro image of the prepared specimen taken inside the ejection chute under LED backlighting [INFERENCE].
3.  **Output:** Pass/Fail flag with detected defect overlay (e.g. "Edge Nick Detected at Gauge Center - Specimen Rejected") [INFERENCE].
4.  **Why Rules Cannot Solve It easily:** Visual edge irregularities and micro-tears on semi-flexible polymer cuts are variable in shape and contrast, making classical thresholding sensitive to light shadows [INFERENCE].
5.  **Data Required:** ~500 images of compliant vs. defective (nicked/burred/crooked) dumbbell cuts [ASSUMPTION].
6.  **Evaluation:** Precision-Recall AUC on defect detection ($\ge 98\%$ recall required to prevent defective specimens from entering UTM testing) [ASSUMPTION].
7.  **Failure Mode & Fallback:** If CV model fails or camera is obscured, system defaults to manual visual inspection prompt on HMI without stopping mechanical production [INFERENCE].
8.  **Training Required:** Yes, edge-detection model trained via YOLOv8-Nano / OpenCV contour analysis [INFERENCE].
9.  **Fine-Tuning:** Fine-tuned on laboratory lighting conditions [INFERENCE].
10. **RAG Required:** No [INFERENCE].
11. **Computer Vision Required:** Yes (Optional inspection stage) [INFERENCE].
12. **Speech Processing Required:** No [INFERENCE].
