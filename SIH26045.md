## 1. Executive Snapshot

`IP-SAKTI Sahayak` is an authoritative, multilingual, source-cited Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant integrated with a relational Knowledge Graph and agentic orchestration. [PS] Designed specifically for the Ministry of Ayush and the All India Institute of Ayurveda, it provides precise legal and regulatory guidance across overlapping intellectual property (IP), Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS), and drug/food regulatory frameworks in national and international jurisdictions. [PS] The core innovation lies in its structured formulation-classification decision engine, an explicit national-vs-international jurisdiction toggle, source citation with statutory precision, and safe abstention guardrails aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. ## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1.  **Regulatory Volatility:** India's biodiversity and patent landscape underwent massive shifts with the Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act 2023, the Biological Diversity Rules 2024, the ABS Regulations 2025, and the WIPO GRATK Treaty 2024. [RESEARCHED] The system must maintain strict version control over its corpus. [INFERENCE]
2.  **Bandwidth & Connectivity in Rural AYUSH Hubs:** Rural MSMEs, cultivators, and traditional practitioners in Tier-2/3 areas often face unreliable 3G/4G connectivity. [KNOWN] The mobile-first frontend must support offline caching for core classification decision trees. [INFERENCE]
3.  **Linguistic Diversity & Literacy:** Technical IP and legal terms (e.g., "prior art", "benefit sharing", "novelty", "synergy") do not translate directly into vernacular languages. [INFERENCE] The system must use Bhashini's IndicTrans models complemented by an Ayurvedic/IP legal dictionary for simplified vernacular terms. [INFERENCE]
4.  **Hardware Limitations:** Student teams operating under hackathon conditions cannot deploy massive 70B parameter models locally on edge hardware. [INFERENCE] Cloud deployment on cost-efficient GPUs or quantized local LLMs (e.g., Llama-3-8B-Instruct) is mandatory. [ASSUMPTION]
5.  **Strict Anti-Hallucination Bar:** Generating a non-existent statutory section or fake patent citation in a legal tool can lead to severe liability or financial loss for a startup. [INFERENCE] Safe abstention triggers must strictly activate when similarity scores fall below threshold. [INFERENCE]

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Naive RAG with Standard Vector Database (ChromaDB + GPT-4o-mini)
*   **Mechanism:** Chunk all legal PDFs into 512-token segments, generate dense embeddings using OpenAI text-embedding-3-small, store in ChromaDB, and retrieve top-k chunks for LLM context generation. [INFERENCE]
*   **Problem Fit:** Moderate. Handles basic legal questions but struggles with complex relational logic across multiple statutes (e.g., linking a plant species to a classical text, then to a patent claim bar, then to an ABS fee calculation). [INFERENCE]
*   **Accuracy Potential:** 70-75% (High hallucination risk on legal citations). [INFERENCE]
*   **Data Requirements:** Vectorized PDFs of legal texts. [INFERENCE]
*   **Complexity:** Low. Fast implementation. [INFERENCE]
*   **Explainability:** Low. Rationale depends purely on chunk similarity. [INFERENCE]
*   **Cost:** Minimal cloud costs ($10-$20/month). [INFERENCE]
*   **Scalability:** Moderate. [INFERENCE]
*   **Hackathon Feasibility:** 10/10. [INFERENCE]
*   **Production Feasibility:** 4/10 (Fails legal accuracy and multi-step reasoning requirements). [INFERENCE]

### Approach 2: Knowledge Graph RAG (GraphRAG using Neo4j + LlamaIndex + Local LLM)
*   **Mechanism:** Extract entities (Plants, Statutes, Sections, Formulations, Treaties, IPC Codes) and build a Neo4j Knowledge Graph. Combine Cypher graph queries with vector retrieval for multi-hop legal reasoning. [INFERENCE]
*   **Problem Fit:** High. Excellent for modeling complex legal relationships (e.g., `(Plant:Tulsi)-[:MENTIONED_IN]->(Book:CharakaSamhita)-[:SUBJECT_TO]->(Section:3p_PatentsAct)`). [INFERENCE]
*   **Accuracy Potential:** 88-92%. [INFERENCE]
*   **Data Requirements:** Structured entity-relationship extraction from legal texts. [INFERENCE]
*   **Complexity:** High. Requires schema modeling and Cypher query generation. [INFERENCE]
*   **Explainability:** High. Explicit graph traversal paths show exactly why a rule applies. [INFERENCE]
*   **Cost:** Low-Medium (Self-hosted Neo4j community edition + local/cloud LLM). [INFERENCE]
*   **Scalability:** High. [INFERENCE]
*   **Hackathon Feasibility:** 6/10 (High modeling overhead for a 36-hour build). [INFERENCE]
*   **Production Feasibility:** 9/10. [INFERENCE]

### Approach 3: Hybrid Agentic GraphRAG with Bhashini Adapter & State-Machine Wizard (RECOMMENDED)

*   **Problem Fit:** Perfect. Fulfills every requirement of SIH26045 explicitly. [INFERENCE]
*   **Accuracy Potential:** 94-98% (Fact-checked by deterministic rule engines and knowledge graph nodes). [INFERENCE]
*   **Data Requirements:** Graph entities + Chunked statutes + Bhashini API key. [INFERENCE]
*   **Complexity:** Moderate-High. Staged implementation allowed. [INFERENCE]
*   **Explainability:** Maximum. Outputs graph trace and statutory clause references. [INFERENCE]
*   **Cost:** Medium (Scalable serverless deployment). [INFERENCE]
*   **Scalability:** Maximum. [INFERENCE]
*   **Hackathon Feasibility:** 9/10 (Can build Stage 1 MVP + Graph core in hackathon window). [INFERENCE]

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

**Selected Approach:** **Approach 3: Hybrid Agentic GraphRAG with Bhashini Adapter & State-Machine Wizard.** [INFERENCE]

**Why Alternatives Were Rejected:**
*   *Approach 1 (Naive Vector RAG)* was rejected because legal text retrieval requires structural logic, not just semantic similarity. Naive RAG frequently misses statutory cross-references (e.g., Section 3(p) reading together with Section 10(4)(d) of the Patents Act) and hallucinates non-existent rule numbers. [INFERENCE]
*   *Approach 2 (Pure GraphRAG without Agents)* was rejected because it lacks deterministic state-machine handling required for the formulation classification flow and ABS fee calculations. Legal guidance requires exact algorithmic rules combined with semantic retrieval. [INFERENCE]

**Why Approach 3 Wins:**
1.  **Staged Implementation Alignment:** Matches the explicit problem statement call for a staged build: "a citation-grounded retrieval MVP first, then the graph and agentic layers, then paid-source connectors and the full multilingual and voice experience." [PS]
2.  **Absolute Zero-Hallucination Guardrails:** Combining deterministic Python rule engines for ABS calculations with Neo4j Knowledge Graph verification ensures statutory citations are 100% real and verifiable. [INFERENCE]
3.  **Complete Requirement Coverage:** Solves formulation classification, dual-jurisdiction separation, TKDL prior-art mapping, Bhashini voice/text translation, and DPDP audit compliance in a single cohesive architecture. [INFERENCE]

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative

1.  **User Input & Translation:** The user asks a question in Hindi/Tamil via voice or text. The frontend captures audio or text and routes it to the Bhashini Integration Layer, converting it into English for internal processing while preserving original language metadata. [RESEARCHED] [INFERENCE]
2.  **Formulation Classification Check:** If the query relates to a new product, the `Formulation Wizard Agent` intercepts the workflow, presenting a <= 4 question dynamic questionnaire (Ingredients, Reference in First-Schedule text, Processing Method, Intended Claims). [PS] [INFERENCE]
3.  **Jurisdiction Routing:** The `Jurisdiction Router Agent` reads the state of the active Jurisdiction Switch (India vs International). [PS]
    *   *If Domestic (India):* Sets retrieval filter to Indian Statutes (Patents Act 2024 Rules, BD Act 2023, FSSAI Ayurveda-Aahar, D&C Act). [PS]
    *   *If International:* Sets retrieval filter to International Treaties (WIPO GRATK 2024, TRIPS, Nagoya, PCT) and target export market rules (US FDA, EMA). [PS]
4.  **Hybrid GraphRAG Search:**
    *   *Vector Search:* Queries Qdrant/ChromaDB for semantic similarity against indexed statutory chunks. [INFERENCE]
    *   *Graph Traversal:* Cypher queries execute on Neo4j to find structural links between botanical species, classical text mentions, patent bars (Section 3(p), Section 3(e)), and ABS requirements. [INFERENCE]
5.  **Deterministic Engine Execution:**
    *   The `ABS Calculator` applies turnover-based logic (BD Rules 2024/2025: 0.2%-0.6% or exemption for cultivated plants/AYUSH practitioners). [RESEARCHED]
    *   The `TKDL Pointer` cross-checks botanical components against IPC Class A61K 36/00 (Herbal Medicinal Preparations) and First-Schedule book indexes. [RESEARCHED]

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### 1. Classification & Jurisdiction Routing Agent
*   **Purpose:** Dynamically guide formulation categorization and select correct legal context windows. [PS]
*   **Model Type:** Small Language Model / Intent Classifier (Fine-tuned Llama-3-8B or GPT-3.5/Llama-3-70B function call). [INFERENCE]
*   **Input:** User prompt + Formulation answers (Ingredients, text reference, claim). [PS]
*   **Output:** Product Class Enum (`CLASSICAL`, `PATENTED_PROPRIETARY`, `NEW_DRUG`, `PHYTOPHARMACEUTICAL`, `AYURVEDA_AAHAR`, `COSMETIC`) + Routing Tag (`DOMESTIC` / `INTERNATIONAL`). [PS] [INFERENCE]
*   **Data Required:** 200 labeled examples of Ayurvedic product profiles. [ASSUMPTION]
*   **Why AI is Preferable:** Unstructured user descriptions of formulations cannot be matched by hardcoded IF-THEN keywords due to complex botanical/synonym phrasing. [INFERENCE]
*   **Evaluation:** Precision/Recall on a benchmark dataset of 50 edge-case AYUSH products. [INFERENCE]
*   **Failure Mode:** Misclassification of product category. [INFERENCE]
*   **Human Oversight:** User is shown classification rationale and can manually override category selection before legal query processing. [INFERENCE]

### 2. Hybrid GraphRAG Citation Synthesis Engine
*   **Purpose:** Synthesize comprehensive legal answers grounded strictly in retrieved statutory chunks and knowledge graph nodes. [PS]
*   **Model Type:** Instruction-tuned LLM (Llama-3-70B-Instruct / GPT-4o-mini). [INFERENCE]
*   **Input:** User query + Retrieved statutory chunks + Neo4j Cypher query results. [INFERENCE]
*   **Output:** Dual-pane structured text with mandatory statutory citations (`[Act, Section X, Rule Y]`). [PS]
*   **Data Required:** Indexed legal vector database and Neo4j graph entities. [INFERENCE]
*   **Why AI is Preferable:** Synthesizing cross-statutory legal logic in natural language requires complex contextual language synthesis. [INFERENCE]
*   **Evaluation:** Citation precision (% of cited clauses actually in context) and Hallucination rate (<1%). [INFERENCE]
*   **Failure Mode:** Generating invalid section numbers or conflating foreign/domestic law. [INFERENCE]
*   **Human Oversight:** Post-synthesis Citation Validator Agent strips unverified citations; persistent legal disclaimer attached. [PS] [INFERENCE]

### 3. Bhashini Multilingual Speech/Text Translation Pipeline
*   **Purpose:** Convert vernacular user input into English and translate system responses back to native language. [PS]
*   **Model Type:** IndicTrans2 (NMT), IndicASR (Speech-to-Text), IndicTTS (Text-to-Speech) via Bhashini ULCA APIs. [RESEARCHED]
*   **Input:** Spoken audio or vernacular text (Hindi, Tamil, Marathi, etc.). [PS]
*   **Output:** English text translation / Native language translated text & audio. [PS]
*   **Data Required:** Bhashini pre-trained government translation models. [RESEARCHED]
*   **Why AI is Preferable:** Multilingual natural language understanding requires deep learning models trained on Indian linguistic nuances. [RESEARCHED]
*   **Evaluation:** BLEU score / WER (Word Error Rate) on legal/herbal terminology test set. [INFERENCE]
*   **Failure Mode:** Mistranslation of technical legal or botanical terms. [INFERENCE]
*   **Human Oversight:** Option to toggle "Show original English legal text" side-by-side with translated text. [INFERENCE]

### **EXPLICIT AI EXCLUSION ZONE:**
*   **ABS Benefit-Sharing Fee Calculation Engine:** **AI SHOULD NOT BE USED HERE.** [INFERENCE]
    *   *Why:* Calculating ABS fees under the Biological Diversity Rules 2024 / ABS Regulations 2025 is a purely deterministic mathematical formula (e.g., Turnover < ₹5 Cr = Exempt; ₹5 Cr-₹50 Cr = 0.2%; Cultivated plant = Exempt). [RESEARCHED] Using an LLM for arithmetic creates severe hallucination risks. A standard Python function MUST execute this calculation. [INFERENCE]

    status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'PENDING', -- PENDING, ASSIGNED, RESOLVED
    facilitator_notes TEXT,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP

### Knowledge Graph Schema (Neo4j Graph Nodes & Edges)

*   **Nodes:** `(:Plant {name, botanical_name, sanskrit_name})`, `(:FirstScheduleText {title, edition})`, `(:Statute {name, jurisdiction})`, `(:Section {number, title})`, `(:ProductClass {type})`, `(:IPCTerm {code, description})`
*   **Relationships:**
    *   `(Plant)-[:MENTIONED_IN]->(FirstScheduleText)`
    *   `(FirstScheduleText)-[:GOVERNED_BY]->(ProductClass)`
    *   `(ProductClass)-[:SUBJECT_TO]->(Statute)`
    *   `(Plant)-[:MAPS_TO_IPC]->(IPCTerm)`
    *   `(Statute)-[:CONTAINS_SECTION]->(Section)`
    *   `(Section)-[:BARS_PATENT]->(ProductClass)`

### Offline & Low-Network Optimization Strategy
1.  **Client-Side Decision Tree Caching:** The 6-category formulation classification wizard and basic ABS threshold rulebook are compiled into a lightweight client-side WebAssembly / JS bundle (~2MB) stored in IndexedDB/PWA cache, allowing users to classify formulations offline. [INFERENCE]
2.  **Bandwidth Reduction:** API requests pass compressed JSON payloads; Bhashini TTS voice audio streaming uses compressed Ogg/Opus codecs. [INFERENCE]
3.  **Synchronization Protocol:** If a user submits a query offline, the request is queued in ServiceWorker IndexedDB and auto-synced when internet connectivity resumes. [INFERENCE]

    *URL:* [WIPO GRATK Treaty](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/23105)  
    *Source Type:* Official International Treaty  
    *Supported Claim:* Mandates source disclosure of genetic resources and traditional knowledge in patent applications globally.  
    *Reliability:* Official Sovereign Source (High) `[RESEARCHED]`

2.  **The Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023 & Biological Diversity Rules, 2024**  
    *URL:* [e-Gazette Biological Diversity Amendment Act 2023](https://egazette.gov.in/WritereadData/2023/247815.pdf)  
    *Source Type:* Official Indian Statute & Statutory Rules  
    *Supported Claim:* Exempts registered AYUSH practitioners and cultivated medicinal plants from NBA prior approval and benefit sharing; establishes digital Certificate of Origin portal.  
    *Reliability:* Official Government Gazette (High) `[RESEARCHED]`

3.  **National Biodiversity Authority ABS Regulations 2025**  
    *URL:* [Down To Earth ABS Rules 2025 Report](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/wildlife-biodiversity/access-and-benefit-sharing-new-rules-for-use-of-biodiversity-put-in-place-by-centre)  
    *Source Type:* Government Notification Analysis  
    *Supported Claim:* Sets turnover-based ABS slabs (0.2% for ₹5 Cr–₹50 Cr, 0.4% for ₹50 Cr–₹250 Cr, 0.6% for >₹250 Cr) and exempts users of cultivated medicinal plants.  
    *Reliability:* Reputable Secondary / Government Gazette (High) `[RESEARCHED]`
