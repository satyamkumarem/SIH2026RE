## 1. Executive Snapshot

This dossier presents a complete technical blueprint, research foundation, and hardware-software system design for Problem Statement **SIH26213**, issued by **AICTE, MIC-Student Innovation** under the **Hardware** category and **Fitness & Sports** theme [PS]. To address the open-ended mandate of boosting physical activity and assisting citizens in maintaining fitness through student innovation, we propose **"FlexiFit IoT"**—a modular, ultra-low-cost, smart wearable sensor band coupled with an edge-AI mobile companion application [INFERENCE]. The hardware band utilizes an ESP32-S3 microcontroller with a 6-axis Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU - MPU6050/BNO085) and an optical photoplethysmography (PPG) heart rate sensor (MAX30102) [ASSUMPTION]. Attached to user limbs, dumbbells, or resistance bands, it captures real-time motion, posture, repetition velocity, and cardiovascular exertion [RESEARCHED]. The companion mobile app leverages MediaPipe Pose computer vision for dual-angle posture verification and Edge Impulse TinyML models on-device for real-time rep counting and biomechanical form correction [RESEARCHED]. Tailored for Indian students, gym-goers, and rural youth, the system aligns directly with the Government of India's **Fit India Movement** and the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports' age-appropriate fitness protocols [RESEARCHED].

## 6. Research Questions

1.  *Can a single 6-axis IMU mounted on the wrist or ankle reliably distinguish between different strength exercises (e.g., bicep curls vs. lateral raises vs. squats) without camera aid?*
    *   **Finding:** Yes, research demonstrates that 1D-CNN or Random Forest classifiers trained on tri-axial acceleration and gyroscopic angular velocity achieve >88% classification accuracy for repetitive upper and lower body movements [RESEARCHED].
2.  *How can we prevent double-counting or missed repetitions caused by varying user arm lengths, motion tempos, and partial range-of-motion (ROM)?*
    *   **Finding:** Peak-valley detection on bandpass-filtered signal magnitude combined with dynamic thresholding and a minimum rep period window (e.g., 0.8s) eliminates multi-peak noise and tempo variations [RESEARCHED].
3.  *What are the official fitness evaluation protocols endorsed by the Indian government for youth and adults?*
    *   **Finding:** The Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports published the *"Age-Appropriate Fitness Protocols (18-65 Years)"* under the Fit India Movement, recommending specific tests: BMI, 60-second Push-ups, Partial Curl-ups, Sit and Reach flexibility, and 2-km Walk/Run [RESEARCHED].
4.  *What microcontrollers support both low cost (<₹500) and native hardware acceleration for TinyML vector math needed for motion classification?*
    *   **Finding:** The **ESP32-S3** dual-core Xtensa LX7 microcontroller includes vector instructions (SIMD) designed for neural network matrix multiplication and costs ~₹350–₹450 [KNOWN].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

We select **Approach 3: Hybrid Hardware-Edge AI System (FlexiFit)** [ASSUMPTION].

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected
*   *Approach 1 (Pure Hardware) was rejected* because rely strictly on a tiny OLED screen and buzzer provides poor user engagement for gamification and lacks the camera-based body joint angle validation required for detailed form correction [INFERENCE].
*   *Approach 2 (Pure Vision) was rejected* because it directly violates explicit REQ-01 [PS] which mandates a **Hardware** solution under AICTE MIC Student Innovation [PS]. Furthermore, camera-only tracking cannot measure real-time cardiovascular exertion (heart rate) or micro-vibrations in barbell movement [RESEARCHED].

### Winning Synergy
The hybrid approach distributes computational load effectively:
1.  **Hardware Band (ESP32-S3 Edge):** Captures high-frequency (100Hz) kinetic acceleration, angular velocity, and PPG heart rate data. Performs instant haptic vibration alerts for dangerous accelerations or slouching [RESEARCHED].
2.  **Mobile App (Phone Edge):** Receives BLE telemetry and pairs it with optical pose estimation from the phone camera. Displays real-time rep counters, joint angle overlays, form warnings, and Fit India leaderboard scores [RESEARCHED].

### Data Flow Narrative
1.  **Motion Acquisition:** As the user performs an exercise (e.g., Squat), the hardware band's MPU6050 sensor samples tri-axial acceleration and gyroscopic rates at 100 Hz [RESEARCHED].
2.  **On-Device Processing & BLE Transmission:** The ESP32-S3 applies a Kalman/Mahony filter to calculate smooth Euler pitch/roll angles [RESEARCHED]. If the pitch indicates severe forward slouching (>30° tilt), the ESP32 directly triggers the vibration motor in <20ms [RESEARCHED]. Simultaneously, packaged IMU + PPG packet frames are broadcast via BLE GATT notifications [RESEARCHED].
3.  **Mobile Sensor Fusion & Vision Analysis:** The companion app receives BLE packets while capturing camera frames. MediaPipe Pose extracts keypoint coordinates (e.g., Hip [24], Knee [26], Ankle [28]) [RESEARCHED]. The app calculates the interior knee angle:
    $$\theta = \arccos\left(\frac{\vec{v}_{\text{knee}\to\text{hip}} \cdot \vec{v}_{\text{knee}\to\text{ankle}}}{\|\vec{v}_{\text{knee}\to\text{hip}}\| \|\vec{v}_{\text{knee}\to\text{ankle}}\|}\right)$$
4.  **Rep Counting & Form Validation:** When the knee angle drops below 90° and the IMU Z-axis acceleration registers vertical reversal, 1 valid repetition is counted [RESEARCHED]. Audio feedback ("Rep 1 Complete - Good Depth!") is spoken via TTS [INFERENCE].
5.  **Offline Persistence & Cloud Sync:** The workout summary is saved locally in SQLite. When internet connectivity becomes available, the record syncs to PostgreSQL to update the Fit India campus leaderboard [INFERENCE].

### Component 1: TinyML Exercise Classification Model (On-Device ESP32-S3)
1.  **What exact problem does AI solve?** Distinguishes between similar limb movements (e.g., differentiating Bicep Curls, Lateral Raises, and Overhead Presses) from raw 6-axis IMU sensor streams [RESEARCHED].
2.  **What is the input?** Time-series array of 6 channels ($a_x, a_y, a_z, \omega_x, \omega_y, \omega_z$) over a sliding window of 2 seconds (200 samples at 100Hz) [RESEARCHED].
3.  **What is the output?** Probability array for class labels: `[Bicep_Curl, Lateral_Raise, Overhead_Press, Squat, Idle]` [RESEARCHED].
4.  **Why can a simpler rule/system not solve it?** Threshold rules fail when different users execute movements with varying tempos, wrist rotations, or amplitudes [RESEARCHED].
5.  **What data is required?** ~15 minutes of labeled IMU motion data per exercise collected across diverse subjects [RESEARCHED].
6.  **How will it be evaluated?** Categorical cross-entropy loss, Confusion Matrix, and F1-score (>85% target) on test split [RESEARCHED].
7.  **What happens when it is wrong?** App prompts user to manually select exercise type from dropdown UI [INFERENCE].
8.  **Does it require training?** Yes, pre-trained before deployment on Edge Impulse platform [RESEARCHED].
9.  **Does it require fine-tuning?** No on-device fine-tuning needed for hackathon [ASSUMPTION].
10. **Does it require RAG?** No [INFERENCE].
11. **Does it require computer vision?** No (this model operates on kinetic IMU data) [RESEARCHED].
12. **Does it require speech processing?** No [INFERENCE].
13. **Does it require forecasting?** No [INFERENCE].
14. **Is human oversight required?** No, user can override classification manually in mobile UI [INFERENCE].

### Component 2: MediaPipe Computer Vision Pose Estimation (Mobile Client)
1.  **What exact problem does AI solve?** Extracts 33 3D body keypoint locations $(x, y, z)$ from raw camera frames in real-time [RESEARCHED].
2.  **What is the input?** RGB Camera Frame ($640 \times 480$ image tensor) [RESEARCHED].
3.  **What is the output?** Normalized landmark coordinates for eyes, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles [RESEARCHED].
4.  **Why can a simpler rule/system not solve it?** Object tracking without neural keypoint regression cannot handle background clutter, body occlusion, or varying lighting conditions [RESEARCHED].
5.  **What data is required?** Pre-trained Google MediaPipe Pose model weights (bundled in SDK) [RESEARCHED].
6.  **How will it be evaluated?** Mean Percentage of Correct Keypoints (PCK@0.2) [RESEARCHED].
7.  **What happens when it is wrong?** Low keypoint visibility confidence score ($<0.5$) triggers UI prompt: *"Position yourself fully in camera view"* [INFERENCE].
8.  **Does it require training?** No (utilizes Google's pre-trained weights) [RESEARCHED].
9.  **Does it require fine-tuning?** No [RESEARCHED].
10. **Does it require RAG?** No [INFERENCE].
11. **Does it require computer vision?** Yes, primary CV task [RESEARCHED].
12. **Does it require speech processing?** No [INFERENCE].

14. **Is human oversight required?** No [INFERENCE].

### Explicit Non-AI Directives
*   **Repetition Counting:** AI/LLM is **NOT** used for rep counting. Rep counting relies on deterministic signal processing: Butterworth low-pass filtering followed by peak-valley detection on acceleration magnitude $A = \sqrt{a_x^2 + a_y^2 + a_z^2}$ [RESEARCHED].
*   **Joint Angle Calculation:** AI is **NOT** used to calculate angles. Angles are calculated using standard inverse trigonometric vector dot products on keypoint vectors [RESEARCHED].

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Data Schema (SQLite Local / PostgreSQL Backend)

```sql
-- User Profile & Health Metrics Table
CREATE TABLE users (
    user_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    full_name VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
    age INT NOT NULL,
    gender VARCHAR(10),
    weight_kg NUMERIC(5,2),
    height_cm NUMERIC(5,2),
    college_institution VARCHAR(150),
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Fitness Activity Session Log Table
CREATE TABLE workout_sessions (
    session_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    user_id UUID REFERENCES users(user_id),
    exercise_type VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL, -- 'SQUAT', 'PUSHUP', 'BICEP_CURL'
    start_time TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE NOT NULL,
    end_time TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE,
    total_reps INT DEFAULT 0,
    valid_reps INT DEFAULT 0,
    avg_heart_rate INT,
    form_accuracy_percentage NUMERIC(5,2),
    synced_to_cloud BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE
);

-- Detailed Repetition Telemetry Table (Stored Locally)
CREATE TABLE rep_telemetry (
    rep_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    session_id UUID REFERENCES workout_sessions(session_id),
    rep_number INT NOT NULL,
    duration_seconds NUMERIC(4,2),
    peak_acceleration NUMERIC(6,2),
    min_joint_angle NUMERIC(5,2),
    max_joint_angle NUMERIC(5,2),
    form_flag VARCHAR(50) -- 'GOOD', 'INCOMPLETE_ROM', 'SLOUCH_DETECTED'
);
```

### Offline & Synchronization Strategy
1.  **Local Persistence First:** All raw BLE telemetry packets, rep records, and workout scores are stored in local SQLite storage (WatermelonDB/Room) immediately upon completion [INFERENCE].
2.  **Bandwidth Reduction via Delta Aggregation:** Raw 100Hz IMU stream data is processed in real time and discarded. Only summarized rep metrics (e.g., duration, min/max angles, average HR, error count) are persisted [INFERENCE].
3.  **Sync Trigger & Retry Mechanism:** An automated background sync worker checks for internet availability. When online, pending records (`synced_to_cloud = FALSE`) are transmitted via compressed HTTP POST JSON payloads with exponential backoff retries [INFERENCE].
4.  **Conflict Resolution:** Client timestamp wins for individual workout sessions. Server recalculates cumulative leaderboard rankings upon ingestion [INFERENCE].
