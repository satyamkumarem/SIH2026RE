## 1. Executive Snapshot

This project delivers a comprehensive research dossier and engineering proposal for **SIH26113**, sponsored by **Autodesk Education Experience**. The project addresses human augmentation through the complete digital design, biomechanical optimization, and computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) setup of an **Active Polycentric Bionic Prosthetic Knee Joint with Monoblock Support Chassis** [INFERENCE]. Designed specifically for transfemoral amputees and gait rehabilitation, the device features a four-bar linkage mechanism with an integrated hydraulic stance controller [KNOWN]. 

The primary deliverable focuses on a critical load-bearing structural component: the **Monoblock Polycentric Knee Chassis**, engineered specifically for **Subtractive 3-Axis CNC Milling** out of High-Strength Aerospace Aluminum (Al 7075-T6) [ASSUMPTION]. Using **Autodesk Fusion** exclusively, the complete CAD modeling, Finite Element Analysis (FEA) structural validation, 3-Axis CAM setup, tool selection, 2D/3D adaptive clearing toolpath optimization, stock removal simulation, and G-code generation are fully executed [INFERENCE]. This dossier provides a student team (4–6 members) with an end-to-end blueprint for both the digital submission and the physical Grand Finale CNC machining round [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

**Selected Approach:** **Approach 1 — Active Polycentric Bionic Prosthetic Knee Joint with 3-Axis CNC Milled Monoblock Chassis** [ASSUMPTION].

### Why This Approach Wins:
1. **Perfect Alignment with Autodesk Objectives:** The Monoblock Knee Chassis provides an ideal canvas for showcasing Fusion’s advanced CAM strategies—specifically **3D Adaptive Clearing**, **Tool Orientation**, **In-Process Stock Simulation**, and **G-Code Post-Processing** [INFERENCE].
2. **Clear CNC 3-Axis Suitability Justification:** Unlike cylindrical parts (best turned), the chassis features prismatic multi-height geometry, internal weight-reduction pockets, high-precision press-fit bearing bores ($H7$ tolerance), and filleted outer contours [INFERENCE]. These features *cannot* be produced on a 2-Axis lathe and strictly require a 3-Axis CNC Milling machine [KNOWN].
3. **High Human & Biomechanical Value:** Solves a major healthcare problem for lower-limb amputees, providing stability during stance phase and smooth knee flexion during swing phase [KNOWN].

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected:
* *Approach 2 (Ergonomic Support Shaft - 2-Axis Turning):* Turned shafts are mechanically simpler, but 2-axis lathe CAM toolpaths (roughing/finishing) offer less scope to showcase high-level CAM optimization (like constant engagement adaptive roughing) compared to 3-axis milling [INFERENCE].
* *Approach 3 (Bionic Ankle Assembly):* Involves thin-walled sheet elements prone to vibration and tool chatter when milled on standard VMC setups without specialized vacuum fixtures [KNOWN].

### Detailed Data Flow Narrative
1. **Design Initialization:** Biomechanical requirements drive parametric sketch profiles in Fusion. Constraints ensure internal pocket fillets ($R = 3.5\text{ mm}$) exceed the radius of the $6\text{ mm}$ end mill ($R = 3\text{ mm}$) to prevent cutter binding [KNOWN].
2. **CAD Assembly & FEA:** The chassis is integrated into the 4-bar linkage assembly. A static stress simulation applies $3000\text{ N}$ down the load line. Stress concentrations are reviewed; pocket wall thicknesses are adjusted natively in CAD until Safety Factor $\ge 2.2$ is achieved [INFERENCE].
3. **CAM Setup Definition:** Switching to the *Manufacture* workspace, a new Milling Setup is created. Stock material is assigned as `Aluminum 7075-T6` block ($120 \times 80 \times 45\text{ mm}$). Work Coordinate System (WCS) $G54$ is assigned to the top-center face of the raw stock [ASSUMPTION].
4. **Toolpath Execution & Optimization:**
   * *Facing:* Cleans top surface ($0.5\text{ mm}$ cut depth).
   * *Adaptive Roughing:* A $10\text{ mm}$ 3-flute flat end mill clears $75\%$ of stock using constant tool load.
   * *Finishing & Boring:* A $6\text{ mm}$ end mill finishes vertical sidewalls and circular bores to precision tolerances. A $4\text{ mm}$ ball mill contours smooth ergonomic outer fillets [ASSUMPTION].
5. **Simulation & G-code Export:** The full toolpath sequence is simulated with stock visualization enabled. Clashes between tool holder and vise fixtures are audited. Upon verification, the post-processor generates clean G-code (.nc file) [INFERENCE].
