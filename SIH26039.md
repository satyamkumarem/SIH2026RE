# 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
Underground coal mining operations in Jharkhand face extreme environmental hazards including flammable methane gas explosions ($CH_4$), toxic carbon monoxide ($CO$) accumulation, oxygen deficiency, flooding, and roof/gallery collapses [PS, KNOWN]. When disasters occur, traditional rescue efforts are severely constrained by zero visibility, loss of wired/wireless communication, and high risk of secondary explosions or collapses that threaten rescuers' lives [PS, KNOWN]. The proposed system is an integrated **AI-Powered Mine Safety and Rescue System** featuring a rugged, tracked ground rover equipped with multi-gas sensors, thermal and night-vision vision systems, LiDAR-based 3D mapping (SLAM), an automated droppable wireless mesh node deployment mechanism, and a tethered mini-drone [INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION]. Powered by an NVIDIA Jetson edge AI processor and microcontrollers running ROS 2 [ASSUMPTION], the rover navigates GPS-denied galleries, detects environmental hazards, locates trapped miners via thermal YOLO vision models, and relays real-time video, gas telemetry, and structural 3D maps back to a surface control station via a multi-hop wireless mesh [INFERENCE].

  * *Source Text:* "...assist in locating trapped workers during emergencies..." [PS]

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

* **Explosive Atmospheric Hazard (Gassy Mines):** Underground coal mines contain explosive Methane ($CH_4$) gas (explosive limit: 5%–15% in air) and combustible coal dust [KNOWN]. Any electrical spark, hot motor casing ($>135^\circ\text{C}$), or open switch can trigger a catastrophic explosion [KNOWN]. Equipment in production must adhere to DGMS flameproof (FLP / Ex-d under IS/IEC 60079-1) or intrinsically safe (IS / Ex-ia under IS/IEC 60079-11) standards [RESEARCHED].
* **Extreme RF Attenuation & Multipath Scattering:** Radio waves at 2.4GHz and 5.8GHz attenuate rapidly around solid coal seams and rock pillars (signal loss $>30\text{ dB}$ around sharp $90^\circ$ bends) [KNOWN]. Through-the-earth (TTE) propagation is infeasible for broadband video; non-line-of-sight (NLOS) multi-hop relaying is strictly required [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Complete Darkness, Thick Dust, and Smoke:** Airborne coal dust and dense smoke render conventional optical cameras blind [KNOWN]. Active infrared illumination and long-wavelength thermal imaging are non-negotiable [INFERENCE].
* **Severe Terrain Obstacles:** Flooded galleries ($10–20\text{ cm}$ water depth), loose rock debris, mud, steep gradients up to $30^\circ$, and narrow cross-cuts ($<1\text{ meter}$) prevent standard wheeled vehicles from progressing [KNOWN, INFERENCE].
* **GPS-Denied Environment:** Satellite positioning signals cannot penetrate rock strata [KNOWN]. All localization must rely strictly on dead-reckoning, wheel encoders, IMU sensor fusion, and LiDAR/Visual SLAM [INFERENCE].
* **Field Operator Constraints:** Mine rescue teams operate in high-stress, low-visibility conditions wearing thick gloves and rebreathers [KNOWN]. Dashboard interfaces must be intuitive, high-contrast, touch-friendly, and require minimal manual configuration [INFERENCE].

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Heavy Tethered Ground Rover with Analog Video Relay
* **Mechanism:** A heavy, multi-wheeled rover connected to the surface base station via a $300\text{-meter}$ reinforced fiber-optic/copper tether cable. Analog video and serial telemetry are transmitted directly through the cable [INFERENCE].
* **Required Data:** Analog video stream, raw analog sensor voltages, encoder ticks [INFERENCE].
* **Complexity & Cost:** Low complexity; moderate cost ($\sim\text{₹}80,000\text{--}\text{₹}1,200,000$) [INFERENCE].
* **Pros & Cons:** Eliminates RF signal attenuation; guaranteed high-bandwidth video [INFERENCE]. However, the tether frequently snags around sharp rock outcrops, gets crushed under falling debris, severely restricts mobility, and adds massive weight [INFERENCE].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High | **Production Feasibility:** Low (Tether snagging is unacceptable in collapse zones) [INFERENCE].

### Approach 2: Autonomous Aerial Drone Swarm with Wi-Fi Mesh
* **Mechanism:** A fleet of 2–3 compact quadcopters equipped with optical flow, thermal cameras, and micro Wi-Fi mesh modules flying autonomously into mine shafts [INFERENCE].
* **Required Data:** High-rate IMU ($>200\text{Hz}$), optical flow vector streams, thermal video, 3D point cloud [INFERENCE].
* **Complexity & Cost:** High complexity; high cost ($\sim\text{₹}1,500,000\text{--}\text{₹}3,000,000$) [INFERENCE].
* **Pros & Cons:** Rapid flight over flooded or blocked gallery floors [INFERENCE]. However, extreme battery drain ($\sim10\text{-minute}$ flight time), high rotor dust turbulence, collision risks in narrow $1\text{-meter}$ cross-cuts, and high power usage make it unreliable for prolonged search operations [INFERENCE].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** Low | **Production Feasibility:** Medium (Flight time constraints) [INFERENCE].

### Approach 3 (RECOMMENDED): Modular Tracked Rover + Droppable Mesh Node Deployer + Surface Dashboard (Hybrid Architecture)
* **Mechanism:** A rugged, tracked ground rover equipped with an onboard NVIDIA Jetson edge AI board, multi-gas array, 2D LiDAR, thermal camera, and an automated servo-actuated droppable mesh node mechanism [INFERENCE]. As the rover drives into the mine gallery, it continuously monitors RF signal strength (RSSI) and automatically drops a small, battery-powered Sub-GHz / Wi-Fi repeater node before turning corners or when signal drops below $-75\text{ dBm}$ [INFERENCE].
* **Required Data:** LiDAR scans (`/scan`), thermal image array, RGB video, gas sensor readings ($CH_4, CO, Temp$), mesh node RSSI telemetry [INFERENCE].
* **Complexity & Cost:** Moderate-High complexity; affordable cost ($\sim\text{₹}60,000\text{--}\text{₹}1,200,000$ prototype) [INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION].
* **Pros & Cons:** Bypasses RF line-of-sight attenuation via droppable breadcrumbs; high mobility over rubble; $2\text{--}3\text{ hour}$ operation time; safe low-center-of-gravity traversal [INFERENCE].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High | **Production Feasibility:** High [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

### Selected Solution
**Approach 3: Modular Tracked Ground Rover with Automated Droppable Wireless Mesh Relay Nodes, Dual Thermal-Visual Sensing, Edge AI, and Surface Command Dashboard** [INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION].

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected
* **Approach 1 (Tethered Rover):** Rejected because physical cable tethers inevitably snag, twist, or get severed by jagged rock falls in collapsed mine galleries, causing complete mission failure [INFERENCE].
* **Approach 2 (Pure Aerial Drone Swarm):** Rejected due to short battery flight times ($<15\text{ min}$), heavy dust displacement from propeller wash interfering with optical sensors, and high crash risk in tight, unmapped cross-cuts [INFERENCE].

### Primary Winning Differentiator
Approach 3 directly solves the single greatest technical bottleneck of underground mining robotics: **RF signal death around rock corners** [KNOWN]. By dynamically deploying low-cost, battery-powered droppable mesh repeater nodes ("breadcrumbs") [RESEARCHED], the rover creates a self-healing communication pipeline back to the surface control station while maintaining continuous ground mobility [INFERENCE].

### Data-Flow Narrative
1. **Sensing & Local Acquisition:** The ESP32 sensor controller samples gas concentrations ($MQ-4, MQ-7, DHT22$) every $100\text{ms}$. If Methane exceeds $1.2\%$ or $CO$ exceeds $50\text{ ppm}$, an immediate local interrupt triggers an audible buzzer and high-priority alert flag [INFERENCE].
2. **Onboard AI & Mapping Processing:** The NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano receives 2D LiDAR scans over USB (`/scan`) and runs `slam_toolbox` to generate a live occupancy grid [RESEARCHED]. Concurrently, thermal imagery from the LWIR sensor is ingested into a TensorRT-optimized YOLOv8n model [RESEARCHED]. When a human thermal signature is detected with confidence $>0.70$, the pipeline projects the bounding box onto the current SLAM map coordinates, generating a "Trapped Miner Marker" [INFERENCE].
3. **Automated Breadcrumb Mesh Deployment:** As the rover moves deeper into the gallery, the Jetson continuously monitors RSSI from the last connected relay node. If RSSI drops below $-75\text{ dBm}$ or a sharp $90^\circ$ turn is detected via IMU, Jetson commands the ESP32 to actuate a servo dispenser, dropping a battery-powered ESP32 mesh node onto the gallery floor [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
4. **Data Transmission & Surface Visualization:** Compressed telemetry packages (gas levels, pose coordinates, system status) are transmitted via Sub-GHz LoRa Mesh, while compressed thermal/visual keyframes ($320\times240$ JPEG @ $10\text{ fps}$) flow over the ESP-NOW / Wi-Fi mesh [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE]. The Surface Base Station receives the packets, parses JSON payloads, and updates the React.js Command Dashboard in real time [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### 1. Thermal Human / Worker Detection Engine
* **Exact Purpose:** Identify trapped or unconscious mine workers in absolute darkness, dust, or smoke using body heat signatures [PS, RESEARCHED].
* **Model Type:** YOLOv8n fine-tuned on custom thermal infrared human datasets [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** $320\times240$ single-channel LWIR thermal image feed from FLIR Lepton sensor [INFERENCE].
* **Output:** Bounding boxes $(x, y, w, h)$, class confidence score (`worker`), and spatial centroid coordinates [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required:** 1,500 annotated thermal image frames depicting humans sitting, lying down, partially occluded by debris, and standing in cold background environments [RESEARCHED, ASSUMPTION].
* **Why AI is Necessary:** Rule-based temperature thresholding fails because warm machinery motors, steam pipes, and ambient heat friction produce severe false positives [INFERENCE]. Deep learning extracts structural shape features in addition to heat intensity [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metrics:** Mean Average Precision (mAP50 $> 0.85$), Recall ($> 0.90$), Inference Latency ($< 25\text{ms}$) [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode & Mitigation:** If thermal contrast is washed out due to mine underground fires ($>40^\circ\text{C}$ ambient), system falls back to IR night-vision camera feed with bounding box confidence flags [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight:** High bounding box detections ($>0.70$) automatically flag a visual alert on the surface dashboard; human rescue operators confirm before deploying personnel [INFERENCE].

### 2. Structural Hazard & Obstacle Classification Engine

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Local SQLite Database Schema

```sql
-- Sensor Telemetry Log Table
CREATE TABLE telemetry_log (
    id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT,
    timestamp DATETIME DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    ch4_concentration REAL NOT NULL, -- Methane %
    co_ppm REAL NOT NULL,            -- Carbon Monoxide PPM
    temp_celsius REAL NOT NULL,       -- Ambient Temperature
    humidity_pct REAL NOT NULL,      -- Relative Humidity
    battery_level REAL NOT NULL,     -- Rover battery %
    rssi_dbm INTEGER NOT NULL        -- Current active mesh link RSSI
);

-- Detected Trapped Miner Log Table
CREATE TABLE miner_detections (
    id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT,
    timestamp DATETIME DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    pose_x REAL NOT NULL,            -- Map relative X coordinate (meters)
    pose_y REAL NOT NULL,            -- Map relative Y coordinate (meters)
    confidence REAL NOT NULL,        -- YOLO confidence score (0.00 - 1.00)
    thermal_snapshot_path TEXT,      -- Local file path to saved JPEG
    status TEXT DEFAULT 'UNVERIFIED' -- UNVERIFIED / CONFIRMED / RESCUED
);

-- Mesh Relay Nodes Registry
CREATE TABLE mesh_nodes (
    node_id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY,
    dropped_timestamp DATETIME,
    battery_v REAL,
    mesh_hop_count INTEGER,
    status TEXT DEFAULT 'ACTIVE'     -- ACTIVE / OFFLINE / DEGRADED
);
```

### Low-Network & Bandwidth Optimization Strategy
* **Adaptive Multi-Tier Data Pipeline:**
  1. *Priority 1 (Critical Telemetry over LoRa 868MHz):* Methane alerts, GPS/SLAM position, battery status, and panic triggers are packed into tiny 24-byte binary structures and broadcast every $1,000\text{ms}$. Transmission succeeds even at extremely low data rates ($1.2\text{ kbps}$) across long distances [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
  2. *Priority 2 (Map & Image Data over ESP-NOW / Wi-Fi Mesh):* Compressed 2D occupancy grid updates (delta compressed) and downsampled thermal keyframes ($320\times240$ JPEG @ $5\text{ FPS}$) are transmitted when Wi-Fi bandwidth is $>100\text{ kbps}$ [INFERENCE].
* **Onboard Local Buffering & Re-Sync:** If total wireless communication is completely lost (e.g., severe rock collapse blocks radio signal), the Jetson board buffers all LiDAR scans, high-res video, and gas logs to an onboard local SQLite database / ROSbag on NVMe storage [INFERENCE]. Once the rover backtracks into range of the nearest functional dropped node, the system executes an automated differential sync protocol [INFERENCE].
