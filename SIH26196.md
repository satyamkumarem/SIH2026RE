## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
The proposed solution, **FitPulse AI**, is an edge-AI powered, privacy-preserving physical fitness and campus engagement platform designed to boost daily physical activity among students and citizens. The system addresses low physical activity adoption in higher education institutions (HEIs) by combining real-time, on-device Computer Vision pose evaluation (via WebGL/WASM) for automated repetition counting and posture correction with an institutional gamification engine natively aligned with the **AICTE Activity Point Program** [RESEARCHED] and the **Fit India Movement Age-Appropriate Fitness Protocols (GOALS 18–65)** [RESEARCHED]. Operating as a progressive web application (PWA), the system requires zero expensive wearable hardware or high-speed internet, executing frame analysis locally on standard smartphones and laptop cameras while maintaining complete user video privacy [INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

* **Geography & Field Conditions:** Students and citizens in Indian colleges often work out outdoors, in hostels with limited indoor space, or on college grounds under varying lighting conditions (bright sunlight, low-light hostel rooms) [KNOWN].
* **Hardware Diversity:** Target users possess smartphones ranging from budget Android devices (2 GB–4 GB RAM, entry-level MediaTek/Snapdragon processors) to high-end laptops [KNOWN]. Deep neural networks requiring heavy GPU computation will fail on budget devices.
* **Connectivity & Bandwidth:** Campus Wi-Fi and mobile networks in tier-2/tier-3 cities frequently experience packet loss and bandwidth throttling [KNOWN]. Streaming HD camera video to cloud servers for computer vision inference is infeasible and cost-prohibitive.
* **Privacy Concerns:** Users, especially female students, are unwilling to record and upload video clips of themselves working out to third-party cloud servers [KNOWN]. Video frames MUST be processed strictly in volatile device memory and discarded immediately.
* **Language & Literacy:** Users across different regions require multi-lingual audio-visual guidance (English, Hindi, regional languages) with simple visual skeletal overlays rather than text-heavy instructions [INFERENCE].

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Wearable & Sensor-Based IoT Tracking
* **Mechanism:** Use smart wristbands (IMU sensors) connected via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to a mobile app to detect movement patterns.
* **Problem Fit:** Good for heart rate and step tracking; poor for multi-joint pose assessment (squat depth, elbow flare).
* **Complexity:** High (requires hardware procurement, BLE firmware debugging, device pairing issues).
* **Cost:** High (₹1,500–₹3,000 per student; infeasible for mass university deployment).
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** Very Low (hardware dependency risks live demo failure).

### Approach 2: Server-Side Cloud Video AI Analysis
* **Mechanism:** User records workout video on phone, uploads file to AWS/GCP, and a server-side PyTorch model processes the video using YOLOv8-Pose and returns form scores.
* **Problem Fit:** High accuracy model potential (can use larger deep learning architectures).
* **Complexity:** Medium-High.
* **Cost:** High (Cloud GPU costs for video transcoding and inference: ~$0.05 per video upload).
* **Privacy & Network Risk:** Extreme (uploading large MP4 files fails on spotty 3G/4G campus networks; user video uploaded to cloud violates student privacy).
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** Medium.

### Approach 3: Edge-AI Computer Vision + Progressive Web App (PWA) + AICTE Gamification Framework (RECOMMENDED)
* **Mechanism:** A multi-platform Progressive Web Application (PWA) running Google MediaPipe Pose directly in the browser via WebGL/WASM [RESEARCHED]. Joint angle trigonometry and biomechanical state machines process pose keypoints locally on the device at 30 FPS. Only lightweight JSON activity telemetry (reps, duration, posture score) is synced to a lightweight backend.
* **Problem Fit:** Perfect (zero hardware cost, instant access via URL/QR code, total video privacy, low bandwidth).
* **Complexity:** Medium (requires robust client-side biomechanical state machine logic).
* **Cost:** Extremely Low (runs on free tier infrastructure; client device handles computation).
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High (100% buildable and live-demonstrable using standard laptops/smartphones).

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

**Selected Approach:** **Approach 3 (Edge-AI PWA + Local Biomechanical State Machine + AICTE Activity Point Sync)**

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected:
* **Approach 1 (IoT/Wearables)** was rejected because requiring students to purchase smart wearables violates the principle of keeping fitness accessible and free ("Fitness as easy, fun, and free" - Fit India Mission) [RESEARCHED].
* **Approach 2 (Cloud Video Processing)** was rejected due to heavy cloud GPU hosting expenses, network latency in hostels, and severe student privacy resistance regarding video clip uploads.

### Winning Edge of Approach 3:
1. **Zero Setup Cost:** Works instantly on any camera-enabled smartphone or laptop browser via WebGL.
2. **Privacy by Design:** Camera frames never leave RAM memory; only numerical landmark vectors (X, Y, Z coordinates) are evaluated locally.
3. **Institutional Alignment:** Seamlessly logs validated fitness hours directly into the college's AICTE Activity Point approval portal.

### Data Flow Narrative

1. **Camera Stream Capture:** The user opens the PWA and selects an exercise (e.g., Squats, Push-Ups, Planks, or Fit India Assessment Test). The app requests camera access via `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia()`.
2. **Local Frame Processing:** The HTML5 Video Element feeds raw frames directly into the in-browser MediaPipe Pose WASM runtime.
3. **Landmark Extraction:** MediaPipe predicts 33 keypoint 3D spatial coordinates $(x, y, z, \text{visibility})$ locally in $<15\text{ms}$ per frame [RESEARCHED].
4. **Biomechanical Evaluation:**
   * The app's math module calculates vector angles (e.g., Hip-Knee-Ankle angle for Squats, Shoulder-Elbow-Wrist angle for Push-ups).
   * A finite state machine (FSM) transitions states: `START` $\rightarrow$ `DESCENDING` $\rightarrow$ `BOTTOM_THRESHOLD` (Depth Check) $\rightarrow$ `ASCENDING` $\rightarrow$ `REP_COMPLETE`.
   * Form checks (e.g., "Knees caving in", "Back sagging", "Incomplete depth") trigger real-time audio and visual visual overlays on the HTML5 Canvas.
5. **Session Aggregation & Cryptographic Signing:** On workout completion, the client generates a session summary payload containing rep count, time duration, average posture score, timestamp, device telemetry, and optional coarse GPS coordinates. The payload is signed with a SHA-256 client session token.
6. **Offline Queue or Cloud Sync:**
   * *If offline:* The payload is saved to IndexedDB. A Service Worker registers a `background-sync` task.
   * *If online:* The client posts JSON payload to `/api/v1/workouts/submit`.
7. **Backend Validation & Gamification:** The Node.js API verifies token integrity, logs the entry into PostgreSQL, updates the user's weekly AICTE Activity Point hours, and updates live campus leaderboards stored in Redis.

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Component 1: On-Device Pose Landmark Detection (MediaPipe Pose)
1. **Exact Problem AI Solves:** Converts raw 2D pixel matrices from camera frames into standardized 3D keypoint coordinates $(x, y, z)$ of human joints [RESEARCHED].
2. **Input:** 640x480 RGB Video Frames from camera stream.
3. **Output:** Array of 33 Keypoints with 3D coordinates and confidence visibility scores.
4. **Why Simpler Rules Fail:** Pixel-based color thresholding or traditional edge detection cannot robustly identify human joints under varying clothing, backgrounds, and lighting. Neural network regression is strictly required.
5. **Data Required:** Pre-trained BlazePose weight checkpoint (~6 MB).
6. **Evaluation Metric:** Mean Per Joint Position Error (MPJPE) and Percentage of Correct Keypoints (PCK@0.2).
7. **Failure Mode Handling:** If keypoint visibility falls below 0.6 (e.g., body partially out of frame), the UI displays "Step back - full body must be visible".
8. **Training/Fine-tuning:** None needed during hackathon; use pre-trained Google MediaPipe BlazePose Lite model [RESEARCHED].
9. **Human Oversight:** User can manually pause or terminate tracking if misclassifications occur.

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used:
* **Repetition Counting & Depth Validation:** DO NOT use a black-box deep learning model to count reps or judge depth. Use deterministic trigonometric math (joint angles calculated via vector dot products) on top of AI keypoints. This guarantees 100% explainable, predictable, and debuggable rep counting logic.
* **Activity Point Awarding:** Rule-based verification engines must handle credit calculation based on verified duration and rep standards.

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Core Database Schema (PostgreSQL)

```sql
-- Users Table
CREATE TABLE users (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    full_name VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
    email VARCHAR(100) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    role VARCHAR(20) CHECK (role IN ('student', 'faculty', 'admin', 'citizen')),
    college_id VARCHAR(50),
    department VARCHAR(50),
    created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Fitness Activities Log Table
CREATE TABLE activity_logs (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    user_id UUID REFERENCES users(id),
    exercise_type VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL, -- e.g. 'squat', 'pushup', 'fit_india_test'
    rep_count INT NOT NULL,
    duration_seconds INT NOT NULL,
    accuracy_score NUMERIC(5,2), -- Percentage correct posture
    telemetry_hash VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL, -- Cryptographic proof signature
    synced_from_offline BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE,
    created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- AICTE Activity Points Ledger Table
CREATE TABLE aicte_activity_ledger (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    user_id UUID REFERENCES users(id),
    points_awarded INT NOT NULL,
    category VARCHAR(100) DEFAULT 'Sports and Fitness Activities',
    verified_by_faculty UUID REFERENCES users(id),
    status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'pending' CHECK (status IN ('pending', 'approved', 'rejected')),
    created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);
```

### Local Storage & Offline Synchronization Flow
* **Local Caching:** Application Shell (HTML/JS/CSS) and WASM binaries (~6MB) are cached locally via Service Worker Cache API during initial load.
* **Offline Queueing:** Workout sessions completed while offline are written directly to IndexedDB under object store `offline_workouts`.
* **Sync Strategy:** Upon detecting `navigator.onLine == true`, the PWA triggers a sync pipeline:
  1. Fetch un-synced records from IndexedDB.
  2. Send batch request to `/api/v1/sync/batch`.
  3. Server validates timestamps and telemetry hashes.
  4. On successful 200 OK response, local IndexedDB items are marked as `synced: true`.
* **Conflict Handling:** Server uses client-generated immutable UUIDs. If a record already exists in PostgreSQL, duplicate submissions are ignored cleanly (idempotent write).

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

* **Camera Feed Privacy:** Camera frames are strictly processed in device volatile RAM memory via HTML5 Canvas context. No image or video data is ever saved to disk or transmitted across the network.
* **DPDP Act 2023 Compliance:** Complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 [KNOWN]. Explicit consent prompts notify users that health telemetry (rep count, activity duration) is collected solely for fitness scoring and activity point credit.
* **Anti-Spoofing & Fraud Prevention:**
  * To prevent users from shaking phone or playing pre-recorded videos in front of camera:
    * **Temporal Kinematic Analysis:** The system checks natural velocity and acceleration profiles of joint angles. Instantaneous static jumps indicate pre-recorded loop injection.
    * **Challenge Mode:** Prompts user to perform a randomized prompt during workout (e.g., "Raise left hand now") to prove live participation during institutional tests.
* **Regulatory Disclaimer:** The system is an educational and fitness engagement platform. Fitness assessments based on Fit India Guidelines are informational and do not constitute certified medical advice [INFERENCE].

## 19. Testing & Validation Plan

* **Functional Verification:** Test rep counting state machine across 100 repetitions of varied speeds (fast, slow, paused) to ensure zero missed or double counts.
* **Pose Accuracy Testing:** Benchmark MediaPipe keypoints against Kaggle Workout Pose Dataset [RESEARCHED] to verify joint angle calculation error is within $\pm 3^\circ$.
* **Low-Light & Angle Robustness:** Test camera tracking across 5 camera placement angles ($0^\circ$ frontal, $45^\circ$ diagonal, $90^\circ$ side-profile) and low-light hostel room environments ($<100\text{ lux}$).
* **False Positive / Negative Testing:**
  * *False Positive Test:* Have user jump or wave hands; verify squat counter does NOT increment.
  * *False Negative Test:* Have user do deep squats with partial side occlusion; verify rep IS correctly registered if depth threshold is crossed.
* **Offline Resilience:** Disconnect network mid-workout, complete session, force app crash/reload, verify data persists in IndexedDB and syncs upon network restoration.
