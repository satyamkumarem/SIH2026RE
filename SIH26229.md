## 1. Executive Snapshot

This research and design dossier presents an end-to-end technical, operational, and architectural strategy for **SIH26229: Kabadiwala Connect – Bringing the Informal Collector into the Formal Recycling Chain**. India’s informal waste ecosystem collects over 80%–90% of the country’s post-consumer electrical and electronic waste (e-waste) [KNOWN]. However, because informal scrap pickers (*kabadiwalas*) lack direct access to authorized recyclers, transparent price discovery, and digital handover tools, massive quantities of high-value e-waste undergo hazardous backyard processing (acid leaching, open cable burning, manual CRT smashing) [PS]. This practice leads to the irreversible loss of critical raw materials—such as lithium, cobalt, neodymium, tantalum, gallium, and indium—while exposing workers to severe occupational health risks [PS]. 

To bridge this institutional and technological gap, we propose an offline-first, vernacular, voice-guided mobile platform for Android devices paired with a cloud-based web application for CPCB-authorized recyclers. The system empowers low-literacy collectors to capture material photos, obtain instant AI-assisted component classification and fair market valuations, discover nearby authorized recyclers, generate verifiable digital handover receipts with GPS and timestamp tracking, and maintain a clear visual/audio earnings ledger [PS].

## 2. Problem Deconstruction

The explicit problem statement provided by the Ministry of Mines and JNARDDC has been deconstructed into atomic, non-overlapping requirements:

*   **Requirement A (Lot Creation & Instant Valuation):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Allow collectors to photograph, categorize, and create digital lots of collected materials such as CRTs, LCD panels, PCBs, cables, batteries, motors and magnet-bearing assemblies, and mixed plastics, enter approximate weight, and receive an instant value estimate." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* The mobile app must feature a visual workflow to take or select photos of 7 distinct material categories, capture weight input (via simple numeric or visual slider/keypad), and run a valuation algorithm to return an estimated price range in rupees [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Requires an on-device/edge computer vision model (lightweight object classification) and a local lookup table/rule engine for price calculation based on weight $\times$ material category rate [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement B (Price Discovery & Trends Dataset):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Provide a price discovery and historical price dataset containing material category, sub-category, location, date, prevailing buying price, unit of measurement, approximate market range, and recycler/aggregator offered price. The system should use this dataset to provide transparent and current price information to collectors and identify basic price trends." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* The system must maintain a structured dataset tracking temporal and spatial price variations across sub-categories (e.g., PCB Class A high-grade vs PCB Class C low-grade) and expose simple trends (rising, stable, falling) [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Needs a backend database schema for time-series pricing and a synchronization mechanism to push updated price boards to mobile devices during network availability [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement C (Material & Transaction Dataset / Traceability):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Maintain a material and transaction dataset containing lot/reference ID, material category, material description, photograph/image reference, approximate weight, estimated value, quoted price, final sale value, date and time of collection, collection location, recycler details, and transaction status. This dataset should enable traceability of material from collection to authorized recycling." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* Every batch of e-waste created by a collector must have an auditable digital trail from origin to recycler acceptance, fulfilling Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) traceability needs [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Database must generate unique cryptographic or hash-based Lot IDs (UUID v4) and link media assets, GPS coordinates, and recycler confirmations [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement D (Authorized Recycler Dataset & Matching):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Maintain an authorized recycler/aggregator dataset containing recycler/aggregator name, facility location, materials accepted, authorization/registration details, authorization status, contact details, offered rates, pickup availability, and service area. The platform should use this information to identify and rank suitable authorized recyclers for a collector's lot." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* The platform must store CPCB/SPCB registration metadata and match lots with nearby recyclers based on geo-distance, accepted material categories, price offers, and pickup radius [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Requires PostGIS or spatial queries (Haversine/spatial indexing) to rank recyclers by distance and offered rate [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement E (AI/ML Integration):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Use the collected material image, category, weight, location, historical price, and transaction data to support AI/ML-based features such as material classification, approximate valuation, recycler matching, and identification of abnormal or inconsistent transaction values, wherever sufficient training data is available." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* ML must be applied selectively across four distinct domains: image-based material classification, dynamic valuation, smart recycler ranking, and fraud/anomaly detection [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Hybrid architecture using edge AI (TFLite/ONNX) for visual classification and server-side ML (Isolation Forest / Regressors) for anomaly detection and pricing models [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement F (Spoken & Visual Price Board):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Provide current buying rates for different material categories and locations through a simple price board, including spoken price information and basic price trends." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* Prices must be displayed using clear visual cards and read aloud in native languages via Text-to-Speech (TTS) [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Integration with native TTS engines or speech APIs (e.g., AI4Bharat Bhashini) with cached local audio fallback [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement G (Digital & Verifiable Handover Record):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Generate a digital and verifiable handover/transfer record containing photographs, weight, timestamp, GPS/location details, and a unique reference that can be confirmed by the recycler." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* Creates a proof-of-transfer (digital receipt / QR code) that both collector and recycler sign off on [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Mobile app generates an offline QR code containing signed transaction payload; recycler scans and confirms on their interface [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement H (Earnings Ledger & Flexible Payments):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Maintain an easy-to-understand earnings ledger showing transactions, payments, and pending dues, thereby building a usable financial and transaction history for collectors... Allow cash-based transactions while keeping digital payment optional and not making it a prerequisite for using the platform." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* Financial records must accommodate cash-on-hand transactions without forcing digital bank accounts or UPI, helping collectors build creditworthiness through documented history [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Double-entry visual ledger supporting payment status tags (`Cash Received`, `Digital Paid`, `Pending Recycler Payment`) [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement I (Safety Guidance):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Provide pictorial and/or audio-based safety guidance on hazardous practices, including improper burning or opening of materials and safe handling of batteries and CRTs." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* Interactive safety modules warning against toxic exposures (e.g., lead in CRTs, hydrofluoric acid in PCB leaching, battery thermal runaway) [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Offline vector graphics/illustrations paired with localized audio clips triggered during material selection [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement J (Vernacular & Low-Literacy UI):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Support Marathi and Hindi at a minimum and provide a genuinely usable interface for users with limited literacy." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* Interface must rely on iconographic navigation, color coding, audio narration, and minimal required text input [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Language toggle at launch, high-contrast visual cards, voice assistant button on key screens [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement K (Offline-First Architecture):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Operate in low-connectivity environments through an offline-first architecture, allowing core activities to be completed offline and synchronized when connectivity becomes available." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* Lot creation, valuation, safety reading, and local ledger entries must function 100% without cellular signal [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Embedded relational database (SQLite / WatermelonDB) with persistent sync queue and delta synchronization protocol [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement L (Entry-Level Device Compatibility):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Support entry-level Android devices with a small application size and low memory requirements." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* Binary executable size must be minimal ($\le$25 MB APK) with low memory footprint ($\le$150 MB RAM usage) to run smoothly on cheap Android hardware [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Quantized AI models (INT8), optimized asset compression (WebP image format, compressed vector graphics), and lightweight JavaScript runtime [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

*   **Requirement M (Field Research & Unit Economics):**
    *   *Source Text:* "Field research involving at least two working scrap collectors or aggregators, and a live usability demonstration. Teams should also provide a short unit-economics assessment comparing the collector's existing earnings with the potential earnings through the proposed platform and explaining how the platform can sustain its operations." [PS]
    *   *Interpretation:* Design must be informed by actual field interviews with 2+ scrap pickers/aggregators and accompanied by a validated unit economics model [PS, INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Field survey documentation, income uplift analysis, and platform monetisation strategy via EPR certificate generation fees or recycler subscription models [INFERENCE].
    *   *Tag:* `[PS]`

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

*   **Connectivity Constraints:** Scrap yards, urban slums, and aggregator godowns frequently suffer from dead zones or degraded 2G/3G connections [KNOWN]. *Design Mandate:* The app must function seamlessly offline for 100% of collection tasks, deferring network requests to a background sync queue [PS].
*   **Device & Hardware Realities:** Informal collectors typically own low-cost Android smartphones (e.g., Redmi, Realme, Samsung A-series) running Android 8.0 to 12, with 2GB RAM and limited internal storage (<32GB) [KNOWN, INFERENCE]. *Design Mandate:* Total APK download size must remain below 25 MB, and RAM consumption during active camera scanning must not exceed 150 MB [PS, INFERENCE].
*   **User Literacy & Language:** Literacy rates among informal waste pickers are low; many cannot fluently read complex written text even in their native script [KNOWN]. *Design Mandate:* UI must prioritize large touch targets ($\ge$48dp), universal color coding (Green = Cash In, Red = Hazard), high-contrast visual icons, and single-tap audio narration in Marathi and Hindi [PS].
*   **Operating Conditions & Environment:** Field usage occurs in dusty, brightly lit outdoor scrap markets or dimly lit indoors [KNOWN]. *Design Mandate:* High-contrast color palette, readable under harsh sunlight, with large camera preview reticles [INFERENCE].
*   **Financial Behavior & Cash Reliance:** Over 85% of informal scrap transactions are settled in physical cash to provide immediate liquidity for daily living expenses [KNOWN, INFERENCE]. *Design Mandate:* The app must not mandate bank account linking or digital payments; cash-based handovers must be first-class citizens in the system [PS].

### 1. CPCB E-Waste Management System Portal
*   **Description:** Official Central Pollution Control Board portal (`eprewaste.cpcb.gov.in`) managing registration and EPR certificate trading for producers, recyclers, and refurbishers [1.1, 1.2].
*   **Strengths:** Regulatory authority; official registry of verified recyclers; legal basis for EPR targets [1.2].
*   **Weaknesses:** Administrative web interface only; zero integration with informal street collectors; requires GST, PAN, and corporate documentation [1.2, INFERENCE].
*   **Relevance:** Primary source for authorized recycler verification [PS, 1.1].
*   **Gap:** Completely unreachable for informal *kabadiwalas* who lack corporate registration [INFERENCE].
*   **Source:** [CPCB EPR Portal](https://eprewaste.cpcb.gov.in/) [1.1]. Confidence: `[RESEARCHED]`.

### 2. Recykal (Enterprise Waste-Commerce Platform)
*   **Description:** India's largest digital B2B marketplace and EPR fulfillment platform connecting brands, large aggregators, and recyclers [4.1, 4.3].
*   **Strengths:** Extensive network (650+ brands, 675+ recyclers); robust B2B bidding and logistics tracking [4.2, 4.3].
*   **Weaknesses:** Designed for B2B enterprises; requires smartphone literacy, high bandwidth, and formal invoice billing [4.4, INFERENCE].
*   **Relevance:** Proves market viability of waste digitization in India [4.3].
*   **Gap:** Does not cater to low-literacy, offline street pickers handling small, heterogeneous e-waste lots [INFERENCE].
*   **Source:** [Recykal Platform](https://www.recykal.com/epr-e-waste) [4.1]. Confidence: `[RESEARCHED]`.

### 3. Kabadiwalla Connect (Commercial Enterprise - Chennai)
*   **Description:** A Chennai-based social enterprise integrating informal scrap shops (*kabadiwallas*) into city waste management using mapping and reverse logistics [3.1, 3.2].
*   **Strengths:** Pioneered mapping of informal scrap infrastructure; strong social inclusion model [3.1, 3.5].
*   **Weaknesses:** Focuses primarily on municipal PET plastics and general recyclables; limited presence in complex e-waste component valuation (PCBs, CRTs, critical mineral components) [3.2, 3.6, INFERENCE].
*   **Relevance:** Proves informal sector integration model [3.5].
*   **Gap:** Lacks automated AI component recognition, offline edge valuation for critical minerals, and national CPCB EPR direct handover linkage [INFERENCE].
*   **Source:** [Kabadiwalla Connect](https://www.kabadiwallaconnect.in/) [3.1]. Confidence: `[RESEARCHED]`.

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

**Selected Strategy:** **Approach 2 – Hybrid Edge-AI Native Mobile Application with Offline-First Relational Data Engine**.

### Rationale for Selection
1.  **Field Constraints Realism:** Informal waste collection happens in environments with unreliable cellular connectivity (scrap yards, basement godowns, rural transit) [KNOWN]. Approach 2 guarantees that camera scanning, lot creation, price estimation, audio safety playback, and local ledger updates work 100% offline [PS].
2.  **Zero Cloud Cost per Scan:** By deploying an INT8-quantized YOLOv8-Nano model directly on the Android device via TensorFlow Lite / ONNX Runtime, inference takes under 15ms on Snapdragon processors with zero cloud API costs [3.3].
3.  **Low-Literacy Optimization:** A native mobile application allows direct access to native Text-to-Speech (TTS) hardware, high-performance camera reticles, and tactile audio feedback impossible to render smoothly over web or SMS [INFERENCE].

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected
*   *Rejection of Approach 1 (Cloud PWA):* Cloud-dependent image uploading fails completely in network dead zones, causing long loading spinners that erode user trust among low-literacy collectors [KNOWN, INFERENCE].
*   *Rejection of Approach 3 (SMS/USSD):* SMS/USSD cannot process camera photos of e-waste components, missing explicit Requirement A ("photograph, categorize, and create digital lots") [PS].

### End-to-End Data Flow Narrative

1.  **Lot Creation & Scanning (Client Edge):**
    *   The collector opens the app in an offline scrap yard.
    *   They point the camera at a pile of e-waste (e.g., printed circuit boards).
    *   The embedded ONNX/TFLite YOLOv8-Nano model runs frame inference in $\sim 12$ms, placing a bounding box around the component and tagging it as `PCB_CLASS_A` with $92\%$ confidence [3.3, 3.10].
    *   The collector enters approximate weight ($5.5$ kg) using a large visual slider.
2.  **Instant Offline Valuation & Guidance:**
    *   The local WatermelonDB SQLite database looks up the cached buying rate for `PCB_CLASS_A` in the collector's region ($₹280$/kg) [INFERENCE].
    *   The local valuation engine calculates estimated range: $5.5 \times 280 = ₹1,540$ (Range: $₹1,450 - ₹1,600$).
    *   The app triggers an audio clip in Marathi/Hindi: *"High-grade circuit board detected. Estimated value: 1,540 Rupees. Do not burn wires or submerge in acid."* [PS].
3.  **Local Persistence & Handover Generation:**
    *   The transaction is saved locally to WatermelonDB with state `DRAFT_LOT_CREATED`.
    *   The app renders an offline QR code containing a JSON payload signed with a local device key: `{"lot_id": "UUID-8841", "cat": "PCB", "wt": 5.5, "val": 1540, "ts": 1725530000, "lat": 18.5204, "lon": 73.8567}` [INFERENCE].
4.  **Recycler Handover & Scanning:**
    *   The collector arrives at an authorized recycler facility.
    *   The recycler scans the collector’s offline QR code using their web dashboard or mobile scanner.
    *   The recycler verifies the weight on their physical weighbridge, inputs final agreed price ($₹1,550$), selects payment mode (`CASH`), and clicks "Confirm Receipt".
5.  **Synchronization & CPCB Traceability:**
    *   When the recycler's device connects to the internet, the transaction payload is pushed to the Fastify API Gateway.
    *   The server's Anomaly Detection Engine (Isolation Forest) validates that $5.5$ kg PCB at $₹1,550$ is within 2 standard deviations of regional market norms [INFERENCE].
    *   PostgreSQL updates the status to `COMPLETED_HANDOVER` and generates a verifiable CPCB EPR audit log containing SHA-256 hash of the complete lot trajectory [1.2, INFERENCE].
    *   When the collector's phone later gains signal, WatermelonDB receives the server confirmation, marking the earnings ledger entry as `SETTLED`.

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### 1. On-Device Material Classification Model
*   **Purpose:** Automatically identify e-waste material category from camera snapshot [PS].
*   **Model Type:** Quantized YOLOv8-Nano Object Detection / Classification (INT8) [3.3].
*   **Input:** Live camera frame / JPEG image tensor ($640 \times 640 \times 3$) [3.3].
*   **Output:** Bounding box coordinates, class label (`CRT`, `LCD`, `PCB_Class_A`, `PCB_Class_B`, `Cable`, `Li_Battery`, `Motor_Magnet`, `Plastic`), confidence score [3.2].
*   **Data Required:** 2,500+ annotated e-waste images from open datasets (Roboflow, Kaggle) [1.1, 1.2].
*   **Why AI is Preferable:** Low-literacy collectors cannot accurately distinguish PCB grades or CRT vs LCD panel compositions by text lists [INFERENCE].
*   **Evaluation Metric:** mean Average Precision (mAP@50) $\ge 85\%$, inference latency $<20$ms [3.3].
*   **Failure Mode:** If confidence $<60\%$, system prompts visual fallback selection with large icon cards [INFERENCE].
*   **Human Oversight:** Recycler confirms/adjusts category during physical handover [PS].

### 2. Price Estimation & Dynamic Trend Engine
*   **Purpose:** Compute fair market price range based on material sub-category, weight, region, and historical trend [PS].
*   **Model Type:** LightGBM Regressor / Quantile Regression Model.
*   **Input:** Material class, weight (kg), location geo-hash, historical time-series price index.
*   **Output:** Estimated value range ($90\%$ confidence interval in INR).
*   **Data Required:** Historical transaction prices, regional scrap market feeds.
*   **Why AI is Preferable:** Captures non-linear price fluctuations driven by global metal commodities (copper, lithium, gold) [INFERENCE].
*   **Evaluation Metric:** Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) $<8\%$.
*   **Failure Mode:** Falls back to static CPCB/Regional baseline rate matrix ($₹/kg$) cached in SQLite [INFERENCE].
*   **Human Oversight:** Collector and recycler can negotiate final sale price within transparent market bounds [PS].

### 3. Transaction Anomaly & Fraud Detection Engine
*   **Purpose:** Detect fake transactions, unrealistic weights, or price inflation attempts [PS].
*   **Model Type:** Isolation Forest / Unsupervised Autoencoder.
*   **Input:** Lot weight, estimated value, quoted price, collector transaction history, GPS distance.
*   **Output:** Anomaly Risk Score ($0.0$ to $1.0$).
*   **Data Required:** Platform historical transaction logs.
*   **Why AI is Preferable:** Identifies sophisticated fraud patterns (e.g., collusion between collector and fake recycler) across multidimensional variables [INFERENCE].
*   **Evaluation Metric:** Receiver Operating Characteristic - Area Under Curve (ROC-AUC) $\ge 0.92$.
*   **Failure Mode:** High-risk lots flagged for manual admin audit without blocking immediate cash trade [INFERENCE].
*   **Human Oversight:** System administrator reviews flagged anomalies before issuing official EPR compliance certificates [INFERENCE].

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
*   *Ledger Calculations & Balances:* Must use deterministic double-entry accounting code [INFERENCE].
*   *Digital Handover Verification:* Must use cryptographic signature / hash comparison [INFERENCE].
*   *Recycler Registration Status Check:* Must be a deterministic database boolean check (`is_cpcb_authorized == true`) [INFERENCE].

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Relational Database Schema (PostgreSQL / WatermelonDB)

```sql
-- Collector Profiles Table
CREATE TABLE collectors (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    phone_number VARCHAR(15) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    preferred_language VARCHAR(5) DEFAULT 'hi', -- 'hi', 'mr'
    operating_city VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Authorized Recyclers Table
CREATE TABLE recyclers (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    facility_name VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
    cpcb_registration_no VARCHAR(50) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    authorization_status BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE,
    accepted_materials TEXT[] NOT NULL, -- ARRAY of material codes
    location GEOGRAPHY(POINT, 4326) NOT NULL,
    contact_phone VARCHAR(15) NOT NULL,
    service_radius_km NUMERIC(5,2) DEFAULT 25.0
);

-- Material Lots Table
CREATE TABLE material_lots (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    collector_id UUID REFERENCES collectors(id),
    category VARCHAR(30) NOT NULL, -- 'PCB', 'BATTERY', 'CRT', etc.
    sub_category VARCHAR(30),
    approx_weight_kg NUMERIC(8,2) NOT NULL,
    estimated_value_inr NUMERIC(10,2) NOT NULL,
    photo_uri TEXT,
    gps_location GEOGRAPHY(POINT, 4326),
    status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'DRAFT', -- 'DRAFT', 'SYNCED', 'ACCEPTED', 'COMPLETED'
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Transactions & Handover Table
CREATE TABLE transactions (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    lot_id UUID REFERENCES material_lots(id),
    recycler_id UUID REFERENCES recyclers(id),
    quoted_price_inr NUMERIC(10,2) NOT NULL,
    final_sale_value_inr NUMERIC(10,2) NOT NULL,
    payment_mode VARCHAR(10) DEFAULT 'CASH', -- 'CASH', 'UPI'
    handover_timestamp TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE,
    handover_gps GEOGRAPHY(POINT, 4326),
    verification_qr_hash TEXT NOT NULL,
    anomaly_score NUMERIC(3,2) DEFAULT 0.0,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);
```

### Bi-Directional Delta Synchronization Protocol
The app utilizes WatermelonDB's native synchronization protocol to ensure zero data loss during network dropouts [2.4, 2.13]:

```
      LOCAL APP (WatermelonDB)                           CENTRAL BACKEND (Fastify/Postgres)
   +----------------------------+                       +----------------------------------+
   |  Local SQLite Changes Queue |                       |  PostgreSQL Database             |
   +----------------------------+                       +----------------------------------+
                 |                                                       |
                 | --- 1. PUSH: Local Changes Since Last Sync ------->   |
                 |      (Lot creations, local ledger updates)            |
                 |                                                       | (Processes & Validates)
                 |                                                       |
                 | <--- 2. PULL: Server Changes Since Last Sync -------- |
                 |      (Updated prices, new recycler bids, status)     |
                 |                                                       |
   +----------------------------+                       +----------------------------------+
   |  Merge & Resolve Conflicts |                       |  Update Global Audit Trail       |
   |  (Strategy: Server-Wins)   |                       |  (Generate EPR Traceability Log) |
   +----------------------------+                       +----------------------------------+
```

### Bandwidth & Memory Optimization Strategy
*   **Image Compression:** Photos captured in the app are auto-compressed on-device to WebP format ($800 \times 600$ resolution, $<80$ KB file size) before sync [INFERENCE].
*   **Batch Push:** Pending local transactions are bundled into a single compressed JSON payload sent via `POST /api/v1/sync/push` [2.13].
*   **Audio Asset Bundling:** All core Marathi and Hindi safety audio files are stored in the local APK bundle, eliminating audio streaming network overhead [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

*   **Minimization of Personal Data:** In compliance with the explicit problem statement and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, the system collects only essential collector metadata (phone number for OTP login, preferred language, and general operating district) [PS, KNOWN]. No Aadhaar numbers or home addresses are collected from informal pickers [PS].
*   **Location Data Privacy:** GPS coordinates are captured only during active lot creation and physical handover confirmation [PS]. Location is obfuscated using geo-hashing (fuzzy radius of $1.5$ km) when broadcasting available lots to nearby recyclers to protect collector privacy [INFERENCE].
*   **Authentication & Access Control:**
    *   *Collectors:* Passwordless OTP authentication via SMS + 4-digit offline PIN fallback [INFERENCE].
    *   *Recyclers:* Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) requiring verified CPCB EPR registration certificates before posting rates or receiving lots [1.2, INFERENCE].
*   **Data Encryption & Integrity:** All client-server communication uses TLS 1.3 encryption [KNOWN]. Local SQLite databases on Android devices are encrypted using SQLCipher [INFERENCE]. Transaction receipts contain SHA-256 HMAC signatures to prevent tamper fraud [INFERENCE].
*   **Regulatory & Safety Disclaimer:** The application explicitly notes that AI price estimations are market indicators and that physical validation occurs during handover [PS, INFERENCE].

### Final Recommendation
The team should **proceed with building the proposed hybrid Edge-AI, offline-first mobile application**. The problem statement is highly viable, strategically aligned with national priorities (Ministry of Mines & NCMM), and technically achievable for a 4–6 student team within the SIH timeframe [PS, 2.1, 2.7].

*   **Mobile App:** React Native (Expo) + WatermelonDB (SQLite) [2.3, 2.10].

Availability of annotated, localized e-waste component image datasets for fine-tuning the YOLOv8-Nano vision model across damaged Indian scrap components [1.1, 1.2].

### Biggest Risk
Resistance from existing informal scrap middlemen (*raddiwalas*) who may attempt to prevent collectors from adopting transparent pricing tools [PS, INFERENCE].

Optimizing the React Native camera frame processing pipeline and WatermelonDB delta sync engine to execute reliably on 2GB RAM Android devices without memory leaks [2.3, 2.6].
