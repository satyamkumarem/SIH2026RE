## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
This dossier outlines a comprehensive product architecture, hardware design, AI integration, and hackathon execution strategy for **SIH26225**, issued by **AICTE** under the **Student Innovation** theme [PS]. The goal is to design and prototype an interactive "phygital" (physical + digital) smart game platform—titled **"Sanskriti-Setu" (Cultural Bridge)**—that combines tactile wooden playboards embedded with RFID/NFC microcontrollers, multi-sensory audio feedback, and an offline-capable mobile AR/AI companion app [INFERENCE]. Aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 Toy-Based Pedagogy guidelines [RESEARCHED], Sanskriti-Setu brings Indian civilization, historical epochs (Indus Valley, Maurya, Gupta, Chola, Maratha, and Freedom Struggle), and traditional games (such as *Moksha Patam*, *Pachisi*, and *Pallanguzhi*) to life for school children [INFERENCE]. The system operates completely offline in resource-constrained classrooms while providing real-time interactive audio storytelling, 3D AR visualizations, and AI-powered cultural Q&A grounded in verified Indian Knowledge System (IKS) datasets [INFERENCE].

* **Req-1 [PS]: Conceptualize and develop unique toys and games**
  * *Interpretation:* The solution must be an original, engaging plaything (toy/game) designed specifically for interactive learning and entertainment.
  * *Implementation Implication:* The system must balance entertainment value with pedagogical rigor, avoiding dry textbook-style delivery [INFERENCE].

  * *Interpretation:* Content, gameplay mechanics, narrative elements, and physical artifacts must represent authentic Bharatiya heritage (e.g., Harappan urban planning, ancient trade routes, Vedic science, Indian dynasties, traditional folk games) [INFERENCE].
  * *Implementation Implication:* All narrative scripts, historical assets, and game boards must be audited against official curriculum standards (NCERT / Archaeological Survey of India documentation) to avoid factual distortion [INFERENCE].

* **Req-3 [PS]: Hardware Category Alignment**
  * *Interpretation:* As explicitly assigned to the "Hardware" category in the SIH portal [PS], the submission must include a tangible physical hardware component (e.g., embedded sensors, microcontrollers, physical tokens, tactile actuators) rather than being a standalone mobile app or website [INFERENCE].
  * *Implementation Implication:* A custom physical board/housing with embedded microcontrollers (ESP32-S3), RFID tag readers, I2S audio modules, and rechargeable power circuitry must be engineered [ASSUMPTION].

* **Inf-1 [INFERENCE]: Alignment with NEP 2020 & NCF-FS 2022**
  * *Interpretation:* The National Education Policy 2020 explicitly mandates Toy-Based Pedagogy and experiential learning for foundational and preparatory school stages [RESEARCHED].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Gameplay must include structured learning objectives, progress tracking for educators, and multi-sensory engagement [INFERENCE].

* **Inf-2 [INFERENCE]: Child Safety and Durability Standards**
  * *Interpretation:* Hardware toys intended for children must be physically safe, durable, and non-toxic [KNOWN].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Physical components must use smooth, non-toxic polished wood or eco-friendly PLA/bamboo composites with rounded edges and child-safe low-voltage (3.3V/5V) lithium battery enclosures [ASSUMPTION].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

### Hardware & Electronics Constraints
* **Power & Battery Life:** Prototype must run on a rechargeable 3.7V Li-ion battery (18650 cell with TP4056 USB-C charging module) for at least 4 hours of continuous classroom play [ASSUMPTION].
* **BOM Cost Target:** Total bill-of-materials (BOM) cost for the hackathon prototype must remain under ₹2,500–₹3,500 ($30–$40 USD) to ensure commercial feasibility for Indian schools [INFERENCE].
* **Sensor Interference:** Close proximity of multiple RFID coils can cause electromagnetic crosstalk; coil spacing must be at least 6 cm apart or multiplexed via SPI/I2C multiplexer (TCA9548A) [INFERENCE].

### Field & Classroom Constraints
* **Connectivity Reality:** Rural government schools (PM SHRI schools / Anganwadis) often lack reliable broadband or high-speed Wi-Fi [KNOWN]. The core hardware and audio playback **must operate 100% offline** without server dependencies [INFERENCE].
* **Multilingual Diversity:** Children in different Indian states require audio content in regional languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Kannada, Gujarati, etc.) [KNOWN].
* **Ruggedness & Durability:** Physical tokens and boards must withstand accidental drops, liquid spills, and rough handling by children [INFERENCE].

### Safety & Compliance Constraints
* **Materials & Toxicity:** Plastic parts must be non-toxic PLA/ABS; wooden parts must use lead-free vegetable dyes or natural varnish (aligned with BIS IS 9873 Indian Toy Safety Standards) [RESEARCHED].
* **Voltage Safety:** Maximum internal operating voltage bounded to 5V DC; no exposed high-voltage components or bare heating elements [KNOWN].

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Standalone Hardware Board with Embedded Display & Audio (Pure Hardware)
* **Mechanism:** A standalone wooden playboard containing an integrated 7-inch LCD display, ESP32-S3 microcontroller, RFID coil matrix, and internal speaker.
* **Problem Fit:** High alignment with Hardware category, completely independent of external smartphones.
* **Accuracy Potential:** High (direct wire connections).
* **Data Requirements:** Low (pre-stored assets on internal SD card).
* **Complexity:** High hardware complexity (display driving, power draw, mechanical enclosure depth).
* **Cost:** High (₹4,500–₹6,000 per unit due to 7-inch LCD panel and power demands).
* **Scalability:** Moderate (hardware unit cost limits wide distribution).
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** Moderate (wiring display + matrix in short time frame is error-prone).
* **Risks:** High battery drain; brittle LCD screen susceptible to breaking in child handling.

### Approach 2: "Phygital Hybrid" - Tactile Wooden Board + BLE + AR/AI Companion App (RECOMMENDED)
* **Mechanism:** A smart wooden board housing an ESP32-S3, RFID multiplexed reader matrix, local I2S audio speaker, and BLE transmitter. Connects wirelessly to any budget Android/iOS smartphone or tablet for optional 3D AR visuals, interactive quizzes, and AI voice Q&A.
* **Problem Fit:** Perfect fit for SIH Hardware category while maximizing interactive modern features (AR/AI).
* **Accuracy Potential:** Very High (RFID detection >99.5% accuracy; low BLE latency <80ms).
* **Data Requirements:** Moderate (local audio on board SD card; 3D models and AI RAG corpus in mobile app).
* **Complexity:** Balanced (Hardware complexity kept manageable; software handles heavy graphic/AI workloads).
* **Cost:** Low-to-Moderate (₹2,200–₹2,800 per hardware board; uses user’s existing mobile device for visuals).
* **Scalability:** High (low hardware unit cost; mobile software updates over-the-air).
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High (clear separation of hardware firmware and mobile app development among team members).
* **Risks:** BLE pairing drops (mitigated by auto-reconnect logic and standalone board audio playing even without phone).

### Approach 3: Camera Vision-Based Play Mat (Raspberry Pi + Overhead Camera)
* **Mechanism:** An overhead Raspberry Pi 4 camera mounted on a frame looking down at a printed play mat, using OpenCV color/ArUco marker recognition to identify physical token positions.
* **Problem Fit:** Fulfills Hardware category, but bulky physical gantry structure.
* **Accuracy Potential:** Moderate (highly sensitive to room lighting, shadows, hand occlusion).
* **Complexity:** High software vision pipeline; bulky mechanical frame.
* **Cost:** High (Raspberry Pi 4 + Camera + Mounting Frame ~ ₹6,500+).
* **Scalability:** Poor (bulky setup difficult to transport and deploy in varied classroom lighting).
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** Low (lighting variations in competition hall can fail optical recognition live on stage).
* **Risks:** Optical occlusion when child's hand covers tokens; frame tip-over hazard.

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

### Selection
**Approach 2 ("Phygital Hybrid")** is selected as the optimal architecture for the 4–6 student hackathon team.

### Justification
1. **Hardware Integrity with Smart UX:** Unlike pure software apps, Approach 2 builds a genuine, tangible smart hardware device (fulfilling SIH Hardware category [PS]) while leveraging smartphone processing power for rich 3D AR graphics and natural language AI [INFERENCE].
2. **Classroom Dual-Mode Operation:** In resource-constrained rural schools without smartphones, the board operates in **Standalone Mode**—children move tokens, and the embedded ESP32 immediately plays clear regional audio stories, chants, and sound effects through its built-in speaker [INFERENCE]. In homes or tech-enabled classrooms, it enters **Enhanced Mode**, syncing via BLE to unlock 3D AR re-enactments and AI Q&A [INFERENCE].
3. **Rejection of Alternatives:**
   * *Approach 1 (Standalone Display Board)* was rejected because adding a large LCD screen inflates unit cost by ~60%, reduces battery life to under 1.5 hours, and increases screen-addiction rather than promoting peer-to-peer tactile play [INFERENCE].
   * *Approach 3 (Camera Gantry)* was rejected because camera vision is extremely vulnerable to room lighting changes and hand occlusions, carries a high Raspberry Pi component cost, and requires a clumsy physical overhead mounting rig [INFERENCE].

### Data Flow Narrative
1. **Physical Interaction:** A child places a smart wooden token (e.g., "Indus Valley Terracotta Seal Token") onto the "Lothal Dockyard" spot on the physical playboard [INFERENCE].
2. **Sensor Detection:** The ESP32-S3 firmware scans the RFID matrix loop every 100ms via SPI. The MFRC522 reader detects the unique 7-byte UID of the token [ASSUMPTION].
3. **Local Audio Trigger:** The ESP32 immediately maps the UID to a local sound file stored on the micro-SD card (e.g., `/audio/hi/lothal_dock.wav`) and streams audio via I2S to the MAX98357A amplifier, producing instant audio storytelling through the 3W speaker within 120ms [ASSUMPTION].
4. **BLE Broadcast:** Simultaneously, the ESP32 updates its BLE GATT characteristic packet containing `[Token_ID: 0x0A, Zone_ID: 0x03, Event_Type: PLACE, Battery: 88%]`.
5. **Mobile Companion Sync:** The Flutter app running on a nearby Android/iOS phone receives the BLE notification [ASSUMPTION].
6. **AR Scene Render:** The Flutter app's AR module loads the low-poly 3D animated model of ancient Lothal port with ships unloading cargo, superimposing it over the smartphone camera view [INFERENCE].
7. **Interactive Quiz & AI Q&A:** The app prompts a quick audio challenge ("How did Harappan merchants weigh their goods?"). If the child taps the microphone button and asks a question, the "Sanskriti-Bot" AI engine queries its Indian Knowledge System (IKS) vector database and returns an age-appropriate voice response in Hindi or English [INFERENCE].
8. **Offline Progress Persistence:** Gameplay score and concepts mastered are saved locally in SQLite, scheduling background cloud sync when network connectivity becomes available [ASSUMPTION].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### AI Component 1: "Sanskriti-Bot" - Interactive Cultural Knowledge Assistant
* **Purpose:** Answer natural language questions asked by children regarding historical tokens, civilization facts, and cultural stories in regional languages [INFERENCE].
* **Model Type:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) using FAISS vector store + Quantized Llama-3-8B-Instruct (or GPT-4o-mini API) [ASSUMPTION].
* **Input:** Voice/Text query from child (e.g., *"Why did ancient Indians build brick bathrooms in Mohenjo-daro?"*) [INFERENCE].
* **Output:** Short, child-friendly 2-sentence explanation + follow-up interactive question [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required:** Digitized e-NCERT Social Science textbooks (Classes 6–10), ASI monument guides, and Indian Knowledge System (IKS) published reference materials [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI is Necessary:** Rule-based trees cannot handle unpredictable, open-ended questions asked by inquisitive children in natural spoken regional dialects [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation:** Human expert verification on factual fidelity score (>95%) and Flesch-Kincaid readability grade level (Grade 3–6 appropriate) [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode & Mitigation:** If confidence score is below threshold or query is out-of-domain, system falls back to a cheerful canned audio response (*"That is a great mystery! Let us move our token to the next historical site to discover more!"*) [ASSUMPTION].
* **Human Oversight:** Pre-filtered prompt guardrails prevent political, religious controversies, or non-educational topics [INFERENCE].

### Non-AI Components (Where AI SHOULD NOT Be Used)
1. **Token Identification & Board State:** Token detection MUST be strictly deterministic using RFID UIDs. Machine learning / computer vision guessing of token positions must be avoided to prevent game glitches [INFERENCE].
2. **Game Rules & Scoring:** Turn progression, dice rolls, and points must be controlled by deterministic state-machine code in Flutter/C++, not LLMs [INFERENCE].

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Database Schema (Local SQLite / Drift)

```sql
-- Tokens Table: Maps physical RFID UIDs to cultural entities
CREATE TABLE tokens (
    token_uid TEXT PRIMARY KEY,
    entity_name TEXT NOT NULL,
    epoch TEXT NOT NULL, -- e.g., 'Indus_Valley', 'Chola_Dynasty'
    description_hi TEXT,
    description_en TEXT,
    3d_model_path TEXT
);

-- Lessons & Quizzes Table
CREATE TABLE quizzes (
    quiz_id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT,
    token_uid TEXT,
    question_text TEXT NOT NULL,
    option_a TEXT NOT NULL,
    option_b TEXT NOT NULL,
    option_c TEXT NOT NULL,
    correct_option INTEGER NOT NULL, -- 1, 2, or 3
    FOREIGN KEY(token_uid) REFERENCES tokens(token_uid)
);

-- Student Progress Table (Offline First)
CREATE TABLE student_progress (
    progress_id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT,
    student_id TEXT DEFAULT 'local_child',
    token_uid TEXT,
    mastery_score INTEGER DEFAULT 0,
    times_played INTEGER DEFAULT 0,
    last_played_timestamp DATETIME DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    sync_status INTEGER DEFAULT 0 -- 0 = Pending Cloud Sync, 1 = Synced
);
```

### Offline & Synchronization Architecture

```
[ Physical Board Interaction ]
             |
             v
[ ESP32 Local SD Card ] ---> (Plays Audio directly via Speaker - ZERO Internet needed)
             |
             +---> [ BLE Sync ] ---> [ Mobile App Local SQLite DB ]
                                                 |
                                     (Network Check: Online?)
                                       /                  \
                                    (Yes)                 (No)
                                     /                      \
                                    v                        v
                      [ Cloud Firebase Sync ]    [ Store in Queue (sync_status=0) ]
```
