## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
The National Capital Region (NCR) of Delhi suffers from severe seasonal air pollution driven by complex meteorological-chemical interactions [PS]. Existing forecasting systems often decouple atmospheric physics from chemical transport, failing to capture the dynamic two-way feedback loops where particulate matter ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$) blocks solar radiation, cools the surface, and suppresses the Planetary Boundary Layer (PBL) height, which in turn traps pollutants under strong atmospheric inversion layers [PS, KNOWN]. This research dossier defines an end-to-end, coupled air quality and weather forecasting platform tailored for Delhi NCR [PS]. The proposed architecture integrates open-source numerical weather-chemistry modeling (WRF-Chem) with a fast AI-based surrogate model (Physics-Informed Graph Neural Network) to deliver high-resolution 72-hour predictions for $\text{PM}_{2.5}$, $\text{PM}_{10}$, $\text{O}_3$, and $\text{NO}_x$ [INFERENCE]. The platform incorporates geostationary satellite fire detections (INSAT-3DS/VIIRS) to capture late-afternoon stubble burning, computes real-time Atmospheric Inversion Indices, and provides actionable 72-hour decision metrics via a web GIS dashboard [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].

## 2. Problem Deconstruction

### Atomic Requirements Extraction

1. **[REQ-01] High-Resolution 72-Hour AQI Forecasting**
   * *Source Text:* "The challenge is to build a high-resolution, coupled forecasting system specifically tailored for Delhi NCR that predicts AQI for the next 72 hours." [PS]
   * *Interpretation:* The system must output hourly concentrations for key criteria pollutants ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$, $\text{PM}_{10}$, $\text{O}_3$, $\text{NO}_2$, $\text{SO}_2$, $\text{CO}$) across Delhi NCR at a spatial grid resolution of $\le 3\text{ km}$ over a 72-hour prospective window [INFERENCE].
   * *Implementation Implication:* Requires time-series modeling with spatial discretization, coupled numerical or surrogate inference engines, and automated daily model execution pipelines [INFERENCE].
   * *Confidence Tag:* [PS]

2. **[REQ-02] Advanced Weather-Chemistry Coupled Framework Integration**
   * *Source Text:* "The solution must leverage advanced weather-chemistry models (such as WRF-Chem or similar open-source coupled frameworks) to dynamically interlink meteorology with pollution dispersion (specifically PM2.5 and Ground-level Ozone)." [PS]
   * *Interpretation:* Standard uncoupled models (e.g., standalone WRF or AERMOD) are insufficient; chemical transport equations must be solved concurrently with Navier-Stokes equations [KNOWN].
   * *Implementation Implication:* Integration with WRF-Chem v4.4+ (or a surrogate emulator trained on WRF-Chem outputs) incorporating gas-phase mechanisms (e.g., MOZART/RADM2) and aerosol schemes (MOSAIC/MADE) [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
   * *Confidence Tag:* [PS]

3. **[REQ-03] Modeling Atmospheric Inversion & External Pollution Spikes**
   * *Source Text:* "A core focus should be accurately modeling the impact of atmospheric inversion on external pollution spikes, such as regional stubble burning..." [PS]
   * *Interpretation:* The model must ingest regional biomass burning emissions (Punjab, Haryana, Western UP) and quantify how thermal inversions trap these transboundary plumes near the ground in Delhi [INFERENCE].
   * *Implementation Implication:* Pipeline must ingest active fire hotspots, estimate Fire Radiative Power (FRP) and plume injection height, and track thermodynamic vertical profiles ($\frac{dT}{dz} > 0$) [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
   * *Confidence Tag:* [PS]

4. **[REQ-04] Two-Way Meteorology-Chemistry Feedback Workflow**
   * *Source Text:* "Implement a workflow that handles two-way feedback between meteorology (temperature, wind, PBL height) and chemistry (PM2.5, PM10, O3, NOx)." [PS]
   * *Interpretation:* Direct aerosol radiative forcing (aerosols reflecting/absorbing solar shortwave radiation) must update local temperature, wind speed, and PBL height in real time within the simulation step [KNOWN, INFERENCE].
   * *Implementation Implication:* Radiation parameterizations (e.g., RRTMG) must be coupled with aerosol optical properties so that aerosol optical depth (AOD) dynamically modifies surface heating rates [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
   * *Confidence Tag:* [PS]

5. **[REQ-05] User-Friendly Real-Time 72-Hour Dashboard**
   * *Source Text:* "A user-friendly, real-time dashboard displaying high-resolution AQI forecasts for Delhi NCR with a 72-hour outlook." [PS]
   * *Interpretation:* Interactive Web GIS portal providing spatio-temporal slider controls, station-level breakdown, pollutant sub-index maps, and forecast heatmaps [INFERENCE].
   * *Implementation Implication:* WebGL-rendered maps (MapLibre GL / Deck.gl) connected to a REST/WebSocket API back-end serving cached tile vectors and JSON spatial series [INFERENCE].
   * *Confidence Tag:* [PS]

6. **[REQ-06] Explicit Inversion Tracking & Plume Dispersion Analytics**
   * *Source Text:* "Features that explicitly track atmospheric inversion strength and predict how stubble-burning plumes will disperse under prevailing weather conditions." [PS]
   * *Interpretation:* Dedicated dashboard widgets calculating Inversion Intensity ($^\circ\text{C/100m}$), Lapse Rate anomalies, PBL height dynamics, and forward trajectory vector overlays for farm fire plumes [INFERENCE].
   * *Implementation Implication:* Compute vertical thermodynamic gradients from atmospheric profile data and run HYSPLIT / Lagrangian particle dispersion overlays [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
   * *Confidence Tag:* [PS]

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1. **Computational Limits for Student Hackathons:** Running full 3D WRF-Chem numerical simulations at $1\text{ km}$ resolution for 72 hours requires HPC clusters with $64\text{--}128$ CPU cores taking $4\text{--}8$ hours per forecast run [KNOWN, INFERENCE]. Hackathon teams cannot execute full WRF-Chem live during a 36-hour pitch [INFERENCE].
2. **Satellite Pass & Fire Detection Gaps:** NASA MODIS/VIIRS polar satellites pass over Northern India between 10:30 AM and 1:30 PM IST [RESEARCHED]. Recent 2025 research proves farmers have shifted stubble burning to 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM IST to evade detection, requiring geostationary INSAT-3DS data [RESEARCHED].
3. **Data Access Constraints:** Official CPCB CAAQMS APIs on `data.gov.in` have strict rate limits, occasional station downtime, and data format inconsistencies (e.g., string representation of numbers, missing values) [RESEARCHED].
4. **Bandwidth & Field Connectivity:** Mobile devices used by field officers or citizens in peri-urban NCR may experience spotty 3G/4G coverage during peak hours [KNOWN].
5. **Language & Literacy:** Dashboard must support English and Hindi with intuitive color-coded CPCB AQI bands (Good, Satisfactory, Moderate, Poor, Very Poor, Severe) [KNOWN].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

### Selected Architecture: Approach 3 — Hybrid Physics-Informed GNN Surrogate Framework

The team will build a **Hybrid Coupled System** that combines:
1. **Pre-computed High-Resolution WRF-Chem Baseline Runs:** Generating synthetic boundary layer-chemistry feedback training sets representing distinct Delhi weather regimes (Strong Inversion, Weak Wind, Stubble Burning Peak, Clear Days) [INFERENCE].
2. **Physics-Informed Graph Neural Network (P-GNN) Surrogate Engine:** Graph nodes represent monitoring stations/grid points across NCR, and edges capture wind vector advection [INFERENCE]. The GNN predicts hourly pollutant concentrations and local weather updates while enforcing mass-conservation and aerosol radiative extinction constraints [INFERENCE].
3. **Real-Time Inversion & Stubble Plume Processor:** Ingesting NRT INSAT-3DS / NASA FIRMS active fire locations and computing vertical temperature gradient metrics ($\Delta T = T_{925\text{hPa}} - T_{2\text{m}}$) [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected:
* **Approach 1 (Decoupled ML) Rejected:** Completely ignores aerosol-radiative feedback loops and temperature inversion physics, violating explicit requirements [REQ-02, REQ-04] [PS].
* **Approach 2 (Full WRF-Chem Live Execution) Rejected:** Running a 72-hour 3D coupled WRF-Chem simulation live during an SIH judging demonstration is computationally infeasible due to time and resource constraints [INFERENCE].

### Data Flow Narrative

1. **Ingestion Step:** Every hour, background workers fetch latest ground air quality metrics from CPCB CAAQMS via API, weather forcing from NOAA GFS, and active farm fire locations from NASA FIRMS / INSAT-3DS [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
2. **Inversion & Plume Calculation:** The Inversion Module evaluates vertical temperature profiles. If $T_{925\text{hPa}} > T_{2\text{m}}$, an active inversion layer is flagged [INFERENCE]. Active fire radiative power (FRP) is converted into emission rates and plume injection heights [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
3. **Surrogate Model Execution:** The P-GNN model takes the current state, computes spatial advection along wind vectors across NCR grid nodes, and iteratively calculates 72 future 1-hour steps [INFERENCE]. At each step, predicted aerosol loading updates shortwave extinction, adjusting local surface temperature and suppressing PBL height for the next time step [INFERENCE].
4. **Data Persistence & Serving:** Output arrays are stored in PostGIS and cached in Redis [INFERENCE].
5. **Visualization:** The Web GIS frontend queries the API, rendering animated aerosol heatmaps, wind vectors, and inversion gauges on MapLibre GL [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### 1. Physics-Informed Graph Neural Network (P-GNN)
* **Purpose:** Emulates two-way coupled WRF-Chem atmospheric physics and chemical transport in real time [INFERENCE].
* **Model Type:** Spatial-Temporal Graph Neural Network (ST-GNN) with Message Passing over geographical spatial grids [INFERENCE].
* **Input:** Station/Grid features (Current $\text{PM}_{2.5}$, $\text{PM}_{10}$, $\text{O}_3$, $\text{NO}_x$, Temperature, RH, Wind Speed/Direction, PBL Height, Inversion Index, Regional Fire FRP) [INFERENCE].
* **Output:** Predicted pollutant concentrations ($\mu\text{g/m}^3$) and updated local weather variables ($T$, Wind, PBL Height) for $T+1$ to $T+72$ hours [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required:** 2 years of CPCB station data, ERA5 weather reanalysis, and pre-computed synthetic WRF-Chem coupled runs [INFERENCE].
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Running 3D differential equations numerically takes hours on HPC; P-GNN approximates solutions in $<2$ seconds while preserving physical relationships [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation:** Root Mean Square Error (RMSE), Mean Absolute Relative Error (MARE), and Index of Agreement (IOA) against ground CPCB readings [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode:** Extreme unobserved weather events (e.g., sudden unseasonal cyclonic rainfall) [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight:** Automated fallback to simple persistence/advection models if model confidence score drops below threshold [INFERENCE].

### 2. Stubble Fire Plume Injection & Trajectory Estimator
* **Purpose:** Estimates vertical smoke plume height and downwind dispersion path [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Model Type:** Random Forest Regressor + Lagrangian Particle Dispersion Approximation [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Input:** Fire Radiative Power (FRP from INSAT-3DS/VIIRS), Surface Temperature, Wind Profile [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Output:** Plume injection height ($\text{meters}$) and 2D spatial trajectory coordinates over 72 hours [INFERENCE].
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Empirical plume rise formulas (e.g., Sofiev algorithm) require detailed thermodynamic inputs that are often missing; ML infers height reliably from satellite FRP and boundary layer stability [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation:** Comparison against satellite aerosol vertical profiles (CALIPSO / Sentinel-5P TROPOMI) [RESEARCHED].
* **Failure Mode:** High cloud cover obscuring satellite thermal sensors [RESEARCHED].
* **Human Oversight:** Flagged as "Satellite Cloud Obscured - Using Climatological Fallback" on dashboard UI [INFERENCE].

### Explicit Exclusion of AI
* **AQI Calculation Engine:** Standard Indian CPCB AQI calculation **MUST NOT** use AI/ML [INFERENCE]. The conversion of pollutant concentration ($\mu\text{g/m}^3$) to Sub-Index and final AQI is defined by exact linear breakpoint formulas established by CPCB guidelines [RESEARCHED]. Using ML for AQI formula calculation introduces non-deterministic errors in a deterministic regulatory standard [INFERENCE].

### Low-Network & Offline Strategies
1. **Client-Side Vector Tile Caching:** The Next.js frontend uses Service Workers and IndexedDB to cache GeoJSON forecast layers for offline viewing when field teams lose cellular connectivity [INFERENCE].
2. **Compact Protobuf Payload Protocol:** Forecast API payloads are serialized using Protocol Buffers (Protobuf) instead of verbose JSON, reducing network payload size by up to $70\%$ [INFERENCE].
