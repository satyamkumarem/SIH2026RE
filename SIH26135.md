## 1. Executive Snapshot

To evaluate the long-term impact of vocational skilling initiatives in Maharashtra, government agencies require verifiable, longitudinal outcome data that tracks trainees beyond course completion [PS]. Current skill monitoring systems struggle with incomplete post-training data due to candidate migration, phone number changes, inconsistent employer reporting, and fragmented program identifiers across departments [PS]. The proposed solution—**MahaSankalp (Maharashtra Skill Outcomes Analytics & Longitudinal Progress Tracker)**—is a privacy-conscious, multi-signal outcome tracking and analytics system [INFERENCE]. It links training enrollment with multi-modal employment signals (including EPFO/ESIC digital verification, GSTIN employer validation, self-employment GST/UDYAM records, and apprenticeship portal data), automates candidate follow-ups via multilingual AI conversational agents (WhatsApp, Voice IVR in Marathi/Hindi/English), and equips field workers with offline-capable mobile tools for assisted tracking [INFERENCE]. The system provides predictive attrition modeling, skill gap diagnostics, provider ranking dashboards, and demographic impact analytics to drive evidence-based policy and resource allocation [PS].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1. **Geography & Regional Diversity:** Maharashtra spans 36 districts ranging from highly urbanized MMR (Mumbai Metropolitan Region) and Pune to rural and tribal belts (e.g., Gadchiroli, Nandurbar, Melghat) `[KNOWN]`. Systems must function seamlessly across diverse economic zones `[INFERENCE]`.
2. **Connectivity:** High-speed 4G/5G is widespread in urban centers, but 2G/3G or periodic network blackouts occur in rural/tribal pockets `[KNOWN]`. Solution must support offline data collection with automatic background sync `[INFERENCE]`.
3. **Language & Literacy:** Trainees exhibit varying levels of digital literacy `[INFERENCE]`. While English is used in corporate settings, regional trainees predominantly speak Marathi and Hindi `[KNOWN]`. Text-only solutions fail; voice-based IVR and interactive messaging in Marathi are necessary `[INFERENCE]`.
4. **Phone Number Churn:** Rural/migrant workers frequently switch SIM cards or lose access to registered mobile numbers `[KNOWN]`. Relying solely on a single mobile number leads to 40–50% tracking loss within 12 months `[INFERENCE]`. Alternate contact harvesting (secondary phone, WhatsApp, family contact) is required `[INFERENCE]`.
5. **Employer Reporting Overhead:** Small MSMEs in MIDC industrial estates lack HR portals and will not fill complex 10-page surveys `[KNOWN]`. Employer verification must be lightweight (e.g., SMS link, 1-click WhatsApp confirmation, or GSTIN payroll cross-reference) `[INFERENCE]`.
6. **Regulatory / DPDP Act 2023:** Storing sensitive personal identifier data (Aadhaar, salary, employer details) requires strict encryption, explicit consent, and data minimization under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 `[KNOWN]`.

### Approach 1: Manual TP Call Center & Portal Reporting
* **Mechanism:** Web portal where Training Partners log monthly call records after contacting alumni `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Pros:** Simple architecture; low initial technical complexity `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Cons:** High operational costs; zero protection against TP data manipulation; high dropout rate as trainees change phone numbers `[INFERENCE]`.

### Approach 2: Direct Automated WhatsApp & Conversational Voice Bot
* **Mechanism:** Automated WhatsApp bot and IVR voice dialer outreach sent directly to candidate at months 3, 6, 12, 24, and 36 `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Pros:** Low cost per outreach; direct candidate interaction; scalable `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Cons:** Fails for candidates who change phone numbers; cannot verify if candidate's self-reported salary is true; misses candidates without digital access `[INFERENCE]`.

### Approach 3: Hybrid Tri-Layer Engine (RECOMMENDED)
* **Mechanism:** Combines (1) Passive Digital Signal Integration (EPFO, GSTIN, UDYAM, MYKPY, NAPS APIs), (2) Multilingual Conversational AI (WhatsApp Bot + Bhashini Voice IVR), and (3) Field-Agent Assisted Tracking (Mobile App for DSDOs/ITIs) with an ML-driven Attrition Diagnostic Module `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Pros:** Maximum reach across urban and rural demographics; tamper-proof verification via official signals; automated fallback handling for changed phone numbers; DPDP compliant `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Cons:** Requires integration across multiple mock API protocols during hackathon stage `[INFERENCE]`.

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

The **Hybrid Tri-Layer Engine (Approach 3)** is selected as the winning architecture for the following reasons `[INFERENCE]`:

1. **Solves the Core Data Credibility Deficit:** Relying solely on candidate self-reporting or TP reporting leads to inaccurate data `[INFERENCE]`. Triangulating data between official payroll signals (EPFO/ESIC), self-employment registries (UDYAM), and direct conversational feedback creates an audit-ready outcome record `[INFERENCE]`.
2. **Handles Phone Number & Geographic Churn:** When a candidate changes their primary number, the system automatically triggers alternate contact routing (secondary mobile provided during eKYC, WhatsApp ID, or DigiLocker address update) `[INFERENCE]`. If digital contact fails, the candidate is routed to the DSDO field agent app for assisted local follow-up `[INFERENCE]`.
3. **Low Trainee Burden & High Accessibility:** Micro-surveys take under 60 seconds over WhatsApp or Marathi Voice IVR, powered by Bhashini models, accommodating low-literacy candidates `[INFERENCE]`.
4. **Actionable Policy Insights:** Combining quantitative wage metrics with NLP-driven qualitative attrition analysis gives the Maharashtra State Innovation Society and SEEID clear evidence to reward high-performing TPs and redesign underperforming courses `[INFERENCE]`.

*Why Alternatives Were Rejected:* Approach 1 was rejected due to lack of scalability and vulnerability to TP fraud `[INFERENCE]`. Approach 2 was rejected because pure self-reporting misses candidates who change phone numbers or lack smartphones, violating the requirement for comprehensive state-wide tracking `[INFERENCE]`.

### Detailed Step-by-Step Data Flow

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                 STEP-BY-STEP DATA FLOW NARRATIVE                                  |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
 1. Trainee Enrollment & Consent Capture:
    - Candidate completes training course at TP/ITI.
    - TP uploads candidate batch details to MahaSankalp via API/CSV.
    - Trainee receives SMS/WhatsApp with Aadhaar e-KYC consent link.
    - Trainee grants DPDP consent for 36-month outcome tracking; Consent Vault stores hash.

 2. Automated Passive Signal Check (Months 3, 6, 12, 24, 36):
    - Scheduled Cron Worker triggers background verification.
    - Verification Engine queries Mock EPFO API using candidate masked Aadhaar/PAN.
    - If EPFO match found -> Employment status updated to "Formal Sector Employed", Wage calculated.
    - If no EPFO match -> System queries Mock UDYAM / NAPS / MYKPY registries for self-employment.

 3. Conversational Follow-Up (Active Signal):
    - If passive verification is inconclusive, Messaging Microservice triggers Marathi WhatsApp bot.
    - If candidate un-responded after 3 days -> Voice IVR Bot dials candidate using Bhashini Marathi TTS/STT.
    - Candidate answers 4 short micro-questions: Current Job, Salary Bracket, Course Relevance, Reasons for Attrition.

 4. Assisted Field Escalation (Fallback):
    - If phone number fails or candidate un-contactable after 14 days -> Task routed to local DSDO Field Agent App.
    - Field agent visits last known address/community center, conducts offline interview, syncs data when connected.

 5. Analytics & Policy Engine Execution:
    - Raw event logs streamed into ClickHouse OLAP database.
    - XGBoost ML Model analyzes responses, classifies attrition root causes (e.g., low wage, distance, skill mismatch).
    - Analytics Engine updates District Skill Scorecards, Course Effectiveness Index, and Provider Rankings.
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1. Multilingual Conversational Follow-Up Agent
* **Purpose:** Conduct voice and text micro-surveys in Marathi, Hindi, and English `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Model Type:** Bhashini Speech Recognition/TTS + Fine-tuned LLaMA-3-8B Intent Classifier `[RESEARCHED]`.
* **Input:** Candidate audio streams or text inputs via WhatsApp/Voice call `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Output:** Structured JSON containing: `employment_status`, `monthly_salary_range`, `training_relevance_rating`, `attrition_reason` `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Data Required:** 500 annotated audio/text conversations in Marathi/Hindi skilling terminology `[ASSUMPTION]`.
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Fixed DTMF IVR systems have high drop-off rates (>70%) among non-digitally literate candidates; conversational AI allows natural regional speech `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Evaluation Metric:** Intent classification accuracy (>85%), WER (Word Error Rate < 15% for Marathi TTS) `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Failure Mode & Oversight:** If confidence score is <0.60, candidate response is flagged for human DSDO review `[INFERENCE]`.

### 2. Skill Gap & Attrition Cause Classifier
* **Purpose:** Categorize unstructured candidate qualitative feedback into actionable policy buckets (e.g., "Wage below market standard", "Location/Migration issue", "Skill taught outdated", "Gender/Family constraints") `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Model Type:** TF-IDF + XGBoost Classifier & DistilBERT multi-class classifier `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Input:** Raw text response from candidate chat or voice transcript `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Output:** Multi-label classification tags with severity weighting `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Data Required:** 1,500 labeled feedback text samples `[ASSUMPTION]`.
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Manual reading of thousands of monthly feedback notes is infeasible for state officers `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Evaluation Metric:** Macro F1-Score (>0.80) `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Failure Mode & Oversight:** Unclassified feedback falls into an "Other / Manual Review" bucket for district policy team audit `[INFERENCE]`.

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
* **Official Employment Signal Verification:** Verification against EPFO/ESIC/GST registers must be strict deterministic database matching, NOT AI probabilistic estimation `[INFERENCE]`.
* **Financial Payouts / Provider Penalties:** Penalizing Training Partners or approving milestone payments MUST be governed by deterministic rule-based business logic, NOT black-box ML models `[INFERENCE]`.

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

1. **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Compliance:**
   * **Data Minimization:** Store only hashed values for primary identity keys (Aadhaar, PAN). Raw Aadhaar numbers are never saved in plain text `[INFERENCE]`.
   * **Explicit Consent Vault:** Every candidate must explicitly opt-in via OTP or signature during enrollment. Consent details (timestamp, purpose, version) are digitally stored in a dedicated `consent_records` table `[INFERENCE]`.
   * **Right to Withdraw:** Trainees can revoke consent at any time via WhatsApp command (`STOP`) or portal opt-out, immediately halting automated verification `[INFERENCE]`.
2. **Data Encryption Standard:** All data in transit uses TLS 1.3 encryption. Sensitive data at rest (personal contact details, salary levels) uses AES-256 encryption `[INFERENCE]`.
3. **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):**
   * **State Super Admin (SEEID/MSInS):** Full aggregated read access; masked PII views `[INFERENCE]`.
   * **Training Partner (TP):** Restricted view of only candidates enrolled in their specific courses `[INFERENCE]`.
   * **DSDO Field Agent:** Access limited to candidates assigned within their district jurisdiction `[INFERENCE]`.
4. **Data Retention & Privacy-Preserving Analytics:**
   * Longitudinal logs are retained for a maximum of 5 years post-course completion, after which records are automatically anonymized for research use `[INFERENCE]`.
   * Public dashboard reports apply K-Anonymity ($k \ge 10$) so no individual candidate can be identified from course or district aggregations `[INFERENCE]`.
