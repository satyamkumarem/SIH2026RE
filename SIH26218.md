## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Executive Summary
The proposed solution, **AeroResQ-Med**, is a dual-capability Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) and ground robotics platform designed specifically for Indian disaster management and emergency medical logistics [INFERENCE]. Operating under the regulatory framework of DGCA Drone Rules 2021 [KNOWN], the platform addresses two critical Indian emergency vectors: (1) time-critical, temperature-controlled delivery of life-saving medical supplies (blood bags, antivenom, rabies vaccines, diagnostic samples) to isolated rural/flood-hit Primary Health Centres (PHCs) [RESEARCHED], and (2) real-time thermal search and rescue (SAR) for locating trapped disaster victims in dense vegetation, floodwaters, or debris fields using edge-accelerated AI [RESEARCHED]. The system pairs a custom quadrotor/VTOL drone with an onboard NVIDIA Jetson companion computer running ROS 2 and lightweight YOLOv8-thermal models [RESEARCHED], communicating via a low-latency MAVLink telemetry pipeline to an offline-capable Ground Control Station (GCS) [KNOWN].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

### Field & Operational Realities
* **Connectivity Restrictions:** Disaster zones (e.g., Uttarakhand floods or Odisha cyclones) suffer $90\%+$ cellular tower outages [RESEARCHED]. Solution must rely on point-to-point RF telemetry (868/915 MHz) or local Wi-Fi Broadcast (WFB-ng) [KNOWN].
* **Power & Battery Limitations:** LiPo/LiHV batteries offer $20\text{--}35\text{ minutes}$ flight endurance under payload weight ($1.5\text{--}2.5\text{ kg}$) [KNOWN]. Swap-and-charge battery stations or field generators are required at base camps [ASSUMPTION].
* **Weather & Environmental Harshness:** Operating in high humidity ($90\%+$), light rain ($IP54$ requirement), dust, and ambient temperatures up to $45^\circ\text{C}$ in plains or sub-zero in Himalayan passes [RESEARCHED].
* **Regulatory & Airspace Limits:** DGCA mandates flying below $400\text{ feet}$ AGL in Green Zones without prior ATC permit [RESEARCHED]. Operations in Yellow/Red zones require DigitalSky authorization [RESEARCHED].
* **Operator Digital Literacy:** Primary Health Centre staff and local emergency volunteers may lack specialized drone pilot training [RESEARCHED]. The interface must feature a simplified "Single-Tap Mission Request & Autonomous Land/Drop" UI [INFERENCE].

## 8. Existing Solutions & Gap Analysis

### 1. ICMR i-DRONE Program
* **Description:** Indian Council of Medical Research initiative using drones for delivering vaccines, blood bags, and TB sputum samples in North-East India, Himachal Pradesh, and Telangana [RESEARCHED].
* **Strengths:** Proven real-world impact; reduced TB diagnostic turnaround from 15 days to 5 days in Telangana [RESEARCHED].
* **Weaknesses:** Highly specialized for point-to-point medical transit; lacks onboard real-time SAR computer vision capabilities [INFERENCE].
* **Relevance & Gap:** Proves medical drone viability in India. **Gap:** Lacks integrated dual-use SAR victim location and automated drop/winch capability on a single open platform [INFERENCE].

### 2. AIIMS Rishikesh Medical Drone Delivery
* **Description:** TechEagle Vertiplane X3 deployed at AIIMS Rishikesh for transporting antitubercular drugs and blood components across mountainous Uttarakhand terrain [RESEARCHED].
* **Strengths:** Long range VTOL fixed-wing design; successfully handles steep Himalayan elevation changes [RESEARCHED].
* **Weaknesses:** High capital expenditure ($>\text{₹}15\text{--}25\text{ Lakhs}$); proprietary closed ecosystem; requires skilled operators [INFERENCE].
* **Relevance & Gap:** Demonstrates mountain logistics feasibility. **Gap:** Extremely expensive, proprietary, and incapable of low-altitude search and rescue hover missions [INFERENCE].

### 3. NDRF / Garuda Aerospace Disaster Response Drones
* **Description:** Commercial quadrotors deployed during floods (e.g., Andhra Pradesh, Vijayawada) and tunnel collapses for aerial surveillance and dry food packet drops [RESEARCHED].
* **Strengths:** Rapid deployment; high availability of off-the-shelf multirotors [RESEARCHED].
* **Weaknesses:** Unmonitored, non-insulated payload drops; manual visual inspection by operators causes human fatigue and delayed victim detection [RESEARCHED].
* **Relevance & Gap:** Highlights operational need during disasters. **Gap:** Lacks automated thermal AI detection and active cold-chain stabilization [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

### Selection: Approach B (Custom Multirotor UAV + Jetson Edge AI + Cold-Chain/Winch Payload + Offline ROS 2 GCS)

#### Why Approach B Wins
1. **Zero Cloud Dependency:** Running thermal object detection locally on the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano ensures instant victim localization ($<50\text{ ms}$ inference latency) even when all cellular towers are destroyed [RESEARCHED].
2. **Hover & Precision Winch Capability:** Unlike fixed-wing aircraft, a multirotor can hover steadily at $15\text{ m}$ elevation to lower delicate medical payloads via winch into tight clearings or flooded rooftops [RESEARCHED].
3. **Hackathon Feasibility:** Uses mature open-source stacks (PX4 Autopilot, MAVLink, ROS 2 Humble, QGroundControl) allowing the team to demonstrate a functional physical hardware prototype alongside a digital twin SITL simulation within the hackathon timeline [RESEARCHED].

#### Rejection Justifications
* **Approach A Rejected:** Highly dependent on commercial proprietary APIs (DJI SDK restrictions), vulnerable to cellular tower blackout in disaster zones, and legally restricted from custom payload modification under warranty [INFERENCE].
* **Approach C Rejected:** Mechanical transition mechanisms for tilt-rotor VTOLs and ground track clearance for UGVs introduce single points of mechanical failure that cannot be reliably validated within student hackathon constraints [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative
1. **Pre-Flight Authorization:** The operator inputs mission waypoints into the Web GCS. The DigitalSky NPNT module verifies airspace coordinates against cached Green Zone maps and signs a digital Permission Artefact (PA) [RESEARCHED].
2. **Flight Execution:** The GCS transmits the signed mission profile to the Pixhawk 6X over RF telemetry. The PX4 autopilot arms the motors and executes autonomous takeoff [KNOWN].
3. **Perception Pipeline:** During flight, the FLIR thermal camera feeds video frames to the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano. The ROS 2 `thermal_perception_node` passes frames through a quantized TensorRT YOLOv8-thermal model running at 30 FPS [RESEARCHED].
4. **Target Localization:** Upon detecting a human signature ($>85\%$ confidence), the Jetson fuses bounding box pixel coordinates with live GPS/altitude data from Pixhawk via MAVLink to compute exact latitude/longitude coordinates of the survivor [INFERENCE].
5. **Alert & Winch Delivery:** The companion computer immediately broadcasts a priority MAVLink alert to the GCS. If delivering medical supplies, the drone hovers at $15\text{ m}$, activates the PWM servo winch, lowers the insulated box, monitors line slack via current feedback, releases the latch, and retrieves the line before initiating Return-to-Launch (RTL) [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### AI Component 1: Thermal Human / Survivor Detection
* **Purpose:** Automatically detect trapped, unconscious, or obscured victims in low-visibility environments [RESEARCHED].
* **Model Type:** YOLOv8n-thermal (Quantized to INT8/FP16 via TensorRT) [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** Raw thermal video stream ($640 \times 512$ pixel resolution @ 30 FPS) [RESEARCHED].
* **Output:** Bounding box coordinates $[x, y, w, h]$, class tag (`human`), confidence score, and calculated target GPS coordinate [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required:** 15,000+ annotated thermal IR images across mountain, flood, forest, and urban rubble backgrounds [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI:** Classical thresholding fails under high ambient heat, heated rocks, or sunlight reflections; CNNs learn structural heat distribution shapes [RESEARCHED].
* **Evaluation Metric:** mAP@0.5 $> 88\%$, Inference time $< 35\text{ ms}$ on Jetson Orin Nano [RESEARCHED].
* **Failure Mode & Oversight:** High thermal background clutter may cause false positives. All detected targets trigger an audiovisual flag on the GCS for human operator validation [INFERENCE].

### AI Component 2: Precision Drop Zone Clearance Verification
* **Purpose:** Ensure winch drop zone is clear of power lines, tree branches, or bystanders [INFERENCE].
* **Model Type:** Lightweight Semantic Segmentation (Fast-SCNN / MobileNetV3-U-Net) [INFERENCE].
* **Input:** Downward-facing RGB camera feed ($1080\text{p}$) [INFERENCE].
* **Output:** Obstacle mask identifying safe landing/winch area [INFERENCE].
* **Why AI:** Simple ultrasonic sensors miss thin power lines or wire mesh [KNOWN].
* **Failure Mode:** In poor light, fallback to manual winch control mode operated by GCS pilot [INFERENCE].

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
* **Flight Control Stabilization:** Primary PID flight stability loops, motor mixing, and fail-safe return-to-launch logic must remain strictly deterministic inside PX4 C++ firmware. Neural networks must **never** sit directly in the primary flight control loop [INFERENCE].

### Offline & Sync Mechanics
* **Local Storage First:** All telemetry, thermal detections, temperature logs, and flight paths are written locally to the Jetson's NVMe SSD inside an SQLite database (`flight_log.sqlite`) [INFERENCE].
* **Store-and-Forward Protocol:** When RF connection drops, the companion computer buffers telemetry. Once the drone returns within RF range ($1\text{--}3\text{ km}$ from base camp), an asynchronous Sync Manager uploads missed packets to the GCS PostGIS database [INFERENCE].
* **Bandwidth Optimization:** Thermal video is processed at $100\%$ quality on the edge, but only compressed low-resolution thumbnail crops ($128 \times 128$ PNG) of confirmed detections are transmitted over constrained RF telemetry links [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

* **Data Privacy (DPDP Act 2023 Compliance):** Facial recognition is deliberately excluded. Thermal imagery captures non-identifiable heat signatures, ensuring compliance with Indian data privacy mandates [RESEARCHED]. Video streams recorded during missions are anonymized and auto-purged after 30 days unless flagged for disaster audits [INFERENCE].
* **Airspace & Regulatory Governance:** Compliant with DGCA Drone Rules 2021 [RESEARCHED]. Enforces hard-coded hardware geofences in PX4 firmware preventing entry into airport Red Zones without verified cryptographic authorization artefacts from DigitalSky [RESEARCHED].
* **Link Encryption:** Telemetry over 868 MHz and Wi-Fi Broadcast is encrypted using AES-128 / AES-256 CTR mode, preventing rogue takeover or signal spoofing during public disaster operations [KNOWN].
* **Liability & Fail-Safe Governance:** Hardware features an independent physical kill switch, automatic Return-to-Launch (RTL) triggered on battery drop below $20\%$, loss of telemetry link ($>15\text{ seconds}$), or geofence breach [KNOWN].

* **True Off-Grid Independence:** Operates completely disconnected from the public internet or cellular infrastructure using onboard TensorRT edge AI and local mesh RF communications [INFERENCE].
* **Integrated Indian Regulatory Stack:** Native integration with DGCA Drone Rules 2021, DigitalSky NPNT validation, and eGCA UIN record keeping, ensuring legal operational readiness for Indian agencies [RESEARCHED].
* **Precision Hover Drop vs. Crash Landing:** Avoids landing in hazardous floodwater by utilizing a closed-loop current-sensing winch system, protecting the aircraft and ensuring payload safety [INFERENCE].
