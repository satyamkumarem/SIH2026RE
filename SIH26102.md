## 1. Executive Snapshot

This dossier presents a complete technical, data, AI, and strategic architecture for **SIH26102**, commissioned by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) - Data Informatics & Innovation Division (DIID) [PS]. The Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS) processes over ₹4,000 crore annually across hundreds of parliamentary constituencies, thousands of implementing agencies, and tens of thousands of individual civil works [KNOWN]. While the launch of the e-SAKSHI portal (effective April 1, 2023) and mobile application digitized the recommendation and sanction workflow [RESEARCHED], manual oversight remains overburdened by the sheer volume of unstructured cost estimates, milestone updates, geo-tagged photos, and multi-agency payment releases [INFERENCE]. This solution architecture defines an **AI-powered intelligence and risk-analytics platform** that ingests multi-modal data (structured financial records, text estimates, GIS locations, and photo evidence) to automatically detect duplicate works, inflated estimates, execution delays, vendor collusion, and guidelines non-compliance in real time [INFERENCE]. Designed specifically for a 4–6 student engineering team competing in Smart India Hackathon 2026, this plan bridges the gap between hackathon demonstrability and real-world government deployment [ASSUMPTION].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1. **Connectivity & Low-Bandwidth in Rural Areas:** Implementing Agencies (IAs) and field engineers operating in remote districts often face spotty 2G/3G connectivity [KNOWN].  
   *Design Implication:* The mobile app for field inspections must operate full offline-first with local SQLite caching, queuing photo uploads, and compressing images before sync [ASSUMPTION].
2. **Fragmented & Heterogeneous Estimate Formats:** Cost estimates submitted by District Engineers vary from scanned PDFs in regional languages to Excel sheets and manual text entries [KNOWN].  
   *Design Implication:* Optical Character Recognition (OCR) pipeline with multi-lingual parsing capabilities is necessary to extract structured line-items [INFERENCE].
3. **Rigid Statutory Guidelines (MPLADS 2023 Guidelines):** Works must be sanctioned within 45 days of MP recommendation [RESEARCHED]. Disbursal flows via Single Nodal Account (SNA) / Central Nodal Account (CNA) on PFMS [RESEARCHED].  
   *Design Implication:* Hardcoded rule validation layer must run BEFORE AI anomaly scoring to ensure regulatory compliance without black-box opacity [INFERENCE].
4. **Data Privacy & Geolocation Sensitivity:** Exact geo-coordinates of certain public/defense assets must not be leaked publicly [INFERENCE].  
   *Design Implication:* Role-based data redaction (blurring precise coordinates on public dashboards while retaining exact coordinates for District/MoSPI audits) [ASSUMPTION].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

**Selected Solution:** **Approach 2 — Hybrid Intelligence Engine** (Deterministic Rules + Machine Learning Anomaly Detection + Geospatial/Vector Semantic Deduplication + XAI).

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected:
1. **Approach 1 (Pure Rule Engine) Rejected:** Static thresholds cannot capture evolving fraud schemes, such as two different contractors submitting identical cost estimates under slightly reworded project titles (e.g., "Construction of Concrete Road at Ward 4" vs "PCC Paving near Ward No. IV Community Center") [INFERENCE].
2. **Approach 3 (Pure LLM Agents) Rejected:** Generative models are prone to hallucinating numerical values during budget auditing, suffer from high API latencies, and lack deterministic auditability required by government financial inspectors [INFERENCE].

### Architectural Superiority of the Hybrid Approach:
* **Layer 1 (Deterministic Rule Engine):** Immediately filters hard guideline violations (e.g., 15% SC / 7.5% ST quota shortfall, projects exceeding ₹50 Lakh outside constituency, projects recommended on prohibited private lands) [RESEARCHED].
* **Layer 2 (Spatial Vector & NLP Engine):** Uses sentence embeddings (SBERT) + PostGIS spatial indexing to flag duplicate asset recommendations within a 200-meter radius across current and historical datasets [ASSUMPTION].
* **Layer 3 (Unsupervised & Supervised ML):** Uses Isolation Forest and XGBoost trained on cost-per-unit metrics (e.g., cost per meter of drainage, cost per square foot of roofing) to flag statistical cost outliers and predict completion delays [INFERENCE].
* **Layer 4 (Explainable AI - XAI):** Generates human-readable risk summaries using SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations), enabling District Collectors to understand exactly why a project scored high risk [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data Flow Narrative

1. **Proposal Ingestion:** An MP or District Staff member inputs a work proposal (Title, Location, Category, Estimated Cost, Beneficiary Group) via the web interface or API ingestion pipeline [INFERENCE].
2. **Rule Compliance Pass:** The system instantly runs **Layer 1 Rules**. If the recommendation violates statutory guidelines (e.g., MP's cumulative SC area allocation drops below 15%), an immediate compliance warning is raised [INFERENCE].
3. **Spatial & Semantic Deduplication:** The proposal text is converted into a 384-dimensional dense vector using `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`. PostGIS performs a spatial query for existing assets within a 200-meter radius. If a high semantic similarity (>0.82) AND high spatial proximity (<200m) are found, a **"Potential Duplicate Asset"** alert is flagged [ASSUMPTION].
4. **Cost Estimate Audit:** The itemized cost estimate PDF/Excel is parsed. The system calculates cost per unit (e.g., ₹/sq ft) and runs **Isolation Forest**. If the unit cost deviates significantly from historical district averages (e.g., >2.5 standard deviations), it is flagged as an **"Inflated Cost Estimate"** [INFERENCE].
5. **Predictive Delay & Risk Scoring:** An **XGBoost regressor** evaluates historical performance of the assigned Implementing Agency, current active project load, and project magnitude to output an expected delay duration and an aggregate **Project Risk Score (0–100)** [INFERENCE].
6. **Dashboard & Alert Dispatch:** Results are written to PostgreSQL. High-risk proposals (Score > 75) trigger real-time notifications to the District Collector and MoSPI DIID dashboard for mandatory manual review before administrative sanction [INFERENCE].

### Module 1: Semantic & Spatial Duplicate Asset Detector
1. **Exact Problem Solved:** Identifies duplicate work recommendations submitted across different financial years or schemes [INFERENCE].
2. **Input:** Proposed Project Title, Detailed Scope Text, Latitude/Longitude coordinates.
3. **Output:** Duplicate Probability Score (0–100%), Matched Historical Asset ID, Spatial Distance (meters).
4. **Why Rule Engine Fails:** Variations in titles (e.g., "Solar Street Light at Gram Panchayat 3" vs "Installation of 10W Solar Lights in GP-3") defeat simple string matching [INFERENCE].
5. **Required Data:** Historical list of MPLADS and state scheme assets with titles and coordinates [ASSUMPTION].
6. **Evaluation Metric:** Precision@K, Cosine Similarity Threshold (>0.82) [ASSUMPTION].
7. **Failure Mode Handling:** Low-confidence matches (50–75%) are marked as "Needs Manual Inspection" rather than auto-rejected [INFERENCE].

### Module 2: Cost Estimate Inflation & Anomaly Engine
1. **Exact Problem Solved:** Flags estimates where material or labor unit rates exceed standard Schedule of Rates (SoR) benchmarks [INFERENCE].
2. **Input:** Extracted estimate line items (Item Name, Unit, Quantity, Rate per Unit).
3. **Output:** Anomaly Score, List of Inflated Line Items with % deviation from benchmark.
4. **Why Rule Engine Fails:** Price thresholds vary dynamically across districts, terrains (hilly vs plain), and inflation periods [INFERENCE].
5. **Required Data:** Historical SoR rate tables and past approved estimates [ASSUMPTION].
6. **Evaluation Metric:** Mean Absolute Error (MAE) on benchmark rates, Isolation Forest Contamination Factor = 0.05 [ASSUMPTION].

### Module 3: Project Delay & Completion Time Predictor
1. **Exact Problem Solved:** Forecasts risk of execution delays beyond the mandated 1-year window [RESEARCHED].
2. **Input:** Agency Historical Performance, Sanctioned Amount, Sector, District Load, Monsoon Seasonality Index.
3. **Output:** Predicted Duration (Days), High-Risk Delay Alert (True/False).
4. **Why Rule Engine Fails:** Delay depends on complex multi-variable interactions (e.g., monsoon impact combined with high agency workload) [INFERENCE].
5. **Required Data:** Historical project start, sanction, and completion dates [ASSUMPTION].
6. **Evaluation Metric:** Receiver Operating Characteristic - Area Under Curve (ROC-AUC > 0.85) [ASSUMPTION].

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used:
* **Statutory Compliance Checks:** Checking whether an MP has allocated 15% to SC areas and 7.5% to ST areas MUST be handled by deterministic mathematical code [RESEARCHED].
* **Entitlement Balance Calculation:** Calculating remaining drawing limit (₹5 Crore - Sanctioned Amount) MUST use exact ledger arithmetic, never ML [RESEARCHED].

2. **Sync Queue:** Outgoing requests are stored in an IndexedDB / SQLite `sync_queue` table with status `PENDING` [ASSUMPTION].
3. **Chunked Upload & Compression:** Photos are compressed locally to <300KB before upload [ASSUMPTION].
4. **Conflict Resolution:** Last-Write-Wins (LWW) based on server timestamp, with manual override option for District Engineers if server state has advanced [ASSUMPTION].
