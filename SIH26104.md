## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
Generative AI and neural speech synthesis allow bad actors to clone voices from seconds of audio, executing social engineering and financial fraud across VoIP, PSTN, and enterprise collaboration platforms [PS]. Current communication tools lack real-time mechanisms to analyze acoustic, prosody, and spectral artifacts to detect synthetic speech while a call is active [PS]. The proposed solution is **VaniShield AI** — a privacy-preserving, multi-layered real-time voice integrity verification framework that inspects incoming audio streams via sliding-window inference (200ms–500ms latency window), computes a dynamic impersonation risk score, enriches it with call/transaction metadata, and triggers multi-channel alerts and pre-transaction blocks before sensitive actions occur [INFERENCE]. Designed for Indian multilingual contexts and compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, the framework exposes REST/gRPC APIs and lightweight SDKs for banking, enterprise PBX, and telecom carrier integration [PS].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

* **Telephony Codec Degradation:** Standard mobile calls (PSTN / 2G / 3G) use narrow-band codecs (AMR-NB at 8 kHz sampling rate, G.711 $\mu$-law) [KNOWN]. Higher-frequency acoustic artifacts above 4 kHz (vital for neural vocoder detection) are heavily attenuated [INFERENCE]. Models must be trained specifically on band-pass filtered and compressed audio [INFERENCE].
* **VoIP Jitter and Packet Loss:** Real-time VoIP (WebRTC, SIP) suffers from network packet loss, jitter buffers, and frame drops over 4G/5G cellular networks [RESEARCHED]. The pipeline must tolerate missing 20ms–50ms audio frames without crashing or misclassifying natural audio loss as synthetic distortion [INFERENCE].
* **Ultra-Low Latency Overhead:** In an active call, security analysis must complete within 200ms–300ms [RESEARCHED]. Inference pipelines cannot rely on slow multi-billion parameter LLMs or heavyweight unquantized diffusion pipelines [INFERENCE].
* **Indian Multilingual & Accent Variability:** India features 22 officially recognized languages and hundreds of regional dialects with distinct intonation, pitch ranges, and code-switching (e.g., Hinglish, Tanglish) [RESEARCHED]. The detector must avoid false positives triggered by legitimate accent variations or non-native pronunciations [INFERENCE].
* **Hardware Constraints in Tier-2/3 Financial Branches:** Bank branch terminals and field units often operate on mid-spec desktop computers (Intel Core i3/i5 without discrete GPUs) [KNOWN]. Centralized cloud inference or highly optimized CPU ONNX runtimes are mandatory [INFERENCE].
* **Strict Legal Compliance (DPDP Act 2023):** Storing voice biometrics without explicit consent is illegal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, carrying fines up to ₹250 Crore [RESEARCHED]. Raw audio buffers must reside strictly in volatile RAM and be purged within milliseconds after feature extraction [INFERENCE].

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Classical Audio DSP + Rule-Based Feature Thresholding
* **Mechanism:** Extract handcrafted acoustic features (MFCC, LFCC, Spectral Flux, Pitch Jitter, Zero-Crossing Rate) using Librosa/PyWorld and pass them to an XGBoost / SVM classifier [INFERENCE].
* **Required Data:** ASVspoof acoustic features [RESEARCHED].
* **Architecture:** Ingestion $\rightarrow$ Feature Extraction Pipeline $\rightarrow$ XGBoost Scoring $\rightarrow$ Alert [INFERENCE].
* **Complexity:** Low [INFERENCE].
* **Cost:** Very Low (runs on single-core CPU) [INFERENCE].
* **Explainability:** High (exact acoustic metrics can be visualized) [INFERENCE].
* **Scalability:** High throughput [INFERENCE].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** 100% buildable in 2 days [INFERENCE].
* **Production Feasibility:** Poor (easily bypassed by modern diffusion/neural vocoders like HiFi-GAN) [RESEARCHED].
* **Risks:** High false negative rate against modern neural clones [RESEARCHED].

### Approach 2: End-to-End Deep Learning Raw Waveform Model (AASIST / RawNet2)
* **Mechanism:** Feed raw 16kHz audio PCM directly into SincNet filters followed by Spectro-Temporal Graph Attention Networks (AASIST) for binary classification [RESEARCHED].
* **Required Data:** Raw PCM audio files (ASVspoof 2021) [RESEARCHED].
* **Architecture:** Audio Stream $\rightarrow$ SincNet Convolution $\rightarrow$ Graph Attention Network $\rightarrow$ Binary Logit [RESEARCHED].
* **Complexity:** Medium-High [RESEARCHED].
* **Cost:** Moderate (requires GPU for training, CPU/ONNX for inference) [INFERENCE].
* **Explainability:** Low (black-box neural network) [INFERENCE].
* **Scalability:** Moderate (10ms per 3-second frame on CPU) [INFERENCE].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High (pre-trained PyTorch weights available) [RESEARCHED].
* **Production Feasibility:** Medium (struggles with compressed G.711 telephony audio unless heavily augmented) [RESEARCHED].
* **Risks:** Overfitting to academic datasets, high domain shift drop [RESEARCHED].

### Approach 3: Multi-Layer Dynamic Ensemble Framework (SSL Wav2Vec2/XLS-R + AASIST + Prosodic DSP + Contextual Fusion) — RECOMMENDED
* **Mechanism:** Combines Facebook's Wav2Vec2 XLS-R self-supervised representation encoder (capturing Indic multilingual phone structures) fine-tuned with AASIST spectro-temporal graph layers, running in parallel with a deterministic PyWorld prosody/pitch jitter analyzer [RESEARCHED]. Output logits are weighted alongside metadata risk factors (call origin, transaction value) in a real-time dynamic scoring engine [INFERENCE].
* **Required Data:** ASVspoof 2021, AI4Bharat Kathbath, augmented telephony audio, synthetic Indic clones [RESEARCHED].
* **Architecture:** Dual-path Stream Ingestion (WebRTC / SIP) $\rightarrow$ Parallel Feature Extractors (SSL Neural + Prosodic DSP) $\rightarrow$ Dynamic Fusion Engine $\rightarrow$ gRPC Risk Broadcaster $\rightarrow$ Multi-Channel Intervention [INFERENCE].
* **Complexity:** High [INFERENCE].
* **Cost:** Balanced (quantized ONNX models on edge CPU nodes) [INFERENCE].
* **Explainability:** Medium-High (Neural score complemented by concrete prosody & spectral phase metrics) [INFERENCE].
* **Scalability:** High (Horizontal scaling of worker nodes) [INFERENCE].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** Feasible with modular student allocation [INFERENCE].
* **Production Feasibility:** Excellent (Carrier-grade architecture) [INFERENCE].
* **Risks:** Requires careful optimization to meet the <250ms latency budget [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

The **Multi-Layer Dynamic Ensemble Framework (Approach 3)** is selected [INFERENCE].

### Justification
1. **Robustness to Neural Vocoders:** Simple DSP (Approach 1) fails against modern neural vocoders (BigVGAN, HiFi-GAN) [RESEARCHED]. Standalone deep learning (Approach 2) collapses under telephony compression (G.711) and unseen Indic accents [RESEARCHED]. Approach 3 uses self-supervised XLS-R representations which preserve cross-lingual robustness across 128 languages including Indian dialects [RESEARCHED].
2. **Explainability for Fraud Operations:** Bank security operators cannot block million-rupee transactions based on an opaque deep learning logit alone [INFERENCE]. Approach 3 provides secondary explainable metrics (e.g., "Abnormal $F_0$ pitch variance: 0.02 Hz", "Spectral Phase Inconsistency Detected") alongside the neural confidence score [INFERENCE].
3. **Regulatory Alignment:** Combining metadata enrichment with feature-only vector evaluation satisfies the strict DPDP Act 2023 requirements without persisting raw voice recordings [RESEARCHED].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative
1. **Audio Capture:** The client (WebRTC app, Asterisk PBX AudioSocket, or Mobile Android SDK) captures continuous live audio in 20-millisecond PCM frames [INFERENCE].
2. **Chunking & Buffer Management:** Frames are buffered into a rolling 2-second sliding window with a 200ms step-size in volatile memory (RAM), ensuring sub-second continuous updating [INFERENCE].
3. **Parallel Feature Extraction:**
   * **Path A (Neural):** The raw 2-second clip is downsampled/upsampled to 16kHz and fed into a quantized ONNX Wav2Vec2 XLS-R model to extract 1024-dimensional contextual speech embeddings [RESEARCHED].
   * **Path B (Prosody & Phase DSP):** In parallel, C++ PyWorld bindings compute fundamental frequency ($F_0$), pitch jitter, micro-pause distribution, and Linear Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (LFCC) phase continuity [INFERENCE].
4. **Cross-Session Vector Match:** If the caller claims a specific identity (e.g., CXO or verified customer ID), the current 1024-dim embedding is compared against an opt-in FAISS vector index using cosine similarity [INFERENCE].
5. **Dynamic Risk Score Calculation:** The dynamic risk score $R_{call} \in [0, 100]$ is computed as:
   $$R_{call} = w_1 \cdot \text{Logit}_{\text{AASIST}} + w_2 \cdot \text{Anomaly}_{\text{prosody}} + w_3 \cdot (1 - \text{Cosine}_{\text{identity}}) + w_4 \cdot \text{Risk}_{\text{metadata}}$$
   where $w_1=0.45, w_2=0.25, w_3=0.20, w_4=0.10$ [INFERENCE].
6. **Workflow Enforcement & Alerting:**
   * If $R_{call} < 40$: Transaction/Call proceeds normally (`SAFE` status) [INFERENCE].
   * If $40 \le R_{call} < 75$: In-app UI displays a yellow warning badge; secondary verification prompt is primed (`WARN` status) [INFERENCE].
   * If $R_{call} \ge 75$: The system triggers an immediate WebSocket alert to frontline staff, blocks downstream transaction approval REST endpoints, and sends an FCM push alert requiring out-of-band MFA or supervisor sign-off (`HIGH_RISK` status) [INFERENCE].
7. **Immediate Buffer Purge:** The raw PCM audio frame is instantly overwritten in RAM; only non-reversible numeric risk scores and feature vectors are written to the immutable PostgreSQL audit log [PS].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Component 1: Wav2Vec2 XLS-R + AASIST Audio Deepfake Classifier

3. **Output:** Synthetic probability score $P_{\text{fake}} \in [0.0, 1.0]$ [RESEARCHED].
4. **Why Simpler Rule Cannot Solve:** Modern neural vocoders (HiFi-GAN, WaveGlow) synthesize human-like pitch and spectral envelopes that bypass static threshold rules [RESEARCHED].
5. **Data Required:** ASVspoof 2021 DF/LA dataset, AI4Bharat Kathbath speech, synthetic clones [RESEARCHED].
6. **Evaluation Metric:** Equal Error Rate (EER) and Minimum Detection Cost Function (min t-DCF) [RESEARCHED].
7. **Failure Mode:** False positive on heavily distorted or compressed legitimate phone lines [RESEARCHED].
8. **Training Required:** Yes, multi-dataset supervised fine-tuning [RESEARCHED].
9. **Fine-Tuning Required:** Yes, using telephony-augmented (G.711/AMR) datasets [INFERENCE].
10. **RAG Required:** No [INFERENCE].
11. **Computer Vision Required:** No [INFERENCE].
12. **Speech Processing Required:** Yes (Raw waveform spectro-temporal processing) [RESEARCHED].
13. **Forecasting Required:** No [INFERENCE].
14. **Human Oversight:** Mandatory. If $P_{\text{fake}} > 0.75$, require secondary step-up verification before financial execution [PS].

### Component 2: Cross-Session Speaker Embedding Consistency Engine

1. **Exact Problem AI Solves:** Verifies whether the speaker's biometric voiceprint matches historical genuine samples of the claimed identity [PS].
2. **Input:** 2-second PCM audio array + Claimed User ID [INFERENCE].
3. **Output:** Identity match confidence score $C_{\text{match}} \in [0.0, 1.0]$ [INFERENCE].
4. **Why Simpler Rule Cannot Solve:** Vocal tract characteristics vary dynamically with speech content; deep embeddings isolate speaker-specific features invariant to spoken text [RESEARCHED].
5. **Data Required:** Opt-in historical genuine voice samples (10–30 seconds) [PS].
6. **Evaluation Metric:** Cosine similarity threshold validation ($>0.85$ for match) [INFERENCE].
7. **Failure Mode:** False negative if legitimate speaker has severe cold/hoarseness [INFERENCE].
8. **Training Required:** Pre-trained ECAPA-TDNN / Wav2Vec2 speaker recognition model [RESEARCHED].

### Offline & Low-Network Resiliency Design
* **Local Edge Inference:** The C++/ONNX inference engine runs directly on the local enterprise PBX server or edge branch gateway [INFERENCE]. Even if cloud connectivity fails, local voice cloning detection remains 100% operational [INFERENCE].
* **Local Caching:** Configurable tenant thresholds and speaker embeddings are cached in local Redis / SQLite instances [INFERENCE].
* **Synchronization & Retry Strategy:** If the central logging server is unreachable due to network outage, audit records are queued in local SQLite storage and synced using an exponential backoff retry mechanism upon reconnect [INFERENCE].
* **Bandwidth Reduction:** Rather than streaming raw audio to the cloud, edge nodes perform feature extraction locally and send only lightweight JSON risk telemetry packets (~1 KB/sec vs. 256 KB/sec raw audio) [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

### DPDP Act 2023 Compliance & Privacy Preservation
1. **Zero Raw Audio Retention Policy:** Incoming PCM audio streams exist exclusively in volatile RAM buffers during feature extraction (200ms–500ms) and are immediately overwritten [PS]. No raw audio is ever written to disk or central storage [PS].
2. **Feature-Only Logging:** Audit logs store non-reversible numerical embeddings and spectro-temporal metrics [PS]. It is mathematically impossible to reconstruct spoken human sentences from 1024-dimensional deep embeddings [RESEARCHED].
3. **Explicit Consent Management:** Cross-session speaker verification is strictly opt-in [PS]. Users sign a digital consent record before voiceprint vector enrollment [RESEARCHED].
4. **Data Ownership & Anonymization:** Call IDs and User IDs are hashed using SHA-256 before database insertion, rendering audit logs fully anonymized to external observers [PS].

### System Security & Authorization
* **API Security:** All gRPC and REST endpoints require TLS 1.3 encryption and OAuth2 / JWT authentication with short-lived tokens [INFERENCE].
* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):**
  * `Frontline Agent`: Views real-time call risk badge only [INFERENCE].
  * `Branch Manager`: Overrides pre-transaction holds with secondary MFA [INFERENCE].
  * `System Admin`: Configures risk thresholds and alert channels [INFERENCE].
* **False Positive Liability Management:** The platform provides advisory recommendations and step-up prompts (e.g., "Request Call-back") rather than unilaterally terminating emergency calls, mitigating legal liability for false positives [INFERENCE].
