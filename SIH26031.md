# 1. Executive Snapshot

This research and solution architecture dossier defines an end-to-end strategy for building **AgriGrade-AI (Onion Quality Assessment & Digital Grading System)**, an edge-AI mobile application designed to eliminate subjectivity, disputes, and operational opacity in onion procurement under the Department of Consumer Affairs (DoCA) Price Stabilisation Fund (PSF) [PS, RESEARCHED]. By placing a multi-onion sample on a standardized calibration tray with a physical scale reference marker, field procurement officers capture a single image [INFERENCE]. The on-device computer vision engine (YOLOv8/YOLOv11 instance segmentation running via TFLite/ONNX) automatically segments every onion, measures minor/major diameter down to millimeter accuracy, detects visual defects (rot, sprouting, mechanical skin damage, double bulbs), classifies each bulb into **Grade A**, **URS (Under Relaxed Specifications)**, or **Reject**, and instantly computes batch percentage compositions [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE]. The application operates 100% offline in remote mandis, generating tamper-evident, geotagged digital inspection reports with QR code verification that synchronize seamlessly to central buffer stock dashboards when connectivity is restored [INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION].

## 2. Problem Deconstruction

The explicit problem statement requirement text from the Department of Consumer Affairs (DoCA) [PS] is deconstructed into atomic, verifiable requirements below:

### Explicit Requirements
* **`REQ-E1` (Image Processing Quality Assessment):** Uses image processing to assess onion quality. [PS]
  * *Interpretation:* The system must capture visual image data of onions and process spatial, color, and structural characteristics without relying solely on manual visual scoring. [INFERENCE]
  * *Implementation Implication:* High-resolution RGB image capture module on Android/iOS devices with automatic pre-processing, lighting adjustment, and contour segmentation. [INFERENCE]
  * *Confidence Tag:* `[PS]`

* **`REQ-E2` (Defect & Attribute Identification):** Identifies damaged, rotten, sprouted, or undersized onions. [PS]
  * *Interpretation:* The vision algorithm must perform multi-class defect classification and spatial dimension measurement on individual onion instances within a batch image. [INFERENCE]
  * *Implementation Implication:* Multi-task instance segmentation network trained to detect:
    1. *Rotten:* Black mold (*Aspergillus niger*), bacterial soft rot, discolored wet lesions [RESEARCHED].
    2. *Sprouted:* Green foliage emergent from bulb crown [RESEARCHED].
    3. *Damaged:* Cut, bruised, crushed outer scales, or sunburnt/skinned layers [RESEARCHED].
    4. *Undersized:* Bulb diameter smaller than statutory minimum (<45 mm for strict Grade A, or <35 mm for relaxed URS) [RESEARCHED].
  * *Confidence Tag:* `[PS]`

* **`REQ-E3` (Grade A & URS Percentage Estimation):** Estimates Grade A and URS percentages. [PS]
  * *Interpretation:* After detecting individual bulbs, the system must aggregate results across the sample to output precise weight or count percentages for Grade A and URS (Under Relaxed Specifications). [INFERENCE]
  * *Implementation Implication:* Statistical aggregator calculating $\% \text{Grade A} = (N_{\text{Grade A}} / N_{\text{Total}}) \times 100$ and $\% \text{URS} = (N_{\text{URS}} / N_{\text{Total}}) \times 100$, adjusted for estimated volumetric mass [INFERENCE].
  * *Confidence Tag:* `[PS]`

* **`REQ-E4` (Instant Digital Quality Report):** Generates a digital quality report instantly. [PS]
  * *Interpretation:* The mobile app must compile sample inspection metrics, defect counts, grade percentages, timestamp, GPS position, and annotated images into an immediate, shareable report. [INFERENCE]
  * *Implementation Implication:* Local PDF/JSON report generation engine operating on-device in under 3 seconds without requiring active cloud network connectivity. [INFERENCE]
  * *Confidence Tag:* `[PS]`

* **`REQ-E5` (Bias Reduction & Transparency):** Reduces human bias and improves transparency. [PS]
  * *Interpretation:* Inspection records must be objective, deterministic, verifiable by both farmers and inspectors, and protected against manual tampering. [INFERENCE]
  * *Implementation Implication:* Cryptographic hashing of inspection records, side-by-side visual bounding box proof on mobile screen, and sync to central DoCA audit logs. [INFERENCE]
  * *Confidence Tag:* `[PS]`

### Derived & Inferred Requirements
* **`REQ-I1` (Physical Scale Calibration):** Due to varying smartphone camera focal lengths and shooting distances, real-world diameter measurement ($mm$) requires a physical reference marker (e.g., ArUco code or fixed-size calibration mat) [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **`REQ-I2` (Offline-First Execution):** Primary procurement occurs in rural APMC mandis with poor 3G/4G connectivity; inference and report creation must run 100% on-device [KNOWN, INFERENCE].
* **`REQ-I3` (Dual Policy Dynamic Thresholding):** DoCA procurement guidelines alternate between strict Grade A (45–65 mm) and URS relaxed rules (35–70 mm, up to 40% surface staining) via periodic Office Memorandums; classification thresholds must be dynamically configurable via backend policy updates [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

* **Geography & Lighting Constraints:** Procurement occurs in open-air mandis, covered sheds, or tractor trolleys under direct harsh sunlight, uneven shadows, or low-light dusk conditions [KNOWN, INFERENCE]. *Mitigation:* Require a foldable, low-cost blue/green matte calibration tray with built-in shade walls and standard color calibration blocks [ASSUMPTION].
* **Network Connectivity Realities:** Rural procurement yards in Nashik, Ahmednagar, or MP frequently suffer from complete network blackout or high latency 2G/3G connections [KNOWN, RESEARCHED]. *Mitigation:* All AI inference, image processing, database storage, and PDF report creation must execute 100% offline [INFERENCE].
* **Hardware Limitations:** Field agents use low-to-mid-tier Android smartphones (2GB–4GB RAM, MediaTek Helio or Snapdragon 6-series processors) [KNOWN, ASSUMPTION]. *Mitigation:* Quantize deep learning models to INT8 TFLite/NPU format, targeting model weights under 15 MB and memory usage under 250 MB [INFERENCE].
* **Language & Digital Literacy:** Farmers and field helpers prefer regional languages (Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati) with high visual feedback rather than text-heavy UI [KNOWN]. *Mitigation:* Icon-driven UI, voice guidance, and bilingual PDF printouts [INFERENCE].
* **High Operational Throughput:** Procurement centers handle hundreds of trolleys daily; an inspection cannot take longer than 2 minutes per sample lot [KNOWN, INFERENCE].

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Lightweight Edge Computer Vision (YOLOv8s-seg + Physical Calibration Tray Mat) [RECOMMENDED]
* **Mechanism:** The user places a sample batch of 20–30 onions on a standardized $40 \times 40\text{ cm}$ high-contrast blue tray featuring printed Corner ArUco Markers. An on-device INT8-quantized YOLOv8s-seg model runs locally on the smartphone, segmenting each onion instance and detecting defects (rot, sprout, damage). OpenCV detects ArUco markers to compute exact pixel-per-millimeter scale and perspective transformation, measuring minor/major axis diameters for every onion. Rule-based logic applies active DoCA thresholds to compute Grade A, URS, and Reject percentages [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Required Data:** 3,500 annotated onion images with polygon masks and defect labels [INFERENCE].
* **Complexity:** Medium | **Cost:** Low (Free software + ₹200 calibration tray) | **Explainability:** High (Annotated visual overlay on screen) [INFERENCE].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High (100% buildable in Flutter/Python/TFLite) [INFERENCE].
* **Production Feasibility:** Excellent (Runs offline on low-cost Android phones) [INFERENCE].

### Approach 2: Multimodal Cloud Vision LLM (GPT-4o / Claude 3.5 Sonnet API)
* **Mechanism:** The mobile app captures a photo of onions on a tray and sends the raw image to a cloud LLM endpoint with a prompt asking for defect counts, size estimation, and Grade A/URS percentage estimation [INFERENCE].
* **Required Data:** Zero training data required (zero-shot prompt engineering) [KNOWN].
* **Complexity:** Low (API integration) | **Cost:** High ($0.01–$0.03 per inspection API call) [KNOWN, INFERENCE].
* **Explainability:** Low (LLM hallucinations on exact millimeter measurements and counts) [KNOWN].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High (Fast prototype) | **Production Feasibility:** Unviable (Fails completely offline in rural mandis, high operational cost, prone to hallucinated metrics) [KNOWN, INFERENCE].

### Approach 3: Dual-Stage Hybrid Pipeline (Local Feature Detection + Cloud Verification)
* **Mechanism:** On-device Classical OpenCV contour extraction counts and measures sizes; images flagged with ambiguous defects are compressed and queued for cloud ML verification when network becomes available [INFERENCE].
* **Required Data:** 2,000 annotated images [INFERENCE].
* **Complexity:** High (Managing sync queues and dual decision loops) | **Cost:** Medium | **Explainability:** Medium [INFERENCE].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** Medium | **Production Feasibility:** Good, but over-engineered for immediate on-field decision making [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

**Selected Approach:** **Approach 1 (Lightweight Edge Computer Vision: YOLOv8s-seg + Physical Calibration Mat Mat)** [INFERENCE].

### Justification & Rejection of Alternatives
1. **Why Approach 2 was Rejected:** Cloud Vision LLMs fail fundamental operational constraints: rural APMC mandis have zero or intermittent internet access [KNOWN, RESEARCHED]. Furthermore, LLMs hallucinate precise spatial dimensions ($mm$) and exact object counts, making them illegal/unusable for official financial transactions under DoCA [KNOWN, INFERENCE].
2. **Why Approach 3 was Rejected:** Dual-stage hybrid pipelines introduce unnecessary system complexity during a hackathon build without improving instant on-field decision capability. Procurement officers require immediate offline pass/fail decisions before the trolley departs [KNOWN, INFERENCE].
3. **Why Approach 1 Wins:** It combines deterministic physical measurement (ArUco pixel-to-millimeter geometric mapping) with deep learning pattern recognition (YOLOv8 instance segmentation for defects) [RESEARCHED]. It operates **100% offline**, executes in **<2 seconds** on basic smartphones, provides visual explainability (polygon overlays on every bulb), and costs under ₹200 per procurement center for the physical tray [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative
1. **Sample Placement & Capture:** Procurement agent scoops a representative sample of ~30 onions from a farmer's trolley and places them on the standardized $40 \times 40\text{ cm}$ mat [INFERENCE].
2. **Geometric Calibration:** The mobile app captures the frame. OpenCV locates four ArUco markers on the tray corners, calculates the perspective transformation matrix, warps the image to a flat top-down grid, and establishes the exact scale factor: $\text{PPM} = \frac{\text{Pixel Distance}}{\text{Known mm Distance}}$ [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
3. **Instance Segmentation & Measurement:** The cropped inner tray area is fed into the TFLite YOLOv8s-seg model. The model predicts polygon masks for every individual onion. The minor axis of the minimum bounding ellipse is calculated in millimeters: $D_{\text{real}} = \frac{D_{\text{ellipse\_pixels}}}{\text{PPM}}$ [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
4. **Defect Classification:** Each segmented onion patch is processed through defect classification heads predicting probabilities for `Rot`, `Sprout`, `Mechanical Damage`, or `Sound/Clean` [INFERENCE].
5. **Policy Decision Engine:** Active DoCA rule thresholds are applied per bulb:
   * *Grade A:* Diameter 45–65 mm, no rot, no sprout, damage <5% [RESEARCHED].
   * *URS:* Diameter 35–45 mm or 65–70 mm, or surface staining up to 40%, no rot, no sprout [RESEARCHED].
   * *Reject:* Diameter <35 mm, or presence of rot/sprouting/severe physical breakage [RESEARCHED].
6. **Report & Sync:** The app displays the total count, breakdown numbers, and Grade A / URS / Reject percentages on screen with annotated bounding boxes. A digitally signed, geotagged PDF report with a QR code is stored in local SQLite and queued for HTTPS push to the central DoCA cloud server [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Component 1: Multi-Onion Instance Segmentation Engine
* **Purpose:** Detect, separate, and output individual polygon masks for every onion in a dense, touching sample batch [RESEARCHED].
* **Model Type:** YOLOv8s-seg (quantized to INT8 TFLite) [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** $640 \times 640 \times 3$ warped RGB image of the calibration tray.
* **Output:** $N$ instance masks, bounding boxes, and confidence scores ($0.0 - 1.0$).
* **Data Required:** 3,500 annotated multi-onion images with polygon boundary ground truth [INFERENCE].
* **Why AI:** Classical watershed algorithms fail when onions have varying skin tones, overlapping dry outer scales, or shadow clutter [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation:** Mask mAP@50-95 > 88%, Inference time <200ms on Snapdragon 680 [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode:** Extreme overlapping (onions stacked 2-deep). *Mitigation:* User interface enforces "Single Layer Placement" rule via camera frame checks [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight:** Officer can tap on screen to manually adjust or delete misaligned bounding polygons prior to final report lock [INFERENCE].

### Component 2: Defect Classification Head
* **Purpose:** Classify surface defects on each segmented onion patch into `Rot`, `Sprout`, `Mechanical Damage`, or `Sound` [RESEARCHED].
* **Model Type:** Lightweight MobileNetV3-Small classifier backbone [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** Bounding box crop of individual onion instance ($128 \times 128 \times 3$).
* **Output:** Softmax probability vector across 4 defect classes.
* **Data Required:** 6,000 cropped onion images labeled by agricultural quality experts [INFERENCE].
* **Why AI:** Visual patterns of black mold (*Aspergillus*), green sprout shoots, and skin breaks are complex high-level texture variations impossible to capture with fixed color thresholds [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation:** Precision > 92%, Recall > 90% on Rot/Sprout classes [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode:** Internal rot with zero outer skin discoloration. *Mitigation:* Explicit report disclaimer stating visual inspection covers surface characteristics only [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight:** Any high-uncertainty classification (confidence < 60%) prompts manual inspector confirmation [INFERENCE].

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
* **Final Diameter Measurement ($mm$):** Calculated purely through OpenCV spatial ellipse fitting and deterministic ArUco geometric scaling ratio [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Grade A vs URS Percentage Logic:** Calculated using deterministic mathematical formulas matching measured diameters and defect flags against active DoCA policy parameters [INFERENCE].
* **PDF Slip Cryptographic Hashing:** Built using standard SHA-256 cryptographic libraries [INFERENCE].

* **`InspectionBatch` Table:** `batch_uuid` (TEXT PK), `officer_id` (TEXT), `mandi_id` (TEXT), `farmer_id` (TEXT), `trolley_number` (TEXT), `timestamp_utc` (INTEGER), `latitude` (REAL), `longitude` (REAL), `total_count` (INTEGER), `grade_a_count` (INTEGER), `urs_count` (INTEGER), `reject_count` (INTEGER), `grade_a_percent` (REAL), `urs_percent` (REAL), `doca_policy_version` (TEXT), `sync_status` (INTEGER: 0=Pending, 1=Synced), `payload_hash` (TEXT).
* **`OnionInstance` Table:** `instance_id` (TEXT PK), `batch_uuid` (FK), `major_diameter_mm` (REAL), `minor_diameter_mm` (REAL), `defect_type` (TEXT: Sound/Rot/Sprout/Damage), `defect_confidence` (REAL), `assigned_grade` (TEXT: Grade_A/URS/Reject), `crop_image_path` (TEXT).

### Offline Synchronization & Sync Strategy
1. **Local Autonomy:** Inspections are executed entirely against local SQLite. Raw cropped images and JSON payloads are saved to device storage [INFERENCE].
2. **Sync Queue:** When an inspection completes, a record is added to `sync_queue` with status `PENDING`.
3. **Background Worker:** Flutter WorkManager triggers periodic sync checks every 15 minutes. When Wi-Fi/4G becomes available, it executes an idempotent HTTP POST to `/api/v1/sync/batch`.
4. **Bandwidth Reduction:** Only the compressed summary JSON and thumbnail image of the annotated tray (<200 KB) are pushed during cellular sync; full-resolution crops remain local unless explicitly requested during an audit [INFERENCE].
5. **Conflict Handling:** Server utilizes Immutable Write-Once semantics (`batch_uuid` primary key). Re-sent batches with identical UUIDs are safely ignored (Idempotency) [INFERENCE].
