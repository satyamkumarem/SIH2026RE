## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
This project addresses problem statement **SIH26020** issued by the **Ministry of MSME (Coordination Section)**. The objective is to design, prototype, evaluate, and formulate a deployment roadmap for an innovative, lightweight, portable, and ergonomically optimized manually operated hand-spinning system (Charkha) [PS]. Existing decentralized Khadi hand-spinning systems, primarily the 8-spindle New Model Charkha (NMC), suffer from excessive physical weight (~45 kg), severe musculoskeletal drudgery due to repetitive upper-body cranking, inconsistent yarn tension leading to frequent thread breaks, and limited daily earning potential for rural women artisans [KNOWN, RESEARCHED]. This dossier details a complete mechanical engineering, ergonomic, material, and IoT-assisted solution—the **"ErgoSpin Pro" Dual-Drive Ergonomic Charkha**—which replaces heavy steel components with structural aluminum alloy, integrates a dual-drive pedal/hand convertible planetary gear transmission with flywheel momentum smoothing, incorporates ceramic low-friction thread guides, and embeds an offline-first IoT yarn quality and hank-counter module for field validation.

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

*   **Geography & Living Space:** Rural artisan households in Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, and Andhra Pradesh often have mud or unpaved floors and limited indoor room space (<80 sq ft per artisan) [RESEARCHED]. The equipment must fit through narrow doorways and stand stable on uneven ground.
*   **Physical Ergonomics & User Demographics:** Over 85% of Khadi hand spinners are rural women aged 25–60 [RESEARCHED]. Prolonged sitting on the floor with asymmetric trunk rotation leads to high prevalence of Work-Related Musculoskeletal Disorders (WRMSDs) in the lumbar region and shoulders [RESEARCHED].
*   **Operating Conditions & Maintenance:** Dust, cotton linters, humidity, and high room temperatures (up to 45°C) are common. Open gears quickly accumulate lint and seize; grease attracts abrasive dust. Sealed drive enclosures and self-lubricating polymer/bronze bushings are essential.
*   **Power & Infrastructure:** Continuous electricity is absent or unreliable in many interior rural clusters [KNOWN]. The primary spinning drive must be 100% mechanical. Any electronic telemetry must run on ultra-low power (LiFePO4 battery charged via a small 5W solar panel or micro-dynamo).
*   **Cultural & Regulatory Context (Khadi Mark Standards):** To be certified as "Khadi," yarn MUST be hand-spun on a manually operated device [KNOWN]. Complete electrification disqualifies the product from Khadi certification and subsidies. The machine MUST rely on human energy as its primary driver [PS].

### Approach 1: Dual-Drive Ergonomic Mechanical Charkha with Lightweight Frame & Flywheel Drive (SELECTED)
*   **Mechanism:** Converts human mechanical energy via a convertible dual-drive system (reciprocating pedal or ergonomic hand crank). Uses a planetary gear transmission (1:18 overall ratio) with an integrated momentum flywheel (1.2 kg rim-weighted steel/bronze disc) to eliminate torque ripple. Frame built from 6061-T6 structural aluminum extrusion.
*   **Required Data:** Gear pitch, belt tension levels, operator cadence (rpm), spindle torque profiles.
*   **Complexity:** Medium (Precision mechanical fabrication & power transmission alignment).
*   **Cost:** Low to Medium (~₹11,400 mass production BOM).
*   **Explainability:** High (100% pure mechanical physics).
*   **Scalability:** High (Standard off-the-shelf bicycle parts and extruded aluminum profiles).
*   **Hackathon Feasibility:** 9/10 (Buildable 4-spindle functional working model in hackathon timeframe).
*   **Production Feasibility:** 9.5/10 (Easy local cluster manufacturing).
*   **Risks:** Belt slip under high spindle acceleration (mitigated by auto-tensioner).

### Approach 2: High-Gear Precision Desktop Composite Charkha
*   **Mechanism:** Compact 6-spindle desktop spinning unit made of injection-molded recycled polymer / glass-fiber composite. Driven by a high-ratio hand crank through a sealed timing belt box.
*   **Complexity:** High upfront tooling cost (molds required for plastic frame).
*   **Cost:** Low per unit in massive volumes (>10,000 units), but high prototype cost (>₹2.5 Lakhs for tooling).
*   **Hackathon Feasibility:** 5/10 (3D printing large frame parts during hackathon is slow and structurally flexible).
*   **Production Feasibility:** 6/10 (Requires centralized plastic injection facility, not local village vendors).

### Approach 3: Solar-Assist Smart Electro-Mechanical Hybrid Charkha
*   **Mechanism:** Uses a tiny 15W brushless DC motor powered by a solar battery to supply 80% of spinning torque, with a manual hand-crank providing steering/drafting control and 20% power.
*   **Complexity:** Medium-High (Requires motor controller, Li-ion battery, BMS, solar PV panel).
*   **Cost:** High (~₹28,000–₹35,000 per unit).
*   **Hackathon Feasibility:** 8/10 (Easy to assemble with Arduino and small DC motor).

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

### Selection: Approach 1 (Dual-Drive Ergonomic Mechanical Charkha)

#### Justification
Approach 1 is selected because it strictly abides by all explicit problem statement requirements while solving every critical field pain point:

1.  **100% Khadi Compliance [PS]:** Retains manual human power as the sole energy source, preserving true Khadi hand-spun certification and associated artisan price premiums.
2.  **Drudgery Reduction (>50%) [PS]:** Incorporating an optional foot-pedal drive utilizes the body's largest muscle groups (quadriceps and gluteal muscles) rather than relying solely on small wrist and shoulder muscles. Electromyography (EMG) research confirms lower-limb pedaling reduces upper-body musculoskeletal strain by >60% over prolonged periods [RESEARCHED].
3.  **Portability & Weight (<14 kg) [PS]:** Replacing 12-gauge cast iron/steel with lightweight 6061-T6 aluminum alloy reduces overall machine weight from 45 kg to 11.8 kg, making it easily portable by a single woman artisan [INFERENCE].
4.  **Yarn Quality & CSP Improvement [PS]:** The rim-weighted momentum flywheel smooths out irregular human pedaling/cranking jerks, maintaining a constant spindle RPM (8,500 RPM ± 300 RPM). Constant RPM reduces yarn tension spikes, dropping end-breakage rates by 40% and raising yarn CSP from ~1,900 to >2,300 [INFERENCE].

#### Why Alternatives Were Rejected
*   *Approach 2 (Desktop Composite):* Rejected due to excessive injection-molding tooling costs for prototyping and poor local repairability in rural village workshops.
*   *Approach 3 (Solar-Assist Hybrid):* Rejected because adding continuous electrical motor power violates strict KVIC manual spinning criteria, risking loss of Khadi artisan certification and government subsidies [KNOWN].

### Data & Power Flow Narrative

1.  **Mechanical Energy Input:** The artisan inputs rotational power via either the lower-limb pedal mechanism (seated posture) or the hand crank.
2.  **Speed Amplification & Smoothing:** The low RPM input (~50–60 RPM human cadence) enters the primary drive sprocket, engaging the planetary flywheel gearbox. The flywheel accelerates to ~1,000 RPM, absorbing rotational fluctuations and delivering smooth torque to the main drive belt.
3.  **Drafting & Twist Insertion:** The output shaft drives the cotton sliver drafting rollers at a precise differential speed, drawing out the raw cotton sliver. The high-speed spindle whorls (driven by flat cotton/synthetic belts at 8,500 RPM) insert twist (TPI 18–22) into the drawn fibers, forming yarn.
4.  **Winding & Ring-Traveler Motion:** The yarn passes through polished ceramic guide loops onto the ring traveler, which winds the finished yarn uniformly onto bobbins mounted on the spindles.
5.  **Telemetry & Quality Measurement:** An IR reflective sensor on the spindle shaft counts rotations to compute accurate Hank output (1 Hank = 1,000 meters). An inline miniature load cell monitors yarn tension. Data is processed locally on an ESP32-S3 microcontroller and displayed on an e-paper/OLED screen, giving real-time feedback to the spinner regarding speed and production.

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Critical Evaluation: Is AI Required for the Core Spinning Machine?
**NO.** The physical spinning process of a manually operated Charkha is purely governed by classical mechanical physics, kinematics, and material science. Implementing an AI model directly into the mechanical drive mechanism would add unnecessary cost, complexity, power consumption, and failure points without improving mechanical output.

### Where Micro-AI / Analytics CAN Add Value (Edge Analytics Module - Optional)

1.  **Purpose:** Computer Vision (CV) based Yarn Quality Inspection (Offline Quality Control Post-Spinning).
2.  **Model Type:** Edge impulse MobileNetV2 / YOLOv8-Nano quantized model running on an edge smartphone or ESP32-CAM.
3.  **Input:** High-resolution microscopic macro photo of spun yarn placed against a calibrated dark background grid.
4.  **Output:** Automatic estimation of Yarn Count (Ne), Hairiness Index, and Thin/Thick spot defect counts (Imperfection Index - IPI).
5.  **Why AI is Preferable Here:** Manual lab testing with wrap reels and Lea testers requires expensive lab equipment (Uster testers) costing >₹5 Lakhs, which rural Khadi centers cannot afford. A smartphone AI app provides immediate quality verification at zero incremental hardware cost.
6.  **Failure Mode:** Poor lighting or blurry camera focus leading to miscalculated count.
7.  **Human Oversight:** The Khadi quality supervisor manually re-tests any batch flagged red by the edge AI app.

### Synchronization & Conflict Handling Strategy
*   **Offline-First Buffer:** The ESP32-S3 logs every 15-minute chunk of spinning activity to non-volatile flash memory (NVS).
*   **Zero-Data-Loss Sync:** When a field supervisor's smartphone enters within BLE range (10 meters), the app automatically pulls un-synced logs using a checksum-verified protocol.
*   **Conflict Resolution:** Records are timestamped using a real-time clock (RTC). Incoming device logs are append-only; duplicate records are filtered on the server using `charkha_serial_no + session_start_time`.

*   **Artisan Data Privacy:** Artisan production logs directly impact wage payouts (wages paid per hank spun). All telemetry data is cryptographically signed at the edge using an AES-128 key embedded in the ESP32 secure element to prevent wage tampering or fake production reporting.
*   **Physical Safety & Operator Governance:**
    *   *Pinch-Point Protection:* All high-speed planetary gears, flywheels, and drive belts are completely enclosed inside smooth ABS/Aluminum protective shrouds to prevent loose clothing (sarees/dupattas) or hair from catching.
    *   *Emergency Brake:* A foot-actuated quick friction brake stops spindle rotation within 0.5 seconds if yarn tangles.
*   **Regulatory & Quality Validation:**
    *   *KVIC Standards:* Final production models must be submitted to the Central Technological Institute (CTI) KVIC Wardha or Mahatma Gandhi Institute for Rural Industrialization (MGIRI) for official certification.
    *   *BIS Compliance:* All spinning rings, bolsters, and spindles comply with relevant Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications for ring spinning machinery (IS 1671 / BIS standards) [1.2, 3.1].
