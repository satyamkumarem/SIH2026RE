# SIH 2026 Problem Analysis & Solution Architecture Dossier

## 1. Executive Snapshot

This dossier provides a complete technical, operational, and strategic blueprint for **SahakarGig** (cooperative name choice: *Sahakar Seva* / *SahakarGig*), a cooperative-owned digital gig service marketplace platform designed for the **Ministry of Cooperation** and the **National Council for Cooperative Training (NCCT)** [PS]. The platform bridges the gap between skilled workers organized under Primary Labour Cooperative Societies (PLCS) and State/National Labour Cooperative Federations on one side, and urban/semi-urban households, gated communities, and institutions requiring reliable household and community services on the other [PS]. By replacing extraction-heavy private gig platforms with a worker-centric cooperative ownership model, the system delivers verified services, transparent pricing, guaranteed minimum/fair wages, automated welfare and insurance integration, multi-tiered federation governance, and AI-driven spatial demand forecasting and workforce allocation [PS, INFERENCE].

### Fundamental System Objective
To construct a scalable, multi-tenant digital service marketplace owned and governed by Labour Cooperative Federations that connects certified, eShram-registered cooperative workers with domestic and institutional consumers while delivering fair earnings, spatial AI workforce dispatch, and integrated social security [PS, INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1.  **Connectivity Limitations**: Rural-urban peripheral areas in India often experience intermittent 3G/4G connectivity or high network latency [KNOWN]. The worker application must support offline job caching, offline job status updates (queued pings), and lightweight payload serialization [INFERENCE].
2.  **Hardware Constraints**: Primary workers frequently use low-end Android smartphones (2GB–3GB RAM, limited storage) [KNOWN]. The worker app APK size must remain strictly under **25MB**, minimize background GPS battery drain via adaptive geofencing, and avoid heavy client-side AI computations [ASSUMPTION, INFERENCE].
3.  **Digital & Tech Literacy**: A significant percentage of manual laborers have basic formal literacy and limited English fluency [KNOWN]. Input mechanisms must rely on large touch targets, color-coded status badges, voice-assisted navigation, and iconography [INFERENCE].
4.  **Operational Environment**: Outdoor conditions (rain, dust, heat) require robust error handling for missed pings, extended timeout windows for job acceptances, and multi-channel fallbacks (SMS / IVR) when push notifications fail [INFERENCE].
5.  **Regulatory & Cooperative Federalism Structure**: In India, Cooperation is a State subject under the Constitution, while Multi-State Cooperative Societies fall under Central purview [KNOWN]. The platform must accommodate state-specific labor laws, varying society commission limits, and regional federation structures [INFERENCE].

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Monolithic Web & Mobile Platform (Basic CRUD & Rule-Based Matching)
*   **Mechanism**: Monolithic Node.js/Django backend with direct SQL spatial queries (`PostGIS`). Simple rule-based nearest-worker allocation based on straight-line distance.
*   **Required Data**: User profiles, worker profiles, basic coordinates, static booking slots.
*   **Architecture**: Single monolithic application server attached to a PostgreSQL database.
*   **Complexity**: Low.
*   **Explainability**: High (100% rule-based).
*   **Cost**: Low (<$50/month cloud hosting).
*   **Scalability**: Moderate (Vertical scaling limit).
*   **Hackathon Feasibility**: Extremely high.
*   **Production Feasibility**: Poor (Cannot scale across multi-state federations with offline syncing and complex AI demand forecasting).
*   **Risks**: Lacks AI capabilities explicitly required by requirement K [PS].

### Approach 2: Microservices-based Distributed Marketplace with Heavy Deep Learning AI
*   **Mechanism**: Distributed microservices architecture (Kubernetes, Kafka, gRPC) with Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for worker matching and Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) for dynamic pricing and dispatch.
*   **Required Data**: Massive real-time streaming telemetry, historical trajectory data, clickstream logs.
*   **Architecture**: 8+ microservices, Kafka event streams, PyTorch model servers, Redis cache, distributed database (CockroachDB).
*   **Complexity**: Extremely High.
*   **Explainability**: Very Low (Black-box neural networks).
*   **Cost**: High ($500+/month).
*   **Scalability**: Very High.
*   **Hackathon Feasibility**: Unrealistic for a 4-6 student team within hackathon timelines. High probability of integration failure.
*   **Production Feasibility**: Over-engineered for early-stage cooperative deployment.
*   **Risks**: Inability to complete MVP demo; high failure rate.

### Approach 3: Modular Event-Driven Micro-Monolith with Hybrid AI (Recommended)
*   **Mechanism**: Modular backend architecture (FastAPI for AI/ML, Node.js/Express for Core Business Logic) using Uber H3 geospatial indexing, XGBoost/Prophet for time-series demand forecasting, and Hungarian Algorithm (Kuhn-Munkres) for constrained workforce allocation.
*   **Required Data**: Spatial-temporal booking logs, worker availability vectors, eShram/DigiLocker schema, skill matrices.
*   **Architecture**: Clean separation of Consumer/Worker Mobile App (Flutter), Web Admin Dashboard (React), Core Engine (Node.js), Spatial AI Service (Python FastAPI), PostgreSQL/PostGIS + Redis.
*   **Complexity**: Moderate & Balanced.
*   **Explainability**: High (Mathematical optimization & interpretable AI).
*   **Cost**: Low-to-Moderate ($30–$100/month).
*   **Scalability**: High (Horizontally scalable, containerized).
*   **Hackathon Feasibility**: High (Realistic completion with polished UX).
*   **Production Feasibility**: Excellent (Aligns with Ministry of Cooperation IT guidelines and e-PACS ERP architecture patterns [RESEARCHED]).

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

**Selected Approach**: **Approach 3 (Modular Event-Driven Micro-Monolith with Hybrid AI)**.

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected
1.  *Approach 1 (Monolithic Rule-Based)* was rejected because it fails to satisfy **Requirement K** ("AI-based demand forecasting and workforce allocation") [PS] and cannot handle spatial demand density prediction across large cooperative federations.
2.  *Approach 2 (Heavy Deep Learning Microservices)* was rejected due to excessive architectural complexity, high infrastructure costs, black-box unexplainability (violating cooperative transparency principles), and high risk of incomplete implementation during the hackathon.

### Why Approach 3 Wins
*   **Fulfills All Explicit Requirements**: Directly implements spatial AI, multi-tenant federation dashboards, eShram welfare integration, and offline mobile support [PS, INFERENCE].
*   **Explainable & Fair**: Uses mathematically transparent allocation algorithms (Hungarian bipartite matching with distance, skill rating, and worker earnings equity weights), ensuring workers receive equal job distribution [INFERENCE].
*   **Realistic Hackathon Execution**: Allows clear separation of work among 4-6 students (e.g., 2 Mobile/Frontend, 2 Backend/Database, 1 AI/ML Engineer, 1 DevOps/Integration Specialist) [ASSUMPTION].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative

1.  **Onboarding & Verification Flow**: A worker registers via the Multilingual Worker App. The worker enters their Aadhaar number and eShram UAN [PS, RESEARCHED]. The backend invokes the DigiLocker Sandbox API for eKYC verification and validates the UAN via the eShram Aggregator API [RESEARCHED]. The worker selects their primary Primary Labour Cooperative Society (PLCS). The PLCS administrator reviews documents on the Admin Dashboard and issues a digital verification badge [PS, INFERENCE].
2.  **Booking Lifecycle & Allocation Flow**:
    *   A customer opens the Customer App, enters their location, selects a service (e.g., Plumbing Repair), and requests an immediate or scheduled booking [PS].
    *   The `Geo-Spatial Service` converts the customer latitude/longitude into an Uber H3 spatial index cell and queries Redis for active, nearby workers belonging to local certified societies [INFERENCE].
    *   The request is passed to the `AI Service`. The allocation algorithm ranks candidate workers based on spatial proximity, skill badge tier, historical rating, and **worker earnings equity score** (prioritizing qualified workers who have received fewer jobs that week) [INFERENCE].
    *   A dispatch ping is sent via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to the selected worker's app. If accepted within 45 seconds, the state transitions to `ASSIGNED`. If rejected or timed out, it cascades to the next optimal worker [INFERENCE].
3.  **Execution & Settlement Flow**:
    *   The worker navigates to the customer location using OpenStreetMap routing. Upon arrival, the worker enters a customer-generated 4-digit OTP to start the job (`IN_PROGRESS`) [INFERENCE].
    *   Upon completion, the worker generates the digital invoice. The customer pays via UPI [PS].
    *   The payment engine splits funds instantly: 88% is credited directly to the worker's Aadhaar-linked bank account, 7% is routed to the Primary Society's account for operational overhead, and 5% is allocated to the Federation Welfare Fund [INFERENCE].
    *   Micro-deduction details are logged to the worker's welfare ledger and synced with the eShram API [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Component 1: Spatial Demand Forecasting Engine
1.  **What exact problem does AI solve?** Predicts service demand volume per geographic zone (H3 Hexagonal Cell) and trade category for the next 24–72 hours [PS, INFERENCE].
2.  **Input**: Historical booking timestamps, H3 spatial cell IDs, trade categories, local weather data, day-of-week/holiday flags [INFERENCE].
3.  **Output**: Predicted booking volume matrix $\hat{D}_{z, t, c}$ (Zone $z$, Time $t$, Category $c$) [INFERENCE].
4.  **Why can a simpler rule/system not solve it?** Demand varies non-linearly across geography, seasons, weather events, and festivals. Static averages lead to severe worker underutilization or acute customer shortages [INFERENCE].
5.  **Data required**: Minimum 1,000 historical booking records (synthetically generated for hackathon) [ASSUMPTION].
6.  **Evaluation metric**: Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) and RMSE [INFERENCE].
7.  **What happens when it is wrong?** If demand is over-predicted, workers are suggested to relocate to high-demand zones with lower actual bookings; mitigated by showing confidence intervals [INFERENCE].
8.  **Training/Fine-tuning/RAG/CV/Speech**: Standard supervised regression using **XGBoost / Prophet** [INFERENCE]. No fine-tuning or RAG required.
9.  **Human oversight**: Federation admins can override auto-allocated shift recommendations based on ground knowledge [INFERENCE].

### Component 2: Fair & Constrained Workforce Allocation Optimizer
1.  **What exact problem does AI solve?** Solves the Bipartite Matching problem connecting incoming customer bookings with optimal available workers, balancing travel time, skill match, and income equity among cooperative members [PS, INFERENCE].
2.  **Input**: Customer location $(lat_c, lon_c)$, required trade, list of $N$ available nearby workers with distance $d_i$, rating $r_i$, and weekly earnings $e_i$ [INFERENCE].
3.  **Output**: Optimal worker assignment vector $W^*$ maximizing objective function $U(i) = w_1 \cdot \frac{1}{d_i} + w_2 \cdot r_i - w_3 \cdot e_i$ [INFERENCE].
4.  **Why can a simpler rule/system not solve it?** Pure nearest-neighbour rules create a "rich-get-richer" problem where a few top-rated workers near dense areas receive 90% of bookings while other cooperative members starves for work, violating cooperative fairness [INFERENCE].
5.  **Data required**: Real-time worker GPS, skill badges, weekly payout ledger [INFERENCE].
6.  **Evaluation metric**: Mean Match Latency (<2 sec), Gini Coefficient of Worker Earnings Distribution across Society (<0.25) [INFERENCE].
7.  **What happens when it is wrong?** If assigned worker declines, system falls back to top-3 ranked alternatives [INFERENCE].
8.  **Method**: **Operations Research Optimization (Kuhn-Munkres / Linear Programming via SciPy/OR-Tools)** [INFERENCE].
9.  **Human oversight**: None required during instantaneous dispatch; weekly distribution audits available to Society Supervisor [INFERENCE].

### Explicit Non-AI Statement
AI is **EXPLICITY NOT USED** for worker rating calculation, disciplinary actions, account suspensions, or wage rate determinations [INFERENCE]. In a cooperative model, pricing is set democratically by the Federation board, and worker performance issues are routed to human society supervisors for transparent peer review and retraining [INFERENCE].

```sql
-- Core Schema Structure

CREATE TABLE cooperative_federations (
    federation_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),

    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP

CREATE TABLE workers (
    worker_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    society_id UUID REFERENCES primary_societies(society_id),
    full_name VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
    phone_number VARCHAR(15) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    aadhaar_hash VARCHAR(64) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    eshram_uan VARCHAR(12) UNIQUE,
    verification_status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'PENDING', -- PENDING, VERIFIED, REJECTED
    current_h3_index VARCHAR(15),
    is_active BOOLEAN DEFAULT false,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

CREATE TABLE worker_skills (
    skill_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    worker_id UUID REFERENCES workers(worker_id),
    trade_category VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL, -- Electrician, Plumber, Caregiver, etc.
    skill_level VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'L1_CERTIFIED',
    ncct_cert_url TEXT,
    verified_by_society BOOLEAN DEFAULT true
);

CREATE TABLE bookings (
    booking_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    customer_id UUID NOT NULL,
    worker_id UUID REFERENCES workers(worker_id),
    society_id UUID REFERENCES primary_societies(society_id),
    trade_category VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
    booking_status VARCHAR(30) NOT NULL, -- CREATED, MATCHED, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED, CANCELLED
    service_location GEOMETRY(Point, 4326) NOT NULL,
    h3_cell VARCHAR(15) NOT NULL,
    scheduled_time TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE NOT NULL,
    total_amount DECIMAL(10, 2) NOT NULL,
    worker_payout DECIMAL(10, 2) NOT NULL,
    society_commission DECIMAL(10, 2) NOT NULL,
    welfare_contribution DECIMAL(10, 2) NOT NULL,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);
```

### Offline & Low-Network Design
1.  **Local Storage Engine**: The Worker Mobile App utilizes **Hive / SQLite** for encrypted local persistence [INFERENCE].
2.  **Job Execution Offline Queue**: When a worker enters a low-connectivity zone (e.g., basement, rural interior):
    *   Job completion state changes, customer OTP validation, and work timestamps are saved locally in SQLite with a cryptographic hash [INFERENCE].
    *   A background sync worker (`WorkManager` on Android) listens for network availability changes and auto-flushes queued payloads to the backend [INFERENCE].
3.  **Bandwidth Reduction Strategies**:
    *   All API communications use compressed **Protocol Buffers or GZIP JSON** [INFERENCE].
    *   Worker location pings are batched locally and transmitted every 30 seconds or when displacement exceeds 50 meters, reducing cellular data usage by over 70% [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

1.  **PII & Identity Protection**:
    *   Aadhaar numbers are **NEVER** stored in plain text. Only HMAC-SHA256 hashes are stored for deduplication [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].

    *   Double-entry accounting ledger guarantees immutable transaction tracking for every rupee split between worker, society, and welfare fund [INFERENCE].
    *   All payouts are audited against eShram UAN records to prevent phantom worker fraud [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
4.  **Governance & Liability Mitigation**:
    *   Because the platform is owned by Labour Cooperative Federations, disputes regarding service damages or poor quality are managed by an elected District Dispute Committee rather than unilateral platform bans [INFERENCE].
