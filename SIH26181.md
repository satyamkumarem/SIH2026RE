## 1. Executive Snapshot

This dossier provides a comprehensive technical, architectural, and strategic roadmap for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Problem Statement SIH26181 [PS]. The target objective is to design and build a secure, privacy-preserving, edge-AI powered Personal Health Companion capable of continuous physiological and environmental monitoring, on-device health anomaly detection, and real-time early warnings for climate-induced disaster risks (heat waves, floods, air pollution, disease outbreaks) common in India [PS]. The solution operates without relying on continuous cloud connectivity [PS], prioritizing high resilience, low latency, local data governance [PS], and accessibility across both low-cost mobile phones and wearable smart devices [PS].

* **Source Text:** "Monitor heart rate, $\text{SpO}_2$, body temperature, activity levels, and sleep quality. Track changes in baseline health patterns. Generate personalized wellness indicators." [PS]
* **Interpretation:** The system must continuously or periodically ingest physiological telemetry ($\text{HR}$, $\text{SpO}_2$, skin/body temperature, accelerometer-derived physical activity, PPG-derived sleep quality) [PS], compute personalized baseline statistics over moving temporal windows [INFERENCE], and highlight deviations from normal physiological baselines [PS].
* **Implementation Implication:** Requires efficient time-series data storage on-device, streaming feature extraction (e.g., Heart Rate Variability / HRV, Resting Heart Rate / RHR, Motion Intensity), and lightweight baseline statistical tracking algorithms [INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1. **Intermittent / Zero Network Connectivity:** Disasters such as floods, cyclones, and extreme storms routinely destroy cell towers and electrical grids in India [KNOWN]. The entire inference engine, baseline database, and safety advisories must function 100% offline [PS].
2. **Device Hardware Diversity:** Target users in rural or underserved urban areas use low-cost Android smartphones (2GB–4GB RAM, entry-level MediaTek/Qualcomm SoCs) or budget smart bands [KNOWN]. High memory footprint models (e.g., large LLMs or heavy vision models) will cause system thrashing or crash [INFERENCE].
3. **Power & Battery Limitations:** Continuous multi-sensor sampling and high-frequency AI inference quickly deplete battery reserves [KNOWN]. Power budget optimization (duty-cycled sampling, event-triggered inference) is strictly required [INFERENCE].
4. **Language & Digital Literacy:** A large demographic of vulnerable outdoor workers and rural citizens cannot read complex medical charts or English text [KNOWN]. Guidance must be provided via localized vernacular speech, iconography, and color-coded risk alerts [INFERENCE].
5. **Sensor Quality & Artifacts:** Commercial low-cost PPG sensors and accelerometers suffer heavy motion artifacts during manual physical labor [RESEARCHED]. Filtering techniques (e.g., Butterworth bandpass filters, motion-artifact rejection using accelerometer cross-correlation) are vital [RESEARCHED].

### Detailed Step-by-Step Data Flow Narrative

1. **Biosignal Acquisition:** The wearable sensor node samples PPG (100 Hz), skin temperature (1 Hz), and IMU acceleration (50 Hz). Data is framed into binary BLE GATT packets [INFERENCE].
2. **Local Ingestion & Preprocessing:** The mobile Companion App receives BLE packets in the background via Android `ForegroundService`. Signals pass through a Digital Bandpass Filter (0.5 Hz – 4 Hz) to strip motion artifacts [INFERENCE].
3. **On-Device Feature Extraction:** Sliding temporal windows (e.g., 30-second frames) extract physiological features: Heart Rate ($\text{HR}$), RMSSD / SDNN (Heart Rate Variability), Physical Activity Index, and Skin Temperature slope [INFERENCE].
4. **On-Device AI Inference:**
   * **Anomaly Detector:** The extracted feature vector is fed into an on-device 1D Convolutional Autoencoder (quantized INT8 ONNX model running on ONNX Runtime Mobile) [INFERENCE]. If Reconstruction Error exceeds dynamic baseline threshold $\theta_{\text{baseline}}$, an Anomaly Alert is flagged [INFERENCE].
   * **Heat Strain Evaluator:** Combines Skin Temperature, elevated Heart Rate, and local Ambient WBGT/Heat Index to compute the Physiological Strain Index (PSI) ranging from 0 (No Stress) to 10 (Extreme Danger) [RESEARCHED].
   * **Fall / Distress Classifier:** High-G accelerometer impact followed by orientation shift triggers an on-device Fall Classifier [INFERENCE].
5. **Local Rule Matrix & Action Trigger:** If PSI $> 7.5$ or Anomaly Score is severe, the app executes local alerts: triggers haptic vibration, vocalizes localized instructions ("Drink water immediately and move to shade"), and logs the event to SQLCipher [INFERENCE].
6. **Offline Emergency Dispatch:** If a fall or unacknowledged acute distress is detected, a 15-second visual/audio countdown is initiated [INFERENCE]. If user fails to cancel, the system dispatches an SMS SOS containing last known GPS coordinates to pre-configured emergency contacts via native Telephony SMS Manager (requiring zero cellular data) [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### 1. Physiological Anomaly & Baseline Drift Detector
* **Purpose:** Detect subtle cardiovascular or autonomic health anomalies that diverge from user's rolling 7-day personal baseline [PS].
* **Model Type:** Quantized 1D-Convolutional Autoencoder (Unsupervised Anomaly Detection) [INFERENCE].
* **Input Features:** 30-sec feature vector: $[\text{Mean HR}, \text{SDNN}, \text{RMSSD}, \text{Min HR}, \text{Max HR}, \text{Skin Temp}, \text{Activity Count}]$ [INFERENCE].
* **Output:** Anomaly Reconstruction Error Score ($0.0 - 1.0$) + Categorical Flag (Normal, Mild Anomaly, Severe Anomaly) [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required:** Unlabeled continuous PPG/HR time-series (e.g., PPG-DaLiA dataset [RESEARCHED] or baseline personal tracking).
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Static heart rate thresholds (e.g., $\text{HR} > 100\text{ bpm}$) generate massive false positives during exercise and miss subtle baseline shifts in resting HR or HRV caused by onset fever or heat exhaustion [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric:** Mean Absolute Error (MAE) on baseline reconstruction; Precision-Recall AUC on anomaly benchmarks [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode & Risk:** False positive alerts causing user anxiety; false negative missing early illness [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight:** User can dismiss false warnings or mark "I was exercising", updating baseline calibration [INFERENCE].

### 2. Heat Stress & Dehydration Risk Evaluator
* **Purpose:** Estimate physiological heat strain and dehydration risk during heatwaves [PS].
* **Model Type:** Hybrid Physiological Strain Index (PSI) Formula + Gradient Boosted Decision Tree (LightGBM ONNX export) [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** $[\text{Core Temp Proxy (from Skin Temp + HR)}, \text{HR Strain}, \text{Ambient Temp}, \text{Humidity}, \text{Exposure Duration}, \text{Hydration Interval}]$ [RESEARCHED].
* **Output:** Heat Stress Risk Index ($0 - 10$) & Estimated Safe Exposure Time Remaining (minutes) [RESEARCHED].
* **Data Required:** WBGT parameters and physiological heat exposure datasets [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Human thermoregulation is highly non-linear; combining external weather with real-time internal heart rate elevation requires multi-variable non-linear regression [RESEARCHED].
* **Evaluation:** Correlation ($R^2$) with standard WBGT index and physiological heat strain scores [RESEARCHED].
* **Failure Mode:** Underestimating heat strain in high humidity [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight:** Prompt user to verify physical signs (dry mouth, dizziness) before escalating warnings [INFERENCE].

### 3. Fall & Sudden Medical Distress Classifier
* **Purpose:** Automatically recognize fall impacts or sudden loss of consciousness [PS].
* **Model Type:** Lightweight Random Forest / 1D-CNN [INFERENCE].
* **Input:** 3-axis Accelerometer + Gyroscope time series (3-second window at 50 Hz) [INFERENCE].
* **Output:** Event Probability: $[\text{Normal Activity}, \text{Fall Detected}, \text{Sudden Impact}]$ [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required:** MobiAct or similar public fall datasets [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Simple acceleration thresholding triggers false alarms when dropping a phone or jumping; ML temporal feature matching distinguishes true human fall dynamics [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation:** Sensitivity ($> 95\%$), Specificity ($> 98\%$) [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode:** Soft falls onto bedding missed; dropping device triggers false alarm [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight:** Mandatory 15-second audio/visual alert countdown with large "CANCEL" button before dispatching SMS [INFERENCE].

### Explicit Identification: Where AI Should NOT Be Used
* **AQI & Weather Index Thresholding:** Converting PM2.5 concentrations to CPCB AQI categories (Good, Satisfactory, Moderate, Poor, Severe) MUST use standard deterministic lookup tables provided by CPCB guidelines [RESEARCHED]. Using AI for deterministic mathematical formulas introduces unnecessary error and explainability loss [INFERENCE].
* **SOS SMS Dispatch:** Sending emergency SMS notifications must be governed by explicit rule-based state machines, never by probabilistic AI inference [INFERENCE].

### Offline & Low-Network Strategies
1. **Local Storage First:** All sensor data is written directly to local encrypted SQLite [PS].
2. **Geohash Regional Environmental Caching:** When internet IS available, the app pre-fetches and caches 7-day weather/AQI forecast matrices for the user's current district bounded by Geohash keys [INFERENCE].
3. **Resilient SMS Emergency Fallback:** When internet/cellular data is down during floods or cyclones, the app switches to standard 2G GSM SMS protocol to broadcast compact emergency SOS coordinates [INFERENCE].
4. **Bandwidth Reduction:** If opt-in anonymized public health telemetry sync is enabled, data is compressed using Protocol Buffers (protobuf) and transmitted only during Wi-Fi connection [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

1. **On-Device Data Sovereignty:** Raw biosignals, health histories, and location coordinates are stored locally on the user's handset [PS]. No raw personal health data is uploaded to cloud servers [PS].
2. **Encryption Standards:**
   * **At Rest:** Database encrypted using AES-256 via SQLCipher [INFERENCE]. Encryption keys are generated on-device and stored in Android Keystore / iOS Keychain [INFERENCE].
   * **In Transit (Optional Sync):** Anonymized, aggregated public health indicators use TLS 1.3 with Certificate Pinning [INFERENCE].
3. **Location Privacy:** GPS location is accessed locally ONLY to index offline weather/hazard grids or format SOS SMS emergency messages [PS]. Location tracks are never logged permanently [INFERENCE].
4. **User Consent & Data Control:** Clear permission toggles for location access, emergency contact dispatch, and anonymized research contribution [PS].
5. **Regulatory Disclaimer:** The application is explicitly designated as a "Personal Health Companion and Early Warning Prototype", NOT a medical device certified by CDSCO (Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation) or FDA [INFERENCE]. Medical emergencies require professional clinical consultation [INFERENCE].
