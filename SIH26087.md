## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Executive Summary
The National Council for Cooperative Training (NCCT), an autonomous body under the Ministry of Cooperation, operates a nationwide network comprising VAMNICOM (Pune), 5 Regional Institutes of Cooperative Management (RICMs), 14 Institutes of Cooperative Management (ICMs), and 107 Junior Training Centres (JTCs) [KNOWN]. While these institutions train over 2.2 lakh cooperative personnel, Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) members, Self-Help Groups (SHGs), dairy cooperatives, and rural youth annually [RESEARCHED], the current operational model suffers from manual workflows, fragmented databases, inadequate trainee tracking, zero automated employment linkages, and limited learning analytics [PS]. This proposal presents an integrated, cloud-based cooperative capacity-building ecosystem combining an ERP-driven training management platform, an offline-first multilingual Learning Management System (LMS) with AI-powered career guidance, face/QR-based attendance hardware verification, verifiable digital skill credentialing, and a dedicated recruiter exchange [INFERENCE]. Designed for rural low-bandwidth realities, the solution bridges skill acquisition and rural employment, transforming cooperative training into an end-to-end career engine [INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

### Operational & Infrastructure Realities

1. **Connectivity Realities**: 
   Rural training centers (JTCs) and villages often experience frequent internet outages, 2G/3G degradation, and high latency [KNOWN].
   * *System Response*: The system must prioritize an "Offline-First" paradigm. Mobile learning devices store content locally in SQLite/IndexedDB, while attendance kiosks operate completely offline using local facial embedding matching, queuing logs to sync when connectivity recovers [INFERENCE].

2. **Digital Literacy & Language Diversity**: 
   Rural youth, PACS staff, and dairy farmers possess varied literacy levels and speak diverse regional languages (Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, etc.) [KNOWN].
   * *System Response*: UI must feature minimal text, high visual iconography, single-click audio instructions, and voice-assisted navigation [INFERENCE].

3. **Hardware Availability**: 
   RICMs and ICMs have basic desktop PCs and standard webcams or smartphones, but lack high-end AI servers or specialized biometric hardware [INFERENCE].
   * *System Response*: Attendance models must be optimized for CPU/low-cost Android devices using lightweight neural networks (e.g., MobileFaceNet / TFLite) rather than requiring GPU servers [INFERENCE].

4. **Power & Field Conditions**: 
   Rural centers face intermittent power supply [KNOWN].
   * *System Response*: Edge devices must operate on battery backup (tablets/smartphones); mobile client applications must minimize battery consumption by throttling background syncs [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative
1. **Onboarding & Nomination**: A PACS manager nominates 10 members via the Web Portal. The API Gateway authenticates the request, generates pending trainee profiles in PostgreSQL, and dispatches SMS notification links to candidates [INFERENCE].
2. **Offline Classroom Attendance**: At an ICM classroom, the hardware terminal (Android tablet/webcam) captures facial frames. MobileFaceNet generates a 128-dimensional embedding vector, compares it against locally stored trainee face templates, logs timestamped attendance in SQLite, and displays instant visual confirmation [INFERENCE].
3. **Synchronization**: When internet becomes available, the terminal sends encrypted attendance batches to the Backend Sync API. Redis handles deduplication, and PostgreSQL updates master attendance logs [INFERENCE].
4. **Learning & Assessment**: Trainees download SCORM/video modules on their mobile PWA. Assessments taken offline generate cryptographically signed result payloads stored locally in IndexedDB, which automatically sync to the LMS server upon reconnecting [INFERENCE].
5. **Credential Issuance & Verification**: Upon course completion, the Certificate Service generates a PDF certificate embedded with a unique SHA-256 digital signature and QR code. Employers scan the QR code to verify authenticity instantly against the Public Registry API without logging in [INFERENCE].
6. **Career Assistance & Recruitment**: Trainees query the AI Chatbot via voice/text in Hindi or regional languages. The Chatbot retrieves matching courses and job vacancies from PostgreSQL/FAISS. Recruiters filter certified candidates by skill score/district and dispatch interview invitations directly to candidate phones [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### 1. Edge Facial Recognition Attendance System
* **Purpose**: Automate trainee attendance logging in classroom/field environments without manual proxy risks [INFERENCE].
* **Model Type**: MobileFaceNet / FaceNet lightweight CNN quantized to TensorFlow Lite [INFERENCE].
* **Input**: Live video camera frame (640x480 crop) [INFERENCE].
* **Output**: Trainee ID + Confidence score (e.g., Trainee #1042, 98.4% match) [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required**: Onboarding photo (1 front-facing crop per trainee converted to 128-float embedding) [INFERENCE].
* **Why AI is Preferable**: Eliminates proxy attendance, physical paper registers, and manual data entry errors across 2,000+ annual batches [RESEARCHED].
* **Evaluation Metric**: False Acceptance Rate (FAR < 0.01%), False Rejection Rate (FRR < 1%) [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode**: Poor lighting or extreme facial obstruction -> System falls back automatically to QR Code scan + OTP verification [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight**: Classroom instructor reviews and approves daily synced attendance summaries on portal dashboard [INFERENCE].

### 2. Multilingual Career Counseling Chatbot
* **Purpose**: Guide rural youth with low digital literacy to relevant cooperative skill courses and job vacancies [INFERENCE].
* **Model Type**: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) using Quantized Llama-3 / Mistral + Whisper Speech-to-Text [INFERENCE].
* **Input**: User text or audio query in regional language (e.g., "मुझे डेयरी फार्मिंग कोर्स करना है") [INFERENCE].
* **Output**: Audio/text recommendation with direct course registration link and job opening summary [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required**: NCCT course catalogs, VAMNICOM syllabus, cooperative job role descriptions [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI is Preferable**: Static FAQs cannot handle complex, natural-language voice queries in diverse Indian dialects [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric**: Response relevance score (BLEU/ROUGE), answer accuracy > 90% [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode**: Hallucination or off-topic prompt -> System catches low confidence score and responds with standard menu links + human counselor contact [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight**: Option to escalate query to NCCT institute career counselor [INFERENCE].

### Explicit Non-AI Directives
* **Core ERP Operations**: Timetable scheduling, hostel bed allocation, fee receipts, and certificate generation MUST NOT use AI models. They must rely on deterministic, audited relational database transactions [INFERENCE].
* **Attendance Database State**: Attendance verification logs must be immutably recorded via deterministic SQL triggers, never inferred by AI [INFERENCE].

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Core Database Schema (Entity Relationship Highlights)

```sql
-- Trainees & Users Master
CREATE TABLE users (
    user_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    aadhaar_hash VARCHAR(64) UNIQUE,
    full_name VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
    phone VARCHAR(15) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    preferred_language VARCHAR(10) DEFAULT 'hi',
    user_role VARCHAR(20) CHECK (user_role IN ('TRAINEE', 'FACULTY', 'RECRUITER', 'ADMIN')),
    created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Trainee Biometric Template (Privacy Preserving: Embeddings Only)
CREATE TABLE trainee_biometrics (
    biometric_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    user_id UUID REFERENCES users(user_id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
    face_embedding FLOAT8[128] NOT NULL, -- 128-d floating point vector
    created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Institutions (NCCT HQ, RICMs, ICMs)
CREATE TABLE institutions (
    inst_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    inst_code VARCHAR(20) UNIQUE NOT NULL, -- e.g., 'RICM-BLR', 'VAMNICOM'
    name VARCHAR(150) NOT NULL,
    state VARCHAR(50),
    district VARCHAR(50),
    location GEOGRAPHY(POINT, 4326)
);

-- ERP Hostel Rooms Management
CREATE TABLE hostel_rooms (
    room_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    inst_id UUID REFERENCES institutions(inst_id),
    block_name VARCHAR(50),
    room_number VARCHAR(20),
    capacity INT NOT NULL,
    occupied_count INT DEFAULT 0
);

-- Offline Sync Queue Schema (Client/Server Sync)
CREATE TABLE sync_audit_log (
    sync_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    device_id VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    entity_type VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL, -- 'ATTENDANCE', 'ASSESSMENT'
    payload JSONB NOT NULL,
    client_timestamp TIMESTAMP NOT NULL,
    synced_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    sync_status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'SUCCESS'
);
```

### Offline Synchronization Protocol
1. **Local Storage Strategy**: The mobile app/PWA utilizes IndexedDB (via WatermelonDB / Dexie.js) on client browsers and SQLite on mobile React Native apps [INFERENCE].
2. **Delta Sync & Conflict Resolution**:
   * Every record maintains a `updated_at` timestamp and a client-generated `UUID` v4 [INFERENCE].
   * Sync requests transmit only mutated records (`delta`) since the last successful sync timestamp (`last_synced_at`) [INFERENCE].
   * **Conflict Handling**: Server timestamp wins for ERP room allocations; client timestamp with vector-clock validation wins for offline student learning progress [INFERENCE].
3. **Bandwidth Reduction**: Video lessons are compressed using H.265/AV1 codecs with multi-bitrate options (144p to 720p). Offline downloads store encrypted chunked media files locally [INFERENCE].
4. **Offline UX**: Visual offline status banners, transparent upload queue indicators, and seamless background sync triggered via standard browser `BackgroundSync API` when connectivity recovers [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

1. **Biometric Privacy & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance**: 
   Raw face photographs captured during onboarding are discarded immediately after feature extraction. ONLY 128-dimensional floating-point mathematical embedding vectors are encrypted and stored using AES-256 [INFERENCE]. Biometric matching is performed locally or over secure channels [INFERENCE].

2. **Personal Identifiable Information (PII)**: 
   Aadhaar numbers are NEVER stored in plain text. Only truncated reference numbers and salted SHA-256 hashes are retained for record deduplication [INFERENCE].

3. **Authentication & Authorization**: 
   Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) enforced via short-lived JWTs (15 min expiry) with secure HTTP-only refresh tokens. Role tiers: `SUPER_ADMIN` (Ministry), `INSTITUTE_ADMIN` (RICM/ICM Director), `FACULTY`, `RECRUITER`, `TRAINEE` [INFERENCE].

4. **Data Ownership & Encryption**: 
   Data belongs to the Ministry of Cooperation / NCCT [PS]. All data in transit uses TLS 1.3 encryption, and data at rest in PostgreSQL and MinIO uses AES-256 encryption [INFERENCE].

5. **Audit Trails & Governance**: 
   Immutable audit log table records every administrative write action, certificate issuance, hostel allocation, and recruitment outreach for governance compliance [INFERENCE].
