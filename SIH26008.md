## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
The National Mineral Development Corporation (NMDC), under the Ministry of Steel, operates continuous, high-capacity overland and in-pit conveyor belt systems (such as the 4.8 km downhill conveyor at Kirandul/Kumaraswamy and sandwich conveyors at Donimalai) to transport abrasive iron ore across harsh terrains [KNOWN, RESEARCHED]. Belt joint (splice) ruptures, longitudinal tears, edge wear, and idler seizures cause catastrophic unplanned shutdowns, costing millions of rupees per hour in lost throughput, safety risks, and equipment damage [KNOWN]. Existing maintenance relies on manual visual walk-throughs and scheduled maintenance, which fail to detect subsurface vulcanization degradation, micro-tears, or thermal anomalies before failure [PS, RESEARCHED]. This proposal details **BeltGuard-AI**, an integrated Edge-AI, IoT multi-sensor, computer vision, and 3D Digital Twin predictive monitoring ecosystem. By combining high-speed optical vision at splice zones, thermal imaging arrays, wireless idler vibration/temperature nodes, and a physics-informed Digital Twin running on edge gateway hardware integrated via OPC UA/Modbus to SCADA/PLC systems, BeltGuard-AI enables real-time health scoring, 2-to-8 week failure prediction, automated emergency stop (E-stop) interlocks, and actionable maintenance work-order generation [INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

* **Mining Site Environment & Weather:** Iron ore open-cast mines (such as Bailadila/Kirandul in Chhattisgarh and Donimalai in Karnataka) feature high ambient temperatures (up to 45°C), heavy monsoon rain, intense red dust (hematite dust coating optical lenses), and high humidity [KNOWN, RESEARCHED].
  * *Constraint Impact:* Optics require IP67/IP68 stainless steel enclosures equipped with positive air purging (using compressed air) or automatic mechanical wipers [INFERENCE]. Sensors must withstand extreme dust ingress [INFERENCE].
* **High Belt Speed & Heavy Impact:** Mine overland conveyors operate at high speeds (up to 4.0 - 6.0 m/s) with lump iron ore impacts up to 200–300 mm size [KNOWN, RESEARCHED].
  * *Constraint Impact:* Cameras must use high-speed global shutter sensors with synchronized strobe lighting (exposure time < 100 microseconds) to eliminate motion blur [INFERENCE].
* **Network & Connectivity Gaps:** Deep open-pit slopes and long overland sections spanning 4.8+ km lack continuous Wi-Fi or 4G/5G coverage [KNOWN, RESEARCHED].
  * *Constraint Impact:* Architecture must be Edge-First. Edge gateways (NVIDIA Jetson / Industrial PCs) must execute real-time inference and store telemetry locally during network outages, synchronizing with the central server via MQTT once reconnected [INFERENCE].
* **Power Constraints:** Long overland sections do not have accessible AC power outlets at every idler frame [KNOWN].
  * *Constraint Impact:* Idler sensors must use ultra-low-power BLE/LoRaWAN nodes with 3–5 year battery life or energy harvesting (vibration-based piezoceramic generators) [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **SCADA / PLC Interlocking Latency:** Emergency stop signal upon detecting a longitudinal belt rip must be transmitted within < 200 milliseconds to minimize torn belt length [RESEARCHED].
  * *Constraint Impact:* E-stop signal must be generated directly by the local Edge Gateway via hardwired relay output to the PLC safety loop, bypassing cloud latency [INFERENCE].

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Rule-Based SCADA & Threshold System
* **Mechanism:** Deploy conventional wired vibration sensors, temperature probes, and mechanical belt-alignment limit switches wired into plant PLC. Alerts triggered when static thresholds are breached.
* **Evaluation:**
  * *Problem Fit:* Low. Cannot detect surface micro-cracks, splice separation, or longitudinal tears until total failure occurs [INFERENCE].
  * *Accuracy Potential:* Poor (high false positives from temporary load spikes) [INFERENCE].
  * *Complexity & Cost:* Low complexity, low initial hardware cost [INFERENCE].
  * *Feasibility:* Hackathon: 95% | Production: 60% (outdated paradigm) [INFERENCE].
  * *Rejection Reason:* Fails to satisfy requirements REQ-5 (AI Vision), REQ-7 (Digital Twin), and REQ-9 (Predictive Analytics) [INFERENCE].

### Approach 2: Pure Cloud-Based AI Vision & Thermal System
* **Mechanism:** Mount IP cameras along conveyor lines streaming continuous video over LTE/Wi-Fi to cloud processing servers (AWS/GCP) running computer vision models.
* **Evaluation:**
  * *Problem Fit:* Medium. High visual accuracy for surface defects, but highly vulnerable to network outages in mining pits [INFERENCE].
  * *Latency & Reliability:* Cloud processing latency (1–5 seconds) is too high for automated E-stop triggers (<200 ms) during belt rip events [INFERENCE].
  * *Data Cost:* Massive cellular bandwidth cost streaming high-res RTSP video continuously [INFERENCE].
  * *Feasibility:* Hackathon: 80% | Production: 40% [INFERENCE].
  * *Rejection Reason:* Unreliable in low-connectivity open-pit mine environments; high bandwidth cost [INFERENCE].

### Approach 3 (RECOMMENDED): Hybrid Edge-AI, Multi-Modal IIoT & Physics-Informed Digital Twin
* **Mechanism:**
  1. *Edge Layer:* Local industrial edge computing nodes (NVIDIA Jetson / Industrial PC) situated at conveyor head/tail stations process RTSP streams from high-speed RGB global shutter cameras and thermal sensor matrices locally. Real-time vision models (YOLOv11-Nano / Segmentation) run on edge with hardwired digital output to PLC safety loop (<100 ms E-stop response) [INFERENCE].
  2. *IoT Mesh Layer:* Low-power wireless nodes on idlers and tension take-up units transmit vibration, temperature, and tension data via LoRaWAN/BLE mesh to local edge gateways [INFERENCE].
  3. *Predictive & Twin Layer:* Aggregated features and encoder position tags are pushed to a central server hosting an LSTM/XGBoost RUL predictor and a 3D WebGL Digital Twin (Three.js) displaying spatial conveyor status, heatmaps, and splice health scores [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation:**
  * *Problem Fit:* Excellent. Fully covers all explicit requirements (IoT, Vision, Drones, SCADA, Digital Twin, AI Predictive Analytics) [INFERENCE].
  * *Accuracy Potential:* High (multi-modal sensor fusion reduces false alarms) [INFERENCE].
  * *Explainability:* High (visual bounding boxes + physics-informed tension curves) [INFERENCE].
  * *Hackathon Feasibility:* 90% (Can be built with simulated sensor feeds + working Edge-AI vision + interactive WebGL Twin) [INFERENCE].
  * *Production Feasibility:* 95% (Scalable architecture standard in Industry 4.0) [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

### Selection
**Approach 3: Hybrid Edge-AI, Multi-Modal IIoT & Physics-Informed Digital Twin.**

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected
1. **Rule-Based System (Approach 1):** Rejected because simple static thresholds cannot identify visual splice degradation, rubber fatigue, or longitudinal belt tears, which are the primary failure modes cited by NMDC [PS, INFERENCE].
2. **Pure Cloud Vision (Approach 2):** Rejected because streaming continuous high-bitrate video from deep iron ore pits over unstable cellular networks is cost-prohibitive and introduces unacceptable emergency stop latency (>2 seconds), risking severe conveyor damage during a rip event [INFERENCE].

### Architectural Superiority of Chosen Hybrid Approach
* **Zero Latency Safety Interlock:** Critical longitudinal rip detection occurs on the local Edge PC, directly tripping the PLC safety circuit via digital I/O in < 100 milliseconds [INFERENCE].
* **Bandwidth Optimization:** Instead of streaming raw video 24/7, the Edge PC processes video locally and only transmits metadata, alert clips, and feature vectors to the central server and Digital Twin, reducing network bandwidth requirements by over 95% [INFERENCE].
* **Resilience:** If network connectivity to the central server fails, local edge monitoring and emergency interlocks remain fully operational [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative
1. **Data Acquisition:** High-speed RGB cameras positioned at belt underside capture 60 FPS frames synchronized with strobe LEDs. Wireless IIoT nodes on idler frames sample vibration (10 kHz FFT) and temperature every 30 seconds [INFERENCE].
2. **Edge Ingestion & Inference:** The RTSP video feed is ingested by the NVIDIA Jetson Orin via TensorRT pipeline. Frame buffers undergo crop/resize and pass through a custom YOLOv11 deep neural network trained to detect longitudinal rips, surface tears, and splice gapping [INFERENCE].
3. **Emergency Interlocking:** If a longitudinal rip exceeding 150mm is detected, the Edge Gateway immediately drops a GPIO relay output wired directly into the Siemens S7 PLC E-stop safety loop, stopping the belt drive motor in under 100 milliseconds [INFERENCE].
4. **Telemetry Streaming:** Telemetry metrics (vibration RMS, bearing temp, belt speed, splice health score) are bundled into JSON payloads and published via MQTT over TLS to the central server [INFERENCE].
5. **Central Analytics & RUL Estimation:** Central server ingests telemetry into InfluxDB. An LSTM predictive model evaluates multi-day feature trends (vibration spectrum drift + splice gap widening rate) to calculate Remaining Useful Life (RUL) in days [INFERENCE].
6. **Digital Twin Visualization & Work Orders:** The Three.js WebGL dashboard maps data onto a 3D model of the conveyor line. If a splice health drops below 60%, the system automatically flags its exact meter mark on the Digital Twin and generates a structured maintenance ticket with image evidence [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Component 1: Edge Computer Vision (Splice & Surface Damage Segmentation)
* **Purpose:** Detect surface cracks, longitudinal rips, edge fraying, and vulcanized splice joint gap widening on moving belt.
* **Model Type:** YOLOv11 Segmentation / ResNet-based U-Net.
* **Input:** High-speed RGB video frames (1920x1080 @ 60 FPS).
* **Output:** Bounding boxes, segmentation masks, defect class label, confidence score, and defect area ($mm^2$).
* **Data Required:** 3,000+ labeled images of healthy belts, spliced joints, micro-cracks, tears, and edge fraying under varied lighting and dust conditions.
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Classical edge detection fails due to changing specular reflections, ore dust streaks, and variable belt color [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric:** Mean Average Precision (mAP@0.5 > 0.92), Frame Processing Latency (< 15 ms).
* **Failure Mode:** Extreme dust accumulation blocking lens causing high false negatives.
* **Human Oversight:** High-confidence rip alerts (>95%) trigger auto E-stop; medium-confidence alerts flag video clips for control room operator confirmation within 60 seconds [INFERENCE].

### Component 2: Splice Degradation & Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Predictor
* **Purpose:** Forecast time-to-failure (RUL in days) for individual belt splices to enable scheduled vulcanization during planned maintenance windows.
* **Model Type:** Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) Recurrent Neural Network / XGBoost Regressor.
* **Input:** Daily splice gap width sequence, peak dynamic tension at drive pulley, belt speed variance, and operating hours.
* **Output:** Estimated RUL (days), 95% confidence interval, and degradation curve.
* **Data Required:** Multi-week time-series degradation logs of belt splice parameters leading up to failure.
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Linear degradation models fail because splice degradation is non-linear—accelerating rapidly once rubber debonding initiates [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric:** Root Mean Square Error (RMSE < 2.5 days on RUL prediction), Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE < 10%).
* **Failure Mode:** Sensor calibration drift causing erroneous trend prediction.
* **Human Oversight:** Scheduled maintenance recommendations reviewed and signed off by Chief Maintenance Engineer [INFERENCE].

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
* **Primary Belt Speed Calculation:** Rotary encoder pulse counting must use deterministic hardware interrupt logic—not AI computer vision—to ensure exact millimeter position tracking [INFERENCE].
* **Emergency Thermal Threshold Trips:** Idler bearing overheating (>80°C) must trigger alerts via hardcoded deterministic threshold logic, not probabilistic AI models, ensuring 100% deterministic safety response [INFERENCE].

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Database Schema (Entity-Relationship Highlights)

```sql
-- Sensors Master Table
CREATE TABLE sensors (
    sensor_id VARCHAR(50) PRIMARY KEY,
    conveyor_id VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    sensor_type VARCHAR(30) CHECK (sensor_type IN ('vibration', 'temperature', 'tension', 'speed', 'camera')),
    location_meter_mark NUMERIC(8,2) NOT NULL, -- Position along conveyor (0.00m to N.00m)
    ip_address VARCHAR(45),
    installation_date DATE,
    status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'active'
);

-- Splice Joint Master Table
CREATE TABLE belt_splices (
    splice_id VARCHAR(50) PRIMARY KEY,
    conveyor_id VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    splice_number INT NOT NULL,
    installation_date DATE NOT NULL,
    vulcanization_type VARCHAR(30), -- 'hot_vulcanized', 'cold_bonded', 'mechanical_fastener'
    baseline_gap_mm NUMERIC(5,2) NOT NULL,
    current_gap_mm NUMERIC(5,2) NOT NULL,
    health_index NUMERIC(5,2) DEFAULT 100.0, -- 0.0 to 100.0%
    predicted_rul_days INT,
    last_inspected_at TIMESTAMPTZ
);

-- Real-Time Anomaly & Defect Events
CREATE TABLE defect_events (
    event_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    conveyor_id VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    splice_id VARCHAR(50) REFERENCES belt_splices(splice_id),
    defect_type VARCHAR(40) CHECK (defect_type IN ('longitudinal_rip', 'splice_separation', 'edge_wear', 'bearing_hotspot', 'misalignment')),
    severity VARCHAR(15) CHECK (severity IN ('LOW', 'MEDIUM', 'HIGH', 'CRITICAL')),
    location_meter_mark NUMERIC(8,2) NOT NULL,
    image_snapshot_url TEXT,
    estop_triggered BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE,
    detected_at TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW(),
    acknowledged_by VARCHAR(50)
);
```

### Local Storage, Synchronization & Offline UX
* **Edge Storage:** Edge PC maintains a local SQLite database and circular buffer for video recordings (7-day rolling window) [INFERENCE].
* **Store-and-Forward Sync:** Telemetry packets are queued locally in a persistent RocksDB / SQLite queue during network outages. When connection to central server restores, an exponential backoff retry worker syncs queued data in compressed batches [INFERENCE].
* **Conflict Resolution:** Edge timestamp (GPS-synced via NTP) serves as immutable single source of truth for event order [INFERENCE].
* **Bandwidth Optimization:** Only anomaly frames, defect metadata, and aggregated 1-minute telemetry statistical summaries (min, max, avg, RMS) are pushed to central cloud/server [INFERENCE].
* **Offline Mobile PWA:** Technicians download daily work orders to local PWA IndexedDB before heading into deep open-cast pit zones. Photos taken during offline repairs are stored locally and synced upon returning to Wi-Fi range [INFERENCE].
