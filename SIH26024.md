## 1. Executive Snapshot

This research and technical dossier provides a production-grade blueprint for developing a centralized, offline-capable, AI-enabled smart governance and compliance monitoring platform for Coal India Limited (CIL) [PS]. The Indian coal mining ecosystem operates across eight major subsidiaries, hundreds of opencast and underground mine blocks, and thousands of field workers and contractors [KNOWN]. Currently, statutory compliance (under the Mines Act 1952 and Coal Mines Regulations 2017), safety inspection logs, environmental monitoring, and worker attendance are fragmented across legacy software, paper registers (e.g., DGMS Form 14/15), and isolated spreadsheets [PS, RESEARCHED]. This fragmented approach causes reporting delays, compliance gaps, lack of field accountability, and delayed administrative decision-making [PS]. The proposed system, **MineGov-AI**, bridges these operational gaps through a unified geo-spatial e-governance web platform paired with an offline-first mobile inspection app [INFERENCE]. It incorporates computer vision/OCR for document digitization, an XGBoost hazard prediction engine for proactive risk mitigation, automated multi-tier escalation workflows, and a SHA-256 cryptographic audit trail to enforce tamper-proof paperless governance across CIL mine sites [INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

*   **Geography & Harsh Field Conditions:** Coal India mines are located across remote industrial belts (e.g., Dhanbad, Korba, Singrauli, Asansol) [RESEARCHED]. Opencast pits experience severe dust, high mud during monsoons, and extreme temperatures (up to 48°C), while underground mines suffer from extreme humidity, continuous airborne coal dust, and hazardous explosive atmospheric conditions (Methane/CO) [KNOWN].
*   **Connectivity Realities:** Mobile cellular reception drops to zero inside deep opencast pits (>100 meters deep) and underground galleries [KNOWN]. Cloud connectivity cannot be assumed during field inspections. All field applications must be fully functional offline for at least 72 hours [INFERENCE].
*   **Hardware & Explosive Atmosphere Constraints:** Standard commercial smartphones are not flameproof. In underground mines (DGMS Degree I, II, III gaseous mines), devices must strictly comply with IS/IEC 60079 intrinsically safe standards or be operated only in designated safe zones (e.g., surface office / pithead) [KNOWN, RESEARCHED]. Field smartphones used in opencast mines require IP68 water/dust sealing and drop-resistant casing [INFERENCE].
*   **Power Supply Instability:** Mining field offices frequently experience power fluctuations and extended outages [KNOWN]. Field inspection devices must operate on optimized battery power (minimal background GPS polling), and edge gateways must include UPS power backup [INFERENCE].
*   **Language & Digital Literacy:** Field supervisors and contractor staff possess varying levels of digital literacy [KNOWN]. Interfaces must be minimalist, visually intuitive (color-coded status badges, icon-heavy), and support multilingual input (English, Hindi, Bengali, Odia) with local voice-to-text dictation capabilities [INFERENCE].

    *   *Verification:* Utilize timestamped vector clocks combined with server-authoritative conflict resolution rules (regulatory status state machine overrides local state) [INFERENCE].

    *   *Verification:* A SHA-256 hash-chained append-only database log provides equivalent tamper-evidence with significantly lower computational overhead and zero infrastructure complexity for a hackathon MVP [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

**Selected Approach: Approach 3 — Hybrid Geo-Spatial AI Platform with Offline Mobile Engine, Automated Workflows, and Cryptographic Audit Trail.**

*   **Why Alternatives Were Rejected:**
    *   *Approach 1 (Rule-Based Monolith)* was rejected because it fails to satisfy REQ-C (Predictive AI/Analytics for high-risk identification) and REQ-D (Offline geo-tagged mobile operations) [INFERENCE]. A simple web CRUD app cannot handle zero-connectivity deep pit inspections or proactively flag recurring violation patterns [INFERENCE].
    *   *Approach 2 (Pure Cloud-Native DL/LLM Microservices)* was rejected due to unrealistic deployment constraints in mining environments [INFERENCE]. Generative LLMs and heavy cloud computer vision models cannot run offline inside deep pits, present high cloud latency/costs, and introduce unacceptable hallucination risks when evaluating statutory safety compliance [INFERENCE].
*   **Why Approach 3 Wins:**
    1.  **Field Reality Alignment:** Uses a lightweight Flutter mobile client with SQLite/WatermelonDB, allowing field inspectors to capture observations, photo evidence, and worker attendance offline, syncing automatically when reaching surface Wi-Fi or cellular range [INFERENCE].
    2.  **Explainable AI:** Uses tree-based gradient boosting (XGBoost) for hazard risk scoring and Isolation Forest for operational anomaly detection [INFERENCE]. Feature importance weights (e.g., historical pit slope instability, rainfall intensity, missed maintenance cycles) are fully transparent and explainable to DGMS safety auditors [INFERENCE].
    3.  **Audit Integrity:** Replaces complex, resource-heavy public blockchains with a highly efficient, deterministic SHA-256 cryptographic hash-chain ledger, ensuring that compliance logs and closure approvals cannot be retroactively altered [INFERENCE].
    4.  **Hackathon Feasibility:** Can be fully prototyped by a 4-6 student team within the build window using open-source tools (Flutter, Node.js/Python FastAPI, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, React, Scikit-Learn/XGBoost) [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative

1.  **Field Observation Capture (Offline State):** A Mine Safety Officer in an underground gallery logs an unsafe roof strata condition using the Flutter mobile app [INFERENCE]. The app captures photo evidence, records GPS coordinates (or gallery node ID), logs exact timestamp, and saves the encrypted record locally into SQLite [INFERENCE].
2.  **Edge Sync Trigger:** Upon returning to the surface office, the app detects an active network connection and initiates delta synchronization via REST endpoints [INFERENCE]. The vector clock engine compares local and server timestamps, resolves non-conflicting records, and pushes the payload [INFERENCE].
3.  **Ingestion & Cryptographic Hashing:** The API Gateway validates the JWT token, extracts user role claims, and forwards the record to the Inspection Service [INFERENCE]. The service calculates a SHA-256 hash incorporating the previous log's hash, record contents, and inspector ID, appending it to the cryptographic audit ledger [INFERENCE].
4.  **Risk Scoring & Anomaly Evaluation:** The record triggers the AI Engine [INFERENCE]. XGBoost evaluates current pit parameters (roof condition, local humidity, historic incidents) and outputs a Hazard Risk Score (0-100) [INFERENCE]. If the score exceeds 75, the anomaly engine flags the sector as a "High-Risk Zone" [INFERENCE].
5.  **Automated Escalation & Real-Time Notification:** An unmitigated critical violation triggers the SLA Engine [INFERENCE]. Redis queues a background task with a 24-hour countdown timer [INFERENCE]. If the Colliery Agent fails to record a CAPA action within 24 hours, the system automatically escalates the ticket via SMS/Email to the Subsidiary General Manager (Safety) and updates the web dashboard via WebSocket push [INFERENCE].
6.  **Regulatory Access & Statutory Reporting:** DGMS inspectors log into the read-only Regulatory Portal to inspect the digital Form 14 register [INFERENCE]. The system dynamically renders the verified inspection records with cryptographic hash proofs, allowing one-click PDF export [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### 1. Hazard Risk Predictor
*   **Purpose:** Predict high-risk mine sectors and likelihood of safety violations in upcoming shifts [INFERENCE].
*   **Model Type:** XGBoost Classifier / Regressor [INFERENCE].
*   **Input:** Historical sector violation logs, shift length, rainfall (mm), equipment age, recent maintenance flags, geological strata stability index [INFERENCE].
*   **Output:** Pit Sector Risk Index (0.0 to 1.0) and top 3 contributing risk factors [INFERENCE].
*   **Data Required:** Tabular dataset of 2,000+ past inspection logs with historical environmental and operational parameters [ASSUMPTION].
*   **Why AI is Preferable:** Complex non-linear interactions between environmental variables (e.g., rainfall + slope angle + blast frequency) cannot be captured by simple static threshold rules [INFERENCE].
*   **Evaluation Metrics:** ROC-AUC (>0.85), Precision, and Recall on high-risk hazard classes [INFERENCE].
*   **Failure Mode Handling:** If model inference fails, default to conservative rule-based maximum safety classification [INFERENCE].
*   **Human Oversight:** Risk scores serve as advisory highlights on dashboard; humans perform all physical site closures [INFERENCE].

### 2. Operational Anomaly & Near-Miss Detector
*   **Purpose:** Identify abnormal environmental sensor readings (AQMS/gas) or fraudulent attendance patterns [INFERENCE].
*   **Model Type:** Isolation Forest (Unsupervised Anomaly Detection) [INFERENCE].
*   **Input:** Continuous stream of PM10, PM2.5, Methane, CO sensor values, and worker check-in timestamps [INFERENCE].
*   **Output:** Anomaly Score (-1 for outlier, +1 for normal) with flagged outlier parameters [INFERENCE].
*   **Data Required:** Baseline time-series sensor telemetry [ASSUMPTION].
*   **Why AI is Preferable:** Detects subtle multivariate drifts before fixed hard thresholds are breached [INFERENCE].
*   **Evaluation Metrics:** False Positive Rate (<5%), Precision at K [INFERENCE].
*   **Failure Mode Handling:** Fall back to strict static threshold alerts (e.g., Methane > 0.75% triggers immediate hard alert) [INFERENCE].
*   **Human Oversight:** Safety officer must physically inspect and confirm flagged sensor anomalies [INFERENCE].

### 3. Statutory Document Digitizer (OCR Engine)
*   **Purpose:** Extract structured text fields from scanned physical Form 14/15 and paper inspection sheets [PS, INFERENCE].
*   **Model Type:** Tesseract OCR with OpenCV image preprocessing pipeline (deskew, binarization, noise removal) [INFERENCE].
*   **Input:** Scanned JPG/PNG/PDF image of statutory form [INFERENCE].
*   **Output:** Extracted JSON key-value pairs (Inspector Name, Date, Violation Category, Location Code) [INFERENCE].
*   **Data Required:** 20+ sample form templates [ASSUMPTION].
*   **Why AI is Preferable:** Automates manual data entry from physical paper registers [INFERENCE].
*   **Evaluation Metrics:** Character Error Rate (CER < 5%), Field Extraction Accuracy (>90%) [INFERENCE].
*   **Failure Mode Handling:** Highlight low-confidence text fields in red on the web interface for human manual correction [INFERENCE].
*   **Human Oversight:** Mandatory human approval step before committing OCR-extracted data into official statutory database [INFERENCE].

### Where AI Must NOT Be Used
AI **must NOT** be used to automatically issue statutory penalties, grant mine safety clearances, override safety officers' observations, or automatically close compliance tickets [INFERENCE]. All regulatory actions must remain strictly deterministic and enforce mandatory human approval to prevent legal liability and algorithmic bias [INFERENCE].

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Database Schema (Core Relational Entities)

```sql
-- Mine Subsidiaries and Sites
CREATE TABLE mine_sites (
    mine_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    subsidiary_code VARCHAR(10) NOT NULL, -- e.g., 'BCCL', 'CCL', 'MCL'
    mine_name VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
    boundary_geom GEOMETRY(Polygon, 4326),
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Field Inspection Logs
CREATE TABLE inspections (
    inspection_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    mine_id UUID REFERENCES mine_sites(mine_id),
    inspector_id UUID NOT NULL,
    sector_name VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    location_geom GEOMETRY(Point, 4326),
    observation_text TEXT NOT NULL,
    hazard_level VARCHAR(20) CHECK (hazard_level IN ('CRITICAL', 'HIGH', 'MEDIUM', 'LOW')),
    photo_urls TEXT[],
    sync_status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'SYNCED',
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE NOT NULL,
    client_timestamp TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE NOT NULL
);

-- Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA)
CREATE TABLE capa_tickets (
    ticket_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    inspection_id UUID REFERENCES inspections(inspection_id),
    assigned_official_id UUID NOT NULL,
    action_required TEXT NOT NULL,
    status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'OPEN' CHECK (status IN ('OPEN', 'IN_PROGRESS', 'RESOLVED', 'CLOSED')),
    sla_due_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE NOT NULL,
    escalation_level INT DEFAULT 0,
    resolved_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE
);

-- Cryptographic Audit Ledger
CREATE TABLE audit_ledger (
    entry_id BIGSERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
    entity_type VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    entity_id UUID NOT NULL,
    action VARCHAR(20) NOT NULL,
    payload_json JSONB NOT NULL,
    previous_hash VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
    current_hash VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
    recorded_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);
```

### Offline Synchronization & Bandwidth Reduction Strategies

*   **Local Storage & Encryption:** Mobile device uses SQLite encrypted with SQLCipher (256-bit AES) [INFERENCE]. Unsynced records are flagged with local status `PENDING_SYNC` [INFERENCE].
*   **Vector Clock Delta Sync:** Each inspection record maintains a vector timestamp (`client_timestamp`, `server_timestamp`) [INFERENCE]. Upon connection re-establishment, the app sends only changed delta records since the last successful sync checkpoint [INFERENCE].
*   **Conflict Handling:**
    1.  *Regulatory & Safety Status:* Server-Authoritative (if an admin closed a ticket on the server, local mobile device state is updated) [INFERENCE].
    2.  *Field Inspection Logs:* Client-Append (new field notes logged offline are never overwritten by server state; they are appended with a unique UUID) [INFERENCE].
*   **Bandwidth Reduction:**
    *   **Image Compression:** Photos captured in the field are downsampled on-device to WebP format (80% quality reduction, max 1080p resolution), reducing payload size from ~5MB to ~250KB per photo [INFERENCE].
    *   **Payload Compression:** Sync payloads are compressed using Gzip before transfer over HTTP/2 [INFERENCE].
    *   **Offline UX:** Visual badge displays connectivity status (Green = Online, Amber = Offline - Queued Records). Inspector can continue taking logs seamlessly without blocking UI dialogs [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

*   **Personal & Location Data Protection:** Worker biometric/attendance logs and inspector real-time GPS locations are classified as Sensitive Personal Data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 [RESEARCHED]. Precise GPS tracking is active *only* when an inspector explicitly initiates a field inspection report [INFERENCE].
*   **Authentication & Access Control:**
    *   OAuth2 with JWT (JSON Web Tokens) enforcing strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) [INFERENCE].
    *   Roles: `FIELD_INSPECTOR`, `MINE_MANAGER`, `SUBSIDIARY_HQ_ADMIN`, `REGULATORY_AUDITOR` [INFERENCE].
    *   Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) required for high-privilege corporate and regulatory logins [INFERENCE].
*   **Encryption Standards:** AES-256 encryption at rest for database tables and local mobile SQLite files; TLS 1.3 protocol for all data in transit [INFERENCE].
*   **Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Audit Ledger:** Every statutory submission, violation edit, or approval triggers a SHA-256 cryptographic hash computation:
    $$\text{Current Hash} = \text{SHA-256}(\text{Previous Hash} + \text{Payload JSON} + \text{User ID} + \text{Timestamp})$$
    Any attempt to modify past statutory records in the database invalidates the hash chain, immediately alerting system auditors [INFERENCE].
*   **Regulatory & Legal Safeguard:** The platform explicitly functions as an enterprise decision-support and workflow automation tool [INFERENCE]. Final legal responsibility for mine safety remains with the certified Mine Manager as mandated under Regulation 27 of the Coal Mines Regulations 2017 [RESEARCHED].
