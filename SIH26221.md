## 1. Executive Snapshot

This dossier presents a comprehensive technical research, architecture, and deployment strategy for **SIH26221**, a hardware-driven student innovation challenge under the Smart India Hackathon [PS]. The Indian tourism and hospitality sector—comprising budget hotels, homestays, transport hubs, and heritage sites—faces structural challenges including high energy overheads in accommodations [KNOWN], unmonitored footfall at destinations [RESEARCHED], fragmented guest safety mechanisms [KNOWN], and lack of low-cost digital touchpoints [INFERENCE]. To solve these systemic issues without requiring capital-intensive infrastructure overhauls [INFERENCE], we propose **"SmartYatra IoT": An Integrated Smart Hospitality & Tourist Safety Hardware Platform**. The system combines a low-cost IoT Room Energy & Security Controller (for hotels/homestays), an ultra-low-power BLE/LoRa Tourist Safety & Tracking Badge (for travel/destination safety), and an Edge AI Gateway (for heritage site footfall and environmental monitoring) [INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

* **Geography & Topography**: India’s tourist locations range from high-altitude Himalayan trails (low temperature, weak cellular coverage) to humid coastal areas (salt corrosion risk for electronics) [KNOWN].
* **Connectivity Limitations**: Many tier-2/3 heritage sites and trekking routes suffer from intermittent 2G/3G or complete cellular blackouts [KNOWN]. Hardware devices must operate offline or over sub-GHz LoRa radio networks [INFERENCE].
* **Power Grid Fluctuation**: Rural homestays and budget hotels encounter frequent power surges and brownouts [KNOWN]. Hardware power supplies must include surge protection, step-down voltage regulators (e.g., LM2596), and Li-ion backup batteries [INFERENCE].
* **Budget & Cost Sensitivity**: Indian budget hotels operate on slim margins ($15–$30/night) [KNOWN]. The hardware bill of materials (BOM) per room must remain below ₹1,800 ($22) to ensure rapid adoption [ASSUMPTION].
* **Digital Literacy & Language Diversity**: Hotel housekeeping staff and local transport operators possess varied literacy levels [KNOWN]. Hardware controls must feature tactile buttons, physical LEDs, and clear visual indicators rather than complex screen menus [INFERENCE].
* **Hardware Availability during Hackathon**: Specialty chips (e.g., custom ASIC chips) are unavailable on short notice [KNOWN]. Teams must rely on off-the-shelf microcontrollers like ESP32, ESP8266, STM32, Arduino, and Raspberry Pi [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

We select **Approach 3: Modular IoT Ecosystem (Retrofit Smart Room Controller + LoRa Tourist Safety Badge + Edge AI Gateway)** [INFERENCE].

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected

1. **Approach 1 (Pure Software)** was rejected because SIH26221 is explicitly categorized under the **Hardware Track** [PS]. A pure software entry risks disqualification or severe point deduction during physical booth inspection by SIH hardware evaluation committees [INFERENCE].
2. **Approach 2 (Industrial PLC / High-Cost Infrastructure)** was rejected because 80%+ of Indian tourism accommodations are unorganized budget hotels, homestays, and guesthouses registered under the NIDHI initiative [RESEARCHED]. A high-cost industrial solution fails the fundamental economic feasibility test for Indian mass adoption [INFERENCE].

### Why Approach 3 Wins

* **Multi-Sector Coverage**: Fulfills all explicit mandates of "hotels, travel and others" in one cohesive hardware-software architecture [PS, INFERENCE].
* **Capital-Light Retrofit Design**: The hotel controller plugs parallel to existing switchboards without breaking walls or rewiring, providing ROI within 4–6 months through power savings [INFERENCE].
* **Offline Resiliency**: The safety badge uses 865 MHz LoRa radio (license-free in India) [KNOWN], operating seamlessly in mountain valleys and jungle trails without cellular reception [INFERENCE].
* **Demonstrability**: A 4-6 student team can easily assemble physical microcontrollers, OLED displays, relays, and sensors on custom acrylic test rigs to impress SIH judges [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative

1. **Hotel Room Automation Flow**:
   * When a guest approaches the room, they scan the RFID tag or trigger the NFC/BLE unlock command via the mobile web app.
   * The ESP32 MCU verifies the token locally or via MQTT query to the backend API, actuating a 12V solenoid door lock via Relay 1 [INFERENCE].
   * Upon entry, the PIR Motion Sensor detects presence, and Relay 2/3 energizes room lighting and AC circuits [INFERENCE].
   * The ACS712 Current Sensor continuously samples real-time current consumption, streaming payload `{room_id: 102, current_a: 4.2, occupied: true}` to the MQTT Broker every 10 seconds [INFERENCE].
   * If the guest leaves the room and no motion is detected for 5 minutes (configurable), the ESP32 automatically de-energizes heavy appliances, keeping essential outlets active, cutting electricity waste [INFERENCE].

2. **Tourist Safety & Emergency SOS Flow**:
   * The tourist wears the compact LoRa Safety Badge on a lanyard during trekking or site visits [INFERENCE].
   * In an emergency, pressing the SOS button triggers an immediate high-priority LoRa packet containing Device ID, GPS latitude, longitude, and battery status [INFERENCE].
   * Nearby LoRa Receiver Gateways (or edge nodes deployed at local shops/checkpoints) capture the 865 MHz packet and forward it via MQTT to the Cloud Gateway [INFERENCE].
   * The backend instantly pushes SMS alerts to local authorities and updates the hotel front-desk panel with precise GPS coordinates [INFERENCE].

3. **Heritage Site Crowd & Environmental Flow**:
   * The Raspberry Pi 4 Edge Gateway mounted at site entry points runs an optimized MobileNet-SSD / YOLOv8-nano model on incoming camera frames [INFERENCE].
   * The software computes the current head count per minute, converting raw video locally into a numeric payload `{site_id: "TAJ_WEST_GATE", crowd_count: 84, AQI: 112}` [INFERENCE].
   * Raw video frames are immediately discarded from memory, satisfying strict privacy standards [INFERENCE].
   * Telemetry is uploaded to the central server, driving a public heat map on the Tourist Mobile App to warn travelers of overcrowded conditions [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Component 1: Edge Crowd Density & Footfall Counter

1. **Exact Problem AI Solves**: Replaces manual gate counting with automated optical head/person detection to track site capacity [INFERENCE].
2. **Input**: Low-resolution RGB video stream (640x480 at 10 fps) from USB camera [INFERENCE].
3. **Output**: Integer count of individuals currently present in the camera field of view [INFERENCE].
4. **Why Simpler Rules Fail**: Ultrasonic or single IR break-beam sensors fail in crowded wide pathways where multiple people walk side-by-side [INFERENCE].
5. **Data Required**: Pre-trained COCO dataset weights for person class detection (YOLOv8-nano / MobileNet-SSD) [KNOWN].
6. **Evaluation Metric**: Mean Absolute Error (MAE) compared to manual ground-truth tally [INFERENCE].
7. **Failure Handling**: If camera lens is obstructed or frame drops occur, the gateway falls back to IR break-beam estimation and flags an maintenance alert [INFERENCE].
8. **Training**: None required on-site; uses pre-trained open weights [INFERENCE].
9. **Fine-tuning**: Optional quantization to TensorRT/TFLite for Raspberry Pi execution [INFERENCE].
10. **RAG / Vision / Speech / Forecasting**:
    * RAG: No
    * Computer Vision: **Yes** (Lightweight object detection) [INFERENCE]
    * Speech: No
    * Forecasting: No
11. **Human Oversight**: Site supervisors can manually recalibrate gate counters from the admin dashboard [INFERENCE].

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used

* **Room Occupancy & Power Switching**: Relying on AI/Camera vision inside hotel rooms is a catastrophic invasion of privacy and computationally wasteful [INFERENCE]. **Rule-based hardware logic** combining PIR motion thresholds, door magnetic contacts, and current sensors is 100% deterministic, private, and cost-effective [INFERENCE].
* **SOS Panic Triggering**: Emergency triggers MUST be a physical, immediate hardwired button interrupt on the microcontroller, not a voice-activated or gesture AI model prone to false negatives [INFERENCE].

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Database Schema (Key Entities)

```sql
-- Hotels Table
CREATE TABLE hotels (
    hotel_id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,

-- Telemetry Time-Series Table
CREATE TABLE room_telemetry (
    time TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL,

SELECT create_hypertable('room_telemetry', 'time');

-- Tourist SOS Alerts Table

    alert_id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,

    timestamp TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW(),

    longitude NUMERIC(10,7),
```

### Offline & Synchronization Strategy

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       LOCAL HARDWARE EDGE NODE                        |
|                                                                       |
| +---------------------+   Flash/SPIFFS Storage   +------------------+ |
| | Sensor Readings     | ----> [Local Ring Buff] ---->| Wi-Fi Available? | |
| | (Room / Badge / SOS)|                            +--------+---------+ |
| +---------------------+                                     |         |
+-------------------------------------------------------------|---------+
                                                              |
                                                    +---------+---------+
                                                    |                   |
                                                 NO |                   | YES
                                                    v                   v
                                         +------------------+  +----------------+
                                         | Store in Flash   |  | Flush MQTT     |
                                         | SPIFFS Log File  |  | Queue to Cloud |
                                         +------------------+  +----------------+
```

1. **Local Edge Buffering**: When internet connectivity drops, the ESP32 room controller stores telemetry logs in its internal non-volatile SPIFFS/LittleFS flash memory ring buffer (up to 512 KB) [INFERENCE].
2. **Exponential Backoff Synchronization**: Once Wi-Fi reconnects, the node flushes queued JSON packets to the MQTT broker using QoS 1 (At least once delivery), verifying server acknowledgments [INFERENCE].
3. **Conflict Resolution**: Timestamps generated by local RTC (Real-Time Clock) chips on the hardware nodes serve as the source of truth for ordering buffered telemetry events [INFERENCE].
4. **Bandwidth Minimization**: Data payload formats are compressed into compact JSON string or protocol buffer formats (e.g., `{"r":102,"i":2.1,"p":1}`). Average payload size is under 60 bytes [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

* **Personal & Location Data Protection**:
  * SOS location tracking is activated ONLY when the user physically triggers the panic button on the badge or opts into continuous trekking mode on the app [INFERENCE].
  * Raw video feeds from edge footfall cameras are processed purely in volatile RAM on the Raspberry Pi; zero images or video streams are stored or transmitted over the network [INFERENCE].
