## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE) operates the National Helpline Against Atrocities (NHAA - 14566) and an Integrated Portal to support Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) victims of caste-based violence, harassment, and severe atrocities [PS]. Currently, call center operators and portal intake forms lack a real-time, automated psychological assessment mechanism to gauge the acute trauma, fear, and suicide risk of victims upon initial contact [PS]. This proposed system delivers a multimodal AI engine—integrating voice acoustic analytics (pitch, pause, jitter, shimmer), multilingual Indic Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Emotion AI—to compute a normalized **Stress Vulnerability Index (SVI)** [PS]. The platform categorizes caller risk into four actionable tiers (Low, Moderate, High, Critical) [PS] and automatically triggers protocol-driven referrals for counseling, emergency police intervention, legal aid, and witness protection [PS]. Designed for real-time PSTN telephony streams, IVRS, web portals, and mobile apps [PS], the system enforces strict privacy and consent controls under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 [RESEARCHED].

### Fundamental System Objective
To provide real-time, multilingual, multimodal acoustic and narrative trauma triage during first contact across all NHAA (14566) channels, quantifying victim vulnerability into a normalized Stress Vulnerability Index (SVI) to automatically trigger immediate life-saving interventions and administrative prioritization [PS].

## 2. Problem Deconstruction

### Atomic Requirement Extraction

* **Req-1 (Multi-Channel Ingestion):** The module must assess victims/complainants interacting through NHAA toll-free voice calls (14566), Integrated Web Portal, Chatbot, Mobile Application, IVRS, or approved digital interfaces [PS].
  * *Interpretation:* System must ingest both streaming PSTN/VoIP audio (8kHz telephony audio) and asynchronous text inputs (chats, web complaints) [INFERENCE].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Requires a dual-pipeline engine: low-latency speech stream processing for audio and high-throughput NLP pipeline for text [INFERENCE].

* **Req-2 (Multimodal Voice Analytics):** Analyse voice interactions, speech patterns, pauses, pitch variation, emotional indicators, and textual narratives [PS].
  * *Interpretation:* Assessment cannot rely solely on transcribed text; acoustic prosodic features (fundamental frequency $F_0$, pause durations, speech rate, jitter, shimmer) must be extracted directly from audio [RESEARCHED].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Audio processing pipeline must extract low-level acoustic descriptors (LLDs) via DSP/openSMILE before or alongside Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) [RESEARCHED].

* **Req-3 (Core AI Technologies):** Use Natural Language Processing (NLP), Speech Analytics, and Emotion AI to identify signs of trauma and distress [PS].
  * *Interpretation:* Integration of multilingual Speech-to-Text (ASR), acoustic emotion classification models, and Indic language transformer models [INFERENCE].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Deploy fine-tuned IndicBERT/mBERT models combined with audio classification networks (e.g., Wav2Vec2/HuBERT fine-tuned on emotion datasets) [RESEARCHED].

* **Req-4 (Stress Vulnerability Index - SVI):** Generate a Stress Vulnerability Index (SVI) on a predefined scale [PS].
  * *Interpretation:* Standardized quantitative score (e.g., 0 to 100) combining acoustic stress scores, sentiment/emotion confidence, and legal atrocity severity parameters [INFERENCE].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Mathematical formula mapping acoustic energy, lexical trauma flags, and contextual threat vectors into a calibrated 0-100 SVI score [INFERENCE].

* **Req-5 (Four-Tier Risk Categorization):** Categorize victims into Low, Moderate, High, and Critical Risk categories [PS].
  * *Interpretation:* Discrete risk boundaries that dictate response SLAs and system dispatch behavior [INFERENCE].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Configurable threshold rules (e.g., SVI < 30: Low, 30-55: Moderate, 55-80: High, >80: Critical) driving automated dispatch workflows [INFERENCE].

* **Req-6 (Clinical & Trauma Indicator Detection):** Detect indicators of severe trauma, fear, depression, suicidal ideation, intimidation, social isolation, and extreme vulnerability [PS].
  * *Interpretation:* Specific multi-label classification models targeted at detecting high-risk clinical states and active safety threats [INFERENCE].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Specialized fine-tuned intent and safety models trained on psychiatric crisis lexicons and atrocity threat keywords [INFERENCE].

* **Req-7 (Automated Inter-Agency Recommendation & Dispatch):** Automatically recommend counseling, legal aid, medical assistance, police intervention, witness protection, or emergency support based on risk level [PS].
  * *Interpretation:* Real-time decision matrix linking risk levels and detected needs to specific administrative workflows [INFERENCE].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Rule-based dispatch routing engine connecting to District Magistrates, Superintendent of Police (SP), District Legal Services Authority (DLSA), and Tele-MANAS/mental health rosters [RESEARCHED].

* **Req-8 (Multilingual & Dialect Support):** Support multilingual interactions, including major Indian languages and dialects [PS].
  * *Interpretation:* Must handle code-mixed speech (e.g., Hinglish, Tanglish) and regional Indic dialects spoken by SC/ST populations across rural India [RESEARCHED].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Integration with open-source Indic speech-to-text models (e.g., AI4Bharat IndicWhisper/IndicVoices) and fine-tuned multilingual embeddings [RESEARCHED].

* **Req-9 (Ethical AI, Privacy & Informed Consent):** Maintain privacy, informed consent, confidentiality, and ethical AI standards [PS].
  * *Interpretation:* Full compliance with DPDP Act 2023, requiring explicit caller consent prior to biometric audio analysis, zero unauthorized recording storage, and strict role-based access [RESEARCHED].
  * *Implementation Implication:* Dynamic IVR consent prompt, automated PII redaction on voice recordings, end-to-end encryption, and human-in-the-loop validation for all Critical risk automated actions [INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

* **Telephony & Audio Quality Constraints:** Standard Indian PSTN/GSM voice calls operate at $8\text{ kHz}$ sampling rates using lossy codecs (AMR / G.711) [RESEARCHED]. High-frequency acoustic signals (above $4\text{ kHz}$) are truncated, degrading standard audio emotion models trained on $16\text{ kHz}$ studio audio (e.g., RAVDESS) [RESEARCHED]. Models must be robust to $8\text{ kHz}$ bandpass filtering and mobile channel distortion [INFERENCE].
* **Acoustic Background Noise:** Rural complainants frequently call from noisy outdoor environments, moving transport, or under chaotic field conditions with wind, animal, and crowd noise [INFERENCE]. Real-time noise suppression (RNNoise / DeepFilterNet) is required prior to acoustic feature extraction [INFERENCE].
* **Linguistic Diversity & Code-Mixing:** SC/ST populations across India speak diverse regional dialects (e.g., Gondi, Bhili, Santhali, Bhojpuri, Bundelkhandi) and frequently code-mix with regional state languages or Hindi [RESEARCHED]. Off-the-shelf English/Hindi NLP models fail on dialectical nuance and local slang describing violence/threats [RESEARCHED].
* **Low-Bandwidth Web/App Access:** Complainants in remote rural belts operate on 2G/3G networks or low-cost Android devices [INFERENCE]. Mobile app and web portal components must have payload sizes $<2\text{ MB}$ and function in degraded connectivity modes [INFERENCE].
* **Legal & Stigma Realities:** SC/ST victims facing extreme intimidation or social boycott may speak in hushed tones, display involuntary pauses, or hesitate due to fear of reprisal [PS, INFERENCE]. System must explicitly distinguish between vocal suppression caused by fear versus poor audio quality [INFERENCE].
* **Data Privacy Regulations:** Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, processing voice biometrics and mental health status requires explicit, granular consent, strict access boundaries, and statutory data retention limits [RESEARCHED].

## 8. Existing Solutions & Gap Analysis

### 1. Existing NHAA 14566 Call Center & Web Portal
* **Description:** Standard government helpline portal and IVRS launched by MoSJE in Dec 2021 for registering PoA Act grievances [RESEARCHED].
* **Strengths:** 24/7 availability, toll-free 14566 access, docket generation, auto-routing to district officials [RESEARCHED].
* **Weaknesses:** Completely manual intake. Operators manually type summaries. No real-time acoustic or emotional stress detection; cannot detect silent panic, depression, or suicidal ideation objectively [INFERENCE].
* **Relevance:** Direct target deployment baseline for this SIH problem [PS].
* **Gap Remaining:** Lacks automated AI trauma triage, real-time voice emotion analytics, and quantitative SVI risk scoring [PS].
* **Source & Confidence:** [PIB Press Release 2021](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1780979) [RESEARCHED].

### 2. Tele-MANAS (Mental Health Helpline - 14416)
* **Description:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare's 24/7 tele-mental health helpline across India [RESEARCHED].
* **Strengths:** Qualified clinical counselors, multi-tier referral system for mental health crisis [RESEARCHED].
* **Weaknesses:** Requires human call transfers; does not perform automated real-time voice acoustic risk indexing during live call intake [INFERENCE]. Not integrated with PoA Act legal/police dispatch pipelines [INFERENCE].
* **Relevance:** Potential destination service for counseling referrals recommended by our system [PS, INFERENCE].
* **Gap Remaining:** Does not specialize in caste-atrocity legal trauma or real-time voice biometrics triage [INFERENCE].
* **Source & Confidence:** [Tele-MANAS Portal](https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in/) [KNOWN].

### 3. Commercial Enterprise Call Center Emotion AI (e.g., Genesys / Cogito)
* **Description:** AI platforms for corporate contact centers analyzing agent-customer vocal pitch, tone, and empathy [RESEARCHED].
* **Strengths:** High real-time performance on PSTN streams, agent guidance prompts [RESEARCHED].
* **Weaknesses:** Built for commercial sales/customer support in English/Western accents. Blind to Indic regional dialects, caste-based atrocity context, and clinical trauma/suicide detection [INFERENCE]. Prohibitively expensive proprietary licensing [INFERENCE].
* **Relevance:** Proves architectural feasibility of real-time telephony speech analytics [RESEARCHED].
* **Gap Remaining:** Lack of Indic language support, zero legal alignment with PoA Act, non-compliant with Indian public sector cost and open-source constraints [INFERENCE].

```

| - Low Compute / Rigid              | - Deterministic Safety Rules   | - High Latency / Expensive |
+------------------------------------+--------------------------------+----------------------------+
```

### Approach 1: Pure Rule/Lexicon-Based Text & Audio Thresholding System
* **Mechanism:** Rule engine using static keyword matching for distress words and simple energy/pause duration thresholds computed via basic audio signal processing.
* **Problem Fit:** Low. Fails to capture complex emotional nuance, dialect variations, sarcastic/suppressed tones, or contextual trauma [INFERENCE].
* **Accuracy Potential:** Low ($40-55\%$ F1-score on natural speech) [INFERENCE].
* **Complexity:** Very Low.
* **Explainability:** High (100% deterministic).
* **Cost:** Very Low.
* **Scalability:** High.
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High.
* **Production Feasibility:** Low (unusable due to high false-negative rate on real callers).

### Approach 2: Multimodal Hybrid Deep Learning & Emotion AI Pipeline (RECOMMENDED)
* **Mechanism:** Dual-channel parallel processing:
  1. *Audio DSP Branch:* Real-time extraction of prosodic features (pitch $F_0$, jitter, shimmer, spectral flux, pause ratios) using openSMILE/librosa fed into a lightweight CNN-LSTM acoustic classifier [RESEARCHED].
  2. *Text NLP Branch:* Streaming ASR (IndicWhisper/Bhashini) translating speech to text, followed by an IndicBERT / XLM-RoBERTa multi-label classifier for trauma, fear, depression, and threat detection [RESEARCHED].
  3. *Composite SVI Score:* Fuses acoustic and text probabilities with legal context parameters, routed through a deterministic safety rule engine for referral dispatch [INFERENCE].
* **Problem Fit:** Excellent. Directly addresses all explicit requirements of PS26093 [PS].
* **Accuracy Potential:** High ($82-90\%$ F1-score on speech emotion & text intent) [RESEARCHED].
* **Complexity:** Moderate.
* **Explainability:** High (fused score features token-level and acoustic feature attribution) [RESEARCHED].
* **Cost:** Moderate (open-source models, runs on modest GPU/CPU hardware).
* **Scalability:** High.
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High (feasible within student hackathon scope using pre-trained open-source weights).
* **Production Feasibility:** Excellent.

### Approach 3: End-to-End Large Speech LLM Pipeline (e.g., Gemini / GPT-4o Audio Native)
* **Mechanism:** Stream live raw audio directly into a cloud-hosted multimodal audio LLM, requesting direct JSON generation of SVI scores and referral actions.
* **Problem Fit:** Moderate. Highly fluent but non-deterministic and expensive.
* **Accuracy Potential:** High text understanding, variable acoustic feature precision.
* **Complexity:** High (external API dependence, network streaming latency).
* **Explainability:** Low ("black box" generative reasoning, risk of hallucinations on critical safety dispatch) [INFERENCE].
* **Cost:** Extremely High (API cost per call minute across 600,000+ annual calls) [RESEARCHED].
* **Scalability:** Poor (rate limits, high latency $>3\text{ seconds}$).
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High for simple demo, but vulnerable to API outages.
* **Production Feasibility:** Poor (violates government data sovereignty and cost parameters).

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative
1. **Initiation & Consent:** A victim calls toll-free `14566` or accesses the mobile app [PS]. The IVRS / WebRTC gateway prompts for language selection and captures explicit audio/touchtone consent for AI processing per DPDP Act 2023 [RESEARCHED].
2. **Audio Streaming & Splitting:** Voice audio streams via SIP/WebSocket into the backend. The stream is split into $3\text{-second}$ rolling chunks [INFERENCE].
3. **Parallel Acoustic & Semantic Processing:**
   * *Acoustic Path:* `RNNoise` removes background static. `openSMILE` extracts pitch variation ($F_0$), jitter, shimmer, harmonic-to-noise ratio (HNR), and pause ratios [RESEARCHED]. The PyTorch acoustic model yields an `Acoustic_Stress_Score` ($0.0 - 1.0$).
   * *Semantic Path:* `IndicWhisper` converts audio chunks to text transcripts [RESEARCHED]. `IndicBERT` evaluates semantic embeddings, outputting classification probabilities for `Trauma`, `Fear`, `Depression`, `Intimidation`, and `Suicidal_Ideation`.
4. **SVI Computation:** The scoring engine computes the composite **SVI** ($0-100$).
$$\text{SVI} = (0.35 \times \text{Acoustic\_Score}) + (0.45 \times \text{Semantic\_Trauma\_Score}) + (0.20 \times \text{Contextual\_Threat\_Multiplier}) \times 100$$
5. **Real-Time Operator Alerting:** SVI metrics and real-time transcripts stream to the 14566 Call Operator Dashboard via WebSockets [INFERENCE]. If SVI exceeds $80$ (Critical Risk), a red alert sounds, and the call is instantly flagged for supervisor/counselor intervention [INFERENCE].
6. **Automated Inter-Agency Referral:** Based on the risk category and detected trauma flags, the engine automatically pre-populates docket referrals for Police Emergency (112), District Magistrate, DLSA Legal Aid, and Tele-MANAS Counseling [PS, RESEARCHED].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### AI Module 1: Acoustic Emotion & Stress Classifier
* **Purpose:** Detect vocal panic, pitch instability, trembling, and speech inhibition directly from audio without relying on spoken words [PS].
* **Model Type:** 1D-CNN + BiLSTM Neural Network taking 88 eGeMAPS acoustic features extracted via openSMILE [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** $3\text{-second}$ raw audio buffer ($16\text{ kHz}$ PCM WAV).
* **Output:** `Acoustic_Stress_Score` ($0.00$ to $1.00$).
* **Data Required:** RAVDESS, EMO-DB, and resampled Indic vocal stress samples [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI Required:** Hand-crafted rules cannot generalize across speaker voice variations, male/female pitch baselines, or background noise fluctuations [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric:** Macro F1-score ($>0.82$), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) on continuous valence/arousal [RESEARCHED].
* **Failure Mode:** Background wind/vehicle noise mistaken for vocal pitch turbulence.
* **Human Oversight:** Operator can manually adjust acoustic sensitivity slider on the UI if background noise is high [INFERENCE].

### AI Module 2: Multilingual Speech-to-Text Transcriber
* **Purpose:** Convert spoken regional Indian language audio into text for narrative trauma analysis [PS].
* **Model Type:** Encoder-Decoder Transformer (IndicWhisper / Bhashini ASR Pipeline) [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** $16\text{ kHz}$ streaming audio chunks.
* **Output:** Native script text transcript + translated English transcript.
* **Data Required:** AI4Bharat Kathbath / IndicVoices corpus [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI Required:** Spoken speech cannot be parsed by software without ASR transcription.
* **Evaluation Metric:** Word Error Rate (WER) ($<18\%$ across major Indic languages) [RESEARCHED].
* **Failure Mode:** Low-bandwidth phone audio cutting off words leading to missing context.
* **Human Oversight:** Real-time transcript rendered on operator screen with manual inline editing capability [INFERENCE].

### AI Module 3: Multilingual Trauma & Threat NLP Classifier
* **Purpose:** Detect explicit and implicit indicators of severe trauma, fear, depression, suicidal ideation, and intimidation in text [PS].
* **Model Type:** Fine-Tuned IndicBERT v2 Multi-Label Sequence Classifier [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** Text transcript from ASR or direct web/chatbot text entry.
* **Output:** Probability logits for 6 categories: `[Trauma, Fear, Depression, Intimidation, Social_Isolation, Suicidal_Ideation]`.
* **Data Required:** Atrocity complaint narratives, clinical mental health crisis lexicons, and synthetic PoA Act case summaries [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI Required:** Language expressions for fear and intimidation in Indic dialects are indirect and idiomatic; static keyword matching misses $>60\%$ of contextual threats [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric:** Precision-Recall AUC ($>0.88$) for Suicidal Ideation and Intimidation labels [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode:** Sarcasm or cultural idioms misclassified as active threats.
* **Human Oversight:** Critical risk triggers require operator confirmation before automated dispatch [INFERENCE].

### WHERE AI MUST NOT BE USED
1. **Legal Referral & Statutory Mapping Logic:** AI MUST NOT dynamically decide which legal sections apply or which specific police station receives a docket [INFERENCE]. This MUST be handled by a deterministic, audited lookup table based on PoA Act 1989 statutory rules and district PostGIS spatial queries to prevent hallucination and legal liability [INFERENCE].
2. **Docket ID Generation & Database Writes:** Pure deterministic database transaction logic [INFERENCE].

```sql
CREATE TABLE complainants (
    complainant_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    hashed_phone_number VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL UNIQUE,
    preferred_language VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'Hindi',
    consent_given BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT FALSE,

CREATE TABLE grievance_dockets (
    docket_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    complainant_id UUID REFERENCES complainants(complainant_id),
    channel VARCHAR(20) CHECK (channel IN ('14566_VOICE', 'PORTAL', 'CHATBOT', 'MOBILE_APP', 'IVRS')),
    incident_district VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
    state_code VARCHAR(10) NOT NULL,
    summary_text TEXT,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Real-Time SVI Assessment Logs Table
CREATE TABLE svi_assessment_logs (
    log_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    docket_id UUID REFERENCES grievance_dockets(docket_id),
    acoustic_stress_score NUMERIC(5,2),
    nlp_trauma_score NUMERIC(5,2),
    final_svi_score NUMERIC(5,2) NOT NULL,
    risk_category VARCHAR(15) CHECK (risk_category IN ('LOW', 'MODERATE', 'HIGH', 'CRITICAL')),
    detected_indicators JSONB, -- {"suicidal_ideation": true, "intimidation": false, ...}
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);
```

### Low-Network & Offline Resiliency Design
1. **Mobile App Local Queue:** The mobile app stores text narratives and locally calculated basic speech stress vectors using TensorFlow Lite on device [INFERENCE]. When connection drops, data is encrypted and queued in `AsyncStorage` / SQLite [INFERENCE].
2. **Automatic Retry with Exponential Backoff:** Once network connectivity restores, queued dockets auto-sync to the server with cryptographic checksums [INFERENCE].
3. **IVRS Fallback Mode:** If web/app server APIs experience latency or network outages, the system seamlessly downgrades callers to standard PSTN audio recording, delaying deep AI analytics to batch queue while preserving emergency call routing [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

* **DPDP Act 2023 Compliance:** Under Section 6 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, personal data processing requires explicit, unambiguous consent [RESEARCHED]. The IVRS / Web platform plays an explicit audio/text consent notice: *"This helpline uses AI to evaluate voice tone to prioritize your safety. Press 1 to consent."* [INFERENCE].
* **Voice Biometric Anonymization:** Raw audio recordings are NOT used to build permanent voice biometric profiles [INFERENCE]. Audio buffers used for feature extraction are automatically purged from memory immediately after session termination, keeping only anonymized numerical feature vectors ($F_0$, jitter values) [INFERENCE].
* **Data Encryption Standards:** All data in transit uses TLS 1.3 protocol. All stored database records, dockets, and transcripts are encrypted at rest using AES-256 [INFERENCE]. Phone numbers are salted and hashed using SHA-256 [INFERENCE].
* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Strict permissions matrix enforced via JWT tokens:
  * *Call Operators:* Access active call screen and live SVI score only.
  * *District SP / Officers:* Access dockets auto-routed to their specific geographic jurisdiction.
  * *MoSJE Admins:* Access aggregated, de-identified national trends only.
* **Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards:** Automated dispatch for High/Critical risk dockets triggers immediate SMS/email alerts to authorities, but full legal chargesheet/FIR registration requires human verification by designated district officers to avoid false-alarm legal liability [INFERENCE].
