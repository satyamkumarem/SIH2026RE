## 1. Executive Snapshot

Disasters such as flash floods, landslides, and earthquakes in India severely damage critical communication and power infrastructure, rendering cloud-dependent search-and-rescue (SAR) systems ineffective [PS, KNOWN]. Ground response teams face severe hazards from structural instability, live high-voltage power lines, and toxic leaks while trying to locate victims under intense time pressure during the golden hour of survival [PS, KNOWN]. The proposed system is an edge-native, multi-sensor autonomous search-and-rescue drone platform powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon/QRB5165 hardware running quantized deep learning containers (.dlc) via the Qualcomm Neural Processing SDK [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE]. The platform integrates dual-spectrum (RGB + Long-Wave Infrared Thermal) computer vision for human and hazard detection, visual-inertial SLAM for GPS-denied flight, and a store-and-forward mesh telemetry network that generates live geo-tagged hazard maps and prioritized rescue alerts on an offline command center dashboard without requiring internet connectivity [PS, INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1. **Connectivity Blackouts:** Disasters instantly destroy cellular towers and fiber backhaul. Cloud dependence is unacceptable [PS, KNOWN]. All core detection and navigation logic must run locally on the drone hardware [PS].
2. **GPS Degradation & Jamming:** Heavy canopy, structural collapse voids, and high-rise urban canyons cause GPS multipath interference or complete signal loss [KNOWN]. System must achieve stable hover and trajectory tracking purely through Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO) and range sensors [PS, RESEARCHED].
3. **Severe Environmental Dust & Smoke:** Optical RGB cameras fail in pitch darkness, dense fog, or heavy smoke [KNOWN]. The system mandates Long-Wave Infrared (LWIR) thermal sensing (8–14 $\mu$m) to detect human heat signatures regardless of ambient lighting [PS, RESEARCHED].
4. **Onboard Thermal & Power Envelope:** Drones have tight weight (Payload < 1.5kg) and battery constraints (Flight time 20–35 mins) [KNOWN]. High-power GPUs (e.g., >50W) drastically shorten flight time. The compute platform must operate under 15W while providing >10 TOPS of AI compute (e.g., Qualcomm QRB5165) [RESEARCHED].
5. **Operating Personnel Literacy & Field Conditions:** Rescue operators work under extreme stress in rain, mud, and heat [KNOWN]. GCS software must offer zero-click deployment, high-contrast visual UI, and offline offline-first maps (OSM/Tiles pre-cached) [INFERENCE].
6. **Indian Airspace Regulations (DGCA Compliance):** Operations require adherence to DGCA Digital Sky guidelines, including Green/Yellow/Red zone awareness and emergency kill-switch protocols, even during exempted emergency disaster responses [RESEARCHED, NEEDS VALIDATION].

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Edge-Cloud Hybrid Architecture (Video Streaming to Field GPU Edge Server)
* **Mechanism:** Drone acts as a raw sensor streamer, encoding RGB/Thermal video and transmitting streams over 5G / High-Power Wi-Fi to a heavy ground vehicle equipped with an NVIDIA RTX GPU server running AI inference [INFERENCE].
* **Pros:** Unconstrained compute power at ground station; easy to run heavy FP32 models (e.g., YOLOv11x, Segment Anything) [INFERENCE].
* **Cons:** Complete single point of failure: if RF/Wi-Fi link drops due to terrain, dust, or distance, the drone becomes completely blind and incapable of detecting victims or hazards [INFERENCE]. High bandwidth requirements (>15 Mbps) infeasible in disaster zones [KNOWN].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** Moderate | **Production Feasibility:** Low (Fails core offline requirement) [INFERENCE].

### Approach 2: Pure Onboard Edge-AI Modular Platform (Qualcomm QRB5165 / Flight RB5 + SNPE + PX4 + Mesh Radio)
* **Mechanism:** All perception, SLAM, hazard classification, geotagging, and priority scoring run locally on the drone's Qualcomm QRB5165 SoC (15 TOPS NPU, Hexagon Tensor Accelerator) [RESEARCHED]. Low-bitrate JSON alert packets and spatial coordinate vectors are broadcast over Sub-GHz RF (RFD900) to ground tactical displays. Full resolution imagery is saved to onboard NVMe storage and synced over Wi-Fi 6 upon landing/proximity [INFERENCE].
* **Pros:** 100% resilient to network blackouts [PS]; ultra-low latency (<30ms AI inference); low power consumption (~10W compute power); robust sub-GHz telemetry link range (>3km) [RESEARCHED].
* **Cons:** Model size restricted to lightweight quantized architectures (e.g., YOLOv11s INT8); requires hardware-specific model compilation (.dlc conversion) [RESEARCHED].
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High (using Qualcomm SDKs or proxy edge hardware like Snapdragon/RB5/Jetson Orin Nano simulating SNPE workflow) [RESEARCHED].
* **Production Feasibility:** High (Direct alignment with Qualcomm platform requirements) [PS, RESEARCHED].

### Approach 3: Dual-Drone Swarm Architecture (Lead Mapping Drone + Follower Search Drone)
* **Mechanism:** One drone flies high altitude for macro GPS mapping; second small drone enters collapsed structures for indoor micro SAR [INFERENCE].
* **Pros:** Specialized roles per drone; excellent coverage [INFERENCE].
* **Cons:** High system complexity; swarm coordination algorithms prone to failure in GPS-denied environments; doubles hardware cost and operational burden for hackathon team [INFERENCE].

### Data-Flow Narrative Step-by-Step

1. **Capture & Preprocessing:** Dual RGB (1080p@30fps) and LWIR Thermal (640x512@30fps) frames are captured via MIPI CSI-2 interfaces [RESEARCHED]. The Qualcomm Spectra 480 ISP performs hardware-accelerated noise reduction and contrast enhancement [RESEARCHED].
2. **Parallel Perception Execution:** Frames are fed into the SNPE C++ runtime [RESEARCHED]. The Hexagon Tensor Accelerator executes the quantized dual-head YOLOv11s `.dlc` model in under 18ms per frame [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE]. Concurrently, the Qualcomm EVA/VIO engine processes optical flow and IMU data to estimate 3D velocity and position in GPS-denied environments [RESEARCHED].
3. **Geo-Referencing & Ray-Casting:** When a survivor or hazard is detected with confidence $>0.65$, the spatial extraction node reads the camera gimbal angle $(\theta_p, \theta_y)$, drone altitude $h$, and drone WGS84/VIO coordinates $(x, y, z)$. It casts a ray from the camera optical center through the bounding box centroid to compute ground intersection coordinates $(\text{Lat}_{\text{target}}, \text{Lon}_{\text{target}})$ [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
4. **Alert Generation & Prioritization:** The Emergency Decision Engine calculates spatial proximity vectors $\vec{d} = \|\text{Pos}_{\text{victim}} - \text{Pos}_{\text{hazard}}\|$. If a victim is within $15\text{m}$ of active fire or rising floodwater, the alert priority is elevated to `CRITICAL` [INFERENCE].
5. **Store-and-Forward Telemetry Transmission:** A 64-byte binary telemetry packet (containing Timestamp, Target ID, Priority, Lat, Lon, Hazard Type) is formatted and pushed over the UART bus to the RFD900 Sub-GHz telemetry radio [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE]. Concurrently, the event record and full-resolution cropped snapshot are stored in local onboard NVMe SQLite storage [INFERENCE].
6. **Command Dashboard Visualization:** The Ground Control Station receives the RF packet via USB-to-UART, parses the GeoJSON payload, saves it to the local SpatiaLite database, updates the MapLibre GL tactical map canvas with high-contrast color markers, and triggers an audio-visual push alarm for the Incident Commander [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### 1. Victim / Human Detection Model
* **Purpose:** Detect stranded or unconscious survivors in complex debris and thermal scenes [PS].
* **Model Type:** Single-Stage Object Detector (YOLOv11s quantized to INT8 `.dlc`) [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** Synchronized dual-channel frame (RGB 640x640 + Thermal LWIR 640x640) [RESEARCHED].
* **Output:** Bounding boxes $(x, y, w, h)$, Class (`Person`), Confidence Score [0..1] [RESEARCHED].
* **Data Required:** 15,000+ annotated aerial imagery pairs from VisDrone, HIT-UAV, and SARD datasets [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI Required:** Hardcoded thresholding or classical image processing (e.g., thermal temperature cutoff) produces massive false positives due to sun-heated rocks, engines, or hot water pipes [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric:** mAP@0.5 > 85%, Recall > 90% [RESEARCHED, ASSUMPTION].
* **Failure Mode & Oversight:** False negatives (un-detected victim). Mitigation: Thermal threshold trigger highlights raw heat anomalies for manual human pilot review if confidence is marginal [INFERENCE].

### 2. Hazard Classification Model
* **Purpose:** Identify and classify environmental threats (fires, floods, structural cracks, landslides) [PS].
* **Model Type:** Lightweight Semantic Segmentation (MobileNetV3-UNet `.dlc`) [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** RGB Aerial Frame (640x640) [RESEARCHED].
* **Output:** Pixel-wise multi-class mask (`0: Background, 1: Water/Flood, 2: Fire/Smoke, 3: Debris/Blocked Path`) [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required:** xView Disaster Dataset + FLIR hazard imagery [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI Required:** Disaster hazards lack uniform shapes or colors; deep semantic features are necessary to distinguish mudslides from roads [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric:** Mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) > 0.72 [ASSUMPTION].
* **Failure Mode & Oversight:** False positive fire detection due to bright glare. Mitigation: Cross-check with thermal sensor channel; if thermal heat signature is absent, glare flag is demoted [INFERENCE].

### 3. Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO) State Estimation
* **Purpose:** Estimate drone 3D position and velocity when GPS is unavailable [PS].
* **Model Type:** Computer Vision Feature Tracking + Kalman Filtering (Qualcomm EVA / OpenVINS) [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** High-speed downward optical flow camera (120fps monocular) + 6-DOF IMU (100Hz) [RESEARCHED].
* **Output:** Relative 3D position matrix $(x, y, z, \text{roll}, \text{pitch}, \text{yaw})$ [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI / CV Required:** Mathematical integration of IMU accelerometer alone drifts uncontrollably within seconds [KNOWN]. Optical feature tracking provides essential spatial constraints [RESEARCHED].

### 4. Non-AI Decision Engine (Rule-Based Prioritization)
* **CRITICAL ARCHITECTURAL DECISION:** Generative AI / Large Language Models (LLMs) **MUST NOT** be used for flight control, hazard prioritization, or safety-critical rescue alerting [INFERENCE].
* **Why Rule-Based Over LLM:** LLMs suffer from non-deterministic output, hallucination, high computational latency (>2000ms), and massive memory overhead [KNOWN]. Priority scoring is computed using a deterministic Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) distance matrix $S = w_1 \cdot (1/d_{\text{hazard}}) + w_2 \cdot C_{\text{thermal}}$ in under $1\text{ms}$ with 100% predictable behavior [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].

### Local Storage & Bandwidth Optimization Strategy

1. **Dual-Tier Store-and-Forward Protocol:**
   * **Tier 1 (Sub-GHz RF Link - Real-Time):** High-priority alerts are packed into binary protocol buffers (Protobuf) of 64 bytes. Transmitted immediately over 900MHz RF radio. Contains target ID, coordinates, priority, and classification code [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
   * **Tier 2 (High-Bandwidth Wi-Fi 6 / 5G - Asynchronous):** Full-resolution 4K RGB stills, FLIR thermal radiometric logs, and raw h.265 video recordings are stored locally on onboard M.2 NVMe SSD (256GB). When the drone lands at the base station or passes within Wi-Fi 6 range (~100m), an automated `rsync` background daemon dumps high-res assets to the Command Center server [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].

2. **Conflict Handling & Deduplication:**
   * Spatial clustering algorithm (DBSCAN) runs on the GCS. Multiple detections of the same survivor from different flight passes within a 3-meter radius are deduplicated into a single master victim record, updating the confidence score and thermal history over time [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].

3. **Offline Map Caching:**
   * Ground Control Station utilizes pre-packaged Vector MBTiles of the target district (extracted from OpenStreetMap/Survey of India) stored locally. MapLibre GL renders vector tiles offline without sending external HTTP requests [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
