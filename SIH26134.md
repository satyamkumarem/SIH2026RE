## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
The Maharashtra State Innovation Society (MSInS), under the Department of Skills, Employment, Entrepreneurship and Innovation (SEEID), Government of Maharashtra, seeks a comprehensive Labour-Market Intelligence and Curriculum-Alignment Platform [PS]. Traditional vocational training programs often rely on static, historical occupational categories that fail to keep pace with evolving technological standards, hyper-local employer needs, and emerging industry demands [PS]. This disconnect results in course curricula, equipment, and trainer capabilities lagging behind market reality, creating a double bind: employers face acute shortages of job-ready candidates while trainees complete courses with low placement viability [PS]. 

To solve this, our proposed system—**KaushalyaDrishti (कौशल्यदृष्टी)**—is an AI-assisted, evidence-based Labour-Market Intelligence (LMI) platform. It continuously ingests real-time job market signals (job postings, employer surveys, industry consultations, sector growth metrics) and maps them against national and state qualification frameworks (NCVET QP-NOS, MSBSVET) [PS, INFERENCE]. The platform provides actionable intelligence across the skilling lifecycle: identifying skill gaps by district, recommending precise curriculum revisions, flagging obsolete or oversupplied courses, facilitating employer validation, and generating automated District Skill Development Plans (DSDP) [PS, RESEARCHED].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1. **Geographic & Regional Diversity:**
   * Maharashtra exhibits extreme economic variance between urban/industrial belts (MMR, Pune, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Nashik) and agrarian/rural regions (Vidarbha, Marathwada, Gadchiroli) [KNOWN].
   * *System Impact:* Demand models cannot use global state averages; all intelligence must be localized to district and taluka clusters [INFERENCE].

2. **Connectivity & Infrastructure in Field Settings:**
   * District Skill Officers conducting field visits to rural ITIs or remote industrial estates often face poor 3G/4G connectivity [KNOWN].
   * *System Impact:* Mobile dashboards and survey collection modules must support offline-first operation with local storage (IndexedDB / SQLite) and background sync [INFERENCE].

3. **Multilingual & Digital Literacy Requirements:**
   * Field officers, small enterprise owners (MSMEs), and trainees in rural areas prefer Marathi (`मराठी`) for operational workflows [KNOWN].
   * *System Impact:* Complete UI, survey instruments, guidance bots, and generated reports must support bilingual Marathi and English operation [INFERENCE].

4. **Fragmented Legacy Governance Infrastructure:**
   * Maharashtra’s skilling ecosystem operates across multiple platforms: MahaSwayam (`mahaswayam.gov.in`), MSSDS (`kaushalya.mahaswayam.gov.in`), DVET, and national portals like SIDH (`skillindiadigital.gov.in`) and NQR (`nqr.gov.in`) [RESEARCHED].
   * *System Impact:* The system must not try to replace these platforms immediately; it must act as an intelligence layer consuming APIs/exports and generating actionable outputs for them [INFERENCE].

5. **Computing Hardware Constraints:**
   * District administration offices and government ITIs run standard low-tier desktop computers (Intel i3/4GB RAM) with limited bandwidth [KNOWN].
   * *System Impact:* All heavy AI processing (NLP, vector search, predictive modeling) must run asynchronously in the cloud; client-side web interfaces must remain lightweight (<2MB bundle size) [INFERENCE].

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Rule-Based & Survey-Centric Aggregator
* **Mechanism:** A web portal relying on structured surveys sent to employers and manual keyword matching between job title categories and existing course names.
* **Data Requirements:** Manual employer survey submissions, static drop-down lists of courses.
* **Architecture:** Traditional 3-tier MVC architecture (Node.js, PostgreSQL, React).
* **Complexity:** Low.
* **Cost:** Very Low.
* **Explainability:** High (100% rule-driven).
* **Scalability:** Low (cannot scale to millions of unstructured job postings).
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** Very High.
* **Production Feasibility:** Poor (fails to solve the problem statement's requirement for automated signals and emerging technology trend detection).
* **Risks:** Low employer survey response rate leads to dead data pipeline.

### Approach 2: Full Deep Learning & Automated Dynamic NLP Pipeline
* **Mechanism:** An autonomous end-to-end deep learning system (fine-tuned LLMs like Llama-3/Mistral) that scrapes job portals, auto-generates new course curricula, and updates databases without human intervention.
* **Data Requirements:** Millions of scraped job descriptions, complete raw course syllabi text.
* **Architecture:** Microservices with Python, PyTorch, Vector Databases (Pinecone/Milvus), LLM inference endpoints.
* **Complexity:** Extremely High.
* **Cost:** High (GPU inference and scraping overhead).
* **Explainability:** Low ("black box" AI generating curriculum content).
* **Scalability:** High.
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** Low (risk of hallucinations, unmanageable build scope for 4-6 students).
* **Production Feasibility:** Risky (regulatory bodies like NCVET/MSBSVET will not accept unvalidated AI-generated curricula without human-in-the-loop oversight).
* **Risks:** High hallucination rate, non-explainable skill mappings, lack of regulatory acceptance.

### Approach 3: Hybrid AI Knowledge Graph & Human-in-the-Loop Intelligence Engine (RECOMMENDED)
* **Mechanism:** A multi-stage architecture where lightweight NLP models (spaCy / sentence-transformers) extract skill vectors from job postings, map them to an NCVET QP-NOS Knowledge Graph, auto-detect skill deltas, and present actionable recommendations to humans (Employers, District Skill Committees, MSBSVET boards) for validation [INFERENCE].
* **Data Requirements:** Job postings, NQR QP-NOS JSON, sample employer surveys, district training data.
* **Architecture:** React/Tailwind frontend, Node.js API Gateway, FastAPI Python AI service, PostgreSQL (relational) + pgvector / Neo4j (Knowledge Graph), automated PDF report compiler.
* **Complexity:** Medium-High.
* **Cost:** Low-Medium (efficient open-source embeddings, zero GPU requirements for inference).
* **Explainability:** High (every recommendation links directly to source job postings and specific NOS codes).
* **Scalability:** High.
* **Hackathon Feasibility:** High (clean separation of ML pipeline, backend API, and rich UI dashboards).
* **Production Feasibility:** Excellent (complies with government administrative workflows by keeping humans in the loop).
* **Risks:** Data pipeline throttling by job boards (mitigated via mock/cached feeds during demo).

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

The **Hybrid AI Knowledge Graph & Human-in-the-Loop Intelligence Engine (Approach 3)** is selected [ASSUMPTION].

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected
1. **Approach 1 (Rule-Based Aggregator) was rejected** because it cannot process unstructured job descriptions, cannot detect subtle skill shifts (e.g., transition from manual lathe to CNC or PLC programming), and fails to fulfill the explicit requirement for continuous automated signal extraction [PS, INFERENCE].
2. **Approach 2 (Full Deep Learning / Unchecked LLM) was rejected** because pure generative AI poses severe hallucination risks when drafting government-accredited educational curricula [INFERENCE]. Educational regulators (NCVET, MSBSVET) mandate strict adherence to National Occupational Standards and require auditability for every proposed curriculum change [RESEARCHED].

### Why Approach 3 Wins
* **Traceable Semantic Mapping:** By using vector embeddings anchored to official NCVET QP-NOS standards, every recommended curriculum update can explicitly cite: *"Demanded by 340 job postings in Pune district across Q3 2026, corresponding to NOS code ELE/N0102"* [INFERENCE].
* **Governance Alignment:** It bridges cutting-edge AI with bureaucratic realities by embedding an **Employer & Expert Validation Layer**, ensuring no curriculum change is finalized without human sign-off [PS, INFERENCE].
* **Actionable District Outputs:** It directly produces statutory-compliant District Skill Development Plans (DSDP) in PDF format for District Collectors [RESEARCHED].

### Data Flow Narrative
1. **Ingestion Phase:** Asynchronous scraping workers (built with Python & Celery) collect public job postings from National Career Service (NCS) and private job boards, standardizing raw text into structured JSON records containing job description, company name, location (district), and salary details [INFERENCE].
2. **AI Processing Phase:** The job descriptions are processed by the Python NLP engine. Micro-skills are extracted and converted into dense vector embeddings using `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`. These embeddings are queried against the `pgvector` database holding vector representations of all official NCVET Qualification Packs and NOS descriptions [INFERENCE].
3. **Gap & Delta Calculation:** The system compares extracted demand vectors for a specific district (e.g., Nashik) against current course syllabi offered in Nashik ITIs. Unmatched skills above a threshold demand count are tagged as "Skill Gaps" and aggregated into "Curriculum Revision Proposals" [INFERENCE].
4. **Validation Phase:** The identified gaps and proposed revisions appear on the **Employer Micro-Validation Portal**. Local employers review and vote on the relevance of these skills for their hiring needs [PS, INFERENCE].
5. **Output & Report Generation:** The validated gaps, course obsolescence flags, and employer inputs feed into the **DSDP Engine**. The engine computes recommended batch capacities, required equipment investments, and trainer ToT requirements, rendering an official District Skill Development Plan PDF and interactive dashboard for the District Collector [PS, RESEARCHED].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### AI Component 1: Job Description NLP Skill & Role Extractor
* **Purpose:** Convert unstructured text from job postings into discrete, standardized micro-skills and job roles with district location tags [PS, INFERENCE].
* **Model Type:** Named Entity Recognition (NER) + Sentence Transformer (`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`) [INFERENCE].
* **Input:** Raw job description string + Job location string [INFERENCE].
* **Output:** JSON object containing `{ extracted_role, micro_skills: [...], location_district, experience_level, NSQF_level_estimate }` [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required:** 5,000+ annotated job descriptions, skill taxonomy dictionary [ASSUMPTION].
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Regular expressions or keyword matching fail on varied phrasing (e.g., "PLC programming", "Ladder Logic", "Allen Bradley operation" all refer to Industrial Automation skills) [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric:** Precision, Recall, and F1-score on skill extraction benchmark dataset (Target: F1 > 0.82) [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode & Mitigation:** If the model misidentifies a skill, human employers during the validation phase can delete or correct extracted skills [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight:** High; extracted aggregate demand must be validated by regional employers [PS, INFERENCE].

### AI Component 2: Qualification & Curriculum Delta Semantic Matcher
* **Purpose:** Compare extracted skill vectors against official NCVET QP-NOS descriptions to identify missing curriculum competencies [PS, INFERENCE].
* **Model Type:** Vector Cosine Similarity Search via `pgvector` [INFERENCE].
* **Input:** Extracted skill vector ($V_{skill}$) vs. Array of NOS vectors ($V_{NOS}$) [INFERENCE].
* **Output:** Ranked similarity score list + Gap identification flag ($1 - \text{Similarity} > \text{Threshold}$) [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required:** NQR Qualification Pack descriptions and NOS unit text [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Manual mapping of thousands of emerging skills across 38 Sector Skill Councils is humanly impossible to keep updated [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric:** Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) and Cosine Distance Threshold calibration [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode & Mitigation:** Low similarity matches default to "Unmapped Emerging Skill" status for expert manual tagging [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight:** Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) at MSBSVET review flagged gaps before drafting formal curriculum updates [PS, RESEARCHED].

### AI Component 3: Course Health & Obsolescence Predictor
* **Purpose:** Flag courses that are oversupplied or obsolete based on declining demand trends and low placement ratios [PS].
* **Model Type:** Supervised Classification / Threshold Scoring Algorithm (LightGBM or Weighted Rule Ensemble) [INFERENCE].
* **Input:** District vacancy trend (6-month slope), VTP seat enrollment rate, 60-day placement rate, average wage growth [INFERENCE].
* **Output:** Course Health Score (0–100) + Status Tag (`HEALTHY`, `OVERSUPPLIED`, `OBSOLETE_REVISION_REQUIRED`) [INFERENCE].
* **Data Required:** Historical district course enrollment and placement logs [INFERENCE].
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Rule-based thresholds can be rigid; machine learning models weigh compound multi-variable relationships (e.g., high enrollment + zero local vacancies = high risk) [INFERENCE].
* **Evaluation Metric:** ROC-AUC and Precision at Top-K risk predictions [INFERENCE].
* **Failure Mode & Mitigation:** False positive flagging triggers a mandatory manual review by the District Skill Committee prior to course suspension [INFERENCE].
* **Human Oversight:** Final authority rests entirely with the District Collector and MSSDS [RESEARCHED].

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
1. **Final Curriculum Approval:** AI must NEVER automatically push syllabus changes to live training centers; approval requires formal MSBSVET/NCVET governance sign-off [RESEARCHED].
2. **Budget Allocation & Equipment Sanctions:** Financial disbursements for training infrastructure must be governed by statutory financial rules, not automated AI model outputs [INFERENCE].

   * Every automated curriculum revision proposal maintains an immutable audit log storing: (a) Raw job postings triggering the recommendation, (b) AI semantic similarity score, (c) Employer validation signatures, and (d) Final MSBSVET officer approval timestamp [INFERENCE].
