## 1. Executive Snapshot

Indian Railways handles over 23 million daily passengers across 7,300+ stations [KNOWN]. However, the vast rail network is exploited for illicit drug trafficking—accounting for 15–20% of India's illegal drug movement [PS]—and faces severe security threats from concealed explosives and improvised explosive devices (IEDs) [PS]. In 2025 alone, the Railway Protection Force (RPF) intercepted narcotics worth approximately ₹300 Crores across more than 2,100 instances [PS]. Current security operations lack dedicated mobile quadruped platforms and integrated handheld detection hardware for non-intrusive, real-time field identification of CBRNE/narcotics threats in GPS-denied, crowded, or hazardous environments [PS]. This dossier presents an edge-native, multi-sensor defense ecosystem combining an autonomous/semi-autonomous quadruped surveillance robot with a ruggedized, offline-first handheld dual-mode spectrometer device. Powered by onboard edge AI, sensor fusion (LiDAR, Thermal, Optical, Electronic Nose/IMS), and a cryptographically secure, tamper-proof blockchain evidence log compliant with the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023 [RESEARCHED], this architecture provides real-time alerts and actionable threat intelligence for RPF field commanders.

*   **[PS-07] Quadruped Payload & Operational Capabilities:**
    *   *a. Underframe Inspection:* Visual and thermal inspection of coach/wagon under-carriages.
    *   *b. Surveillance & Patrolling:* Yard surveillance, platform patrolling, and night patrolling.

    *   *e. Thermal & Video Analytics:* Dual optical and thermal imaging with real-time video streaming.

*   **[PS-09] Handheld Device Specifications:**
    *   *a. Form Factor & Ergonomics:* Lightweight, ruggedized, easy to operate by RPF personnel with minimal training.
    *   *b. Screening Capability:* Rapid, non-intrusive screening in crowded railway environments.
    *   *c. Operational Connectivity:* Offline operation capability with local encrypted storage and automatic synchronization upon network restoration.
    *   *d. Multi-Modal Alerting:* Automated real-time alerts via sound (audible buzzer), visual indicators (LEDs/Screen), and tactile vibration.
    *   *e. Metadata Tagging:* Automatic timestamping, event logging, and GPS/cell-tower location tagging.
    *   *f. Usability & Integration:* Multilingual user interface and integration with centralized Railway monitoring systems/RPF control rooms.

### Inferred & Derived Requirements [INFERENCE]

*   **[INF-01] Cryptographic Chain of Custody (Theme Alignment):** Because the theme is "Blockchain & Cybersecurity", all detection alerts, sensor spectra, raw images, and GPS/time logs must be hashed and recorded on an immutable ledger to satisfy Section 57/63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023 for court admissibility [INFERENCE].
*   **[INF-02] Sensor Complementarity:** No single sensor detects both bulk concealed substances and airborne trace vapors. The system must combine spectroscopic optical sensing (Raman/NIR) with vapor/particulate sniffing (IMS/E-Nose) [INFERENCE].
*   **[INF-03] Edge AI Processing:** Real-time feedback (<3 seconds) in offline or GPS-denied environments mandates that AI model inferencing (spectral matching, object detection, face matching) run locally on onboard edge compute modules (e.g., NVIDIA Jetson Orin) [INFERENCE].

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

|  [Dense Crowds & RF Interference] --> Requires Discreet Haptics & Local Edge AI   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1.  **GPS Denial & Metallic Shielding:** Train coach interiors (Faraday cage effect), covered platforms, subterranean yards, and tunnels block GNSS signals. Navigation and positioning must rely on LiDAR SLAM, visual odometry, and Wi-Fi/BLE beacon RSSI positioning [INFERENCE].
2.  **Climatic & Environmental Extremes:** Operating temperatures across India range from -10°C (Kashmir rail) to +55°C (Rajasthan yards). Environments present high humidity (coastal zones), fine brake dust, coal particles, and flying ballast debris [KNOWN]. Hardware requires IP67 ingress protection and active thermal management [INFERENCE].
3.  **Connectivity Intermittency:** Mobile networks drop inside coaches, remote freight corridors, and underground stations. All primary alert processing and model inference MUST execute locally without reliance on cloud APIs [INFERENCE].
4.  **Physical Terrain Constraints:** Railway tracks, ballast beds (coarse gravel), high platform gaps (300–400mm), narrow coach aisles (600mm width), and steep coach steps require quadruped legged locomotion rather than wheeled rovers [RESEARCHED].
5.  **User Digital Literacy:** RPF personnel vary in technical familiarity. Operating interfaces must use intuitive single-button workflows, red/green visual alerts, haptic feedback, and localized vernacular audio prompts [INFERENCE].

### Approach 1: Teleoperated Quadruped + Standalone Optical Handheld (Low Integration)
*   **Mechanism:** Quadruped controlled manually via RC joystick with standard RGB camera. Handheld device uses basic optical sensor for simple colorimetric/spectral lookup.
*   **Pros:** Easy to assemble during a short timeframe; low initial software complexity.
*   **Cons:** Fails requirement for autonomous navigation [PS]; fails in low-light/hazardous environments [PS]; lacks centralized automated alert logging [PS].
*   **Verdict:** **Rejected.** Fails core autonomy and integration requirements [INFERENCE].

### Approach 2: Cloud-Centric Autonomous Quadruped + IoT Handheld
*   **Mechanism:** Onboard sensors stream raw point clouds and video directly to AWS/GCP cloud servers where heavy AI models (YOLO, FaceNet) process images and return alerts to handheld devices.
*   **Pros:** Offloads computation from edge devices; enables large AI model deployment.
*   **Cons:** Completely fails inside subterranean tunnels, metallic train coaches, and remote yards with zero cellular connectivity [INFERENCE]; high cloud egress bandwidth cost [INFERENCE].
*   **Verdict:** **Rejected.** Unusable under Indian Railways field connectivity constraints [INFERENCE].

### Approach 3 (Selected): Edge-Native Hybrid System with Multi-Sensor Fusion & Decentralized Cryptographic Logging
*   **Mechanism:** Autonomous Quadruped utilizes onboard 3D LiDAR SLAM, Thermal/Optical camera payload, and E-Nose/Miniature IMS sniffing array running local Jetson Orin Nano edge inference. Handheld device combines a 1064nm NIR/Raman micro-spectrometer with PID vapor sensor, running local spectral chemometrics. Both devices sync locally over BLE/Wi-Fi Mesh and commit cryptographically signed evidence logs to an append-only hash chain.
*   **Pros:** 100% offline-capable; fully autonomous navigation in GPS-denied zones [PS]; robust against multi-environmental hazards [PS]; legally compliant evidence logging [RESEARCHED].
*   **Cons:** Higher firmware and embedded system integration complexity.
*   **Verdict:** **Selected.** Fits all explicit requirements and field operational realities [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

Approach 3 is selected because it directly satisfies every explicit mandate of PS SIH26026 while respecting the field constraints of Indian Railways.

### Key Justifications
1.  **Edge Independence:** Railway coaches act as Faraday cages that disrupt cellular signals. Placing the AI inference engine (spectral matching and YOLOv8 object/face detection) directly on the onboard NVIDIA Jetson edge compute hardware guarantees sub-second threat detection independent of external network availability [INFERENCE].
2.  **Locomotion Superiority:** A quadruped form factor equipped with 3D LiDAR SLAM handles high platform steps, loose ballast stone terrain, and narrow coach corridors where wheeled or tracked rovers fail [RESEARCHED].
3.  **Dual-Sensor Synergy:** Optical Raman/NIR spectroscopy detects bulk concealed substances through packaging, while Ion Mobility / E-Nose gas sensors detect airborne trace micro-vapors of volatile explosives (e.g., TATP, Gunpowder) and narcotics [RESEARCHED].
4.  **Legal Chain of Custody:** Implementing SHA-256 cryptographic signing and local Hyperledger Fabric / local hash chaining on all detection logs directly addresses the theme ("Blockchain & Cybersecurity") and satisfies Indian legal mandates under BSA 2023 [RESEARCHED].

### Detailed Data Flow Narrative

1.  **Sensing & Acquisition:**
    *   The quadruped platform patrols a railway yard or coach corridor. Its 3D LiDAR continuously streams 100,000 points/sec to the ROS2 navigation stack, while its E-Nose micro-pump continuously samples airborne particulate vapors.
    *   Simultaneously, an RPF officer presses the nose cone of the handheld device against a suspicious parcel inside a coach.
2.  **Onboard Pre-processing & Feature Extraction:**
    *   Handheld optical spectral readings are normalized, background-subtracted, and passed through a Savitzky-Golay filter to isolate specific chemical absorbance peaks.
    *   Quadruped visual frames are fed into a low-light enhancement pipeline before reaching the object/face detection models.
3.  **Local AI Inference:**
    *   The 1D-CNN spectral classification model running on the handheld's local microcontroller or Jetson node compares the sample curve against 150+ pre-compiled reference spectra (RDX, TNT, Heroin, Cocaine, etc.).
    *   If correlation exceeds 88%, a POSITIVE THREAT MATCH event is generated in <1.5 seconds.
4.  **Multi-Modal Threat Alerting:**
    *   Handheld triggers instant tactile vibration (haptic motor), flashes RED LEDs, and displays substance identity and confidence percentage on the touchscreen.
    *   Quadruped activates its local visual alert beacon and relays the alert packet over BLE/Wi-Fi Mesh to the officer's handheld device.
5.  **Cryptographic Evidence Logging & Storage:**
    *   The event payload (Timestamp, GNSS/Cell-ID, Sensor Spectral Fingerprint, Officer ID, Threat Class) is hashed using SHA-256, cryptographically signed with the device's private key (Ed25519), and written to the local encrypted SQLite database.
6.  **Central Synchronization & Dashboard Display:**
    *   When the devices enter range of a station Wi-Fi access point or 4G/5G signal, queued evidence packets automatically push via MQTT over TLS to the Central RPF Security Dashboard, appending the transaction to the permissioned blockchain ledger.

## 13. AI/ML Justification

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    AI/ML MODEL PIPELINE SUMMARY                                    |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [Spectra Input] --> [Savitzky-Golay Filter] --> [1D-CNN Model] --> Substance ID + Confidence %   |
|  [LiDAR/IMU]     --> [Fast-LIO / Nav2 Stack] --> [Real-time SLAM] --> Autonomous Path Velocity   |
|  [RGB Camera]    --> [YOLOv8-Face Model]     --> [ArcFace Embedding]--> Watch-list Match Alert     |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1. Spectral Chemical Identification Model
*   **Purpose:** Classify unknown chemical absorbance/reflectance curves into target narcotics or explosive categories.
*   **Model Type:** 1D Convolutional Neural Network (1D-CNN) / Random Forest Classifier ensemble.
*   **Input:** Vector of 18 to 256 spectral intensity points across UV-Vis-NIR frequencies.
*   **Output:** Classified substance label (e.g., "TNT", "Heroin", "Clear") + Confidence score (0.00 – 1.00).
*   **Data Required:** 5,000+ augmented spectral curves across target substances, cutting agents, and ambient background noise.
*   **Why AI is Necessary:** Standard direct database lookup fails when substances are mixed with cutting agents (e.g., Paracetamol in Heroin) or dirty packaging material.
*   **Evaluation Metric:** F1-Score (>0.95), Precision for explosives (>0.99 to minimize false negatives).
*   **Failure Mode Handling:** If confidence is between 50%–80%, alert outputs "Inconclusive - Manual Swab Verification Required".
*   **Human Oversight:** High-confidence positive matches require physical verification by RPF Bomb Disposal or Forensic Officers before public evacuation.

### 2. Autonomous Navigation & Obstacle Avoidance
*   **Purpose:** Enable quadruped to traverse unmapped railway yards and coach interiors autonomously without human intervention.
*   **Model Type:** Fast-LIO2 (LiDAR-Inertial Odometry) + Costmap2D obstacle avoidance in ROS2 Nav2.
*   **Input:** 3D Point clouds from Livox Mid-360 LiDAR + 6-axis IMU acceleration/gyroscope data.
*   **Output:** Continuous motor angular velocity commands (`cmd_vel`) to quadruped leg actuators.
*   **Why AI/Rule Hybrid is Necessary:** Pure rules cannot handle dynamic human crowds, moving luggage carts, and uneven ballast stones simultaneously.
*   **Failure Mode Handling:** If SLAM tracking is lost (e.g., dense smoke), quadruped halts immediately, lowers chassis to safe posture, and broadcasts SOS beacon.

### 3. Facial Recognition & Watch-list Matching
*   **Purpose:** Detect and flag known wanted drug peddlers or criminal suspects on railway platforms.
*   **Model Type:** YOLOv8-Face (Detection) + ArcFace / MobileFaceNet (Feature Extraction & Vector Cosine Similarity).
*   **Input:** 1080p optical camera video stream from quadruped pan-tilt head.
*   **Output:** Bounding box + Match Alert with CCTNS record ID.
*   **Why AI is Necessary:** Manual visual scanning by constables in crowds of 10,000+ passengers is humanly impossible.
*   **Failure Mode Handling:** All face matches trigger a "Potential Match - Human Verification Required" prompt on the control room screen. No automated arrest actions take place.

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

### Cryptographic Security Matrix

*   **Data at Rest:** Local SQLite databases on handhelds and quadrupeds are encrypted using SQLCipher with AES-256 keys tied to device TPM (Trusted Platform Module) chips [RESEARCHED].
*   **Data in Transit:** All device-to-device and device-to-cloud communications use TLS 1.3 encryption with Mutual Certificate Authentication (mTLS) to prevent rogue device injection [RESEARCHED].
*   **Evidence Chain of Custody (BSA 2023 Compliance):** Every threat alert payload creates a SHA-256 hash derived from:
    $$\text{Hash} = \text{SHA256}(\text{EventID} + \text{Timestamp} + \text{GPS} + \text{Spectrum} + \text{OfficerID})$$
    This hash is signed via Ed25519 using the hardware's private key before being committed to the central Hyperledger Fabric blockchain. Any tampering with stored logs breaks hash verification in court [RESEARCHED].

### Governance & Privacy Compliance (DPDP Act 2023)

*   **Facial Recognition Privacy:** Facial scanning algorithms convert live camera feeds directly into anonymized 512-dimensional vector embeddings in volatile RAM. Raw video frames of non-matching bystanders are discarded instantly without persistent storage to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 [RESEARCHED].
*   **Zero Autonomous Weaponization:** The quadruped platform is strictly designated as a non-lethal, unarmed surveillance and detection system.
