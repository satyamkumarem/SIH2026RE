## 1. Executive Snapshot

This research and technical architecture dossier defines a complete, production-ready design for an **AI-Driven Hyper-Local Business Advisory and Financial Structuring Assistant** tailored for rural micro-entrepreneurs in India. Promoted under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE) [PS], the system addresses the critical problem of high business failure and stagnation rates among first-time rural entrepreneurs from marginalized communities [PS]. While concessional credit programs (such as the Micro Finance Scheme and Term Loan Scheme) offer up to 90% funding [PS], beneficiaries often select business ideas based on anecdotal evidence rather than hyper-local market data [PS]. This platform bridges that institutional gap by providing voice-first, multilingual business feasibility analysis and a deterministic smart financial calculator that auto-selects government loan schemes and generates quarterly EMI schedules [PS].

## 2. Problem Deconstruction

The explicit problem statement requirement text has been extracted into atomic, verifiable specifications:

### Core System Inputs
- **Requirement INP-1**: User provides geographic location at the level of Village / Block / District [PS].
- **Requirement INP-2**: User provides available margin capital in INR (e.g., ₹1,00,000) [PS].
- **Requirement INP-3**: User selects or inputs proposed business category (e.g., Dairy, Retail, Textiles, Agro-processing) [PS].

### Module 1: Hyper-Local Business Feasibility Report
- **Requirement M1-1 (Market Reach)**: Estimate the immediate consumer base within a 5–10 km radius of the specified village/block and identify primary distribution channels [PS].
- **Requirement M1-2 (Opportunity Analysis)**: Highlight unserved or underserved niches within the chosen business sector in that local economy [PS].
- **Requirement M1-3 (General Business Analysis - SWOT)**: Provide a foundational breakdown of Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats tailored specifically to the micro-enterprise budget [PS].
- **Requirement M1-4 (Threats Identification)**: Pinpoint localized operational risks including supply chain bottlenecks, seasonal demand fluctuations, and single-buyer dependency [PS].
- **Requirement M1-5 (Competitor Mapping)**: Utilize localized demographic and economic data to estimate the density of existing similar enterprises in the block [PS].
- **Requirement M1-6 (Product Market Value)**: Suggest optimal pricing strategies and predict the local market value of goods/services based on regional purchasing power [PS].

### Module 2: Smart Financial Calculator & Scheme Router
- **Requirement M2-7 (Financial Structuring)**: Automatically calculate Total Feasible Project Cost ($\text{Project Cost} = \frac{\text{Available Margin}}{0.10}$) and Maximum Loan Amount ($90\% \times \text{Project Cost}$) [PS].
- **Requirement M2-8 (Scheme Auto-Selection)**:
  - **Logic A**: If $\text{Project Cost} \le \text{₹1.40 Lakh}$, route to **Micro Finance Scheme** (6.5% p.a. interest, 3-year tenure, 3-month moratorium) [PS].
  - **Logic B**: If $\text{₹1.40 Lakh} < \text{Project Cost} \le \text{₹50.00 Lakh}$, route to **Term Loan Scheme** (8.0% p.a. interest, 7-year tenure, 6-month moratorium) [PS].
- **Requirement M2-9 (EMI & Moratorium Generator)**: Output a clear quarterly repayment schedule, factoring in specific moratorium periods, estimated operational costs, and working capital requirements [PS].

### Multilingual & Accessibility Requirements
- **Requirement ACC-1**: Provide an NLP-powered, multilingual interface tailored for rural and semi-urban geographies [PS].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

- **Connectivity**: Rural Gram Panchayats frequently experience 2G/3G connectivity, packet loss, or total outage [KNOWN]. *System design requirement*: Offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) with local SQLite caching [INFERENCE].
- **Hardware Limitations**: Target users rely on low-cost Android smartphones (2GB–4GB RAM, entry-level processors) [KNOWN]. *System design requirement*: Lightweight client payload (<5MB initial JS bundle), audio compression for speech, offloaded AI execution [INFERENCE].
- **Language & Literacy**: High rate of semi-literacy in regional scripts; strong preference for oral interaction [KNOWN]. *System design requirement*: Voice-in / Voice-out capability in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Kannada, and Odia [INFERENCE].
- **Field Conditions**: CSC VLEs and field agents conduct mobile assessments outdoors in varying power conditions [KNOWN]. *System design requirement*: Low battery usage design, dark mode, offline draft saving [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

**Selected Approach: Approach 3 (Hybrid Architecture)**

### Justification:
1. **Zero Financial Hallucination**: Financial structuring and scheme selection (`Project Cost = Available Margin / 10%`, Micro Finance vs Term Loan routing) are handled by a **100% deterministic Python/TypeScript engine** [INFERENCE]. Large Language Models are explicitly prohibited from performing arithmetic calculations [INFERENCE].
2. **Context-Bounded Advisory**: The feasibility report (SWOT, local threats, niche identification) utilizes a quantized Large Language Model (Llama-3.2-3B or Mistral-7B) operating strictly within a **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** framework [INFERENCE]. The prompt is injected with real geospatial and demographic data from the local Gram Panchayat [INFERENCE].
3. **Voice Accessibility**: Integrates India's open-source **Bhashini AI Speech Stack** to convert speech inputs from regional dialects into text and play back generated advisory audio [RESEARCHED].

### Rejection of Alternatives:
- *Approach 1* was rejected because static template matching fails to offer dynamic, hyper-local business insights for hundreds of diverse product categories [INFERENCE].
- *Approach 2* was rejected because cloud LLMs frequently hallucinate financial terms, require continuous high-speed internet, and introduce unaffordable token API costs for government deployment [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative
1. **Input Stage**: The user enters location (Village/Block/District), available capital (e.g., ₹1,00,000), and business type via voice or dropdown.
2. **Geospatial Resolution**: The LGD Resolver maps the village to its LGD code and triggers a PostGIS spatial buffer query (5–10 km radius) to pull population density, road connectivity, and estimated business density [RESEARCHED].
3. **Financial Execution**: The Financial Engine calculates:
   $$\text{Project Cost} = \frac{₹1,00,000}{0.10} = ₹10,00,000 \quad [\text{PS}]$$
   $$\text{Loan Amount} = 90\% \times ₹10,00,000 = ₹9,00,000 \quad [\text{PS}]$$
   Because ₹10.00 Lakh is $> \text{₹1.40 Lakh}$ and $\le \text{₹50.00 Lakh}$, it auto-routes to **Term Loan Scheme** (8% interest, 7-year tenure, 6-month moratorium) [PS].
4. **AI Generation Stage**: The calculated financials and geospatial metrics are formatted into a structured JSON context payload and passed to the RAG pipeline. The LLM generates the hyper-local SWOT, niche opportunity analysis, and pricing guidance without modifying financial values [INFERENCE].
5. **Output Stage**: The report is returned to the client as an interactive dashboard, rendered as a downloadable PDF, and synthesized into local audio for playback [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### 1. Multilingual Speech-to-Text (ASR) & Text-to-Speech (TTS)
- **Purpose**: Enable semi-literate rural entrepreneurs to interact using oral local language [PS].
- **Model Type**: Bhashini Indic ASR / OpenAI Whisper Small [RESEARCHED].
- **Input**: Audio stream (e.g., spoken Hindi/Tamil).
- **Output**: Transcribed text string / Synthesized audio.
- **Why AI**: Rule-based systems cannot parse natural voice in diverse Indian accents [INFERENCE].
- **Evaluation**: Word Error Rate (WER) $< 15\%$ on Indic evaluation datasets [RESEARCHED].
- **Failure Mode**: Low-quality audio input leads to mistranscription. *Fallback*: User shown simple UI buttons/icons for manual selection [INFERENCE].

### 2. Hyper-Local Feasibility & SWOT Generator
- **Purpose**: Dynamically construct localized SWOT, threat analysis, and niche market opportunities [PS].
- **Model Type**: Quantized Llama-3.2-3B with RAG [RESEARCHED].
- **Input**: Structured JSON (Location, Demographics, Business Type, Calculated Budget).
- **Output**: Structured Markdown advisory covering SWOT, Threats, Niches, and Pricing.
- **Why AI**: Manually drafting customized SWOT matrices for thousands of rural product-location combinations is infeasible [INFERENCE].
- **Evaluation**: ROUGE-L similarity against benchmark business reports; human expert evaluation.
- **Failure Mode**: Generic or hallucinated advice. *Mitigation*: Prompt constrained to official domain context with temperature = 0.2 [INFERENCE].

### 3. Explicit Prohibition of AI Usage
- **AI MUST NOT BE USED FOR**: Total Project Cost calculation, Loan Eligibility calculation, Interest Rate selection, EMI quarterly schedule generation, or Moratorium duration determination [INFERENCE].
- **Reason**: These operations are strictly governed by MoSJE guidelines [PS]. Using probabilistic AI models for financial calculations introduces hallucination risks that violate loan compliance standards [INFERENCE].

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Schema Architecture
```sql
-- LGD Hierarchy Mapping
CREATE TABLE lgd_villages (
    lgd_code INT PRIMARY KEY,
    village_name VARCHAR(100),
    block_name VARCHAR(100),
    district_name VARCHAR(100),
    state_name VARCHAR(100),
    location GEOMETRY(Point, 4326)
);

-- Scheme Definitions (MoSJE Rules)
CREATE TABLE mosje_schemes (
    scheme_id VARCHAR(50) PRIMARY KEY,
    scheme_name VARCHAR(100),
    min_project_cost NUMERIC,
    max_project_cost NUMERIC,
    max_loan_percentage NUMERIC DEFAULT 90.0,
    interest_rate_pa NUMERIC,
    tenure_years INT,
    moratorium_months INT
);

-- Assessment Cache
CREATE TABLE feasibility_assessments (
    assessment_id UUID PRIMARY KEY,
    user_id UUID,
    lgd_code INT REFERENCES lgd_villages(lgd_code),
    margin_capital NUMERIC,
    calculated_project_cost NUMERIC,
    calculated_loan_amount NUMERIC,
    selected_scheme_id VARCHAR(50) REFERENCES mosje_schemes(scheme_id),
    report_json JSONB,
    created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);
```

### Offline Synchronization Strategy
1. **Local Caching**: The mobile PWA pre-downloads compressed LGD lookup tables and offline business templates (top 20 rural enterprise categories) into client-side SQLite/IndexedDB [INFERENCE].
2. **Offline Mode Execution**: In zero-connectivity environments, the smart financial calculator and local business rules run 100% locally on the device [INFERENCE].
3. **Queue & Sync**: When connectivity is restored, cached offline assessments automatically background-sync with the central PostgreSQL database [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

- **Personal Data Protection**: User personal identifiers (Name, Aadhaar, Phone Number) are hashed using SHA-256 and stored separately from business assessment data in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 [KNOWN].
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**:
  - `Beneficiary`: Read/Write own feasibility assessments.
  - `CSC Agent`: Create assisted assessments on behalf of village citizens.
  - `SCA / Bank Officer`: Read-only access to submitted assessment dossiers for loan verification.
- **Financial Audit & Non-Repudiation**: Every generated financial report contains a deterministic execution timestamp, input snapshot, and rule version hash to prevent fraud or post-hoc tampering [INFERENCE].
- **Regulatory Status Disclaimer**: Reports generated by this platform are explicitly labeled as **"Pre-Application Feasibility Studies"** to clarify that final credit sanction remains subject to official SCA underwriting [INFERENCE].
