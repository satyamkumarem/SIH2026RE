## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

*   **Harsh Outdoor & Semi-Public Conditions:** Bins in public or educational spaces endure physical vibrations, rain, high humidity, and extreme dust [KNOWN]. Optical camera lenses and optical sensors can become coated with grime, degrading CV accuracy over time [INFERENCE].
*   **Variable Waste Physical Properties:** Waste items in India are often crushed, mixed, wet, or wrapped in plastic bags [KNOWN]. Pure computer vision often fails when an item (e.g., wet food) is inside a black plastic bag [INFERENCE].
*   **Power Availability:** Public locations may lack continuous AC mains supply [KNOWN]. The hardware must be power-efficient, supporting 12V battery operation or potential solar auxiliary power [INFERENCE].
*   **Connectivity Interruptions:** Cellular connectivity (3G/4G/5G) in Indian urban pockets and semi-urban campuses can be intermittent [KNOWN]. Hardware must maintain local operation and queue telemetry data offline in SQLite [INFERENCE].
*   **Cost Limits:** High-cost industrial sorting machines (costing over ₹10 Lakhs) cannot be deployed at every public corner or institutional building [KNOWN]. The target hardware BOM for a localized smart bin must be under ₹15,000–₹25,000 for realistic adoption [ASSUMPTION].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

### Selected Solution: Hybrid Edge-AI & Sensor Fusion Waste Segregator with Active Sanitization Pod

The recommended approach is **Approach 3**. The rationale is as follows:

1.  **Multi-Modal Precision:** Opaque plastic wrappers hide food waste from cameras; pure moisture sensors miss dry plastic bottles containing leftover water. A hybrid matrix (Visual Feature + Inductive Metal + Capacitive Moisture) solves both failure modes [INFERENCE].
2.  **SWM Rules 2026 Compliance:** It is the only approach capable of classifying items into all 4 mandatory streams: Wet (kitchen/food), Dry (plastic/paper/glass), Sanitary (diapers/pads/wipes), and Hazardous (batteries/e-waste/chemicals) [RESEARCHED].
3.  **Active Hygiene Control:** Incorporating an automated sanitation subsystem (solenoid-driven disinfectant sprayer + UV-C chamber) directly fulfills the explicit "improve sanitization system" mandate of SIH26212 [PS].
4.  **Hardware Realism for Hackathon:** Using a modular architecture (ESP32-S3 co-processor handling real-time GPIO/servos while Raspberry Pi 5 handles camera inference) prevents single-thread timing lags and ensures smooth demo execution [ASSUMPTION].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative
1.  **Detection & Tray Opening:** User approaches the bin. An ultrasonic/IR sensor detects user presence and opens the main intake hatch via Servo 1 (touchless operation) [INFERENCE].
2.  **Item Placement & Sensing:** User drops an item onto the inspection plate. Load cell measures weight. Inductive proximity sensor checks for metal. Capacitive moisture sensor checks wetness [INFERENCE].
3.  **Visual Inference:** RPi Camera captures an image under ring LED lighting. The YOLOv8-nano model predicts visual category (e.g., plastic bottle, food waste, battery) [INFERENCE].
4.  **Sensor Fusion Rule Engine:**
    *   *If Visual = Plastic Bottle AND Moisture = Dry -> Stream: DRY (Blue)*
    *   *If Visual = Food Container OR Moisture > Threshold -> Stream: WET (Green)*
    *   *If Visual = Battery / E-Waste OR Metal = High & Chemical visual tag -> Stream: HAZARDOUS (Yellow)*
    *   *If Visual = Sanitary packaging / Tissue -> Stream: SANITARY (Red)* [INFERENCE].
5.  **Actuation & Disposal:** RPi 5 commands the ESP32-S3 via UART. ESP32-S3 rotates the 2-axis stepper/servo rotary chute to align with the target compartment and opens the drop flap [INFERENCE].
6.  **Sanitization Pulse:** If the drop is WET or SANITARY, the ESP32 activates the solenoid valve for a 1-second fine disinfectant mist spray and triggers the internal UV-C LED array for 10 seconds [INFERENCE].
7.  **Telemetry Sync:** Ultrasonic fill sensors measure updated compartment levels. ESP32 sends a JSON telemetry packet to Raspberry Pi 5, which logs it locally in SQLite and publishes it via MQTT over LTE/Wi-Fi to the municipal dashboard [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### AI Component 1: Edge Object Waste Classifier

1.  **Exact Problem AI Solves:** Classifying visually complex waste objects (e.g., distinguishing paper cups, plastic wrappers, tin cans, glass bottles, packaging) where simple threshold sensors fail [INFERENCE].
2.  **Input:** 640x640 RGB image captured by Raspberry Pi Camera inside inspection tray [ASSUMPTION].
3.  **Output:** Multi-class label confidence vector (`[Plastic: 0.92, Paper: 0.05, Food: 0.03...]`) mapped to one of 4 waste streams [INFERENCE].
4.  **Why Simpler Rules Fail:** Physical attributes like reflection, shape deformation, and surface text cannot be represented by hardcoded sensor thresholds [INFERENCE].
5.  **Required Data:** ~1,500 annotated images of common Indian solid waste items categorized into 4 streams [ASSUMPTION].
6.  **Evaluation Metric:** Mean Average Precision (mAP@0.5) > 85%, Inference Latency < 200ms [ASSUMPTION].
7.  **When It Is Wrong:** Sensor fusion fallback engages: Moisture > 40% forces WET stream; Inductive metal detection forces DRY/HAZARDOUS stream [INFERENCE].
8.  **Training Required:** Yes, fine-tuning YOLOv8-nano on custom annotated dataset [ASSUMPTION].
9.  **Fine-Tuning Required:** Yes, transfer learning on pre-trained COCO weights [ASSUMPTION].
10. **RAG Required:** No [INFERENCE].
11. **Computer Vision Required:** Yes [INFERENCE].
12. **Speech Processing Required:** No [INFERENCE].
13. **Forecasting Required:** No [INFERENCE].
14. **Human Oversight Required:** No real-time oversight during sorting; periodic municipal audit of bin contents [INFERENCE].

### AI Component 2: Municipal Fill-Level & Route Optimization Model

1.  **Exact Problem AI Solves:** Solving the Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP) to dynamically route municipal waste trucks only to bins exceeding 80% capacity [INFERENCE].
2.  **Input:** Array of bin GeoJSON coordinates, current fill percentages, road network graph [INFERENCE].
3.  **Output:** Optimized sequential GPS waypoint route for collection drivers [INFERENCE].
4.  **Why Simpler System Fails:** Fixed route schedules waste 30-40% fuel on empty bins while overflowing bins remain unserviced [KNOWN].
5.  **Data Required:** Bin location coordinates, real-time ultrasonic fill telemetry [INFERENCE].
6.  **Evaluation:** Distance saved (km) and fuel reduction (%) compared to static routes [ASSUMPTION].
7.  **When It Is Wrong:** Driver manual override to visit skipped bins [INFERENCE].
8.  **Training/Fine-Tuning/RAG/CV/Speech:** None (Uses deterministic graph optimization - Google OR-Tools / Dijkstra algorithm) [INFERENCE].
9.  **Human Oversight:** Driver approves generated route on mobile app before departure [INFERENCE].

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
*   **Metal Detection:** Pure hardware Inductive Proximity Sensor must be used directly via ESP32 GPIO interrupts [INFERENCE].
*   **Fill Level Calculation:** Raw Ultrasonic Time-of-Flight math (`Distance = (Time * Speed of Sound) / 2`) must be used directly [KNOWN].
*   **Sanitizer Spray Trigger:** Direct threshold rule (`If Methane > X ppm OR Wet item dropped -> Spray = ON`) [INFERENCE].

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Database Schemas (SQLite Local / PostgreSQL Cloud)

```sql
-- Local Edge SQLite Schema (Raspberry Pi 5)
CREATE TABLE telemetry_queue (
    id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT,
    timestamp DATETIME DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    wet_fill_percent REAL,
    dry_fill_percent REAL,
    sanitary_fill_percent REAL,
    hazardous_fill_percent REAL,
    methane_ppm REAL,
    ammonia_ppm REAL,
    last_action TEXT,
    synced_to_cloud INTEGER DEFAULT 0
);

CREATE TABLE sorting_events (
    event_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY,
    timestamp DATETIME DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    detected_class TEXT,
    confidence REAL,
    final_stream TEXT,
    sensor_override INTEGER DEFAULT 0
);
```

### Offline Synchronization Strategy
1.  **Local Buffering:** When cellular/Wi-Fi connection drops, telemetry packets are appended to the local SQLite database (`synced_to_cloud = 0`) [INFERENCE].
2.  **Heartbeat & Ping:** A background cron daemon pings the cloud MQTT broker every 30 seconds [INFERENCE].
3.  **Batch Sync:** Upon reconnect, buffered records are transmitted in compressed JSON batches over MQTT with QoS Level 1 (At Least Once delivery guarantee) [INFERENCE].
4.  **Conflict Handling:** Time-series telemetry events use hardware-assigned UUIDs and UTC timestamps, eliminating primary key collision risks [INFERENCE].
5.  **Bandwidth Reduction:** Only telemetry JSON strings (~150 bytes per event) are sent over cellular networks. Video frames are discarded locally after inference and NEVER uploaded over mobile data [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

*   **Privacy Preservation (Camera Placement):** The camera module is housed internally inside the closed inspection chute, angled strictly downward at the waste platform [INFERENCE]. No human faces or public areas are ever captured [INFERENCE].
*   **Data Protection Compliance:** Aligns with the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023** as zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is captured, stored, or transmitted [KNOWN].
*   **Hardware Security & Device Authentication:** Every bin unit is flashed with a unique X.509 client certificate stored in ESP32-S3 secure storage [INFERENCE]. Cloud MQTT connection requires TLS 1.3 mutual authentication [INFERENCE].
*   **Physical Safety Cut-Offs:** UV-C light tubes pose skin/eye hazards [KNOWN]. A mechanical safety limit switch cuts power to the UV-C array immediately if the bin maintenance door is opened [INFERENCE].
*   **Tamper Alerts:** An onboard 3-axis accelerometer (MPU6050) detects physical vandalism, tipping, or unauthorized moving of public bins and triggers an immediate high-priority alert to the dashboard [INFERENCE].
