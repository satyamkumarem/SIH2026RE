## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
The Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), guarding India's 1,751 km porous border with Nepal and 699 km border with Bhutan, faces major operational challenges at border outposts (BOPs) and transit checkposts due to high passenger volumes and manual verification of diverse travel and identity documents [PS, KNOWN, RESEARCHED]. Malicious actors exploit open borders using fake passports, altered photographs, modified birth dates, forged visa stamps, and impersonation [PS, RESEARCHED]. This solution dossier proposes an **AI-Powered Multi-Modal Identity & Travel Document Screening System** specifically architected for edge deployment at remote border posts [INFERENCE]. Combining localized Optical Character Recognition (OCR), ICAO Doc 9303 algorithmic verification, deep learning forensic tampering detection (Error Level Analysis + Convolutional Neural Networks), and lightweight facial biometrics (ArcFace / MobileFaceNet), the platform analyzes identity documents in under 5 seconds [INFERENCE, RESEARCHED]. It delivers an explainable Risk Score (0-100) and actionable decision guidance to SSB field personnel, even under offline or low-connectivity conditions [INFERENCE].

## 2. Problem Deconstruction

The problem statement provided by the SSB / Ministry of Home Affairs specifies explicit modules, operational challenges, expected impacts, and document types [PS].

### Explicit Requirements Deconstruction

#### 1. Background & Operational Challenges
* **1.1 Fake Passports & Visas:** System must identify counterfeit travel documents lacking valid structural formats [PS].
* **1.2 Altered Photographs:** System must detect photo replacement or physical/digital splicing on photo pages [PS].
* **1.3 Modified Dates of Birth:** System must detect text manipulation or font inconsistencies in demographic fields [PS].
* **1.4 Tampered Visa Stamps:** System must identify forged, altered, or misaligned entry/exit stamps [PS].
* **1.5 Identity Impersonation:** System must verify that the person presenting the document matches the photo on the document [PS].
* **1.6 Multiple Identities:** System must maintain local and central records to flag individuals using different aliases [PS, INFERENCE].
* **1.7 Expired or Blacklisted Documents:** System must check documents against watchlists and validate expiration dates [PS, INFERENCE].
* **1.8 High Passenger Volume Delays:** Processing time must be reduced from several minutes to a few seconds per passenger [PS].

#### 2. Core Functional Modules

##### Module 1: OCR Extraction
* **Objective:** Automatically extract all relevant information from identity documents [PS].
* **Inputs:** Passport image, Visa image, National ID image, Driving license, Permit documents [PS].
* **Extracted Fields (Passport):** Name, Passport Number, Nationality, Date of birth, Date of expiry, Gender [PS].
* **Extracted Fields (Visa):** Visa Number, Visa Type, Entry Validation, Stay Duration [PS].
* **Implementation Implication:** OCR pipeline must support standard Machine Readable Zones (MRZ) and Visual Inspection Zones (VIZ) across heterogeneous layouts [INFERENCE].

##### Module 2: Document Validation
* **Objective:** Verify whether extracted information follows official document standards [PS].
* **Implementation Implication:** Programmatic execution of ICAO Doc 9303 7-3-1 modulo-10 checksum algorithms, ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 country code validation, expiration logic, and cross-field data consistency (MRZ vs. VIZ) [INFERENCE, RESEARCHED].

##### Module 3: Tampering Detection (Core AI Innovation)
* **Objective:** Detect digitally or physically altered documents [PS].
* **Use Cases:**
  * Photo Replacement detection [PS].
  * Text Manipulation detection [PS].
  * Stamp Forgery Detection [PS].
  * Image Metadata Analysis (EXIF, compression artifacts, ELA) [PS, RESEARCHED].

##### Module 4: Face Verification
* **Objective:** Ensure document owner matches the presented individual [PS].
* **Implementation Implication:** 1:1 facial biometric matching between the document photo and a live camera capture, integrated with passive liveness detection [INFERENCE, RESEARCHED].

#### 3. Expected Impacts & Decision Support
* **Speed:** Verification time reduced from minutes to seconds [PS].
* **Accuracy:** Enhanced detection of forged/tampered documents over human visual inspection [PS].
* **Standardization:** Consistent, data-driven risk assessment across all border checkpoints [PS].
* **Digital Trail:** Immutable logging of every screening event for legal prosecution and intelligence analysis [PS].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

* **Geographical & Operational Context:** SSB guards the 1,751 km Indo-Nepal border and 699 km Indo-Bhutan border [KNOWN, RESEARCHED]. Unlike international airports, these borders feature open, porous transit points (e.g., Raxaul, Panitanki, Sonauli) with high pedestrian and vehicular movement [RESEARCHED].
* **Connectivity Restrictions:** Many Border Outposts (BOPs) operate in rural/hilly terrain with intermittent 2G/3G or zero cellular connectivity [KNOWN, INFERENCE]. The platform must perform 100% of core inference (OCR, tampering, face match, risk scoring) **locally on edge devices** [INFERENCE].
* **Hardware Availability:** Field jawans cannot be assumed to carry expensive servers or GPU rigs. Deployment targets standard Android rugged tablets or low-cost Intel i5/i7 edge laptops equipped with basic webcams or handheld passport scanners [ASSUMPTION].
* **Environmental Lighting & Field Conditions:** Document capture at outdoor checkposts suffers from glare, shadows, dust, and rain [KNOWN]. Camera preprocessing must handle brightness normalization and perspective correction [INFERENCE].
* **Diverse Languages & Document Formats:** Passports and IDs presented at Indo-Nepal/Bhutan borders include Indian Aadhaar/Voter ID, Nepalese citizenship cards, Bhutanese Citizenship IDs, international passports (ICAO compliant), and hand-stamped transit permits [RESEARCHED]. OCR must handle Latin and Devanagari scripts [INFERENCE].

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Pure Rule & Heuristic-Based System
* **Mechanism:** Tesseract OCR extracts text from document -> Regex parses MRZ lines -> Programmatic 7-3-1 check digit validation -> EXIF metadata check -> Basic pixel variance around photo frame.
* **Problem Fit:** Low. Fails against modern digital image editing, high-quality photo swaps, and neural text modifications [INFERENCE].
* **Accuracy Potential:** Poor for forgery detection (approx. 40-50%); High for MRZ syntax validation [INFERENCE].
* **Complexity & Cost:** Very Low complexity; Zero license cost [INFERENCE].
* **Feasibility:** Extremely easy for Hackathon, but fails core AI requirement [INFERENCE].

### Approach 2: Cloud-Centric Deep Learning & Heavy Vision Transformer Pipeline
* **Mechanism:** Mobile client captures image -> Sends payload to AWS/GCP -> Multi-stage Vision Transformer (ViT) + LayoutLMv3 + Heavy ResNet-152 Forgery Classifier + Cloud Face Engine -> Returns risk score.
* **Problem Fit:** Moderate to Low due to deployment constraints. Extremely accurate in controlled environments, but useless when border outposts lose cellular signal [INFERENCE].
* **Accuracy Potential:** Very High (95%+ in lab settings) [INFERENCE].
* **Complexity & Cost:** High cloud compute cost, high network latency (3-10 seconds depending on network) [INFERENCE].
* **Feasibility:** High for cloud demo, but operationally unviable for SSB field conditions [INFERENCE].

### Approach 3: Edge-First Hybrid AI Architecture (Recommended)
* **Mechanism:** Local ONNX-optimized pipeline running on edge device:
  1. **Fast Local OCR Engine** (PaddleOCR / EasyOCR) for MRZ & VIZ extraction.
  2. **Rule & Standard Validation Module** (ICAO 9303 7-3-1 Modulo-10 checksums, ISO 3166 lookup, expiry check).
  3. **Dual-Branch Tampering AI** (Adaptive Error Level Analysis [ELA] + Lightweight ResNet-18 / MobileNetV3 CNN for photo-splicing, text anomaly, and stamp validation).
  4. **Lightweight Biometric Engine** (InsightFace / MobileFaceNet) extracting 512-dim facial embeddings for 1:1 cosine matching + passive liveness.
  5. **Explainable Risk Scoring Engine** combining all signals into a weighted score (0-100).
  6. **Background Sync Engine** to update central databases when network is restored.
* **Problem Fit:** Excellent. Specifically solves offline border outpost constraints while providing high AI forensic accuracy [INFERENCE].
* **Accuracy Potential:** High (92-96% overall forgery/face accuracy) [RESEARCHED, INFERENCE].
* **Complexity & Cost:** Moderate development complexity; Zero runtime API cost; Runs entirely on standard i5 laptop or Android tablet [INFERENCE].
* **Feasibility:** Highly realistic for a 4-6 student team within the SIH timeline [INFERENCE].

### Justification & Rejection of Alternatives
* **Why Approach 1 was rejected:** Rule-based systems cannot detect sophisticated photo replacements, tampered visa stamps, or re-printed forged cards. Relying on checksums alone is insufficient because a skilled forger can easily recompute valid ICAO 7-3-1 checksums for fake text [RESEARCHED].
* **Why Approach 2 was rejected:** Cloud-centric architectures fail SSB's fundamental operational constraint—intermittent or zero network access at remote border outposts along the Nepal and Bhutan borders [KNOWN, RESEARCHED]. Sending unencrypted biometric and passport data over public cloud networks introduces severe privacy and legal compliance risks under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 [KNOWN, INFERENCE].
* **Why Approach 3 Wins:**
  1. **Zero-Latency & Offline Capability:** Executes full inference locally on edge hardware in under 3 seconds [INFERENCE].
  2. **Multi-Layer Defense:** Combines mathematical rigor (ICAO checksums) with deep learning vision forensics (ELA + CNN) and facial biometrics [INFERENCE].
  3. **Explainability:** Provides border officers with specific reasons for flagging (e.g., *"Photo Splicing Probability: 88%, DOB Checksum: FAILED, Face Match: 94%"*) rather than an unexplainable black-box decision [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative

1. **Document & Live Image Capture:** The SSB officer captures an image of the traveler's document (Passport, Visa, ID) and a live facial snapshot using the device camera.
2. **Preprocessing & Quality Check:** OpenCV evaluates lighting, blur (Laplacian variance), and rotation. It applies perspective correction to crop and warp the document into a standard rectangular frame [INFERENCE].
3. **Module 1 (OCR Extraction):** The image is routed to the MRZ and VIZ OCR pipelines. MRZ characters are extracted via fixed-width character recognition; VIZ fields (Name, Passport No, DOB, Expiry, Gender) are parsed using PaddleOCR [RESEARCHED].
4. **Module 2 (Rule & Standard Validation):** Extracted MRZ text undergoes algorithmic check-digit validation using the ICAO 9303 7-3-1 modulo-10 formula [RESEARCHED]. DOB is checked against current date; expiry date is checked; country code is verified against ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 standards [RESEARCHED].
5. **Module 3 (Forensic Tampering Detection):**
   * *Metadata Check:* System inspects EXIF data for editing software tags or missing camera signatures [PS].
   * *Error Level Analysis (ELA):* Resaves the image at a known JPEG compression rate (e.g., 90%) and calculates the absolute pixel difference. Manipulated regions show distinct error levels [RESEARCHED].
   * *Deep Learning Classifier:* ELA map and RGB photo page are fed into the ResNet-18 model to output tamper probabilities for **Photo Replacement**, **Text Editing**, and **Stamp Forgery** [RESEARCHED].
6. **Module 4 (Face Verification):** The document photo crop and live camera photo are processed through MobileFaceNet to extract two 512-dimensional vector embeddings [RESEARCHED]. The system computes Cosine Distance; if similarity exceeds the threshold (e.g., 0.75), the face is verified [RESEARCHED]. Passive liveness checks for print/screen replay attacks [RESEARCHED].
7. **Risk Engine Fusion & Decision Output:** The system aggregates individual confidence scores into a Composite Risk Index (0-100).
   * **Score 0 - 25 (Green / PASS):** Automatic clearance.
   * **Score 26 - 60 (Yellow / WARNING):** Secondary manual inspection required; specific anomalies highlighted.
   * **Score 61 - 100 (Red / ALERT):** High threat; immediate escalation to Commandant & local database flag.
8. **Logging & Async Sync:** The complete log (timestamp, location, OCR data, risk score, encrypted image hash) is committed to local SQLite and queued for central HQ sync [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Comprehensive AI Component Matrix

#### 1. Module 1: Document OCR & Layout Parsing
* **Purpose:** Extract structured demographic and document text fields from diverse document layouts.
* **Model Type:** PaddleOCR (Detection: DBNet, Recognition: SVTR LCNet).
* **Input:** Cropped RGB Document Image (1080p).
* **Output:** Key-value pairs (Name, Passport No, DOB, Expiry, Gender, Nationality).
* **Data Required:** MIDV-2020 / MIDV-500 document images [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Template-based rule parsing fails because passports and national IDs vary across hundreds of formats and orientations.
* **Evaluation:** Character Error Rate (CER) < 2%, Field Accuracy > 95%.
* **Failure Mode:** Poor lighting/blur causing character misreads (e.g., '0' vs 'O').
* **Human Oversight:** Officer can view side-by-side bounding box highlighting to correct misread fields in UI.

#### 2. Module 3: Document Forensics & Tampering Detection
* **Purpose:** Identify photo replacement, text modification, and stamp forgery.
* **Model Type:** Dual-Branch CNN (ResNet-18) accepting RGB Image + Error Level Analysis (ELA) map.
* **Input:** Document Photo Page crop (512x512).
* **Output:** Probability scores for `photo_replaced`, `text_edited`, `stamp_forged` (0.0 to 1.0).
* **Data Required:** MIDV-DM (8,000 manipulated document images) [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Human eyes cannot detect micro-compression discontinuities, subtle re-sampling artifacts, or digital splicing.
* **Evaluation:** Precision, Recall, ROC-AUC (>0.92 target on test set).
* **Failure Mode:** False positive on heavily crumpled or water-damaged genuine documents.
* **Human Oversight:** System flags specific image regions with heatmaps (Grad-CAM) so officers can visually inspect the suspicious zone.

#### 3. Module 4: 1:1 Facial Verification & Liveness
* **Purpose:** Match document photo against live individual and verify liveness.
* **Model Type:** MobileFaceNet trained with ArcFace loss + MiniFASNet for passive liveness [RESEARCHED].
* **Input:** Cropped Document Photo + Live Camera Capture.
* **Output:** Cosine Similarity Score (0.0 to 1.0) and Liveness Probability.
* **Data Required:** LFW, VGGFace2, public liveness datasets [RESEARCHED].
* **Why AI is Preferable:** Manual face comparison by humans is error-prone due to aging, hairstyle changes, or lighting differences.
* **Evaluation:** False Acceptance Rate (FAR) < 0.01% at False Rejection Rate (FRR) < 1.0%.
* **Failure Mode:** Extreme glare or heavy facial occlusion (masks, dark glasses).
* **Human Oversight:** Secondary verification dialog pops up for scores between 0.60 and 0.75.

### Explicit Non-AI Exclusions
* **ICAO Checksum Validation:** MUST NOT use AI. Must use exact, deterministic ICAO Doc 9303 7-3-1 modulo-10 mathematical rules [RESEARCHED].
* **Expiration & DOB Logic:** MUST NOT use AI. Deterministic date comparison logic (`Expiry_Date >= Current_Date`).
* **Watchlist Lookup:** MUST NOT use AI for exact document number matching. Must use exact SQL queries / SHA-256 hash matching for blacklisted numbers [INFERENCE].

-- Core Table: Local Encrypted Watchlist
CREATE TABLE watchlist (
    watchlist_id VARCHAR(36) PRIMARY KEY,
    document_number_hash VARCHAR(64) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    full_name VARCHAR(100),
    reason_code VARCHAR(50), -- WANTED, EXPIRED_PASSPORT, SUSPECTED_TERROR
    severity VARCHAR(10), -- HIGH, CRITICAL

    updated_at DATETIME

### Offline Synchronization & Conflict Management
1. **Local-First Write:** Every scan log is written immediately to the local encrypted SQLite store [INFERENCE].
2. **Background Sync Worker:** A background daemon monitors network connectivity. When ping to central server succeeds, it pushes un-synced logs in batches of 20 records using HTTP/2 TLS 1.3 [INFERENCE].
3. **Conflict Resolution Strategy:**
   * **Rule:** "Local Field Log is Immutable; Central Watchlist Wins."
   * Verification logs are append-only; conflict never occurs for logs.
   * For Watchlist updates, Central Server uses monotonically increasing version timestamps (`updated_at`). Local edge nodes pull delta updates (`WHERE updated_at > last_sync_time`) [INFERENCE].
4. **Bandwidth Reduction:** Images are converted to WebP format and thumbnail-cropped. Full high-resolution raw images are stored locally and only uploaded upon central command request for high-risk alerts (Red status) [INFERENCE].
