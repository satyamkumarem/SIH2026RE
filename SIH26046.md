## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
The **All India Institute of Ayurveda (AIIA)**, functioning as a premier academic clinical research center and host to the **National Pharmacovigilance Coordination Centre (NPvCC)** for Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, and Homoeopathy (ASU&H) drugs, requires a unified, real-time, cloud-based Clinical Trial Management System (CTMS) and analytics dashboard `[PS]`. Currently, study tracking, recruitment monitoring, milestone management, and adverse event surveillance across interventional and multi-center trials rely on fragmented spreadsheets and disconnected tools, introducing compliance risks under the New Drugs and Clinical Trials (NDCT) Rules 2019, GCP-ASU, and ICMR ethical guidelines `[PS]`. 

The proposed solution—**AIIA-CTMS Pulse**—is a cloud-native, microservices-driven CTMS platform providing a role-based, auditable view of trial lifecycles `[INFERENCE]`. It natively incorporates **CDISC standards** (CDASH, SDTM, ADaM, Define-XML) for regulatory submission readiness, **HL7 FHIR R4 / ABDM** building blocks for hospital EMR/EDC interoperability, and an integrated **Pharmacovigilance (PV) Module** with automated MedDRA/WHODrug coding and regulatory alert routing for adverse event (AE/SAE) monitoring `[PS]`. The architecture strictly adheres to **ALCOA+ data integrity**, DPDP Act 2023 / 2025 Rules, 21 CFR Part 11 compliant audit trails, and CERT-In/ISO 27001 data residency guidelines `[PS]`.

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1. **Multi-Centric & Rural Clinical Trial Sites**:
   - *Reality*: AYUSH clinical trials often occur at regional peripheral institutes or rural centers with inconsistent broadband connectivity `[INFERENCE]`.
   - *Impact*: The system cannot depend on uninterrupted cloud access for field data entry or patient screening.
   - *Mitigation*: Progressive Web App (PWA) with Offline-First IndexedDB caching and automated background synchronization when network reconnects `[INFERENCE]`.

2. **Heterogeneous Hospital Information Systems (HIS)**:
   - *Reality*: Participating hospitals use legacy proprietary Electronic Health Record (EHR) software without standardized APIs `[KNOWN]`.
   - *Impact*: Direct API integration with every local HIS is infeasible within a standardized rollout.
   - *Mitigation*: Provide dual input vectors: ABDM FHIR R4 API gateway for modernized HIS, and standardized CSV/CDASH batch upload adapters for legacy setups `[INFERENCE]`.

3. **Complex Traditional Medicine Lexicon**:
   - *Reality*: Ayurveda trial interventions involve botanical formulations, Prakriti classifications, and Panchakarma procedures that standard Western EMRs cannot represent `[KNOWN]`.
   - *Impact*: Standard medical dictionaries like MedDRA lack granular Ayurvedic disease terminology (e.g., *Jwara*, *Amavata*) `[RESEARCHED]`.
   - *Mitigation*: Integrate NAMASTE Portal (National AYUSH Morbidity and Standardized Terminology Electronic Portal) codes alongside MedDRA and WHODrug mappings `[RESEARCHED]`.

4. **Strict Indian Data Residency & Privacy Mandates**:
   - *Reality*: The DPDP Act 2023 / 2025 Rules prohibit transferring sensitive personal health data outside India without explicit authorization `[KNOWN]`.
   - *Impact*: Third-party cloud services or LLM APIs hosted outside India (e.g., US-based cloud endpoints) cannot be used for direct health data processing `[INFERENCE]`.
   - *Mitigation*: All cloud hosting must strictly reside in AWS Mumbai or GCP Delhi data centers certified by MeitY / CERT-In `[PS]`.

5. **Device Diversity among Site Staff**:
   - *Reality*: Study coordinators at peripheral centers often rely on low-cost Android tablets or mid-tier smartphones `[INFERENCE]`.
   - *Impact*: High client-side processing loads or heavy web bundles will render the UI unusable.
   - *Mitigation*: Lightweight Frontend (React/Vite with TailwindCSS) optimized for low RAM devices and low-bandwidth asset loading `[INFERENCE]`.

## 6. Research Questions

To ensure technical and legal accuracy during system design, the following research questions were evaluated:

1. *What are the precise regulatory SAE reporting timelines under NDCT Rules 2019 for investigator-initiated trials?*
   - **Finding**: The investigator must report any Serious Adverse Event (SAE) to the Central Licensing Authority (CDSCO), Ethics Committee, and Trial Sponsor within 24 hours of occurrence `[RESEARCHED]`. The detailed causality report must be submitted within 14 days `[RESEARCHED]`.

2. *How does the ABDM FHIR R4 profile represent clinical research consent and trial data?*
   - **Finding**: ABDM utilizes `HealthInformationExchange-ConsentManager` (HIE-CM) and standard FHIR R4 profiles (`Patient`, `Encounter`, `Condition`, `DocumentReference`). For clinical research, trial eConsent artifacts are wrapped in `Consent` resources referencing the patient's 14-digit ABHA number `[RESEARCHED]`.

3. *How are MedDRA and WHODrug dictionaries updated, and what are their licensing constraints?*
   - **Finding**: MedDRA is maintained by the MSSO (updated biannually in March/September), and WHODrug is maintained by Uppsala Monitoring Centre (UMC) `[RESEARCHED]`. Both require commercial or institutional licenses for production deployment `[RESEARCHED]`. For hackathon prototypes, synthetic medical terminology datasets or open-source subset dictionary mock services must be used `[ASSUMPTION]`.

4. *What are the specific requirements for 21 CFR Part 11 / GCP compliant electronic signatures?*
   - **Finding**: Must require two distinct identification components (e.g., user ID + password re-prompt or 2FA OTP) at the time of signing, accompanied by printed name, exact timestamp, and the explicit operational meaning of the signature (e.g., "Approved by PI") `[KNOWN]`.

## 9. Candidate Solution Approaches

### Approach 1: Monolithic Legacy CTMS Architecture
- **Mechanism**: Single monolithic application (e.g., Django or Laravel) handling web UI, database access, CDISC exports, and alerting logic in a single server process `[INFERENCE]`.
- **Required Data**: Monolithic PostgreSQL database storing all relational models `[INFERENCE]`.
- **Evaluation**:
  - *Problem Fit*: Moderate. Handles basic forms and dashboards `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Complexity*: Low initial development effort `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Explainability*: High `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Cost*: Very Low `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Scalability*: Poor under heavy FHIR bundle transformations or multi-center offline sync `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Hackathon Feasibility*: High `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Production Feasibility*: Low (hard to maintain high availability and modular certification) `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Risks*: High coupling between regulatory reporting logic and UI code.

### Approach 2: Microservices Hybrid Architecture with Event-Driven Safety Router (RECOMMENDED)
- **Mechanism**: Modular micro-services backend (Node.js/FastAPI) split into: (1) Core CTMS Operational Engine, (2) CDISC/FHIR Data Transformation Gateway, (3) Pharmacovigilance & MedDRA Safety Service, and (4) ALCOA+ Immutable Audit Engine `[INFERENCE]`.
- **Required Data**: Relational PostgreSQL for transactional CTMS data, Redis for real-time alerting/caching, and PostgreSQL JSONB for raw FHIR resources `[INFERENCE]`.
- **Evaluation**:
  - *Problem Fit*: Excellent. Perfectly addresses operational tracking, interoperability, and safety routing `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Accuracy Potential*: Very High (isolated transformation pipelines eliminate data corruption) `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Complexity*: Moderate `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Explainability*: High `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Cost*: Low-to-Medium `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Scalability*: Excellent (services scale independently during heavy trial recruitment or batch reporting) `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Hackathon Feasibility*: Very High when modularized effectively `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Production Feasibility*: High (aligned with ISO 27001 enterprise cloud patterns) `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Risks*: Higher API communication overhead between services.

### Approach 3: Fully Serverless / Low-Code BaaS Architecture
- **Mechanism**: Client-heavy frontend connected directly to Firebase/Supabase with serverless edge functions for transformations `[INFERENCE]`.
- **Required Data**: NoSQL / Cloud Firestore database `[INFERENCE]`.
- **Evaluation**:
  - *Problem Fit*: Moderate for MVP, poor for complex CDISC SDTM exports and ALCOA+ compliance `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Complexity*: Low initially, extremely high when implementing 21 CFR Part 11 compliant audit trails `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Explainability*: Medium `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Cost*: Low for prototype, unpredictable at production scale `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Scalability*: High `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Hackathon Feasibility*: High `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Production Feasibility*: Low (violates strict data residency and ALCOA+ audit requirements if third-party managed BaaS is used) `[INFERENCE]`.
  - *Risks*: Data lock-in and non-compliance with CERT-In on-premise/sovereign cloud mandates `[INFERENCE]`.

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

### Selected Solution
**Approach 2: Microservices Hybrid Architecture with Event-Driven Safety Router** `[INFERENCE]`.

### Justification & Rejection of Alternatives
- **Why Approach 1 was rejected**: A monolithic system creates severe technical debt when managing distinct concerns such as real-time WebSocket KPI streaming, CPU-heavy CDISC Define-XML generation, and sensitive pharmacovigilance causality assessment. A bug in dashboard UI rendering could disrupt critical 24-hour SAE notification delivery `[INFERENCE]`.
- **Why Approach 3 was rejected**: Serverless BaaS solutions (like Firebase) introduce compliance roadblocks regarding CERT-In cloud empanelment, absolute data sovereignty, and deterministic ALCOA+ cryptographic audit trail generation `[INFERENCE]`.
- **Why Approach 2 Wins**:
  1. *Decoupled Regulatory Safety Engine*: Ensures AE/SAE reports are ingested, coded, and routed within legal timelines regardless of overall system load `[INFERENCE]`.
  2. *Interoperability Isolation*: The FHIR R4 and CDISC converter operates as an isolated worker service, converting operational data into SDTM/ADaM without polluting core CTMS transactional tables `[INFERENCE]`.
  3. *Audit Trail Integrity*: A dedicated audit service guarantees append-only logging with cryptographic hashing, satisfying GCP-ASU and 21 CFR Part 11 requirements `[INFERENCE]`.

### Detailed Data-Flow Narrative
1. **Participant Registration & Consent Flow**:
   - The Study Coordinator inputs participant details or scans the ABHA QR code `[INFERENCE]`.
   - The **ABDM/FHIR Adapter** validates the 14-digit ABHA ID against the ABDM Sandbox `[RESEARCHED]`.
   - The participant signs the electronic consent (eConsent) form on an Android PWA tablet. The **Privacy Engine** seals the document with an AES-256 digital signature, stores the PDF in Object Storage, and registers the consent token `[INFERENCE]`.

2. **eCRF Clinical Data Entry & Audit Log Flow**:
   - The Coordinator enters visit observations (e.g., vital signs, Ayurvedic drug administration) into CDASH-aligned eCRFs `[PS]`.
   - Upon form submission, the system prompts for e-Signature re-authentication if completing a critical milestone `[PS]`.
   - The data is written to PostgreSQL, and simultaneously, the **ALCOA+ Audit Service** creates a time-stamped ledger entry capturing `User ID`, `IP Address`, `Timestamp`, `Field ID`, `Old Value`, `New Value`, `Reason for Change`, and the `SHA-256 hash` of the previous log entry `[INFERENCE]`.

3. **Adverse Event (AE/SAE) & Pharmacovigilance Flow**:
   - If an Adverse Event is flagged, the **PV Safety Router** intercepts the submission `[PS]`.
   - The system triggers an automated MedDRA lookup tool to auto-suggest Preferred Terms (PT) and System Organ Classes (SOC) `[RESEARCHED]`.
   - If marked as *Serious (SAE)*, a 24-hour countdown timer is initiated `[RESEARCHED]`. The **Notification Engine** immediately dispatches urgent alerts to the PI, Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC), and NPvCC lead `[PS]`.
   - The PV Officer performs a causality assessment (WHO-UMC scale) and generates a standardized SAE reporting bundle `[PS]`.

4. **CDISC Submission Export Flow**:
   - Upon trial completion or interim DSMB analysis, the PI requests a regulatory export `[PS]`.
   - The **CDISC Export Engine** fetches validated CDASH eCRF data, executes transformation rules, and maps records into SDTM domains (`DM`, `AE`, `EX`, `VS`) and ADaM analysis datasets (`ADSL`, `ADAE`) `[PS]`.
   - It automatically generates `Define.xml` (version 2.1) specifying metadata, origin, and controlled terminology, delivering a downloadable `.zip` package ready for regulatory submission `[PS]`.

## 13. AI/ML Justification

AI should **NOT** be used for core compliance logic, data integrity validation, access control, or regulatory audit logging `[INFERENCE]`. These processes must remain 100% deterministic, transparent, and rule-based `[INFERENCE]`. 

However, specialized ML modules add significant value in specific operational domains:

### Module 1: MedDRA / WHODrug Auto-Coding Assistant
1. *Exact Problem Solved*: Natural language adverse event descriptions written by field coordinators in free text (e.g., "patient developed severe burning stomach pain after taking *Guggulu*") must be mapped to standardized MedDRA Low Level Terms (LLT) and Preferred Terms (PT) `[PS]`.
2. *Input*: Free-text clinical reaction string and concomitant drug name `[INFERENCE]`.
3. *Output*: Top 3 ranked MedDRA LLT/PT codes with confidence scores and matching System Organ Class (SOC) `[INFERENCE]`.
4. *Why Simpler System Fails*: Exact keyword matching fails due to spelling variations, clinical synonyms, and multi-word phrase nuances `[INFERENCE]`.
5. *Data Required*: MedDRA v27.0 dictionary term hierarchy and historical coded AE logs `[RESEARCHED]`.
6. *Evaluation*: Top-1 and Top-3 accuracy against expert human coder assignments `[INFERENCE]`.
7. *When Wrong*: Human PV Officer can override the recommendation with a single click `[INFERENCE]`.
8. *Requires Training*: No `[INFERENCE]`.
9. *Requires Fine-Tuning*: No `[INFERENCE]`.
10. *Requires RAG*: Yes, vector search (Cosine similarity over MedDRA embedding index) `[INFERENCE]`.
11. *Requires Computer Vision*: No `[INFERENCE]`.
12. *Requires Speech Processing*: No `[INFERENCE]`.
13. *Requires Forecasting*: No `[INFERENCE]`.
14. *Human Oversight Required*: **Yes, Mandatory**. All auto-coded terms remain in "Pending Review" status until confirmed by a certified PV Officer `[PS]`.

### Module 2: Recruitment Lag & Dropout Forecasting Engine
1. *Exact Problem Solved*: Predicts study target completion dates and identifies sites likely to miss recruitment targets based on historical enrolment velocity `[PS]`.
2. *Input*: Historical recruitment rate, site location, target sample size, protocol complexity index `[INFERENCE]`.
3. *Output*: Predicted enrolment curve, probability of delay (0-100%), and predicted completion month `[INFERENCE]`.
4. *Why Simpler System Fails*: Linear extrapolations fail to capture seasonal dropouts or site activation lags `[INFERENCE]`.
5. *Data Required*: Anonymized historical trial enrolment timelines `[INFERENCE]`.
6. *Evaluation*: Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) on target completion dates `[INFERENCE]`.
7. *When Wrong*: Visual warning indicator displayed; non-critical operational decision `[INFERENCE]`.
8. *Requires Training*: Yes (Classical Random Forest Regressor / XGBoost) `[INFERENCE]`.
9. *Requires Fine-Tuning*: No `[INFERENCE]`.
10. *Requires RAG*: No `[INFERENCE]`.
11. *Requires Computer Vision*: No `[INFERENCE]`.
12. *Requires Speech Processing*: No `[INFERENCE]`.
13. *Requires Forecasting*: **Yes**. Time-series forecasting `[INFERENCE]`.
14. *Human Oversight Required*: Optional advisory metric for institutional leadership `[INFERENCE]`.

## 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Database Schema Core Design (PostgreSQL Relational + JSONB)

```sql
-- 1. STUDIES TABLE
CREATE TABLE studies (
    study_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    ctri_number VARCHAR(50) UNIQUE NOT NULL, -- e.g. CTRI/2024/05/065123
    protocol_title TEXT NOT NULL,
    pi_user_id UUID NOT NULL,
    phase VARCHAR(20) CHECK (phase IN ('Phase I', 'Phase II', 'Phase III', 'Phase IV', 'Observational')),
    target_sample_size INT NOT NULL,
    current_enrolled INT DEFAULT 0,
    iec_approval_status VARCHAR(30) DEFAULT 'Pending',
    status VARCHAR(30) DEFAULT 'Draft', -- Draft, Active, Completed, Suspended
    created_at TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW()
);

-- 2. SUBJECTS & CONSENT TABLE
CREATE TABLE subjects (
    subject_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    study_id UUID REFERENCES studies(study_id),
    site_id UUID NOT NULL,
    screening_number VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    randomization_number VARCHAR(50),
    abha_number_hash VARCHAR(64), -- SHA-256 de-identified ABHA identifier
    consent_status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'Consented',
    consent_timestamp TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL,
    created_at TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW()
);

-- 3. ALCOA+ IMMUTABLE AUDIT TRAIL TABLE
CREATE TABLE audit_ledger (
    audit_id BIGSERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
    entity_name VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL, -- e.g., 'ecrf_entries'
    entity_id UUID NOT NULL,
    action VARCHAR(20) NOT NULL, -- INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE, SIGN
    performed_by UUID NOT NULL,
    role VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    timestamp TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW(),
    old_values JSONB,
    new_values JSONB,
    reason_for_change TEXT,
    client_ip VARCHAR(45),
    previous_hash VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL, -- SHA-256 Hash Chain
    current_hash VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL
);

-- 4. ADVERSE EVENTS & PHARMACOVIGILANCE TABLE
CREATE TABLE adverse_events (
    ae_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    study_id UUID REFERENCES studies(study_id),
    subject_id UUID REFERENCES subjects(subject_id),
    verbatim_term TEXT NOT NULL,
    meddra_llt_code VARCHAR(20),
    meddra_pt_code VARCHAR(20),
    is_serious BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE,
    onset_date TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL,
    reporting_deadline TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL, -- Onset + 24 Hours for SAE
    causality_score VARCHAR(30), -- WHO-UMC scale: Certain, Probable, Possible, Unlikely
    pv_status VARCHAR(30) DEFAULT 'Logged' -- Logged, Coded, Reported_to_IEC, Finalized
);
```

### Offline & Low-Network Synchronization Flow
1. **Local Offline Storage**:
   - The PWA uses **IndexedDB** managed via `Dexie.js` to cache CDASH form definitions, subject queues, and offline eCRF entries locally `[INFERENCE]`.
2. **Optimistic Local Execution**:
   - Coordinators can complete visit forms offline. Records are tagged with `sync_status: 'pending_upload'` and a client-side timestamp `[INFERENCE]`.
3. **Background Synchronization & Conflict Resolution**:
   - A Service Worker listens for network restoration (`navigator.onLine`).
   - *Conflict Resolution Rule*: **Server-Wins for Protocol Meta, Client-Preserves for eCRF Observations**. If two coordinators modify different fields in the same subject record, changes are merged. If the same field is modified, the server flags a "Data Query" requiring explicit CRA/PI manual review, preserving both entries in the audit ledger `[INFERENCE]`.
4. **Bandwidth Reduction**:
   - Payloads are compressed using gzip/Brotli. Only JSON deltas (changes) are transmitted over the wire rather than full eCRF structures `[INFERENCE]`.

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

1. **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 / 2025 Rules Alignment**:
   - *Data Minimisation*: Only clinically necessary data elements defined in the trial protocol are collected `[PS]`.
   - *Purpose Limitation*: Patient data collected for Trial X cannot be repurposed without explicit re-consent `[PS]`.
   - *Consent Manager Integration*: Connects with ABDM Consent Manager (HIE-CM) allowing participants to view and revoke research consent `[RESEARCHED]`.

2. **21 CFR Part 11 & GCP Data Integrity (ALCOA+)**:
   - **Attributable**: Every action logged with user ID, role, and digital signature `[PS]`.
   - **Legible**: Audit logs stored in structured JSON and human-readable timeline formats `[PS]`.
   - **Contemporaneous**: Automatic server timestamping (NTP time-synced) at exact execution time `[PS]`.
   - **Original**: Primary eCRF submissions stored without destructive overwrites `[PS]`.
   - **Accurate**: Built-in real-time field validation (range checks, mandatory field checks) `[PS]`.
   - **+ Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available**: Immutable SHA-256 hash chaining prevents retrospective log tampering `[PS]`.

3. **Encryption & Access Control**:
   - *Data at Rest*: AES-256 encryption on PostgreSQL database volumes and S3 document buckets `[PS]`.
   - *Data in Transit*: Enforced TLS 1.3 with strict HTTPS redirects `[PS]`.
   - *Authentication*: OAuth2 / JWT with mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for PI, CRA, and Admin roles `[INFERENCE]`.
   - *Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)*: Strict permission isolation (e.g., Ethics Committee members cannot edit clinical trial data; Monitors cannot edit eCRFs) `[PS]`.

4. **Cloud Infrastructure Security**:
   - Hosted on AWS Mumbai / GCP Delhi data-resident cloud certified for ISO/IEC 27001, ISO 27017, and MeitY/CERT-In security guidelines `[PS]`.
