# CropHealth-AI: Integrated Pest & Disease Early Detection, Forecasting, and Surveillance Platform for Maharashtra

---

## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
CropHealth-AI is an end-to-end, offline-first digital agricultural platform designed specifically for smallholder farmers, extension officers (Gram Sevaks/Krishi Sahayaks), and state agricultural administrators in Maharashtra [INFERENCE]. By fusing mobile edge computer vision for multi-symptom diagnosis, automated/manual pest-trap sensor logging, multi-factor micro-climate forecasting (combining weather, soil, crop stage, variety, and historical incidence), and geospatial hotspot analytics, the platform shifts crop protection from reactive chemical treatment to proactive Integrated Pest & Disease Management (IPM/IDM) [INFERENCE]. The system operates seamlessly in low-connectivity rural environments, routes ambiguous diagnoses to Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) agronomists for human-in-the-loop validation, dispatches localized Marathi advisories, and continuously improves model accuracy via field confirmations [INFERENCE].

To deliver a field-ready, offline-capable digital surveillance and advisory ecosystem that combines multi-modal AI diagnosis, weather-agronomic forecasting, expert validation, and GIS hotspot mapping to reduce crop yield loss and pesticide overuse across rainfed and irrigated farming clusters in Maharashtra [INFERENCE].

## 2. Problem Deconstruction

The problem statement explicitly outlines key operational gaps and desired system capabilities [PS]. The atomic breakdown preserves all original requirements:

*   **Requirement 2.a — Visual Disease & Pest Diagnosis:** "supports image based symptom identification" [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* Farmers and extension workers can take leaf/stem/fruit photos on budget Android devices and receive instant diagnostic predictions [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* On-device Quantized Computer Vision (TFLite/ONNX) model paired with cloud fallback for low-confidence scans [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.
*   **Requirement 2.b — Trap & Sensor Data Ingestion:** "pest-trap or sensor inputs" [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* The platform must log insect counts from manual pheromone traps, sticky traps, or automated camera-enabled IoT traps [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Structured input forms for extension staff and API endpoints for IoT trap telemetry [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.
*   **Requirement 2.c — Multi-Factor Weather & Agronomic Risk Forecasting:** "weather-based risk forecasting... Weather, crop stage, variety, soil condition and local pest history influence risk, but these inputs are rarely combined into actionable farm-level alerts." [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* Risk assessment cannot rely on images alone; it must synthesize 5 key variables: weather forecast, crop phenological stage, crop variety susceptibility, soil characteristics, and localized historical infestation trends [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* A multi-factor predictive risk engine that generates daily farm-level vulnerability scores before visual symptoms appear [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.
*   **Requirement 2.d — Geospatial Hotspot Mapping:** "geospatial hotspot mapping" [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* Pest and disease outbreaks must be aggregated geographically to identify cluster spreading at Gram Panchayat, Taluka, and District levels [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Spatial clustering algorithms (e.g., DBSCAN/Getis-Ord Gi*) rendering real-time heatmaps on an interactive GIS administrative dashboard [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.
*   **Requirement 2.e — Expert Human-in-the-Loop Validation:** "expert validation" [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* Automated AI diagnosis can be uncertain or wrong; field cases requiring high accuracy or involving quarantine pests must be escalated to human agronomists [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Triage queuing system for KVK scientists/extension officers to review flagged images, update diagnoses, and approve targeted advisories [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.
*   **Requirement 2.f — Localized Multilingual Communication:** "multilingual advisories" [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* Information must be delivered in local languages (primarily Marathi, with Hindi/English options) through audio/text to overcome literacy barriers [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Voice-enabled localized advisory engine using Marathi text-to-speech (TTS) and localized structured advisories [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.
*   **Requirement 2.g — Actionable IPM/IDM & Safe Input Guidance:** "recommend integrated pest and disease management actions, safe input usage" [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* Recommendations must prioritize biological/cultural controls and provide exact chemical dosage and safety guidelines (dosage per acre, water ratio, PHI - Pre-Harvest Interval) to eliminate residue and overuse issues [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Rule-based agronomic decision tree aligned with ICAR/CIB&RC package of practices and dosage calculators [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.
*   **Requirement 2.h — Escalation & Diagnostic Referral:** "referral to extension or laboratories" [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* Severe, unknown, or systemic outbreaks must trigger physical visits from extension workers or physical sample collection for laboratory testing [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Digital ticket system generating referral passes with geotagged plot locations for state diagnostic labs [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.
*   **Requirement 2.i — Follow-up Monitoring & Efficacy Feedback:** "follow-up monitoring" [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* System must track whether recommended actions were taken and whether the pest/disease was successfully controlled after 7–14 days [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Automated push/SMS reminders prompting farmers to upload follow-up pictures or update status [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.
*   **Requirement 2.j — Active Field Confirmation & Continuous Model Learning:** "It should learn from field confirmations" [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* Ground truth validations by extension officers and laboratory reports must be fed back into the training pipeline to prevent concept drift [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Active learning pipeline and dataset versioning system for re-training AI models on verified local field images [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.
*   **Requirement 2.k — Administrative Surveillance Dashboards:** "dashboards for agriculture officials" [PS].
    *   *Interpretation:* State/District officials need macro-level visibility for resource allocation (pesticide inventory, extension staffing, emergency containment) [INFERENCE].
    *   *Implementation Implication:* Multi-tier web dashboard displaying real-time incidence rates, outbreak vectors, response times, and pesticide usage patterns [INFERENCE]. Tag: `[PS]`.

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1.  **Connectivity Limitations:** Over 60% of rural farmlands in Marathwada and Vidarbha experience frequent 2G/3G degradation or complete signal loss [INFERENCE]. *System must execute image diagnosis and display local advisory offline [INFERENCE].*
2.  **Hardware Constraints:** Farmers use low-end Android smartphones (2GB–3GB RAM, budget processors, low-resolution cameras) [INFERENCE]. *Mobile application binary size must be under 30MB, with model footprint under 12MB [INFERENCE].*
3.  **Language & Digital Literacy:** The primary language across rural Maharashtra is Marathi [KNOWN]. Many smallholder farmers struggle with text-heavy UI [INFERENCE]. *UI must be icon-driven with voice input/output in Marathi [INFERENCE].*
4.  **Agro-Climatic Diversity:** Maharashtra features 9 distinct agro-climatic zones ranging from heavy-rainfall Konkan coastal belt to drought-prone Rain Shadow areas in Madhya Maharashtra and Eastern Vidarbha [KNOWN]. *Risk models must be tuned to zone-specific microclimates [INFERENCE].*
5.  **Seasonal Crop Dynamics:** Major Kharif crops (Cotton, Soybean, Paddy, Tur) and Rabi crops (Gram, Jowar) have tight cultivation windows where delay in advisory (>48 hours) leads to total crop loss [RESEARCHED] [1.10].

*   **Mechanism:** Offline mobile app (Flutter + On-device TFLite model) classifying disease from leaf photos [INFERENCE].
*   **Required Data:** Image dataset only [INFERENCE].
*   **Architecture:** Mobile app with embedded ML weights [INFERENCE].
*   **Complexity:** Low [INFERENCE].
*   **Cost:** Minimal [INFERENCE].

*   **Risks:** High false-positive rate under bad lighting; complete lack of context (weather/stage) [INFERENCE].

### Approach 2: Server-Centric Rule-Based Risk Forewarning Portal
*   **Mechanism:** Web backend calculating pest risk purely from weather thresholds and crop stage tables, dispatching SMS alerts [INFERENCE].
*   **Required Data:** Weather forecasts, crop calendar tables [INFERENCE].
*   **Architecture:** Relational DB + Scheduled Cron jobs + SMS Gateway [INFERENCE].
*   **Complexity:** Medium [INFERENCE].
*   **Cost:** Low [INFERENCE].
*   **Explainability:** High (transparent IF-THEN rules) [INFERENCE].
*   **Scalability:** High [INFERENCE].
*   **Hackathon Feasibility:** High [INFERENCE].
*   **Production Feasibility:** Medium (lacks visual symptom identification and ground-truth confirmation) [INFERENCE].
*   **Risks:** High false-alarm rate without visual/trap field verification [INFERENCE].

### Approach 3: Hybrid Multi-Modal Surveillance & Advisory Platform (Recommended)
*   **Mechanism:** An integrated architecture combining:
    1. Offline Mobile Edge AI for initial image-based symptom classification [INFERENCE].
    2. Agronomic Risk Engine synthesizing weather, phenology, variety, soil, and trap sensor data using hybrid decision rules + XGBoost [INFERENCE].
    3. PostGIS-based Geospatial Hotspot Engine for administrative surveillance [INFERENCE].
    4. Human-in-the-Loop Expert Triage Workflow for low-confidence or high-impact diagnoses [INFERENCE].
    5. Continuous Feedback Loop retraining edge models from confirmed field cases [INFERENCE].
*   **Required Data:** Multi-modal (Images, Weather APIs, Trap Telemetry, Soil Maps, Historical DB) [INFERENCE].
*   **Architecture:** Mobile Client (Offline First) + Cloud Backend (FastAPI, PostGIS, PyTorch Server, Celery Workers) + Web GIS Dashboard [INFERENCE].
*   **Complexity:** High (modular, well-defined components) [INFERENCE].
*   **Cost:** Moderate [INFERENCE].
*   **Explainability:** High (AI output combined with explicit agronomic weather rule explanation) [INFERENCE].
*   **Scalability:** High (decoupled microservices) [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

**Selection:** **Approach 3 (Hybrid Multi-Modal Surveillance & Advisory Platform)** [INFERENCE].

### Why Alternatives Were Rejected
*   **Approach 1 Rejection:** Pure computer vision apps fail when symptoms are early or non-visual (e.g., root rot, stem borer before deadheart appears). They completely ignore explicit PS requirements for weather forecasting, trap sensor integration, and official GIS dashboards [PS, INFERENCE].
*   **Approach 2 Rejection:** Pure weather-rule systems miss actual localized pest occurrences and lack visual confirmation tools. They generate excessive false alerts, causing alert fatigue among farmers [INFERENCE].

### System Superiority
Approach 3 fulfills every explicit requirement of problem statement `SIH26131` [PS]. It provides immediate value offline to the farmer via edge AI, validates edge predictions against local micro-climate risk profiles, routes doubtful cases to experts, and aggregates field data into actionable intelligence for government officials [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data Flow Narrative

1.  **Field Capture & Edge Inference:** A farmer or Krishi Sahayak selects crop type, crop stage, and takes a leaf/pest photo. The local TFLite model runs inference on the mobile GPU/NPU in under 300ms [INFERENCE].
2.  **Confidence Check & Local Storage:**
    *   If confidence $\ge 85\%$ and the diagnosis aligns with local weather risk thresholds, the app displays the Marathi advisory immediately (offline) [INFERENCE].
    *   If confidence $< 85\%$ or the pest is a high-risk quarantine species, the app marks the item for "Expert Verification" [INFERENCE].
3.  **Background Synchronization:** Once internet connectivity (2G/3G/Wi-Fi) is detected, the app syncs geotagged image payloads, trap counts, and farm logs to the API Gateway via encrypted compressed JSON [INFERENCE].
4.  **Multi-Factor Risk Assessment:** The backend fetches weather forecast data (Open-Meteo), retrieves the crop stage and variety vulnerability, and runs the XGBoost risk forecaster [INFERENCE].
5.  **Geospatial Hotspot Processing:** A background Celery worker executes DBSCAN spatial clustering over the incoming geotagged reports. If a cluster of $>5$ similar pest cases is detected within a 2km radius inside 48 hours, a "Hotspot Warning" is automatically raised [INFERENCE].
6.  **Expert Triage & Laboratory Escalation:** Low-confidence scans land in the KVK Agronomist Web Portal. The scientist reviews high-resolution images alongside historical plot weather data, updates the diagnosis, and clicks "Approve Advisory" [INFERENCE]. If necessary, a diagnostic lab ticket with a QR code is issued to the farmer [INFERENCE].
7.  **Farmer Advisory & Continuous Learning:** The confirmed diagnosis updates the farmer's mobile app via push notification/SMS in Marathi audio/text [INFERENCE]. The validated image and metadata are committed to the ML Retraining Staging Bucket [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### 1. Leaf Symptom Classifier
*   **Purpose:** Instant visual identification of disease/pest symptoms from leaf/stem/fruit photographs [PS].
*   **Model Type:** Dual-tier vision system: Quantized MobileNetV3-Small on Edge; Swin Transformer Small on Server [INFERENCE].
*   **Input:** RGB Image ($224 \times 224$ edge / $384 \times 384$ cloud) [INFERENCE].
*   **Output:** Class probabilities across top 30 crop-disease pairs + Bounding boxes for symptoms [INFERENCE].
*   **Data Required:** 25,000+ annotated images (PlantVillage, Kaggle Indian Agriculture subsets) [RESEARCHED].
*   **Why AI:** Manual diagnostic knowledge takes years of agronomic training; visual AI grants instant expertise to rural farmers [INFERENCE].
*   **Evaluation Metric:** Top-1 Accuracy ($\ge 90\%$), Macro F1-Score ($\ge 0.88$) [INFERENCE].
*   **Failure Mode:** Blurry images, low light, or complex mixed infections. *Mitigation:* Blur detector prompts re-take; low confidence triggers KVK expert triage [INFERENCE].
*   **Human Oversight:** High. Scans $<85\%$ confidence are auto-escalated to human agronomists [INFERENCE].

### 2. Multi-Factor Agronomic Risk Forecasting Engine
*   **Purpose:** Forecast pest outbreak likelihood 3–7 days before visible symptoms spread [PS].
*   **Model Type:** Hybrid Rule-Based Decision Logic + Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (XGBoost) [INFERENCE].
*   **Input:** 7-day weather forecast (Temp, RH, Rainfall), Crop Phenology Stage (days post-sowing), Variety Susceptibility Index (0–1), Soil Moisture Stress Index, Historical Tehsil Infestation Index [PS, INFERENCE].
*   **Output:** Outbreak Risk Level (Low / Medium / High / Severe) + Key Driving Weather Triggers [INFERENCE].
*   **Data Required:** Historical weather-pest correlation datasets (ICAR-NCIPM rulebooks) [RESEARCHED] [3.8, 4.7].
*   **Why AI/ML:** Biological pest development exhibits non-linear interactions between humidity, temperature, and crop stage that simple threshold rules cannot fully capture [INFERENCE].
*   **Evaluation Metric:** Receiver Operating Characteristic - Area Under Curve (ROC-AUC $\ge 0.85$) [INFERENCE].
*   **Failure Mode:** Inaccurate weather forecast inputs. *Mitigation:* Fall back to deterministic ICAR "thumb rules" if forecast variance is high [RESEARCHED] [4.7].
*   **Human Oversight:** District agronomists receive forewarning summaries prior to mass farmer broadcast [INFERENCE].

### 3. Non-AI System Components
*   *Dosage & Water Volume Calculator:* Pure deterministic math based on CIB&RC label claims ($\text{Dose/Acre} \times \text{Plot Area}$) [RESEARCHED] [3.8].
*   *Geospatial Hotspot Clustering:* Deterministic spatial query (`ST_ClusterDBSCAN`) [INFERENCE].
*   *Referral Ticket Routing:* Database relational lookup by proximity to nearest registered lab/KVK [INFERENCE].
