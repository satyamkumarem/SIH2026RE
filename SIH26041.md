# 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
The Government of Jharkhand faces a critical occupational safety challenge in its mining and manufacturing belts (Dhanbad, East/West Singhbhum, Ranchi). Hundreds of thousands of workers—many newly recruited tribal contract workers—enter high-risk underground coal mines, steel plants, and mica processing units with minimal practical safety orientation. Static classroom manuals yield under 20% knowledge retention after one week, while VR headsets remain cost-prohibitive and impractical for remote small-scale operations. Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS) records show 48 fatal accidents in Jharkhand in 2022–23, predominantly involving workers in their first 30 days. To eliminate this gap, we propose **SurakshaAR**, a lightweight, offline-first mobile Augmented Reality (AR) safety training and verification platform. Running on mid-range Android 10+ smartphones without external headsets, SurakshaAR overlays interactive safety protocols—such as fire extinguisher operation and gas leak response—onto the worker’s physical environment in Hindi and Santali (Ol Chiki script). It pairs gamified AR simulation with a tamper-proof, offline-verifiable QR certificate system and a central web compliance dashboard for mine management and safety inspectors under the Mines Act, 1952 and Factories Act, 1948.

# 2. Problem Deconstruction

The explicit problem statement supplied in the SIH JSON is broken down into atomic requirements below. Each requirement is mapped to its source text, domain interpretation, implementation implications, and confidence rating.

### Requirement 1: Mobile AR-Based Delivery without External Headsets
* **Source Text:** "Design and develop a mobile AR-based vocational training and safety certification platform running on mid-range Android smartphones (Android 10+, no external headset required)..." [PS]
* **Interpretation:** The application must deliver spatial augmented reality using standard smartphone camera feeds and inertial measurement units (IMU). Hardware dependencies like dedicated LiDAR, dual-depth cameras, or VR/AR smart glasses (Meta Quest, Apple Vision Pro, HoloLens) are strictly prohibited.
* **Implementation Implication:** Must utilize Google ARCore (via Sceneview SDK / Filament) for feature-point tracking, surface detection, and spatial anchor placement, optimized for devices with $\ge 2\text{ GB}$ RAM and mid-tier SoCs (e.g., Snapdragon 600/700 series, MediaTek Helio G-series).
* **Confidence Tag:** [PS]

### Requirement 2: Domain-Specific Interactive AR Safety Modules
* **Source Text:** "...interactive AR training modules covering five industrial safety domains: (1) Fire & Explosion Response-exit identification, extinguisher use, and evacuation sequencing overlaid on real surroundings via phone camera; (2) Gas Leak & Confined Space Protocol-hazard zone recognition, PPE selection, and buddy-system procedures simulated in AR; (3) Machinery..." [PS]
* **Interpretation:** Must support 5 distinct safety domains, with explicit detailed requirements for Domain 1 (Fire & Explosion) and Domain 2 (Gas Leak & Confined Space). By domain inference from the truncated text and statutory mining regulations (Mines Act 1952 / CMR 2017), Domain 3 covers Heavy Machinery Safety / Lockout-Tagout (LOTO), Domain 4 covers Roof Fall & Ground Control, and Domain 5 covers Electrical Hazards & First Aid.
* **Implementation Implication:** Modules must include 3D asset interaction (glTF/GLB models), step-by-step gesture inputs (tap to select PPE, sweep camera to locate emergency exits, pull fire extinguisher pin), and spatial audio instructions.
* **Confidence Tag:** [PS] (for Domains 1 & 2); [INFERENCE] (for explicit scope of Domains 3, 4, 5).

### Requirement 3: Multi-Lingual Localisation (Hindi & Santali)
* **Source Text:** "...Hindi and Santali localisation..." [PS]
* **Interpretation:** All UI text, audio narrations, instruction cards, and assessment questions must be fully localized into Hindi (Devanagari script) and Santali (Ol Chiki script).
* **Implementation Implication:** Must embed custom Unicode fonts (`Noto Sans Ol Chiki`) [RESEARCHED] and pre-rendered audio voiceover files in the APK assets to ensure Ol Chiki rendering and spoken guidance work seamlessly offline without system font dependency.
* **Confidence Tag:** [PS]

### Requirement 4: Complete Offline Functionality
* **Source Text:** "...offline functionality..." [PS]
* **Interpretation:** The mobile app must perform all core features—AR rendering, module progression, scoring, assessment grading, certificate generation, and local audit logging—100% offline, without requiring active cellular or Wi-Fi connectivity.
* **Implementation Implication:** 3D assets, audio clips, local SQLite/Room database, and cryptographic keypairs for offline digital signatures must be bundled directly within the Android client. Async background sync queues must push completion logs when network connection is restored.
* **Confidence Tag:** [PS]

### Requirement 5: Assessment Engine & Comprehension Verification
* **Source Text:** "...an assessment engine... physical certificates have no mechanism to verify comprehension." [PS]
* **Interpretation:** After completing an AR practical simulation, workers must undergo a practical assessment (in-AR hazard identification) and a short visual/audio quiz to measure retention and competency before certification.
* **Implementation Implication:** Objective scoring algorithm combining AR interaction speed, hazard spot accuracy, PPE selection correctness, and quiz scores. Must enforce a minimum passing threshold (e.g., 80%) before generating a certificate.
* **Confidence Tag:** [PS]

### Requirement 6: QR-Based Certificate Generation & Verification
* **Source Text:** "...QR-based certificate generation and verification..." [PS]
* **Interpretation:** Successful assessment completion must generate a digital certificate containing a cryptographically signed QR code that can be scanned offline by mine safety inspectors or shift supervisors to verify authenticity and comprehension stats.
* **Implementation Implication:** The app must generate a PDF/Image certificate containing a QR code embedded with an HMAC-SHA256 signature or asymmetric RSA/Ed25519 payload containing Worker ID, Module ID, Score, Date, and Hash. The verification engine (web or mobile scanner) verifies this signature against a public key without needing server round-trips.
* **Confidence Tag:** [PS]

### Requirement 7: Web Admin Compliance Dashboard
* **Source Text:** "...and a web admin compliance dashboard..." [PS]
* **Interpretation:** Mine managers, DISH (Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health) officers, and DGMS auditors require a web portal to view site-wide compliance metrics, worker pass rates, pending certifications, and individual worker assessment history.
* **Implementation Implication:** Responsive Web Portal (React/TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Node.js/FastAPI) displaying compliance charts, worker registries, downloadable audit reports, and QR verification tools.
* **Confidence Tag:** [PS]

* **Source Text:** "A working Android APK demonstrating at least two complete AR training modules, an assessment engine, QR-based certificate generation and verification, Hindi and Santali localisation, offline functionality, and a web admin compliance dashboard- submitted with a demo video and public GitHub repository." # 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

### 1. Underground & Remote Field Connectivity
* **Reality:** Deep underground coal mines (e.g., BCCL/ECL pits in Dhanbad/Jharia) and open-cast mica mines have **zero cellular network or Wi-Fi signal** due to rock mass attenuation. [KNOWN]
* **Impact:** App MUST execute $100\%$ of training, evaluation, certificate generation, and QR verification locally without a single HTTP network call.

### 2. Device Hardware Constraints (Mid-Range Android)
* **Reality:** Workers and local ITIs utilize low-to-mid range Android smartphones (e.g., Redmi, Realme, Samsung A-series running Android 10–12, 2GB–4GB RAM, MediaTek/Snapdragon low-tier SoCs). [RESEARCHED] [4.2]
* **Impact:** High-poly 3D models ($\ge 50\text{ MB}$) or heavy game engines (unoptimized Unity builds resulting in $200\text{MB+}$ APKs) cause thermal throttling, camera lag, and AR Core crashes. Asset budgets must be strictly kept under 50,000 polygons with PBR textures $\le 1024\times 1024$.

### 3. Language & Literacy Profile
* **Reality:** Large proportion of recruits are Santhal tribal youth with limited formal literacy in English or high Hindi, but fully fluent in spoken Santali and Ol Chiki script. [PS]
* **Impact:** Minimal dense text in UI. The system must prioritize **iconographic navigation**, visual 3D cues, and **voice-guided spatial audio** in Santali and simple Hindi.

### 4. Field Environmental Conditions
* **Reality:** Mining sites contain dust, high ambient noise ($\ge 85\text{ dBA}$ from heavy machinery), and varying lighting conditions (bright sun in open-cast, dim headlamps in underground drifts). [KNOWN]
* **Impact:** High-contrast UI elements, subtitle overlays for spatial audio, and robust AR surface detection that relies on ground feature points rather than delicate lighting estimation.

### Approach 1: Standalone Unity AR Foundation Application (C#)
* **Mechanism:** Built using Unity Engine (2022.3 LTS) with AR Foundation plugin mapping to ARCore.
* **Pros:** Rich visual effects, easy particle systems for fire and gas plumes, rapid 3D drag-and-drop scene assembly.
* **Cons:** Large output APK size ($\ge 180\text{ MB}$), high cold-boot time ($\ge 8\text{ seconds}$ on mid-range phones), difficult to render non-standard Unicode fonts like Ol Chiki natively in UI text views without custom shader plugins, high RAM consumption causing crashes on 2GB RAM devices.

### Approach 2: Native Android Kotlin with Sceneview SDK (Filament + ARCore) [RECOMMENDED]
* **Mechanism:** Developed natively in Kotlin using Jetpack Compose for UI and Google’s `Sceneview` (Filament-based Kotlin successor to Sceneform) for 3D/AR rendering. [RESEARCHED] [1.2]
* **Pros:** Ultra-lightweight APK ($\approx 35–45\text{ MB}$), instant startup, zero engine overhead, native Android system font rendering for Santali Ol Chiki (`Noto Sans Ol Chiki`), seamless integration with Jetpack Room DB, Android WorkManager, and native Java Cryptography APIs (`java.security`).
* **Cons:** Particle effects (like realistic fire/smoke) require manual shader customization in Filament compared to Unity's visual particle editor.

### Approach 3: Progressive Web Application (WebAR via MindAR / 8th Wall)
* **Mechanism:** Browser-based WebAR application using Three.js and WebXR APIs.
* **Pros:** Zero app installation required; accessible via browser link.
* **Cons:** Fails completely offline without local PWA pre-caching; camera framerate and plane detection in WebXR on mid-range Android browsers (Chrome Android 10) are unstable and prone to memory leaks; no native background sync workers when the browser is closed.

### Detailed Data Flow Narrative
1. **App Initialization & Worker Selection:** The worker launches the SurakshaAR Android app offline. The app loads localized strings and pre-recorded audio in Hindi or Santali (Ol Chiki) based on user preference stored in Jetpack DataStore.
2. **AR Session Launch:** The worker selects an industrial domain (e.g., *Gas Leak Protocol*). The `ArSceneView` initializes Google ARCore, asking the worker to scan the surrounding floor to detect horizontal/vertical surfaces.
3. **Interactive Simulation:** 
   * The app overlays a virtual 3D gas leak scenario (plume asset + warning indicator) into the camera view.
   * Spoken audio in Santali guides the worker to identify the hazard zone, select the correct 3D PPE (Self-Rescuer mask) from an AR carousel, and complete a buddy-system evacuation sequence.
4. **Real-time Assessment:** The local Assessment Engine tracks interaction accuracy, time taken, and error count. Upon finishing the spatial simulation, a 3-question visual/audio quiz is presented.
5. **Offline Certificate Signing:** If score $\ge 80\%$, the client generates an immutable payload: `WorkerID|ModuleID|Score|Timestamp|DeviceUUID`. The Java Cryptography library signs this string using HMAC-SHA256 with a pre-shared app key, rendering it into a high-density QR code rendered on a digital certificate.
6. **Local Storage & Deferred Sync:** The session record and certificate vector are saved to the local Room SQLite DB. When the device returns to an area with connectivity (e.g., surface mine office), Android `WorkManager` silently triggers an HTTP POST request to sync logs with the Web Admin Portal.

# 13. AI/ML Justification

### Comprehensive AI Component Evaluation

To maintain strict engineering rigor, AI components are evaluated below against the mandatory 14-point criteria:

#### Component 1: Spatial Surface & Motion Tracking (ARCore SLAM / ML)
1. **What exact problem does AI solve?** Maps real-world camera pixels to 3D spatial points and tracks phone movement in 6 Degrees of Freedom (6DoF) without physical external markers.
2. **What is the input?** Real-time camera video frames ($60\text{ FPS}$) + IMU sensor data (accelerometer/gyroscope).
3. **What is the output?** Detected 3D surface planes and camera pose matrices ($X, Y, Z$ coordinates + rotation).
4. **Why can a simpler rule/system not solve it?** Fixed 2D overlays cannot adapt to user movement or room geometry; markerless AR requires feature point tracking and visual-inertial odometry ML models.
5. **What data is required?** Pre-trained ARCore edge vision model weights (bundled into Google Play Services for AR). [RESEARCHED] [4.1]
6. **How will it be evaluated?** Plane detection latency ($\le 2\text{ seconds}$) and spatial drift ($\le 1\text{ cm}$ over $3\text{ meters}$).
7. **What happens when it is wrong?** AR 3D objects jitter or float off the ground; app provides visual prompt: "Slowly move phone across a textured surface".
8. **Does it require training?** No (uses Google pre-trained model).
9. **Does it require fine-tuning?** No.
10. **Does it require RAG?** No.
11. **Does it require computer vision?** Yes (Visual-Inertial Odometry).
12. **Does it require speech processing?** No.
13. **Does it require forecasting?** No.
14. **Is human oversight required?** No (handled in real-time camera loop).

#### Component 2: On-Device Voice Guidance (Text-to-Speech / Pre-rendered Audio)
* **AI Evaluation Note:** Generative AI / LLMs / Online Speech APIs are **EXPLICITLY REJECTED** for the mobile app runtime. The app MUST use pre-rendered studio/human-validated audio files in Hindi and Santali. Generative LLMs exhibit hallucinations, high latency, and fail completely underground without internet connection.

#### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
* **Assessment Grading & Certification:** Must be $100\%$ deterministic rule-based logic (e.g., `Score = (Correct_Actions / Total_Actions) * 100`). AI/LLM evaluation must never decide whether a miner passes a statutory safety test due to non-deterministic hallucination risks.

# 14. Data Architecture & Offline/Low-Network Design

### Database Schema (Room SQLite Local Entity Design)

```sql
-- Worker Profile Table
CREATE TABLE workers (
    worker_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY NOT NULL,
    full_name TEXT NOT NULL,
    native_language TEXT NOT NULL, -- 'HI' or 'SAT'
    sector TEXT NOT NULL,          -- 'MINING', 'STEEL', 'MICA'
    assigned_mine_id TEXT NOT NULL
);

-- Safety Training Modules Table
CREATE TABLE modules (
    module_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY NOT NULL,
    title_hi TEXT NOT NULL,
    title_sat TEXT NOT NULL,
    domain_category TEXT NOT NULL, -- 'FIRE', 'GAS', 'MACHINERY', 'ROOF_FALL', 'FIRST_AID'
    min_passing_score INTEGER DEFAULT 80
);

-- Assessment Results & Completion Ledger
CREATE TABLE assessment_logs (
    log_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY NOT NULL,
    worker_id TEXT NOT NULL,
    module_id TEXT NOT NULL,
    score INTEGER NOT NULL,
    passed INTEGER NOT NULL,      -- 1 for True, 0 for False
    time_taken_seconds INTEGER NOT NULL,
    completed_at INTEGER NOT NULL, -- Epoch timestamp
    digital_signature TEXT NOT NULL, -- HMAC-SHA256 Hash
    is_synced INTEGER DEFAULT 0,  -- 0 = Pending Sync, 1 = Synced
    FOREIGN KEY(worker_id) REFERENCES workers(worker_id),
    FOREIGN KEY(module_id) REFERENCES modules(module_id)
);
```

### Offline Synchronization & Conflict Handling
* **Storage Strategy:** All training modules, 3D GLB models, and audio files reside in the app's `assets/` directory. Zero runtime network download is needed for training.
* **Sync Strategy:** Completed assessment records are saved locally with `is_synced = 0`. Android `WorkManager` executes a periodic background task with `NetworkType.CONNECTED` constraint.
* **Conflict Resolution:** **Append-Only Event Ledger.** Since worker assessment logs are unique immutable event records tied to a UUID and timestamp, server-side conflicts cannot occur. The server simply ingests new log IDs and marks `is_synced = 1` on the client via HTTP 200 response.
* **Bandwidth Optimization:** Payload size per synced record is under $1\text{ KB}$ (JSON containing string IDs, score, timestamp, and cryptographic hash). A batch of 50 offline completions consumes $< 50\text{ KB}$ of data during sync.

# 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

### Sensitive Information & Data Protection
* **Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** Worker names, employee ID numbers, and mine assignments are stored locally using **EncryptedSharedPreferences** and SQLCipher-backed Room DB (AES-256 encryption).
* **Compliance with DPDP Act 2023 (India):** Explicit consent screen in Hindi and Santali explaining that assessment data is stored locally and synced only for official mine safety compliance records under the Mines Act, 1952.

### Cryptographic QR Certificate Verification
To prevent fraudulent certificate generation (a major issue cited in statutory audit background [PS]):

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                      CRYPTOGRAPHIC QR VERIFICATION WORKFLOW                                       |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

  [ STEP 1: ASSESSMENT PASSED (MOBILE CLIENT) ]
  Worker completes module with Score >= 80%
                                 |
                                 v
  [ STEP 2: PAYLOAD CONSTRUCTION ]
  Payload String = "WID:10042|MID:MOD02|SCORE:90|TS:1725500000"
                                 |
                                 v
  [ STEP 3: HMAC-SHA256 SIGNATURE GENERATION ]
  Signature = HMAC-SHA256(Payload, App_Secret_Key)
                                 |
                                 v
  [ STEP 4: QR CODE ENCODING ]
  QR Code Data = Base64(Payload + "." + Signature)
                                 |
                                 v
  [ STEP 5: OFFLINE VERIFICATION (INSPECTOR DEVICE) ]
  Inspector scans QR code using SurakshaAR Inspector Mode
  App splits Payload and Signature -> Recomputes HMAC using same Key
  Match = VALID CERTIFICATE (Green Checkmark + Details)
  Mismatch = TAMPERED / INVALID (Red Warning)
```
