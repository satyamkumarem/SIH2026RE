## 1. Executive Snapshot

This project defines the complete architecture, research dossier, and implementation strategy for **SIH26180**, sponsored by **Qualcomm Inc** under the **Hardware** category. The core problem addresses the acute vulnerability of Indian smallholder farmers to climate variability, pest infestations, crop diseases, nutrient deficiencies, and water stress, compounded by poor rural cellular connectivity [PS]. The proposed solution is **KrishiDrishti-Edge**, a field-deployable Edge AI Smart Farming Assistant. It utilizes local camera modules and multi-depth soil/microclimate sensors connected to a low-power Qualcomm Edge AI compute unit (such as the Qualcomm Dragonwing™ / Snapdragon IoT platform using the Qualcomm AI Engine Direct SDK) [RESEARCHED]. By executing lightweight computer vision models (e.g., YOLOv8-Nano / MobileNetV3 optimized via Qualcomm AIMET) and localized soil-water mass balance algorithms on-device, the system detects early-stage stress, identifies diseases/pests, generates actionable multi-lingual advisories via an offline-first mobile app and OLED field display, and operates autonomously without continuous cloud access [INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1. **Connectivity**: Rural Indian farms suffer from frequent network dead zones, 2G/3G fallbacks, or intermittent 4G signal `[KNOWN]`. Continuous cloud API calls for vision models are completely infeasible `[INFERENCE]`.
2. **Power Supply**: Grid electricity in rural India is often erratic (4-8 hours of intermittent power daily) `[KNOWN]`. Field devices must operate completely off-grid via a 5W-10W Solar PV panel andLiFePO4 battery pack `[ASSUMPTION]`.
3. **Thermal & Weather Extreme**: Operational ambient temperatures range from 5°C (North Indian winters) to 48°C (Monsoon heatwaves) `[KNOWN]`. Enclosures must be IP65 dust/water resistant, and hardware must avoid thermal throttling under direct sunlight `[INFERENCE]`.
4. **Language & Literacy**: Literacy rates among smallholders vary; technical agronomic terms (e.g., "Volumetric Water Content", "Chlorosis") are confusing `[KNOWN]`. UX must rely heavily on visual color codes (Green/Yellow/Red), intuitive icons, audio prompts in regional languages (Hindi, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, etc.) `[ASSUMPTION]`.
5. **Hardware Constraints**: The edge device must balance compute power vs energy draw. Running heavy LLMs continuously on-field will drain batteries within hours `[INFERENCE]`. Deep learning vision models must be quantized (INT8/FP16) using Qualcomm AIMET to fit within < 3W power budgets `[RESEARCHED]`.

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

The recommended approach is **Approach 3: Qualcomm Edge AI Field Gateway + Multi-Sensor Node (KrishiDrishti-Edge)**.

### Why Approach 1 Was Rejected:
Cloud-centric architectures fail fundamental rural field requirements [PS]. In vast agricultural belts across Madhya Pradesh, Bihar, and Odisha, cellular data throughput inside fields is unreliable [KNOWN]. Streaming HD leaf imagery or continuous sensor telemetry to cloud servers incurs high latency, packet loss, and recurring cellular data costs that smallholders cannot afford [INFERENCE].

### Why Approach 2 Was Rejected:
A smartphone-only approach relies entirely on the farmer physically walking to the field, noticing symptoms, and manually capturing photos [INFERENCE]. This breaks the requirement for *early warning* pest detection and automated irrigation scheduling [PS]. Pests such as nocturnal armyworms or rapid water-stress events happen when the farmer is not actively inspecting every leaf [KNOWN].

### Why Approach 3 Wins:
Approach 3 pairs continuous field-level environmental monitoring (soil VWC, ambient temp, VPD) with an on-demand/automated camera module driven by a high-efficiency **Qualcomm Edge AI Platform** (utilizing the Hexagon Tensor Processor / NPU) [RESEARCHED].
1. **Zero Cloud Dependency**: Vision models (YOLOv8-Nano / MobileNetV3) and sensor fusion engines run 100% locally [INFERENCE].
2. **Instant Decision Making**: Latency is under 300ms, enabling direct trigger of local relay actuators (pumps) and immediate local display updates [INFERENCE].
3. **Power Efficiency**: Using Qualcomm AIMET (AI Model Efficiency Toolkit) quantized models, the unit can enter deep-sleep states and wake up on sensor interrupts or periodic camera passes, running seamlessly on a small 5W solar panel [RESEARCHED].

### Step-by-Step Data Flow Narrative

1. **Environmental Sampling**: The low-power microcontroller wakes every 15 minutes, samples soil volumetric water content ($VWC_{15}$, $VWC_{45}$), ambient temperature, relative humidity, and rainfall pulse counters.
2. **Thermal Stress & ET Calculation**: Microcontroller transmits telemetry over UART to the main Qualcomm host. The agronomic engine calculates Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) and daily Reference Evapotranspiration ($ET_0$).
3. **Triggered / Scheduled Vision Capture**: Camera captures plant images either on a scheduled basis (e.g., 08:00 AM and 04:00 PM) or when motion/pest trap infrared sensors trigger.
4. **Quantized Edge Inference**: The image frame is passed to the **Qualcomm AI Engine Direct (QNN)** runtime. The Hexagon NPU executes the INT8-quantized MobileNetV3 (disease/deficiency classification) and YOLOv8-Nano (pest detection) in under 150 milliseconds.
5. **Rule Fusion & Advisory Generation**: The local decision engine combines vision output with environmental history:
   * *Example*: IF `Disease_Detected == "Early Blight"` AND `Humidity > 85%` FOR `> 6 hours` $\rightarrow$ Elevate severity to **HIGH ALERT**.
   * *Example*: IF `Soil_VWC < Wilting_Point` AND `Forecast_Rain_24h < 5mm` $\rightarrow$ Output "Irrigate 45 Mins Now".
6. **Tri-Modal Action Delivery**:
   * **Field Hardware**: Local OLED screen displays simple icon + text ("IRRIGATE 45 MINS" / "SPOTS OF APHIDS IN SECTOR 1").
   * **Actuator**: If auto-irrigation mode is enabled, fires 12V relay to activate pump.
   * **Mobile App (BLE Sync)**: When farmer comes within 10 meters of the field unit, app automatically pairs via Bluetooth LE, downloads encrypted local SQLite logs, and plays a voice advisory in Hindi/Marathi/Tamil.
   * **Cloud Synchronization**: When farmer's phone reaches cellular coverage, the background sync service pushes aggregated field logs to the Central FPO/Agri Dashboard.

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Component 1: Plant Pathology & Nutrient Deficiency Vision System
1. **Exact Problem**: Identify plant disease species (e.g., Tomato Early Blight, Paddy Blast) and Nitrogen/Iron deficiency chlorosis symptoms from leaf imagery `[PS]`.
2. **Input**: $224 \times 224 \times 3$ RGB leaf image frame captured by camera module.
3. **Output**: Class confidence vector (e.g., `Class: Paddy_Leaf_Blast`, `Confidence: 0.92`, `Severity: Stage_2`).
4. **Why Rule System Fails**: Plant color, lighting, background soil, and complex leaf spot patterns vary non-linearly; handcrafted color rules fail under natural sunlight variations `[INFERENCE]`.
5. **Data Required**: ~10,000 annotated images of healthy/diseased leaves (PlantVillage + custom field augmentations) `[RESEARCHED]`.
6. **Evaluation Metric**: Top-1 Accuracy (>92%), Macro F1-Score (>0.88), Inference Latency (<200ms) `[ASSUMPTION]`.
7. **Failure Mode Handling**: If top prediction confidence is < 65%, tag image as "Uncertain", store locally, and request farmer to re-take image under clearer lighting `[INFERENCE]`.
8. **Training Required**: Yes, supervised pre-training on PyTorch.
9. **Fine-Tuning Required**: Yes, fine-tuned on target Indian crop varieties.
10. **RAG Required**: No.
11. **Computer Vision Required**: Yes (CNN/MobileNet Architecture).
12. **Speech Processing Required**: No (handled by standard mobile TTS engine).
13. **Forecasting Required**: No.
14. **Human Oversight Required**: Yes; high-severity alerts offer a button for the extension worker to verify.

### Component 2: Insect Pest Detection & Counting
1. **Exact Problem**: Locate and count tiny insect pests (e.g., Aphids, Whiteflies, Stem Borers) on sticky traps or plant stems `[PS]`.
2. **Input**: $640 \times 640 \times 3$ RGB image of crop field quadrant / sticky trap.
3. **Output**: Bounding boxes $[x, y, w, h]$, class label, and total pest count per class.
4. **Why Rule System Fails**: Pests cluster randomly and overlap; background dirt/debris prevents static pixel counting.
5. **Data Required**: ~5,000 annotated images with bounding box labels (IP102 Dataset) `[RESEARCHED]`.
6. **Evaluation Metric**: mean Average Precision (mAP@0.5 > 85%).
7. **Failure Mode Handling**: If pest count spikes suddenly (>300%), trigger local confirmation sweep before dispensing automated spray recommendations to prevent false alarms from fallen debris.
8. **Training Required**: Yes (YOLOv8-Nano target training).
9. **Fine-Tuning Required**: Yes.
10. **RAG Required**: No.
11. **Computer Vision Required**: Yes (Object Detection).
12. **Speech Processing Required**: No.
13. **Forecasting Required**: No.
14. **Human Oversight Required**: Yes.

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
* **Irrigation Scheduling & Water Stress Calculation**: **Do NOT use Neural Networks here.** Standard FAO-56 Penman-Monteith / Hargreaves thermodynamic equations based on soil Volumetric Water Content ($VWC$), Soil Wilting Point ($WP$), Field Capacity ($FC$), and Vapor Pressure Deficit ($VPD$) are mathematically precise, 100% explainable, deterministic, and consume near-zero compute cycles `[INFERENCE]`. AI black-box models for irrigation risk hallucinating incorrect pump durations `[INFERENCE]`.

### Offline & Low-Network Synchronization Strategy
1. **Zero-Connection Operation**: All inference, threshold evaluations, and local displays execute without cellular/cloud connection `[PS]`.
2. **Local Storage Buffer**: Telemetry logs and downsampled JPEG thumbnails (30KB each) are stored in the onboard SD/eMMC Flash memory via SQLite `[INFERENCE]`. Local storage supports over 12 months of offline logs `[ASSUMPTION]`.
3. **Opportunistic BLE Sync**: When the farmer opens the mobile app within 10 meters of the field node, the app initiates a low-energy Bluetooth GATT connection `[INFERENCE]`. Unsynced records (`sync_status == 0`) are pulled in compressed binary packets (Protocol Buffers) `[INFERENCE]`.
4. **Cloud Store-and-Forward**: Once the mobile app establishes a 2G/3G/4G or Wi-Fi connection later in the village, it background-posts accumulated SQLite packets to the FastAPI cloud backend `[INFERENCE]`.
5. **Conflict Handling**: The Edge Node acts as the immutable single source of truth for physical sensor readings `[INFERENCE]`. In case of parameter updates from cloud (e.g., updated crop stage coefficients), the cloud update takes precedence via timestamp ordering `[INFERENCE]`.

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

1. **Personal & Location Data Protection**: Sensor data contains precise farm GPS coordinates. Coordinates are stored securely in local encrypted flash and anonymized on the cloud server to prevent land boundary leaks `[INFERENCE]`.
2. **Device Authentication**: Each Qualcomm Edge field node uses a hardware-backed secure element / cryptographic key (e.g., Qualcomm TrustZone) to sign telemetry packets, preventing rogue node spoofing `[RESEARCHED]`.
3. **Authorization**: Mobile app access requires PIN or biometric authentication. Extension workers have role-based access control (RBAC) to update field crop types `[INFERENCE]`.
4. **Data Ownership & Privacy**: The farmer retains sole ownership of field data. Data aggregated by FPOs/Cooperatives is stripped of individual farmer identities `[INFERENCE]`.
5. **Liability & AI Failure Governance**: Automated advisories include explicit legal disclaimers: *"Diagnostic recommendation generated by AI prototype. Verify visually before mass chemical application."* `[ASSUMPTION]`.
6. **False-Positive Mitigation**: Pesticide recommendations require two consecutive image confirmations or farmer manual approval to prevent unnecessary chemical usage `[INFERENCE]`.
