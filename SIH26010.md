## 1. Executive Snapshot

### Summary
Rural land administration in India is severely impaired by legacy chain-and-tape survey records, unrecorded land partitions, cadastral map distortions, and spatial-textual record disconnects [PS]. These deficiencies fuel prolonged litigation—accounting for over 66% of civil cases in India [RESEARCHED]—and prevent farmers from leveraging land as collateral [KNOWN]. To address Problem Statement **SIH26010**, this dossier presents **Bhu-Drishti (भू-दृष्टि)**: an end-to-end, high-precision Land Information & Resurvey Management System (LIMS). The platform ingests High-Resolution Aerial Orthomosaics generated via drone surveys, integrates Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) / Differential GPS (DGPS) mobile field verification via Survey of India's Continuously Operating Reference Station (CORS) network [RESEARCHED], automates parcel boundary detection using computer vision, and generates 14-digit Unique Land Parcel Identification Numbers (ULPIN / Bhu-Aadhaar) [RESEARCHED]. The solution bridges textual Record of Rights (RoR / Khasra / Khatauni) with precise spatial geo-coordinates, enabling real-time automated mutation workflows and conflict resolution for the Department of Land Resources (DoLR), Ministry of Rural Development [PS].


* **Cellular Network Gaps (2G/Offline):** Agricultural fields in interior rural blocks suffer from non-existent or patchy 4G/5G connectivity [KNOWN]. *Impact:* Mobile field app MUST operate 100% offline using vector MBTiles and local SQLite/PostGIS, caching boundary edits until network connectivity is re-established [INFERENCE].
* **Low-Cost Mobile Hardware:** Field Patwaris and village assistants carry budget Android smartphones (2-4GB RAM) [KNOWN]. *Impact:* Heavy GIS raster rendering must be offloaded to server-side vector tiles; local mobile processing must be lightweight [INFERENCE].
* **High GNSS Accuracy Requirement:** Standard smartphone GPS has a location margin error of $\pm 3\text{ m to } 10\text{ m}$ [KNOWN], which is unacceptable for resolving farm boundary disputes where inches matter [PS]. *Impact:* Hardware integration via Bluetooth/USB with external RTK GNSS rovers (e.g., u-blox F9P chipsets) connected to Survey of India's CORS network via NTRIP protocol is mandatory to achieve $\pm 1\text{ to } 2\text{ cm}$ accuracy [RESEARCHED].
* **Visual Obstructions & Seasonality:** Tall standing crops (e.g., sugarcane, paddy) or dense canopy hide field bunds during specific months [RESEARCHED]. *Impact:* Survey timeline guidelines must mandate post-harvest imagery acquisition (typically November-March) [RESEARCHED].
* **Regulatory Drone Compliance:** Drone operations in India must comply with DGCA DigitalSky / Drone Rules 2021 [RESEARCHED]. *Impact:* System must support standard UAV formats (GeoTIFF, EXIF log parsing) generated by DGCA-certified survey drones [INFERENCE].

## 6. Research Questions

To ensure technical feasibility, the team must address these core research questions prior to execution:

1. **CORS Network Latency & NTRIP Stream:** How does Survey of India's CORS network perform over 2G/EDGE cellular networks in remote rural tehsils, and what fallback mechanism exists when real-time NTRIP corrections drop? [RESEARCHED]
2. **AI Segmentation Model Precision:** Can a lightweight Computer Vision model (e.g., SAM-2 or U-Net) distinguish natural vegetation boundaries/earthen bunds from man-made drainage channels with $>90\%$ Intersection-over-Union (IoU) accuracy on $5\text{ cm/pixel}$ aerial orthomosaics? [RESEARCHED]
3. **Legacy Cadastral Map Distortion:** How to geometrically warp and georeference 50-year-old non-projected cloth/paper maps (*Khasra Maps / Sajra*) onto modern WGS84 coordinates with minimal spatial error? [INFERENCE]
4. **Topology Rules for Plot Mutation:** What explicit spatial rules (e.g., no overlapping polygons, zero sliver gaps, total area conservation $\sum A_{\text{sub}} = A_{\text{parent}}$) must be programmatically enforced during parcel splits? [INFERENCE]

### Selected Solution Architecture: Approach 2 (AI-Vision Assisted Orthomosaic Segmentation + Offline CORS/RTK Mobile App + Unified Spatial-Textual WebGIS)

#### Why Approach 2 Wins:
1. **Solves the Speed & Accuracy Bottleneck:** Manual digitization (Approach 1) would take decades to cover India's 600,000+ villages [RESEARCHED]. Approach 2 uses Computer Vision (SAM-2 / Segment Anything Model 2 fine-tuned on agricultural bunds) to auto-generate initial plot polygons in seconds [RESEARCHED], reducing Patwari workload by over 80% [INFERENCE].
2. **True Ground Truth Precision:** By integrating Survey of India's CORS network with RTK GNSS rovers via NTRIP, the mobile app captures boundary vertices with $\pm 1-2\text{ cm}$ accuracy [RESEARCHED], resolving contentious boundary disputes on the spot [PS].
3. **Rejection of Unnecessary Technology:** Blockchain (Approach 3) was explicitly **rejected**. Land titling in India is governed by State Revenue Codes [KNOWN]. A centralized, ACID-compliant PostgreSQL/PostGIS database with cryptographic SHA-256 mutation audit logs fulfills all tamper-proofing requirements without the latency, complexity, and legal incompatibility of blockchain [INFERENCE].

### Step-by-Step Data Flow Narrative
1. **Drone Ingestion & AI Segmentation:** A drone surveys a rural village block; the orthomosaic (GeoTIFF) is uploaded to MinIO Object Storage [INFERENCE]. The AI Processing Engine (SAM-2) scans the raster, detects agricultural mud bunds, and generates candidate GeoJSON parcel polygons [RESEARCHED].
2. **CORS/RTK Field Verification:** The Patwari arrives at the field equipped with the Bhu-Drishti Mobile App connected over Bluetooth to an RTK DGPS rover [INFERENCE]. The app streams NTRIP corrections from Survey of India's CORS server, establishing $\pm 1-2\text{ cm}$ positioning accuracy [RESEARCHED].
3. **On-Field Adjustment & Landowner Sign-off:** The Patwari displays the AI-suggested boundary overlaid on the orthomosaic. If a boundary is disputed, the Patwari walks the field with the RTK rover, logging sub-centimeter vertex coordinates [INFERENCE]. Landowners verify boundaries on-screen and provide digital signatures [INFERENCE].
4. **ULPIN Generation & Topology Check:** The Core Backend receives the confirmed GeoJSON polygon. The Topology Engine verifies that the polygon does not overlap adjacent plots or leave unregistered gaps [INFERENCE]. The ULPIN Engine computes a 14-digit Bhu-Aadhaar code based on the spatial centroid and vertex coordinates [RESEARCHED].
5. **Real-Time RoR & Mutation Sync:** The new spatial parcel is linked with textual RoR records (Khasra No., Owner Name, Area in Hectares) in PostgreSQL/PostGIS [INFERENCE]. Any subsequent land split/mutation automatically subdivides the geometry, recomputes new ULPINs, and creates a SHA-256 hashed audit log in the mutation register [INFERENCE].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

```
                               ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                               │                    AI/ML INTEGRATION DEEP DIVE                 │
                               └───────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────┘
                                                               │
       ┌───────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
       ▼                                                       ▼
[Component 1: Computer Vision]                          [Component 2: NLP / Document Processing]
SAM-2 Orthomosaic Parcel Boundary Extractor             RoR & Deed Entity Extraction
Input: GeoTIFF Aerial Raster                            Input: Scanned Land Mutation Deeds
Output: Polygon GeoJSON Boundary Candidates             Output: Structured JSON Owner/Khasra Attributes
```

### AI Component 1: Orthomosaic Parcel Boundary Extractor

1. **Exact Problem AI Solves:** Automatically identifies physical field bunds, natural boundaries, and fence lines from high-resolution drone orthomosaic imagery [RESEARCHED].
2. **Input:** High-resolution RGB aerial imagery raster (GeoTIFF tile, $5\text{ cm/pixel}$ resolution) [RESEARCHED].
3. **Output:** MultiPolygon GeoJSON containing predicted agricultural plot boundaries with probability confidence scores [INFERENCE].
4. **Why Simpler Rules Fail:** Agricultural field boundaries in India are non-uniform—earthen ridges (*dols*), tree rows, irrigation channels, and dirt tracks vary wildly in color, texture, and lighting. Simple edge detectors (Canny/Sobel) produce unsegmented noise [INFERENCE].
5. **Data Required:** 500+ annotated drone image tiles with ground-truth agricultural parcel boundary vector masks [INFERENCE].
6. **Evaluation Metric:** Intersection over Union ($\text{IoU} > 0.85$) and Structural Similarity Index (SSIM) [INFERENCE].
7. **When Wrong:** False boundaries (e.g., crop row shadows mistaken for plot edges) can occur. *Mitigation:* The AI output is presented merely as a **draft candidate layer** on the Patwari's mobile app. The Patwari can accept, modify, or snap vertices manually [INFERENCE].
8. **Training/Fine-tuning:** Requires fine-tuning Segment Anything Model 2 (SAM-2) weights on agricultural satellite/drone imagery [RESEARCHED].
9. **RAG/CV/Speech/Forecasting:** Requires **Computer Vision (CV)**. Does **NOT** require RAG, Speech, or Time-Series Forecasting [INFERENCE].
10. **Human Oversight:** **Mandatory**. AI predictions are strictly advisory; legal land boundaries require Patwari and landowner digital signature confirmation [INFERENCE].

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
* **DO NOT use AI for legal boundary dispute arbitration or title ownership determination.** Ownership legalities MUST strictly rely on verified textual RoR documents, court decrees, and physical ground truth verified by human Patwaris [INFERENCE].
* **DO NOT use LLMs to auto-approve land mutations without human revenue officer review.** Land ownership transfer has severe legal implications; automated mutation scripts must enforce deterministic SQL topology rules, not probabilistic LLM outputs [INFERENCE].

### Database Schema Definition (PostgreSQL + PostGIS)

```sql
-- Enable PostGIS Extension
CREATE EXTENSION IF NOT EXISTS postgis;

-- 1. Agricultural Parcels Spatial Table
CREATE TABLE agricultural_parcels (
    parcel_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    ulpin VARCHAR(14) UNIQUE NOT NULL, -- Bhu-Aadhaar 14-digit ID
    state_code VARCHAR(2) NOT NULL,
    district_code VARCHAR(3) NOT NULL,
    tehsil_code VARCHAR(4) NOT NULL,
    village_code VARCHAR(6) NOT NULL,
    khasra_number VARCHAR(20) NOT NULL,
    geom GEOMETRY(Polygon, 4326) NOT NULL, -- WGS84 Spatial Polygon
    area_sqm NUMERIC(12, 2) GENERATED ALWAYS AS (ST_Area(geom::geography)) STORED,
    dispute_status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'CLEAR', -- CLEAR, DISPUTED, MUTATION_PENDING
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIMEZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    updated_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIMEZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Spatial GIST Index for Sub-Millisecond Spatial Queries
CREATE INDEX idx_parcels_geom ON agricultural_parcels USING GIST(geom);
CREATE INDEX idx_parcels_ulpin ON agricultural_parcels(ulpin);

-- 2. Precision Boundary Vertices Table (RTK Ground Truth)
CREATE TABLE parcel_vertices (
    vertex_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    parcel_id UUID REFERENCES agricultural_parcels(parcel_id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
    sequence_order INT NOT NULL,
    geom GEOMETRY(Point, 4326) NOT NULL,
    elevation_m NUMERIC(8, 3),
    horizontal_accuracy_cm NUMERIC(5, 2) NOT NULL, -- e.g., 1.2 cm from RTK
    captured_via VARCHAR(20) CHECK (captured_via IN ('CORS_RTK', 'DGPS_ROVER', 'MANUAL_MAP')),
    captured_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIMEZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- 3. Textual Record of Rights (RoR Integration)
CREATE TABLE record_of_rights (
    ror_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    ulpin VARCHAR(14) REFERENCES agricultural_parcels(ulpin),
    owner_name VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
    owner_aadhaar_hash VARCHAR(64), -- SHA-256 Hashed for Privacy
    joint_ownership_share NUMERIC(5, 2) DEFAULT 100.00,
    land_category VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL, -- Irrigated, Unirrigated, Waste
    encumbrance_status BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE, -- Bank Mortgage Flag
    mortgage_bank_name VARCHAR(150)
);

-- 4. Immutable Mutation & Split Register
CREATE TABLE mutation_audit_ledger (
    mutation_id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    parent_ulpin VARCHAR(14) NOT NULL,
    child_ulpins VARCHAR(14)[] NOT NULL,
    mutation_type VARCHAR(30) CHECK (mutation_type IN ('INHERITANCE_SPLIT', 'SALE_TRANSFER', 'BOUNDARY_CORRECTION')),
    approved_by_officer VARCHAR(100) NOT NULL,
    spatial_diff_geom GEOMETRY(Geometry, 4326),
    document_proof_url TEXT,
    ledger_sha256_hash VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
    applied_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIMEZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);
```

### Offline Caching & Synchronization Protocol

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        OFFLINE DATA SYNCHRONIZATION FLOW                          |
|                                                                                   |
|  [Central PostGIS Server]                                                         |
|         │  (1) Export Village MBTiles & GeoJSON Vector Vector Cache               |
|         ▼                                                                         |
|  [Mobile App (SQLite / WatermelonDB Local Cache)]                                 |
|         │  (2) Patwari conducts field survey completely OFFLINE                   |
|         │  (3) Captures RTK GPS Vertices & Enqueued Mutations                     |
|         ▼                                                                         |
|  [Re-establish 3G/4G Network Connectivity]                                        |
|         │  (4) Push Synchronize Queue via HTTP/2 REST Gateway                     |
|         ▼                                                                         |
|  [Server Topology Validation Engine]                                              |
|         ├──> IF NO Conflict: Commit PostGIS Transaction & Issue ULPIN             |
|         └──> IF Overlap Conflict: Reject & Return Discrepancy Map to Mobile        |

```

* **Local Storage Engine:** React Native app uses **WatermelonDB** (built on top of SQLite) for local transactional storage, capable of storing 50,000+ parcel polygons and raster MBTiles directly on the mobile device [INFERENCE].
* **Vector Tile Caching:** Village maps are pre-packaged into compressed `.mbtiles` format and sideloaded or downloaded when on Wi-Fi/4G at the Tehsil office [INFERENCE].
* **Conflict Handling Strategy (CRDT / Deterministic Lock):**
  - While a plot is checked out for field survey, its state in the central database is marked as `IN_FIELD_SURVEY` [INFERENCE].
  - If two survey teams submit conflicting boundary edits for adjacent plots, the server Topology Engine evaluates vertex RTK accuracy scores. The submission with higher RTK horizontal accuracy ($\le 1.5\text{ cm}$) is given priority, while the other is flagged for human Tehsildar dispute review [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

```
                               ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                               │                 SECURITY & GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK                │
                               └───────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────┘
                                                               │
       ┌───────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────┴───────┬───────────────────────────────┐
       ▼                               ▼                               ▼                               ▼
[PII Protection]                [Access Control]                [Data Integrity]                [Legal Compliance]
SHA-256 Aadhaar Hashing,        RBAC: Patwari, Tehsildar,       HMAC Request Signing,           Digital Personal Data
Masked Mobile Numbers           Surveyor, Public Citizen        SHA-256 Mutation Ledger         Protection (DPDP) Act 2023
```

* **PII & Landowner Privacy:** In compliance with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023** [RESEARCHED], landowner Aadhaar numbers are never stored in plain text. Only truncated names and SHA-256 salted hashes of identity numbers are persisted in the database [INFERENCE]. Public citizen views display masked ownership records [INFERENCE].
* **Geospatial Data Security:** Precise high-resolution drone orthomosaics and geodetic reference points are classified as sensitive national infrastructure [RESEARCHED]. Access to raw GeoTIFF rasters is restricted via Signed Cloud URLs with 15-minute expiration times [INFERENCE].
* **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):**
  - **Surveyor / Drone Operator:** Can upload rasters and record RTK ground control points [INFERENCE].
  - **Patwari (Village Officer):** Can edit parcel vertices, verify land bounds on-ground, and initiate mutation requests [INFERENCE].
  - **Tehsildar (Revenue Magistrate):** Holds exclusive cryptographic authority to approve mutations and finalize spatial land records [INFERENCE].
  - **Citizen / Public:** Read-only access to view their own parcel map, verify ULPIN, and download digitally signed RoR extracts [INFERENCE].
* **Audit Trail & Non-Repudiation:** Every boundary shift, vertex movement, or owner modification generates an entry in the `mutation_audit_ledger` table with the officer's digital signature hash and timestamp [INFERENCE].
