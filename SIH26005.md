# SIH 2026 Solution Dossier: Solar-Powered Smart Mini Cold Storage System for Fresh Vegetables in North Eastern Region (NER)

---

## 1. Executive Snapshot

### One-Paragraph Summary
This dossier provides an engineering blueprint, system architecture, data flow model, and deployment roadmap for **SIH26005: Solar-Powered Smart Mini Cold Storage System for Fresh Vegetables in North Eastern Region (NER)**, issued by the **Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER)** [PS]. In the hilly, high-humidity, and cloud-prone terrain of the North Eastern Region of India, smallholder vegetable farmers face post-harvest spoilage rates of 25%–40% due to non-existent farm-gate cold storage, frequent power cuts, and long transport times to regional hubs [RESEARCHED]. This project proposes a **hybrid decentralized solar-powered mini cold storage unit (500 kg–1 MT capacity)** combining direct-drive DC BLDC vapor compression cooling, Phase Change Material (PCM) thermal energy storage (water-glycol / eutectic ice-bank), a LiFePO4 battery buffer, and an offline-first ESP32-based telemetry and alert node [INFERENCE]. Operating on standard 24V/48V DC microgrid architecture, the system eliminates inverter energy losses, provides 30+ hours of off-grid thermal backup during monsoon cloud cover, and uses low-power cellular (GSM/GPRS/LTE-M) and local Bluetooth (BLE) to deliver real-time environmental telemetry, power alerts, and shelf-life indicators directly to local farmer groups, cooperatives, and vendors [INFERENCE].

### Fundamental System Objective
To engineer a low-cost, off-grid-capable, weather-resistant, 500kg-capacity modular mini cold room using solar-DC refrigeration, eutectic thermal storage, and edge-IoT telemetry that maintains $4^\circ\text{C}$ to $10^\circ\text{C}$ temperature and 85%–95% relative humidity for fresh horticultural produce in remote NER agricultural clusters [INFERENCE].

  * *Interpretation*: The system must accommodate mixed horticultural commodities with differing optimal storage temperatures (e.g., tomatoes at $10^\circ\text{C}$–$13^\circ\text{C}$, leafy greens/cabbage at $2^\circ\text{C}$–$4^\circ\text{C}$, chillies/beans at $7^\circ\text{C}$–$10^\circ\text{C}$) and high relative humidity (85%–95% RH) without causing chilling injury or desiccation [RESEARCHED].

* **`Description.b`**: Operate using solar energy with battery backup support for off-grid and low-electricity areas [PS].
  * *Interpretation*: Photovoltaic (PV) array must serve as primary energy source, paired with electrochemical battery backup (LiFePO4) to handle off-peak hours and severe grid outages [INFERENCE].
  * *Implementation Implication*: Sizing solar PV panels for reduced solar insolation levels in NER ($3.5\text{ kWh/m}^2/\text{day}$ to $4.5\text{ kWh/m}^2/\text{day}$ during cloudy seasons) [RESEARCHED].
* **`Description.c`**: Maintain controlled temperature and humidity levels for extending shelf life of vegetables [PS].
  * *Interpretation*: Closed-loop PID feedback control over refrigeration cycle and humidity actuator [INFERENCE].
  * *Implementation Implication*: Calibrated SHT31/SHT40 temperature/humidity sensor arrays and pulse-width modulated (PWM) fan/compressor control [INFERENCE].
* **`Description.d`**: Include smart monitoring features for:
  * Temperature fluctuation [PS]
  * Humidity variation [PS]
  * Power failure alerts [PS]
  * Storage condition status [PS]
  * *Interpretation*: Sensor node must detect out-of-bound conditions, power drops, or sensor faults and generate localized and remote push notifications [INFERENCE].

* **`Description.e`**: Be compact, modular, and suitable for deployment in remote hilly terrain and rural agricultural clusters [PS].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1. **Solar Insolation Variations in NER**: The North Eastern Region experiences heavy monsoon rainfall (May to September) with prolonged cloud cover. Average global horizontal irradiance (GHI) drops to $3.0\text{--}4.0\text{ kWh/m}^2/\text{day}$ in parts of Meghalaya, Mizoram, and Arunachal Pradesh, compared to $>6.0\text{ kWh/m}^2/\text{day}$ in North-West India [RESEARCHED]. System sizing must account for low-light PV charging efficiency.
2. **Terrain and Logistics Constraints**: Remote agricultural clusters in Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, and Manipur are accessible only via unpaved or narrow hill roads prone to landslides [KNOWN]. Monolithic cold storage units cannot be transported. All components must fit into modular packages under $80\text{ kg}$ per sub-assembly for manual carrying [INFERENCE].
3. **Power Reliability**: Grid electricity in rural NER villages suffers from frequent outages lasting 12 to 48 consecutive hours, accompanied by voltage fluctuations ($120\text{V}$–$280\text{V}$) [KNOWN]. The system must be 100% off-grid capable and independent of grid AC power [INFERENCE].
4. **Humidity and Corrosion**: Relative ambient humidity in states like Assam and Tripura remains between 80% and 98% during monsoons [RESEARCHED]. Hardware, structural framing, and electronics must be IP65 sealed, coated against tropical fungus, and built with corrosion-resistant materials (SS304/GI Powder-coated) [INFERENCE].
5. **Connectivity Blackouts**: Cellular networks (2G/4G) in hilly border areas suffer frequent signal loss [KNOWN]. Data logging cannot rely on continuous cloud connectivity; edge devices must support store-and-forward architectures with local flash memory [INFERENCE].
6. **Digital Literacy & Operation**: Village aggregation centres are managed by local farmers and vendors. The UI must rely on simple physical buttons, color-coded indicators (RGB LEDs), and local language audio feedback rather than complex touch menus [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

### Selection
**Approach 2: Direct-Drive Solar DC BLDC Compressor + Eutectic Phase Change Material (PCM) Thermal Bank + ESP32 Edge Telemetry** is selected as the winning architecture [INFERENCE].

### Justification & Rejection of Alternatives
1. **Why Approach 1 Was Rejected**: Standard AC compressors require a massive surge current (inrush current up to $10\times$ running power) during startup [RESEARCHED]. To handle a $250\text{W}$ AC compressor, an inverter rated for at least $2500\text{W}$ peak is required, adding unnecessary cost and weight [INFERENCE]. Furthermore, double conversion (DC solar $\rightarrow$ DC battery $\rightarrow$ AC inverter $\rightarrow$ AC motor) wastes 15%–20% of harvested solar energy as heat [RESEARCHED]. In cloud-heavy NER, every watt-hour is critical [INFERENCE].
2. **Why Approach 3 Was Rejected**: Thermoelectric Peltier modules exhibit abysmal thermal efficiency ($\text{COP} \le 0.5$) [KNOWN]. Cooling $500\text{ kg}$ of freshly harvested produce (with high field heat, e.g., tomatoes harvested at $30^\circ\text{C}$) to $4^\circ\text{C}$ requires removing over $50,000\text{ kJ}$ of energy [INFERENCE]. Peltier elements would draw thousands of watts, generating massive waste heat on the hot side, rendering them physically incapable of meeting the requirements [INFERENCE].
3. **Why Approach 2 Wins**:
   * **Direct DC Coupling**: Eliminates the inverter entirely. A 24V/48V BLDC compressor connects directly to the DC bus fed by the MPPT solar charger [INFERENCE].
   * **Thermal Battery (PCM)**: Uses latent heat of fusion of water-glycol/eutectic salt mixture (phase change at $0^\circ\text{C}$ to $+1^\circ\text{C}$) [RESEARCHED]. Freezing $100\text{ kg}$ of water-based PCM stores $33,300\text{ kJ}$ of cooling energy—equivalent to a $9.2\text{ kWh}$ electrical battery, but at 1/10th the cost and with zero chemical degradation over 10+ years [INFERENCE].
   * **Resilience in NER**: During 2 days of continuous heavy rainfall, the frozen PCM maintains chamber temperatures below $8^\circ\text{C}$ without drawing compressor power [INFERENCE].

### Detailed Data Flow Narrative
1. **Energy Harvesting & Storage Flow**: Solar energy captured by the 1340W PV array passes into the MPPT controller, regulating a 48V DC bus [INFERENCE]. When solar radiation is high ($>400\text{ W/m}^2$), power directly drives the 48V DC BLDC compressor at high speed ($3500\text{ RPM}$) [INFERENCE]. The cooling loop chills the cold room evaporator and freezes the Eutectic PCM plates mounted on the ceiling and walls [INFERENCE]. Excess electrical energy charges the 2.4 kWh LiFePO4 battery [INFERENCE].
2. **Thermal Regulation Loop**: Sensor nodes (SHT31) sample air temperature and relative humidity at 1 Hz [INFERENCE]. The ESP32 PID loop compares readings to target thresholds for stored produce (e.g., $4^\circ\text{C} \pm 1^\circ\text{C}$ and $90\% \text{ RH}$ for leafy greens) [INFERENCE]. If humidity drops below 85%, ESP32 triggers a solid-state relay powering the 24V ultrasonic mist transducer [INFERENCE].
3. **Off-Peak / Low-Sunlight Operation**: At night or during heavy cloud cover, the solar MPPT detects zero PV input [INFERENCE]. The compressor ramps down or turns off [INFERENCE]. The Eutectic PCM thermal bank begins absorbing heat infiltrating through the insulated walls, maintaining chamber temperature between $2^\circ\text{C}$ and $6^\circ\text{C}$ purely via latent phase change for up to 30 hours [RESEARCHED]. The LiFePO4 battery powers only the low-draw ESP32 node and small DC circulation fans ($<15\text{W}$ total draw) [INFERENCE].
4. **Telemetry & Alert Transmission**: The ESP32 logs 1-minute averaged data into its onboard SPIFFS flash memory [INFERENCE]. If temperature exceeds $12^\circ\text{C}$ or battery voltage drops below $44\text{V}$, the ESP32 activates the SIM800L module to transmit an emergency SMS alert directly to pre-configured FPO manager phone numbers [INFERENCE]. ## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Component 1: TinyML Produce Shelf-Life & Spoilage Estimation Model
1. **What exact problem does AI solve?**: Calculates remaining shelf-life (in days) for stored produce based on real-time and historical cumulative thermal and humidity exposure, avoiding static expiration assumptions [INFERENCE].
2. **What is the input?**: Time-series matrix of internal temperature ($T$), relative humidity ($RH$), door opening count ($D$), and stored commodity type (e.g., Tomato, Cabbage, Chilli) [INFERENCE].
3. **What is the output?**: Estimated Remaining Shelf Life (RSL in hours/days) and Quality Risk Score (0-100%) [INFERENCE].
4. **Why can a simpler rule/system not solve it?**: Decay rates follow non-linear Arrhenius kinetics ($k = A \cdot e^{-E_a/RT}$) accelerated by humidity deficits and thermal fluctuations; regression models capture cumulative heat-sum damage better than simple threshold alerts [RESEARCHED].
5. **What data is required?**: Historical post-harvest deterioration curves under varying temperature/humidity profiles (sourced from ICAR/FAO publications) [RESEARCHED].
6. **How will it be evaluated?**: Mean Absolute Error (MAE) in predicted vs. actual days-to-spoilage measured during field validation tests [INFERENCE].
7. **What happens when it is wrong?**: If the model underestimates shelf life, produce is sold earlier (conservative/safe). If it overestimates, a visual inspection by the farmer acts as a safety stop [INFERENCE].
8. **Does it require training?**: Yes, trained offline using Python (Scikit-Learn/TensorFlow) on synthetic and ICAR experimental post-harvest decay datasets [INFERENCE].
9. **Does it require fine-tuning?**: No fine-tuning required on edge; model weights compiled into C++ headers via TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers [INFERENCE].
10. **Does it require RAG?**: No [INFERENCE].
11. **Does it require computer vision?**: No [INFERENCE].
12. **Does it require speech processing?**: No [INFERENCE].
13. **Does it require forecasting?**: Yes, predictive decay forecasting based on upcoming 24-hour ambient weather forecasts [INFERENCE].
14. **Is human oversight required?**: Yes, local farmers visually inspect crates before dispatch [INFERENCE].

### Non-AI Components (Explicit Identification)
* **Refrigeration PID Control Loop**: Uses classical proportional-integral-derivative (PID) feedback algorithm on the ESP32. **AI SHOULD NOT BE USED HERE** due to latency, non-deterministic outputs, and safety risks of runaway cooling [INFERENCE].
* **Power Management / Low Voltage Cut-Off**: Uses hardware comparator and threshold-based state machine logic. **AI SHOULD NOT BE USED HERE** because battery protection requires 100% deterministic, zero-latency execution [INFERENCE].

### Local Storage & Schema
The ESP32 microcontroller utilizes its internal Flash memory allocated via SPIFFS/LittleFS as a non-volatile circular ring buffer [INFERENCE].

CREATE TABLE telemetry_logs (
    timestamp INTEGER PRIMARY KEY,     -- Unix Timestamp (RTC sync or relative offset)
    temp_top REAL NOT NULL,            -- Top zone temperature in Celsius

### Synchronization & Retry Strategy
1. **Store-and-Forward**: Sensor measurements are recorded every 60 seconds [INFERENCE]. If cellular network verification fails (`AT+CREG?` returns offline), records are flagged as `sync_status = 0` and stored locally [INFERENCE].
2. **Bandwidth Reduction**: Data payloads are compressed using Protocol Buffers (Protobuf) or compact binary JSON (CBOR), reducing network payload size by over 70% compared to raw JSON strings [INFERENCE].
3. **Conflict Handling**: The edge device is the absolute authority for sensor logs [INFERENCE]. Cloud servers blindly append historical records based on unique edge timestamps during batch syncs, eliminating conflict resolution complexity [INFERENCE].
4. **Offline UX**: The local OLED screen and smartphone BLE interface render live real-time status directly from the ESP32 RAM regardless of cloud/cellular status [INFERENCE].
