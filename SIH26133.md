## 1. Executive Snapshot

Rural and underserved populations across Maharashtra face systemic barriers to quality public healthcare, including geographical distances to tertiary centres, severe specialist shortages, fragmented patient records, and uncoordinated emergency referrals [PS]. The fundamental objective of this project is to build **"ArogyaSetu-Gramin"**, an offline-first, ABDM-interoperable care coordination and decision-support platform designed to empower frontline healthcare workers (ASHAs, ANMs, CHOs) at Sub-Centres/Ayushman Arogya Mandirs (AAMs) and Primary Health Centres (PHCs) with assisted teleconsultations, digital triage, longitudinal record synchronization, closed-loop referral tracking, and real-time medicine/diagnostic availability dashboards [INFERENCE].

## 5. Constraints & Environment Realities

1. **Connectivity:** Over 40% of rural health sub-centres in hilly and forested regions of Maharashtra (e.g., Gadchiroli, Nandurbar, Palghar) experience frequent cellular outages and intermittent 2G/3G connectivity [RESEARCHED]. System operations must be 100% functional offline and sync asynchronously [INFERENCE].
2. **Hardware Limitations:** Frontline workers (ASHAs/ANMs) use entry-level Android smartphones (2GB-3GB RAM, Android 9-11) with limited storage space [KNOWN]. The client mobile application must be lightweight (<25MB APK size) and energy-efficient [INFERENCE].
3. **Digital Literacy & Language:** Frontline workers have varying levels of digital literacy and primarily read/speak Marathi [KNOWN]. Interfaces must prioritize iconographic UI, voice-guided data entry (Marathi), and minimal text fields [INFERENCE].
4. **Interoperability Mandate:** The Indian Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) mandates compliance with Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) standards [RESEARCHED]. All health records created must validate against official NRCeS HL7 FHIR R4 profiles [RESEARCHED].
5. **Power Reliability:** Rural PHCs experience scheduled and unscheduled power load-shedding [KNOWN]. Edge caching and local database persistence are critical to prevent data loss during sudden battery drain or power disruption [INFERENCE].

## 10. Recommended Approach & Why

### Selected Strategy: Approach 3 — Offline-First Hybrid Smart Edge Architecture

**Justification:**
Approach 3 is selected because it directly solves the fundamental paradox of rural digital health: ** frontline workers operate in network-deprived environments, yet central health administrations require real-time, unified data transparency [INFERENCE].

* **Why Approach 1 was rejected:** Cloud-only web portals crash or hang when Community Health Officers try to load patient history or record vitals in 2G or offline rural pockets, rendering the solution useless in the field [INFERENCE].
* **Why Approach 2 was rejected:** Pure peer-to-peer mesh sync lacks a centralized single source of truth, making it impossible to trigger emergency hospital pre-notifications, run district-wide medicine stock monitoring, or maintain audit-ready ABDM longitudinal records [INFERENCE].

**Key Advantages of Approach 3:**
1. **Uninterrupted Point-of-Care:** CHOs and ASHAs can perform digital triage, capture vitals, and issue offline e-prescriptions without network latency [INFERENCE].
2. **Automated Delta Sync Engine:** As soon as a cellular signal (even 2G) is detected, the app automatically executes a background sync queue, pushing local transactions and pulling specialist teleconsultation notes using compressed JSON patches [INFERENCE].
3. **ABDM Interoperability:** Converts local records into validated HL7 FHIR R4 bundles upon cloud ingestion, ensuring 100% compliance with national standards [RESEARCHED].

### Step-by-Step Data Flow Narrative

1. **Patient Registration & Offline Triage:**
   * A patient visits an Ayushman Arogya Mandir (Sub-Centre). The CHO inputs symptoms, pulse, blood pressure, SPO2, and blood glucose into the mobile application [INFERENCE].
   * The app checks local SQLite storage. If an ABHA ID exists, it links it; if offline, it generates a local temporary UUID [INFERENCE].
   * The local **Clinical Triage Engine** evaluates the vitals against WHO/ICMR clinical decision trees and flags the patient as *Red (High Risk / Emergency)*, *Yellow (Moderate Risk)*, or *Green (Low Risk)* [INFERENCE].

2. **Offline Data Persistence & Sync:**
   * The session is written immediately to local SQLite encrypted with AES-256 [INFERENCE].
   * The **Background Sync Engine** monitors connectivity. Upon detecting network availability, it sends an encrypted JSON delta payload to the API Gateway [INFERENCE].
   * The Gateway validates the JWT token, resolves any data write conflicts using a "last-write-wins with server timestamp override" rule, and persists the record to PostgreSQL [INFERENCE].

3. **Assisted Teleconsultation & Referral:**
   * For *Yellow/Red* cases, the CHO initiates an assisted teleconsultation request [INFERENCE].
   * The backend queues the patient in the District Hospital Specialist Worklist (Redis queue) [INFERENCE].
   * The Specialist reviews the patient's longitudinal FHIR history, opens a low-bandwidth WebRTC video/audio call with the CHO, and issues an e-prescription [INFERENCE].
   * If emergency transfer is needed, a *Closed-Loop Referral Pass* with a unique QR code is auto-generated, pre-notifying the receiving District Hospital casualty ward and triggering an SMS notification [INFERENCE].

4. **FHIR Standard Conversion & ABDM Export:**
   * The platform's FHIR engine converts the e-prescription, consultation notes, and vitals into standard HL7 FHIR R4 JSON bundles (OPConsultRecord, DiagnosticReport) [RESEARCHED].
   * The bundle is pushed to the ABDM Sandbox/Gateway using the patient's ABHA consent artefact [RESEARCHED].

## 13. AI/ML Justification

### Component 1: AI-Guided Clinical Digital Triage & Risk Stratification

1. **Purpose:** Assist CHOs and ANMs in early identification of critical conditions (severe anemia, pre-eclampsia, diabetic ketoacidosis, sepsis) during rural screening [INFERENCE].
2. **Model Type:** Hybrid System: **Deterministic Rule Engine (Primary)** based on WHO/ICMR clinical guidelines + **Gradient Boosted Decision Tree (XGBoost Classifier - Secondary)** for multi-parameter risk scoring [INFERENCE].
3. **Input:** Vitals (BP, HR, SpO2, Temperature, Blood Glucose, Hb levels), symptoms, age, gestational age, and chief complaints [INFERENCE].
4. **Output:** Triage Category (*Red / Yellow / Green*), Risk Score (0-100), and recommended immediate clinical action (e.g., "Refer immediately to District Hospital; administer oral rehydration") [INFERENCE].
5. **Data Required:** ICMR clinical guideline dataset; 5,000+ anonymized primary care screening records (synthetically generated for hackathon) [INFERENCE].
6. **Why AI is Preferable:** Human frontline health workers may miss subtle multi-parameter deterioration patterns (e.g., borderline elevated BP combined with mild proteinuria and headache indicating pre-eclampsia) [INFERENCE].
7. **Evaluation Metrics:** Sensitivity / Recall (>98% required for Red cases to eliminate dangerous false negatives) [INFERENCE].
8. **Failure Mode:** If the model predicts normal for a deteriorating patient (false negative), the fallback is the **hardcoded WHO vital threshold trigger** which overrides ML and flags the patient [INFERENCE].
9. **Human Oversight:** The CHO must physically verify the triage score and can manually upgrade any patient to *Red Emergency* regardless of AI output [INFERENCE].

### Where AI Should NOT Be Used
* **Primary Medical Diagnosis & Drug Prescriptions:** AI MUST NOT autonomously prescribe medications or override a licensed doctor's diagnostic judgment [INFERENCE]. All e-prescriptions must originate directly from an HPR-verified medical practitioner under the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020 [RESEARCHED].

CREATE TABLE referrals (

    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP

### Offline Synchronization & Conflict Handling Protocol

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                  DELTA SYNC & CONFLICT PROTOCOL                                 |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

  [ FIELD WORKER MOBILE APP ]                                      [ BACKEND CLOUD DB ]
              |                                                             |
   1. Offline Data Input                                                    |
      (Saved to local SQLite with client_timestamp & sync_flag = 0)         |
              |                                                             |
   2. Network Connection Detected (2G/3G/Wi-Fi)                             |
              |                                                             |
   3. Push Delta Queue Payload ------------------------------------------->|
      (POST /api/v1/sync/push)                                              | 4. Validate JWT & Check
      Payload: { device_id, changes: [ {id, table, data, client_ts} ] }     |    Server Timestamp
                                                                            |
                                                                            | 5. Conflict Resolution:
                                                                            |    - If server_ts > client_ts:
                                                                            |      Keep server record, append
                                                                            |      to conflict_log.
                                                                            |    - Else: Overwrite server record.
                                                                            |
   6. Receive Sync Acknowledgement <---------------------------------------|
      (Returns server_timestamp & updated IDs)                              |
              |                                                             |
   7. Update Local DB: Set sync_flag = 1                                    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

#### Bandwidth Reduction Techniques
* **Gzip Payload Compression:** All JSON sync streams are compressed using Gzip/Brotli, reducing transmission sizes by up to 80% [INFERENCE].
* **Binary Image Compression:** Diagnostic photos (skin lesions, physical slips) are compressed locally to under 100KB before upload [INFERENCE].
* **Selective Field Syncing:** Only changed fields (deltas) are transmitted rather than full entity representations [INFERENCE].

## 15. Security, Privacy & Governance

### Compliance Frameworks
1. **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (India):** All patient personal health data collection requires explicit informed consent, granular purpose specification, and rights to erasure/revocation [RESEARCHED].
2. **Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020:** Mandates that teleconsultations must be conducted by Registered Medical Practitioners (RMP) verified via HPR, maintaining strict prescription formats [RESEARCHED].
3. **ABDM Health Information Exchange Privacy Standards:** Enforces consent-driven data exchange where health records are decrypted only upon receipt of a valid, time-bound consent artefact generated by the ABDM Consent Manager [RESEARCHED].

### Security Controls Architecture
* **Data at Rest:** SQLite databases on field worker smartphones are encrypted using **SQLCipher (AES-256)** with encryption keys stored in the Android KeyStore / iOS Keychain [INFERENCE]. Server databases utilize AES-256 transparent data encryption [INFERENCE].
* **Data in Transit:** All client-server communications enforce **TLS 1.3 encryption** with Certificate Pinning on mobile applications to prevent Man-In-The-Middle (MITM) attacks [INFERENCE].
* **Access Control:** Strict **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** enforced via JWT tokens:
  * *ASHA/ANM:* Read/Write screening data for assigned village catchment area only.
  * *CHO:* Read/Write sub-centre vitals, initiate teleconsultation, view local inventory.
  * *Doctor:* Read longitudinal record upon patient consent, write teleconsultation notes and e-prescriptions.
  * *DHO:* Read-only aggregated analytical metrics; zero access to unblinded individual patient PII [INFERENCE].
* **Audit Logging:** Immutably logs every patient record access, prescription generation, and referral state change with timestamp, user ID, and IP address [INFERENCE].
